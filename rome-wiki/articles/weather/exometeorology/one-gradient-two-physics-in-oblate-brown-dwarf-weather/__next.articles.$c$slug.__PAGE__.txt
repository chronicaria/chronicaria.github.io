<ul>
<li>its <strong>direction and component ratios</strong> determine surface geometry, tangent planes, metric weights, and <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>f</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>2</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">Ω</mi><mo>⋅</mo><mi>n</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">f=2\Omega\cdot n</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1076em;">f</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">2Ω</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">⋅</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.4306em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span></span></span></span>;</li>

<p>The distinction would be invalid for an arbitrarily rescaled level-set function. It works here because the effective potential fixes the physical normalization.</p>

<h2 id="later-cloud-and-tidal-extensions">Later cloud and tidal extensions</h2>
<p>Program III adds a passive condensible cloud tracer and reports a roughly <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>2.4</mn><mo>×</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">2.4\times</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7278em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">2.4</span><span class="mord">×</span></span></span></span> increase in modeled rotational light-curve variability at a stated fiducial cloud opacity. It also explores a companion-locked tidal egg and a two-layer probe. The source explicitly limits the claims: the cloud does not feed back on dynamics, the amplification depends on opacity, the tidal headline is quoted only inside the stable fill range, and the two-layer eddies are resolution-limited.</p>
<p>The most interesting later observational conclusion is a degeneracy: one rotational light curve can confuse figure, spots, and clouds. Phase locking, harmonic content, wavelength dependence, and time-domain behavior are proposed discriminants, but no real figure is inferred from a light curve.</p>
<h2 id="what-this-does-not-establish">What this does not establish</h2>
<ul>
<li><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">Φ</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\Phi</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord">Φ</span></span></span></span> is a layer geopotential or thickness proxy, not brightness by itself.</li>
<li>Axisymmetric gravity darkening changes inclination-dependent mean flux but produces essentially no rotational modulation without non-axisymmetric weather or clouds.</li>
<li>The Roche point-mass figure overstates some forcing relative to a finite-density Maclaurin figure; the program records roughly a <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>76</mn><mi mathvariant="normal">%</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">76\%</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8056em;vertical-align:-0.0556em;"></span><span class="mord">76%</span></span></span></span> difference at matched flattening.</li>
<li>No brown-dwarf figure in this work is directly measured; flattening is hydrostatically inferred from assumed spin and structure.</li>
<li>The explicit core's calibrated stable range ends before the fastest near-breakup cases.</li>
<li>A successful shallow-water mechanism is not a three-dimensional brown-dwarf atmosphere.</li>
</ul>
