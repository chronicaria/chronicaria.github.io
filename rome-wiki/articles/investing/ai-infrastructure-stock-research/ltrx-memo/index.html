    Evidence

    Lantronix sells edge AI, industrial IoT compute, connectivity, SOMs, gateways, out-of-band management, software, and engineering services. Q3 FY2026 revenue was about 30.2M,andmanagementraisedFY2026dronerevenueexpectationstoabout30.2M, and management raised FY2026 drone revenue expectations to about 30.2M,andmanagementraisedFY2026dronerevenueexpectationstoabout10M-$14M.

    Nine-month FY2026 revenue was down YoY, but operating cash flow was positive at about 8M,cashwasabout8M, cash was about 8M,cashwasabout23.5M before later offering proceeds, and debt was manageable. Post-offering liquidity improves survival odds.

    The weak point is dilution and mix. The company established a $30M ATM, issued shares through it, then sold more shares in an underwritten offering. Product revenue still dominates, while software and services remain a small share.

    Deepening pass

    Nasdaq's July 6, 2026 snapshot showed 5.81pershare,5.81 per share, 5.81pershare,223.4M displayed market cap, and about 1.34M average volume. That displayed market cap likely trails pro forma dilution after the ATM and underwritten offering; mechanical pro forma market cap is probably closer to the mid-$200M range depending on final incremental share count.

    LTRX is the cleanest operating story in the top three. Q3 FY2026 revenue was 30.2M,grossmarginstayednear4330.2M, gross margin stayed near 43%, operating cash flow was positive for the nine-month period, debt was manageable, and the June offering added roughly 30.2M,grossmarginstayednear4332.3M net after full option exercise. The drone business is real enough to track: management raised FY2026 drone expectations to 10M−10M-10M−14M and suggested FY2027 drone revenue can roughly double.

    The bear case is also straightforward. Nine-month FY2026 revenue was down 4.6% YoY because a prior Gridspertise program disappeared, and Software and Services was still only 7.6% of nine-month revenue. If drones are mostly hardware modules and payload integration while customers capture the strategic value, LTRX remains an industrial IoT hardware company with AI-adjacent upside rather than a platform.

    Bear case

    LTRX may be a hardware turnaround with AI vocabulary rather than a high-multiple AI platform. If drone revenue fails to scale or software/services do not expand, the equity is less moonshot and more cyclical small-cap hardware.

    Falsifiers

    • FY2027 guidance fails to show double-digit growth.
    • Drone revenue misses the 10M−10M-10M−14M FY2026 range.
    • Software/services and deferred revenue fail to expand.

    Verdict

    Active research as the risk-adjusted anchor. LTRX is not the flashiest candidate, but it gives the basket one name with real revenue, improving liquidity, positive operating cash flow, and identifiable drone/edge-AI exposure.
