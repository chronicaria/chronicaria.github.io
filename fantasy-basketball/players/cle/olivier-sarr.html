Fantasy profile

  • 9-cat: if signed elsewhere, his speculative skills are BLK, REB, and some stretch-center shooting. There is no Cleveland volume to draft.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: remains unsigned or plays outside an NBA rotation.
  • Base: signs as a third center and appears only during injuries.
  • Ceiling: lands a backup job with 15-18 minutes and becomes a temporary blocks/rebounds streamer.

Returned via Raptors 905 in 2025-26 before Cleveland signed him to a two-way on March 3, 2026 (4 NBA games). Only 50 NBA games across five calendar years — the gaps are roster status and the Achilles, not recurring soft-tissue issues. Trajectory: The record shows a fringe stretch-center whose best sample (2021-22 OKC: 7.0 PPG, 4.2 RPG, .574 FG% in 19 minutes) predates a ruptured Achilles, with steadily shrinking roles since. Now 27 and a free agent, he competes in 2026-27 simply for an NBA contract — likely a two-way or third-center deal wherever he lands. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he secures a guaranteed roster spot and injuries hand him 15-18 minutes, at which point he is a short-term blocks/rebounds streamer in deep leagues.
