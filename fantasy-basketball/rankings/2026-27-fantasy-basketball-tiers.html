How to use this board

This is 12-team, nine-category redraft, with turnovers counted and value shown primarily per game. The # column preserves the mechanical order in the normalized projection snapshot; it is a reference coordinate, not a claim that No. 37 must be drafted before No. 38. Players may move freely within a tier based on build, platform eligibility, injury tolerance, games caps, and category need.

The category transform uses projected 3PM, PTS, REB, AST, STL, BLK, FG impact, FT impact, and inverted TO. Ratio impact is still less mature than counting-stat projections because some dossiers lack projected attempts; volume-sensitive ratio outliers should therefore be checked in the dossier before paying the displayed rank.

Reading the compact codes

  • Position shape: G, G/W, F, F/C, and C are broad draft-room archetypes, not a promise of Yahoo, ESPN, Fantrax, or Sleeper eligibility.
  • Category shape: + lists the player's clearest projected wins; − lists material drains. balanced means no category crosses this sheet's strong-drain threshold, not that the player is above average everywhere.
  • Risk: H = meaningful health/availability risk; H! = extreme availability tail; M = fragile or sub-25-minute role; Tx = contract/team uncertainty; Rk = 2026 rookie translation; Age = age/maintenance; Pct = ratio or punt dependence. A blank code does not mean zero risk.
  • Tot. rank (Δ): availability-adjusted totals rank from the same model. ↓8 is an eight-place totals penalty; ↑4 is a four-place totals boost. The adjustment uses projected games and is not raw 82-game multiplication.

Draft-room adjustment rules

  • Games caps or strong injury slots: Pull high-rate H/H! players upward; raw totals penalties matter less. In weekly lock leagues or shallow benches, move them down.
  • Punt FT: Giannis, Thompson, Gobert, Daniels, Mobley, Clingan, Kessler, Şengün, and several centers can jump a tier. Do not call the punt complete after one player; protect threes and guard stats.
  • Punt FG: High-volume guards gain, especially Ball, Harden, Young, VanVleet, and Brown Jr.; retain rebounds/blocks elsewhere.
  • Scarcity: Assists dry up faster than points and threes. Blocks remain available late, but late blockers often carry FT, minutes, or injury taxes.
  • Rookies: Rk means widen the role and rate distribution. Summer League can confirm on-ball reps or physical readiness; it should not turn a shooting streak into a stable percentage projection.
  • No current ADP: The July market desk found no defensible public post-draft 2026-27 redraft ADP set. This sheet is a value board, not a prediction of where rooms will select players.
