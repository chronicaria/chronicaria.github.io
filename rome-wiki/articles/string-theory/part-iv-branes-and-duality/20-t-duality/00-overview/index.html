    20 — T-Duality (Overview)

    The first genuinely stringy duality: a circle of radius R is physically identical to a circle of radius \alpha'/R , with momentum and winding swapping roles. The radius of a compact dimension is not an observable of string theory.

    Two rulers for one circle

    Curl up one spatial direction into a circle of circumference 2πR2\pi R2πR. A point particle has one way to notice the circle: its momentum around it is quantized, p=n/Rp=n/Rp=n/R, so it carries a ladder of states spaced by 1/R1/R1/R. Shrink the circle and the spacing widens; grow it and the ladder becomes a continuum. That spacing is how you measure RRR.

    A string can do something a particle cannot: it can wind around the circle and get stuck there, because a closed loop wrapped mmm times cannot slip off without cutting. Winding costs energy ∝R\propto R∝R — you paid tension to stretch the string — spaced by R/α′R/\alpha'R/α′. So a string carries two rulers that report opposite things:

    Δp=1R⏟momentum laddervs.Δw=Rα′⏟winding ladder.\underbrace{\Delta p = \tfrac{1}{R}}_{\text{momentum ladder}}\qquad\text{vs.}\qquad\underbrace{\Delta w = \tfrac{R}{\alpha'}}_{\text{winding ladder}}.momentum ladderΔp=R1​​​vs.winding ladderΔw=α′R​​​.

    Shrink the circle: the momentum ladder spreads out (looks small) but the winding ladder crowds together (looks large). The two are mirror images across R=α′R = \sqrt{\alpha'}R=α′​. And since a string carries both labels at once, swapping the two rulers — R↦α′/RR\mapsto\alpha'/RR↦α′/R together with n↔mn\leftrightarrow mn↔m — leaves the entire spectrum unchanged.

    A small circle and a large circle look different to a particle, but to a string they are the same world with the two rulers relabeled. You cannot ask "how big is the extra dimension?" — only "which ruler are you using?"

    The duality is not "replace RRR and keep the state labels." It compares two dictionaries for the same physics:

    R ⟼ R~=α′R,n ⟷ m.R\ \longmapsto\ \tilde R=\frac{\alpha'}{R},\qquad n\ \longleftrightarrow\ m.R ⟼ R~=Rα′​,n ⟷ m.

    The state called "nnn units of momentum" in one description is called "nnn units of winding" in the other. The momentum spacing 1/R1/R1/R of one description equals the winding spacing R/α′=1/R~R/\alpha' = 1/\tilde RR/α′=1/R~ of the other — that is the whole trade.

    Why it matters

    This is the conceptual seed of the entire web of string dualities (S-duality, U-duality, mirror symmetry): a physical equivalence between two backgrounds that look geometrically different. It also gives a minimal length. Every R<α′R<\sqrt{\alpha'}R<α′​ has an equivalent description with R~>α′\tilde R>\sqrt{\alpha'}R~>α′​, so shrinking a circle below the string length opens no new short-distance physics — it just relabels which ladder deserves the name "momentum." The self-dual radius R∗=α′R^*=\sqrt{\alpha'}R∗=α′​ is the edge of the fundamental domain, and there something dramatic happens: extra states go massless and the gauge symmetry is enhanced.

    Finally, T-duality is where D-branes earn their name. For open strings it forces Neumann and Dirichlet boundary conditions to exchange, so a Dppp-brane wrapping the circle becomes a D(p ⁣− ⁣1)(p\!-\!1)(p−1)-brane pinned to a point on the dual circle: the endpoint that was free to slide (Neumann) gets nailed down (Dirichlet). Distance is not destroyed — it is demoted from fixed background to a choice of dual variables, useful only in the regime where its light states are the natural probes.

    Scope

    The whole module is the bosonic string in light-cone gauge (D=26D=26D=26, normal-ordering constant a=−1a=-1a=−1 per sector). We prove the free-string operator equivalence; the interacting (string-loop) statement and the accompanying dilaton shift live in the worldsheet-CFT viewpoint, not here.

    Open questions

    • How much survives to the superstring? The open-string result is derived cleanly only for p≥2p\ge 2p≥2 in light-cone gauge (you need X0,X1X^0,X^1X0,X1 Neumann to build X±X^\pmX±); p=1,0p=1,0p=1,0 are true but need covariant quantization. The Type II version additionally maps IIA↔\leftrightarrow↔IIB.
    • The enhanced spectrum at R∗R^*R∗ includes tachyon-dependent scalar states in the bosonic theory. Which parts of the enhancement survive in tachyon-free (super/heterotic) compactifications is a later question.
