Role and evidence

The July 18 roster lists Huntley as one of Phoenix's three two-way players. The live team page also reported an unspecified Summer League hamstring injury and no firm return timetable. Sources: RealGM current roster, accessed 2026-07-18, NBA Suns team page, accessed 2026-07-18. Confidence: high on status, medium on dynamic injury wording.

Huntley recorded 10 points, four rebounds, an assist, and a steal in 29 minutes in the April 12 short-handed finale. That appearance illustrates emergency utility, not a settled rotation. Source: official NBA gamebook, 2026-04-12. Confidence: high on sample, low on extrapolation.

Phoenix has standard-contract depth at every frontcourt position: Miles Bridges, Dillon Brooks, Rasheer Fleming, Ryan Dunn, Haywood Highsmith, Koa Peat, Mark Williams, Oso Ighodaro, and Khaman Maluach. Huntley's NBA path therefore requires both health and multiple absences.

Huntley has no reliable NBA points projection beyond emergency appearances. Dynasty managers should evaluate G League minutes, health, and any conversion before box-score flashes.

Huntley is not a redraft target. The first actionable checkpoints are hamstring clearance, G League role, and whether Phoenix uses the third two-way slot differently before opening night.
