Ousmane Dieng

Fantasy case

In 30 games with Milwaukee in 2025-26, Dieng averaged 11.0 points, 4.6 rebounds, 3.6 assists, 0.8 steals, and 1.5 threes in 26.8 minutes. The opportunity was real, but .423 shooting and .667 at the line prevented a clean fantasy breakout. The post-Giannis roster offers another development window, even though Jaime Jaquez Jr, Kyle Kuzma, Brayden Burries, and Nate Ament compete at forward.

His passing is the differentiator. If Dieng earns a second-unit connector role, 2-3 assists in limited minutes are realistic. To become draftable, he needs either 22+ minutes or a material rise in steals/blocks and shooting efficiency.

Learning notes

Dieng's per-minute assists make him worth remembering even when his draft rank does not. His waiver signal is 20+ minutes plus stable closing appearances, not a single scoring spike.

Career record

Pre-NBA: French forward developed at INSEP/Centre Fédéral, then the first European in the NBL's Next Stars program — New Zealand Breakers 2021-22: 23 GP, 8.9 PPG, 3.1 RPG, 1.0 APG in about 20 MPG. Draft: 2022, Round 1, Pick 11 — selected by New York, traded on draft night to Oklahoma City for three 2023 first-round picks.

Durability: The low OKC game counts were mostly role-driven — G League assignments and a contender's deep wing rotation — plus a fractured wrist that interrupted his rookie year. No chronic injury pattern. Trajectory: Four seasons in, the record is a lottery pick who never cracked a loaded Thunder rotation (never above 4.9 PPG or 15 MPG in three OKC years) and finally got a real look after the Milwaukee trade: 11.0/4.6/3.6 with 1.5 threes in 26.8 minutes across 30 Bucks games. The passing growth is real — 2.4 APG at 19.3 minutes season-wide — but FG% has sat between .420 and .432 every year of his career, and the stocks remain negligible. In 2026-27 he competes for a second-unit connector role in Milwaukee's post-Giannis forward group, contract security in hand. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he holds 22+ minutes with closing appearances, where 10/5/3 with a three or so is a plausible top-160 outcome; at the projected 8 MPG he is undraftable in standard formats.
