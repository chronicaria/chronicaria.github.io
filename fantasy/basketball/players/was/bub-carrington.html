Bub Carrington

Bub Carrington is a durable young assists handcuff whose fantasy relevance now depends largely on Trae Young's availability.

Role and baseline

  • 2025-26: played all 82 games at 27.7 MPG, the strongest durability baseline on the roster.
  • Role change: Young's arrival and long-term extension remove Carrington's clean starting path, while George and Dybantsa can also initiate.
  • 2026-27 base role: backup point guard; 14 base minutes. A Young absence can lift him toward 26-30.

Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: top 170-210 base, primarily an assists/three stream without strong percentages or stocks.
  • Points: the direct handcuff to Young; his value rises faster in assist-heavy scoring formats.
  • Dynasty: top 100-130, balancing youth and durability against a four-year star blockage.
  • Best use: watch-list rather than draft in standard leagues, then add immediately when Young sits.

Floor, median and ceiling

  • Floor: 10-12 minutes and no standard-league value.
  • Median: 14-minute backup with occasional schedule-stream utility.
  • Ceiling: prolonged Young absence, starting role and top-110 value.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Baltimore native (St. Frances Academy); one season at Pittsburgh (2023-24): 13.8 PTS, 5.2 REB, 4.1 AST per game with a triple-double in his collegiate debut — Freshman All-America, ACC All-Rookie, Honorable Mention All-ACC. Draft: 2024, Round 1, Pick 14, Portland — traded to Washington on draft night.

Durability: 164 of 164 possible games through two seasons — one of the league's few perfect-attendance young guards. No injury history at any level.

Trajectory: The record shows a 20-year-old who has already logged two full seasons of starter-level minutes with a real shooting jump in year two (3P% .339 to .408 on more attempts) while assists ticked up; the costs are FG% still in the low .420s, rising turnovers (2.3), and thin stocks. Trae Young's arrival converts him from incumbent starter to backup initiator, which is why the 2026-27 projection sits far below his career MPG. He competes with George for backup-initiator minutes and becomes fantasy-relevant the day Young sits: his 82-game durability plus a proven 27-30-minute baseline of ~10/3.5/4.5 with two threes makes him the cleanest handcuff-add in the league when that happens.
