<h2 id="decision-layer">Decision layer</h2>
<h2 id="retention-rule">Retention rule</h2>
<p>A candidate remains in the live wiki only while it is part of the current top slate or supplies evidence needed to distinguish those candidates. A scheduled research pass should revise the existing dossier, record changed gates, and retire a candidate when it no longer clears that bar.</p>
