Fantasy case

Burries averaged 16.1 points, 4.9 rebounds, 2.4 assists, and shot 39.1% from three in college. The NBA's draft profile describes a two-way impact wing, and his 26-point July 12 Summer League game supplied an early professional scoring signal.

Update 2026-07-19: the full Las Vegas sample confirmed the signal — 23.5 points, 4.5 rebounds, 4.2 assists, 2.0 steals, and 1.0 blocks per game on .500/.364/.667 splits with only three total turnovers, capped by a 27-point, five-assist game against Philadelphia on July 18 that included the game-winning layup. The steals-plus-ball-security combination speaks directly to the category question below; the crowded-wing obstacle is unchanged. HoopsRumors Bucks Notes, 2026-07-19 He is more likely than Nate Ament to help redraft managers immediately because his body, handle, and shot are further along.

The obstacle is Milwaukee's crowded wing room. Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr, Ousmane Dieng, AJ Green, Gary Trent Jr, and Caris LeVert all compete for adjacent minutes. Burries's best seasonal stretch may come after a veteran trade.

Outcomes and category fit

  • Low: 10-13 MPG with frequent DNPs; deep dynasty hold only.
  • Base: 17 MPG, modest points/threes, about 0.8 steals; waiver-stream upside.
  • High: 24+ MPG after roster consolidation; 12-14 points, 1.2 steals, top-120 push.
  • Strengths: balanced counting stats, steal potential, workable threes.
  • Liabilities: rookie efficiency, turnovers, uncertain FT volume.

Confidence

Roster high · Minutes low-medium · Rates low-medium · Availability medium-high. His draft investment and rebuild fit are secure; the opening-night rotation is not.

Learning notes

Burries is a minutes bet more than a one-category bet. He can stay on the floor without monopolizing the ball, which gives him the best chance among Milwaukee's rookies to earn a stable first-year role.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Arizona, one season (2025-26): team-high 16.1 PPG with 4.9 RPG, 2.4 APG, and a team-leading 1.5 SPG on .491/.391/.805 across 39 games; first-team All-Big 12 and the lead guard on Arizona's first Final Four team in 25 years. Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 10, Milwaukee.

No NBA record — his most recent competitive basketball is the 2025-26 Arizona season plus the July 2026 Las Vegas Summer League (23.5 PTS, 4.5 REB, 4.2 AST, 2.0 STL, 1.0 BLK on .500/.364/.667 with three total turnovers).

Durability: 39 of a possible 39 games as a freshman; no injury history on record.

Trajectory: The record shows a high-volume combo guard who scored efficiently against Big 12 competition and generated steals without turnovers, both of which repeated in Summer League. In 2026-27 he competes with Milwaukee's crowded veteran wing group (Herro, Jaquez, Trent, LeVert, Green) for a 16-minute rookie role. He becomes fantasy-relevant if a veteran trade or injury pushes him past 20 minutes, at which point the points-threes-steals mix projects to a top-150 push rather than a stash.
