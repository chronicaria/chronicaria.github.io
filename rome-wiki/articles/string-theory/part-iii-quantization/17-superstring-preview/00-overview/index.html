    Three diseases of the bosonic string

    You reached the end of Part III with a fully quantized bosonic string and its spectrum from the Mass-shell relation. It is beautiful and unphysical, for three independently fatal reasons:

    1. Only bosons. Every state in the bosonic spectrum is a boson, but matter is made of fermions — electrons, quarks, neutrinos. A theory with no fermions cannot describe our world.
    2. A tachyon. The ground state has M2<0M^2 < 0M2<0. A negative mass-squared is not a fast particle; it is an instability. The vacuum you expanded around is a hilltop, not a valley, and the theory wants to roll off it — like a pencil balanced on its tip, where M2<0M^2<0M2<0 says the wobble grows instead of oscillating.
    3. Twenty-six dimensions. Consistency forced D=26D=26D=26, far more than the world seems to want and a great deal to hide.

    The one repair: worldsheet fermions

    The fix is a single new ingredient. Alongside each coordinate Xμ(τ,σ)X^\mu(\tau,\sigma)Xμ(τ,σ) — the string's position — put an anticommuting partner field ψμ(τ,σ)\psi^\mu(\tau,\sigma)ψμ(τ,σ) living on the same (τ,σ)(\tau,\sigma)(τ,σ) worldsheet:

    Xμ(τ,σ)  ⟶  ( Xμ(τ,σ), ψμ(τ,σ) ).X^\mu(\tau,\sigma) \;\longrightarrow\; \big(\,X^\mu(\tau,\sigma),\ \psi^\mu(\tau,\sigma)\,\big).Xμ(τ,σ)⟶(Xμ(τ,σ), ψμ(τ,σ)).

    "Anticommuting" is the entire point: any two Grassmann values θ1,θ2\theta_1,\theta_2θ1​,θ2​ satisfy θ1θ2=− θ2θ1\theta_1\theta_2 = -\,\theta_2\theta_1θ1​θ2​=−θ2​θ1​, so a single value used twice vanishes (θ2=0\theta^2=0θ2=0). That is the Pauli exclusion principle written into the algebra, and it is what makes some of the resulting states behave as fermions. Quantizing this enriched string — bosonic oscillators from XXX plus fermionic oscillators from ψ\psiψ — is what the rest of the module does. Three payoffs cure the three diseases:

    1. D=10D=10D=10, not 26. The fermions add their own term to the same Lorentz-anomaly balance that fixed D=26D=26D=26 before; the new arithmetic lands on 10.
    2. The tachyon is projected out. The 17.1 GSO projection — a clean truncation of the spectrum — deletes exactly the would-be tachyonic state, so the spectrum now starts at M2=0M^2=0M2=0.
    3. Fermions appear, and boson count = fermion count. The periodic (Ramond) sector supplies spacetime fermions; after GSO the surviving spectrum has equal numbers of bosons and fermions at every mass level. That balance is spacetime supersymmetry.

    Honest scope: what this preview does and does not prove

    The module makes two grades of claim, and keeps them apart.

    • Derived from the bosonic core, re-checkable with what you already have: the sector split from boundary conditions, the mode expansions, the Clifford-algebra count of Ramond ground states, the massless spectra, and the 8=88=88=8 boson/fermion match at the massless level.
    • Quoted as a superstring fact, with its limits marked, not rebuilt from scratch: the critical value D=10D=10D=10, the full consistency of the GSO projection, and that Ramond states are genuine ten-dimensional spacetime spinors. These need the RNS/superconformal machinery, which lives beyond this bosonic-first vault.

    The goal is usable intuition, not a finished RNS course. Read D=10D=10D=10 and GSO below as signposts, not theorems.

    Exercise

    Answer with no notes, then check yourself: Why does a physically realistic string theory need more than the bosonic string — and what single ingredient does the superstring add to fix it? State the three diseases (and why each is fatal), name the one new field, and list the three symptoms it relieves.     Solution

    Three fatal diseases of the bosonic string:

    1. Only bosons. Its entire spectrum is bosonic, so no electrons, quarks, or neutrinos can emerge. Fatal because real matter is fermionic — the theory simply lacks the particles the world is built from.
    2. A tachyon. The ground state has M2<0M^2<0M2<0. Fatal not because anything moves faster than light but because it signals an unstable vacuum: the theory is perched on a hilltop and rolls off. (The pencil-on-its-tip picture: M2<0M^2<0M2<0 means the small wobble grows.)
    3. Twenty-six dimensions. Consistency forces D=26D=26D=26. Fatal as physics: far from the observed dimensionality and a great deal to hide.

    The one new ingredient: an anticommuting worldsheet fermion ψμ(τ,σ)\psi^\mu(\tau,\sigma)ψμ(τ,σ) paired with each coordinate XμX^\muXμ. Anticommuting means any two Grassmann values satisfy θ1θ2=−θ2θ1\theta_1\theta_2=-\theta_2\theta_1θ1​θ2​=−θ2​θ1​, hence θ2=0\theta^2=0θ2=0 — Pauli exclusion in the algebra — which is what lets the quantized string carry fermionic states.

    Three symptoms relieved:

    1. The critical dimension becomes D=10D=10D=10 (the ψ\psiψ modes shift the Lorentz-anomaly balance).
    2. The GSO projection deletes the would-be tachyon; the lightest surviving NS state is massless, so the spectrum starts at M2=0M^2=0M2=0.
    3. The Ramond sector supplies spacetime fermions, and after GSO the boson and fermion counts match at every mass level — spacetime supersymmetry.

    Derived vs. quoted: All three relief statements are quoted at preview level — D=10D=10D=10, the GSO consistency, and full spacetime supersymmetry are asserted, not proved here. What the module genuinely derives is the supporting scaffolding: the NS/R boundary-condition split, the two mass formulas (α′M2=−12+N⊥\alpha'M^2=-\tfrac12+N^\perpα′M2=−21​+N⊥ and α′M2=N⊥\alpha'M^2=N^\perpα′M2=N⊥), the 16=2416=2^416=24 Ramond ground-state count, and the concrete 8=88=88=8 (open) / 128=128128=128128=128 (closed) boson–fermion match at the massless level, which is the visible face of that supersymmetry.
