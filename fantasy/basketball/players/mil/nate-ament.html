Fantasy case

Ament averaged 16.7 points, 6.3 rebounds, and 2.3 assists in college. The NBA's draft review highlights both the fluid perimeter skill and the need to add strength at roughly 6-foot-10, 211 pounds; he also ended the college season with an ankle issue. His Summer League started slowly—about seven points on five shots across his first three games—before a 23-point breakout on July 13.

That variance is the dossier. Ament's combination of size, shooting projection, and weak-side stocks can eventually produce multi-category value, but Kyle Kuzma, Ousmane Dieng, Jaime Jaquez Jr, Kel'el Ware, and Bogoljub Marković all occupy forward development minutes.

Outcomes and category fit

  • Low: G League-heavy season, fewer than 300 NBA minutes.
  • Base: spot duty and late-season auditions; dynasty taxi hold.
  • High: body/shot translate early, 15-18 MPG after deadline, blocks and threes streamability.
  • Strengths: positional size, theoretical threes-plus-blocks, rebounding upside.
  • Liabilities: strength, immediate efficiency, uncertain role and availability history.

Learning notes

Ament should be valued as an archetype bet, not a rookie-minutes bet. His dynasty ceiling comes from eventually combining wing shooting with big-man defensive stats.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Tennessee, one season (2025-26): 35 GP (35 starts), 29.7 MPG, 16.7 PTS, 6.3 REB, 2.3 AST, 1.0 STL, 0.6 BLK on .399 FG / .333 3P / .790 FT; second-team All-SEC and SEC All-Freshman, 584 points (third-most by a Tennessee freshman), Elite Eight run. Consensus five-star and No. 4 recruit in the 2025 class (Highland School, VA; McDonald's All-American). Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 13, Miami Heat — rights traded to Milwaukee on July 6, 2026 in the Giannis Antetokounmpo/Tyler Herro blockbuster; signed four years, $25.98M on July 9, 2026.

No NBA record — 2026 draftee; most recent competitive play is the Tennessee season plus 2026 Summer League (quiet first three games, then a 23-point breakout on July 13).

Durability: Missed two college games (late-season ankle issue); otherwise started every game he was available for.

Trajectory: The record shows a high-usage 6-10 freshman scorer whose volume (16.7 PPG) outran his efficiency (.399 FG) — the classic tools-first lottery profile. In 2026-27 he competes for post-Giannis forward development minutes against Kuzma, Dieng, Jaquez and Marković on a rebuilding roster that has every incentive to play him eventually. He becomes fantasy-relevant in redraft only if the shot stabilizes enough to force 15-18 MPG after the deadline; the dynasty case is the threes-plus-blocks archetype maturing on Milwaukee's timeline, not this season's line.
