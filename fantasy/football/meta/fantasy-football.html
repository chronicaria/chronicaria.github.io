The league — released 10-team state, 2026-08-02

This is a 10-team league with the 1.01 locked in a standard snake. The 16-pick sequence is 1 · 20 · 21 · 40 · 41 · 60 · 61 · 80 · 81 · 100 · 101 · 120 · 121 · 140 · 141 · 160.

Confirmed league settings: ESPN · full PPR with fractional yardage · 9 starters (1QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1D-ST/1K) + 7 bench + 1 IR · no keepers · waivers reset weekly to inverse standings · regular season weeks 1–14 · six-team playoffs weeks 15–17 · drafting in late August. The exact draft date remains unknown.

Money, confirmed as stated: each of the 10 teams pays $100: $75 contribution plus a $25 refundable participation deposit. Prizes are $500 / $250 / $150, totaling $900. The 10 × $75 = $750 contribution total does not equal the $900 prize total; this mismatch is confirmed league information, not an error for this vault to silently reconcile.

Three things that follow, and drive everything else

The payout is convex, so build for ceiling. Finishing 4th through 10th pays nothing; three of ten teams reach the money, and first is worth more than second and third combined. Build a high mean in rounds 1–4 to reach the six-team playoff, then prefer ceiling as the roster fills. The prize/contribution mismatch above is a cash-flow fact, not a reason to change this strategic conclusion.

Waiver priority is use-it-or-lose-it. It resets each week to inverse standings rather than rolling forward. An unspent claim expires; winning reduces access to the next breakout; and losing teams get first claim.

Full PPR reshapes the board. A back who catches 60 passes gains 60 points relative to non-PPR. The frozen board uses that scoring model and the league's 10-team replacement baselines.

Data

The spreadsheet is truth and the notes are the rendering — the reverse of the basketball build's architecture.

Run order — the only ordering constraint in the build:

bash cd "Sports/NFL/Fantasy Football/Data" && ./fetch_nflverse.sh && python3 build_context.py && python3 build_board.py && python3 simulate_season.py

  • Data/players.csv — frozen August 2 canonical board input: 237 rows, sourced from the current projection/ADP import and scored for this league's 10-team setup
  • Data/player_context.csv — 197 skill-player context rows aligned to the promoted player pool; it supplies dated roster, injury, contract, and usage context rather than a second ranking system
  • Data/build_context.py — joins nflverse rosters, season stat lines, weekly injury reports and snap counts with OverTheCap's contract pages. Carries a join validator that catches wrong-player matches
  • Data/build_board.py — renders the board, the tier stacks and Availability and injury risk. --check runs its self-check
  • Data/simulate_season.py — the draft-and-season Monte Carlo behind When to start drafting for upside
  • Data/raw/ — the dated source pulls · Data/check_links.py — the link checker

Fresh research is promoted: FFToday stat-line pages updated Jul. 30, a Fantasy Football Calculator 10-team PPR window covering 4,121 drafts from Jul. 26–Aug. 2, and a FantasyPros PPR consensus audit inform the August 2 freeze. FFC remains a clearly labelled proxy for ESPN ADP. Kicker points, D/ST points, Avail, and Adj VOR remain model outputs.

Why this is not a copy of the basketball build

2026-27 Fantasy Basketball Encyclopedia is 644 notes and roughly 852,000 words, written for someone already fluent. This is about 100 notes, for someone who does not watch NFL football.

  • One scoring format, one board — full PPR.
  • Position is the primary axis. The basketball tiers deliberately ignore position; here position scarcity, the tight-end cliff and bye weeks are the draft.
  • No projection engine. Consensus projections get scored under this league's rules; the effort goes into roster shape.
  • No categories, so no punt builds. Football is one summed number.
  • One 32-row table, not 32 team desks.
  • A literacy layer basketball never needed.
