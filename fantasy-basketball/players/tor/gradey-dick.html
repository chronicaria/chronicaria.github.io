Gradey Dick

Baseline and projection

Dick played 76 games but started only once, averaging 14.0 minutes, 6.0 points, 1.9 rebounds, 0.7 assists, 0.6 steals, 0.1 blocks, 0.7 threes and 0.6 turnovers on .419/.875 shooting. Fantasy value

  • Nine-category / points: outside standard-league range in Toronto; 8.3 simple FP/G.
  • Dynasty: rank 130-190 because a Clippers move could unlock a 22-28 MPG shooting role.
  • Ceiling: trade closes and LA develops him as a movement shooter, producing two-plus threes and low turnovers.
  • Floor: deal collapses and Toronto's defensive wings keep him below 12 MPG.

His fantasy valuation should be destination-weighted. The low Toronto projection is not a talent verdict; it reflects roster congestion and the reported deal.

Durability: 60 GP as a rookie (rotation and development assignments), 54 in 2024-25 — ruled out for the season on April 4, 2025 with a lingering right knee injury — then a fully healthy 76-game 2025-26 in a shrunken role, so the knee did not linger into year three.

Trajectory: The record is a rise-and-squeeze: a 14.4-point, 2.1-three sophomore season at 29 minutes proved the scoring-shooter role works, then Toronto cut him to 14 minutes and one start in 2025-26 even while he stayed healthy, with career-long marks of roughly 34% from three against 86% from the line suggesting the shooting talent has not yet converted to NBA range efficiency. His 2026-27 role is destination-dependent — he is packaged in the held Kawhi Leonard trade to the Clippers — and he becomes fantasy-relevant when either the deal closes into a 22-28 minute movement-shooter role or a Toronto wing exodus restores his 2024-25 minutes; his year-two line is the proven template at that workload.
