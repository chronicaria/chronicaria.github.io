    Transverse coordinates as physical degrees of freedom

    After light-cone gauge fixing, a classical open string does only D-2 things: it wiggles in the D-2 transverse directions X^I . The other two coordinates carry no independent motion — X^+ is a clock, and X^- is fixed by the transverse wiggles up to two constants p^+, x_0^- .

    Imagine a jump rope. You can whip it up and down and side to side — those motions change its shape. But sliding beads along the rope changes nothing about the rope's shape in space; it just relabels which bead sits where. A string is the same. Motion along its own length is not real motion — it is a relabeling of the worldsheet coordinate σ\sigmaσ, which is pure gauge. The only motion that changes the string's shape and position is transverse to the string. Light-cone gauge is the coordinate system that makes this obvious: the physical fields literally are the transverse coordinates XIX^IXI.

    The claim, then: of the DDD embedding coordinates, only D−2D-2D−2 are physical. For D=26D=26D=26 that is 242424 — a number that reappears everywhere in the spectrum.

    Why "along the string" is not motion

    The constraint X˙⋅X′=0\dot X \cdot X' = 0X˙⋅X′=0 says the velocity of each point of the string is perpendicular to the string. Why is longitudinal velocity forbidden as physical motion? Because it is not motion at all — it is a change of label.

    Make the label change infinitesimal. Shift the spatial worldsheet coordinate by σ→σ~=σ−ϵ(τ,σ)\sigma \to \tilde\sigma = \sigma - \epsilon(\tau,\sigma)σ→σ~=σ−ϵ(τ,σ) while keeping the same geometric string. The embedding at the new label is

    Xμ(τ,σ~)=Xμ(τ,σ−ϵ)=Xμ(τ,σ)−ϵ Xμ′(τ,σ)+O(ϵ2),X^\mu(\tau,\tilde\sigma) = X^\mu(\tau,\sigma-\epsilon) = X^\mu(\tau,\sigma) - \epsilon\, X^{\mu\prime}(\tau,\sigma) + O(\epsilon^2),Xμ(τ,σ~)=Xμ(τ,σ−ϵ)=Xμ(τ,σ)−ϵXμ′(τ,σ)+O(ϵ2),

    so the induced change is

    δXμ=−ϵ Xμ′.\delta X^\mu = -\epsilon\, X^{\mu\prime}.δXμ=−ϵXμ′.

    Since Xμ′X^{\mu\prime}Xμ′ is the tangent to the string, δXμ\delta X^\muδXμ points along the string. A displacement along the tangent is exactly what relabeling σ\sigmaσ produces — it is gauge redundancy, not a new configuration. The constraint X˙⋅X′=0\dot X\cdot X' = 0X˙⋅X′=0 then picks the representative whose actual velocity is orthogonal to this gauge direction. Motion orthogonal to the string is physical; motion along it is pure gauge.

    Counting: DDD fields become D−2D-2D−2

    Now do the bookkeeping. Start with DDD coordinate fields Xμ(τ,σ)X^\mu(\tau,\sigma)Xμ(τ,σ) and watch two of them get eliminated.

    1. X+X^+X+ is frozen by the gauge. The gauge choice (Fixing X-plus as worldsheet time) sets, in natural units (ℏ=c=1\hbar=c=1ℏ=c=1), X+=2α′p+τ,x0+=0,αn+=0  (n≠0).X^+ = 2\alpha' p^+\tau, \qquad x_0^+ = 0,\quad \alpha_n^+ = 0\ \ (n\neq0).X+=2α′p+τ,x0+​=0,αn+​=0  (n=0). No zero-mode position, no oscillators — just the single number p+p^+p+. X+X^+X+ is the clock written as a field, carrying zero dynamical data. (One field gone.)

    2. X−X^-X− is solved by the constraints. The constraints (Light-cone constraints) give X˙−±X−′=14α′p+(X˙I±XI′)2,\dot X^- \pm X^{-\prime} = \frac{1}{4\alpha'p^+}\big(\dot X^I \pm X^{I\prime}\big)^2,X˙−±X−′=4α′p+1​(X˙I±XI′)2, which fixes both worldsheet derivatives of X−X^-X− from the XIX^IXI. A field with both partials known is determined up to one additive constant x0−x_0^-x0−​. So X−X^-X− carries zero dynamical fields, just that one number. (Second field gone.)

    3. What survives: the transverse XIX^IXI, I=2,…,D−1I = 2,\dots,D-1I=2,…,D−1 — that is D−2D-2D−2 fields — plus the two constants p+,x0−p^+, x_0^-p+,x0−​.

      Free data={ XI(τ,σ), p+, x0− }  \boxed{\;\text{Free data} = \big\{\,X^I(\tau,\sigma),\ p^+,\ x_0^-\,\big\}\;}Free data={XI(τ,σ), p+, x0−​}​

    Two of the DDD coordinates were gauge or constraint artifacts; the genuine wiggling lives in D−2D-2D−2 transverse directions. In mode language you freely choose x0I,pI,αnIx_0^I, p^I, \alpha_n^Ix0I​,pI,αnI​ (which assemble XIX^IXI) plus p+,x0−p^+, x_0^-p+,x0−​; then X+X^+X+ and every non-constant part of X−X^-X− are computed, never chosen. In particular no αn−\alpha_n^-αn−​ oscillator is ever selected independently — that absence is the content of the claim.

    How X−X^-X− is reconstructed: transverse Virasoro modes

    "Physical = transverse" is only honest if X−X^-X− really is computable from the XIX^IXI. Here is the explicit map, mode by mode (Mode expansions in light-cone gauge):

    2α′ αn−=12p+∑p∈Zαn−pI αpI.\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_n^- = \frac{1}{2p^+}\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\alpha_{n-p}^I\,\alpha_p^I.2α′​αn−​=2p+1​∑p∈Z​αn−pI​αpI​.

    Every oscillator of X−X^-X− is a quadratic combination of the transverse oscillators αI\alpha^IαI. Crucially the spacetime index on the right runs over transverse labels III only — that is the precise sense in which X−X^-X− is a function of the physical data, not independent of it.

    The quadratic combination earns a name, the transverse Virasoro mode:

    L_n^\perp \equiv \frac12\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\alpha_{n-p}^I\,\alpha_p^I, \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_n^- = \frac{1}{p^+}\,L_n^\perp. \tag{1}

    The "⊥\perp⊥" is because the sum is over transverse indices only. These are the classical ancestors of the quantum Virasoro modes that organize the whole spectrum; here they show up humbly as the oscillation modes of X−X^-X−. (Contrast the covariant Ln=12∑mαn−m⋅αmL_n = \tfrac12\sum_m \alpha_{n-m}\cdot\alpha_mLn​=21​∑m​αn−m​⋅αm​, which sums over all μ\muμ because no gauge has been fixed. Light-cone gauge is exactly what collapses the "⋅\cdot⋅" to a sum over "III".)

    Assembling the modes, the reconstructed field is

    X−(τ,σ)=x0−+1p+L0⊥ τ+ip+∑n≠01n Ln⊥ e−inτcos⁡nσ.X^-(\tau,\sigma) = x_0^- + \frac{1}{p^+}L_0^\perp\,\tau + \frac{i}{p^+}\sum_{n\neq0}\frac{1}{n}\,L_n^\perp\, e^{-in\tau}\cos n\sigma.X−(τ,σ)=x0−​+p+1​L0⊥​τ+p+i​n=0∑​n1​Ln⊥​e−inτcosnσ.

    Once the transverse oscillators are chosen, every term here is fixed except the constant x0−x_0^-x0−​.

    Even the energy is transverse

    2p+p−=1α′ L0⊥.2p^+ p^- = \frac{1}{\alpha'}\,L_0^\perp.2p+p−=α′1​L0⊥​.

Nothing physical lives outside the XIX^IXI — which is why the next module can quantize the string by quantizing only the αnI\alpha_n^IαnI​.

    Open questions

    • The count "D−2D-2D−2 physical fields" is classical. Quantum mechanically the same transverse count is used, but only in D=26D=26D=26 does the non-manifest Lorentz symmetry survive without an anomaly — deferred to the light-cone quantization notes.

    Exercise

    The reconstruction relation (1) says 2α′ αn−=Ln⊥/p+\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_n^- = L_n^\perp/p^+2α′​αn−​=Ln⊥​/p+. Verify that the transverse Virasoro modes automatically obey L−n⊥=(Ln⊥)∗L_{-n}^\perp = (L_n^\perp)^*L−n⊥​=(Ln⊥​)∗, using only the transverse reality condition α−mI=(αmI)∗\alpha_{-m}^I = (\alpha_m^I)^*α−mI​=(αmI​)∗. Why is this a nontrivial consistency check on the claim that X−X^-X− is a genuine real coordinate reconstructed from the XIX^IXI?

    Solution

    Start from the definition and conjugate: (Ln⊥)∗=(12∑pαn−pIαpI)∗=12∑p(αn−pI)∗(αpI)∗.(L_n^\perp)^* = \left(\frac12\sum_{p}\alpha_{n-p}^I\alpha_p^I\right)^* = \frac12\sum_{p}(\alpha_{n-p}^I)^*(\alpha_p^I)^*.(Ln⊥​)∗=(21​∑p​αn−pI​αpI​)∗=21​∑p​(αn−pI​)∗(αpI​)∗. Apply the transverse reality condition (αmI)∗=α−mI(\alpha_m^I)^* = \alpha_{-m}^I(αmI​)∗=α−mI​ to each factor: (Ln⊥)∗=12∑pα−(n−p)I α−pI=12∑pα−n+pI α−pI.(L_n^\perp)^* = \frac12\sum_{p}\alpha_{-(n-p)}^I\,\alpha_{-p}^I = \frac12\sum_{p}\alpha_{-n+p}^I\,\alpha_{-p}^I.(Ln⊥​)∗=21​∑p​α−(n−p)I​α−pI​=21​∑p​α−n+pI​α−pI​. Relabel the summation index p→−pp \to -pp→−p (a bijection of Z\mathbb ZZ, so the sum is unchanged): (Ln⊥)∗=12∑pα−n−pI αpI=L−n⊥.(L_n^\perp)^* = \frac12\sum_{p}\alpha_{-n-p}^I\,\alpha_{p}^I = L_{-n}^\perp.(Ln⊥​)∗=21​∑p​α−n−pI​αpI​=L−n⊥​. So L−n⊥=(Ln⊥)∗L_{-n}^\perp = (L_n^\perp)^*L−n⊥​=(Ln⊥​)∗. Then from (1), 2α′ α−n−=L−n⊥/p+=(Ln⊥)∗/p+=(2α′ αn−)∗\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_{-n}^- = L_{-n}^\perp/p^+ = (L_n^\perp)^*/p^+ = (\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_n^-)^*2α′​α−n−​=L−n⊥​/p+=(Ln⊥​)∗/p+=(2α′​αn−​)∗ (using p+p^+p+ real), i.e. α−n−=(αn−)∗\alpha_{-n}^- = (\alpha_n^-)^*α−n−​=(αn−​)∗ — exactly the reality condition a real field's oscillators must obey.

    Why nontrivial: we did not impose that X−X^-X− is real; we derived it. The reconstruction (1) manufactures the αn−\alpha_n^-αn−​ out of transverse data, and it could in principle have produced complex oscillators inconsistent with a real coordinate. That it doesn't — that reality of the XIX^IXI forces reality of the reconstructed X−X^-X− — is a check that the constraint solution genuinely yields a legitimate spacetime coordinate, not just a formal expression.
