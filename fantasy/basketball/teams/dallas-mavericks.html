Team Desk · Southwest Division

Dallas Mavericks

Dallas is a high-variance fantasy ecosystem: Cooper Flagg is the only bankable early-round volume source, while Kyrie Irving's reconstructed ACL and Dereck Lively II's unresolved foot recovery redistribute value throughout the rotation.

  • Format: nine-category, per-game projection. FG% (FGA) and FT% (FTA) preserve percentage-volume impact; 3PM is made threes; turnovers are the ninth category.

Verified roster and depth chart

Dusty May is the head coach, hired from Michigan in June 2026 after Jason Kidd was dismissed following a 26-56 season. The front office is also new: Masai Ujiri became president and alternate governor on May 4, 2026, and Mike Schmitz was named general manager on May 8 after serving as Portland's assistant GM. Ujiri has publicly framed the offseason as future-based, which raises rather than lowers the odds that Washington, Gafford, or Thompson is moved in-season.

The NBA team page listed the full Dallas inventory on July 18, but its acquisition field still described Tarik Biberović as rights-only even after Dallas announced his July 10 signing. Dated transaction records outrank that stale field. Sergio De Larrea signed a four-year rookie-scale contract on July 3, 2026 at 120% of scale ($3.18M for 2026-27); he is a rostered rookie, not a draft-and-stash. Tobi Lawal is on a two-way contract; John Poulakidas was also signed to a two-way in March. Vsevolod Ishchenko remains a draft-rights stash in this model. Same-day transaction uncertainty: restricted free agent Moussa Cisse agreed to a two-year Knicks offer sheet on July 18, leaving Dallas 48 hours to match. His row is therefore a match-branch placeholder, not a claim that he will remain.

Dwight Powell and Brandon Williams are unsigned unrestricted free agents and are not carried in the depth chart or the minutes model. Dallas holds only Early Bird rights on Williams, who has been linked to Phoenix, Golden State, and Boston; Powell's three-year deal expired and he remains unsigned at age 34.

Role and health swing map

Kyrie Irving

Dallas officially ruled Irving out for all of 2025-26 on February 18 while he continued rehabilitation from March 2025 left-ACL reconstruction. A February 20 team story said the extended runway should make him fully ready for 2026-27 camp. That is organizational optimism, not evidence of restored burst, contact tolerance, or back-to-back availability. The 60-game, 31-MPG median is deliberately below a normal star workload.

If cleared without restrictions: raise Irving to 33 MPG and 23–24 PPG; trim Flagg assists/usage slightly but raise his efficiency; reduce Sasser and De Larrea. If delayed or managed: Flagg becomes a first-side initiator and Sasser gains shots and streamable assists (the Nembhard branch of this scenario left with the July 19 trade).

Dereck Lively II

Lively played seven games in 2025-26, underwent season-ending right-foot surgery in December, and said on June 26 that he had shed the boot but had not progressed to running and had no timetable. This is the largest downside tail on the roster. The 38-game projection should move sharply only after running, contact, full practice, and preseason participation are observed.

If healthy for camp: Lively can reach 24–26 MPG and top-100 per-game value through FG%, rebounds, blocks, and low usage. If delayed: Gafford is the primary winner, Morez earns center minutes, and Aldama receives more small-ball-five work.

Santi Aldama

Aldama's 2025-26 season ended after recurrent right-knee trouble and March arthroscopy. His 43-game line—14.0 points, 6.7 rebounds, 2.9 assists, 0.9 steals, 0.7 blocks, 35.0% from three—shows a fantasy-friendly template, but the 24-MPG median discounts both health and Dallas's crowded frontcourt.

Morez Johnson Jr.

The No. 9 pick averaged 13.1 points, 7.3 rebounds, 1.1 blocks, and 0.7 steals at Michigan. He then produced 18.5 points, 6.5 rebounds, 3.0 blocks, and 2.5 steals in two Summer League games before an illness ended his tournament. The defensive events are an upside clue, not a stable NBA rate. His floor is a 12-minute physical backup; his ceiling is a 24-minute center/forward who makes either Washington or Gafford expendable.

Frontcourt transaction risk

Washington, Gafford, Lively, Aldama, Morez, and Flagg cannot all receive their best positions and normal minutes. Marc Stein reports that Thompson, Gafford, and Washington are the players Dallas is most open to moving, and that Dallas's stated preference for Thompson specifically is a trade, with a buyout not ruled out if none materialises by August or September. No unverified destination is included here. A two-for-one trade for a guard would raise the remaining frontcourt players and simplify the projection.

Klay Thompson, two branches. Remains in Dallas: the 38-game, 15-MPG line above, roughly 8.9 points and 2.2 threes, a threes-only streamer. Traded or bought out: zero Dallas value; his shooting minutes go to Biberović and Christie, with a marginal lift to Sasser. Miami has been linked to him only in a buyout scenario; Golden State and Denver have also been floated. His $17.4M expiring is Dallas's cleanest matching salary, which is itself a reason the branch is live.

Correction 2026-07-18: Thompson's projection was cut from 58 GP × 18 MPG to 38 × 15 and split into two branches. The prior line gave a full-season Dallas role to a player the front office is actively shopping (Marc Stein, July 2026). The desk already treats Cisse's offer sheet as a branch; Thompson gets the same treatment.

Rome's existing model baseline is 34.4 wins with a 28–41 p10–p90 band, 49.3% top-10/play-in probability, and 19.2% playoff probability. This desk keeps the 34-win median and uses a wider basketball narrative tail because health news after the model run remains unresolved.

Floor and ceiling summary

  • Team fantasy floor: one high-volume star, a rotating stream of center value, and too many 18–27-minute forwards to trust. Poor shooting lineups can pull Flagg back toward his rookie efficiency.
  • Team fantasy ceiling: Flagg adds one made three and ~4 points per game without losing stocks; Irving returns at 30+ minutes; one of Lively/Morez becomes a reliable blocks center; Aldama's knee and shooting unlock frontcourt spacing.
  • Best draft leverage: do not price every health return simultaneously. If drafting Irving, avoid also paying ceiling cost for Lively. If taking Flagg at pick 16, the roster needs percentage/category support rather than another speculative volume bet.
