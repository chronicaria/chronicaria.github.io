    A loop you cannot slide off

    Put one spatial dimension on a circle: x∼x+2πRx \sim x + 2\pi Rx∼x+2πR. A point particle living there has only its momentum ppp. A closed string can do something extra — it can loop the circle and close up, wrapped mmm times, with no way to slide it off. That trapped loop is a winding mode.

    Two facts make winding the twin of momentum, and they run in opposite directions:

    • Momentum costs less energy as the circle grows. Quantized momentum p=n/Rp = n/Rp=n/R shrinks as R→∞R\to\inftyR→∞: a bigger box means finer momentum spacing.
    • Winding costs more energy as the circle grows. A wrapped string is stretched to length ∝R\propto R∝R, and stretching a taut string costs energy. Bigger circle, more stretch, more energy.

    Momentum energy ∝1/R\propto 1/R∝1/R, winding energy ∝R\propto R∝R: the same string, two rulers pointing opposite ways. That opposition is the entire engine of T-duality, and this note builds winding carefully enough that the engine is undeniable.

    What winding is, precisely

    The string is a map from the worldsheet σ\sigmaσ-circle (σ∈[0,2π]\sigma \in [0,2\pi]σ∈[0,2π]) into the spacetime xxx-circle. As σ\sigmaσ runs once around the string, the image can travel around the target circle a whole number of times. That integer is the winding number mmm: the degree of a circle-to-circle map. It is topological — you cannot continuously unwind an m≠0m \ne 0m=0 string without cutting it, exactly as a rubber band wound twice around a can cannot become singly-wound without being snapped.

    To state this cleanly, lift the target circle to its covering line, so xxx is a genuine real coordinate rather than a value modulo 2πR2\pi R2πR. Then traversing the string once (σ→σ+2π\sigma\to\sigma+2\piσ→σ+2π) need not return XXX to its start: X(\tau, \sigma + 2\pi) = X(\tau,\sigma) + m\,(2\pi R), \qquad m\in\mathbb{Z}. \tag{1} The lifted coordinate has advanced by mmm full circumferences, its sign recording orientation. At m=0m=0m=0 this is ordinary closed-string periodicity, so (1)(1)(1) is the only new ingredient compactification adds.

    The winding www: rescaling into a momentum

    Define the winding www (lowercase, not the integer mmm) by \boxed{\,w \equiv \frac{mR}{\alpha'}.\,} \tag{2} Then (1)(1)(1) reads X(\tau,\sigma+2\pi) = X(\tau,\sigma) + 2\pi\alpha'\, w. \tag{3} Why rescale? Units. With [R]=[R]=[R]= length and [α′]=[\alpha']=[α′]= length2^22, the winding www has units of inverse length — the units of momentum. So www is not a bookkeeping integer but a physical quantity that will sit on symmetric footing with the ordinary momentum ppp. The full mode expansion confirms it: X=x0+α′p τ+α′w σ+oscillatorsX = x_0 + \alpha' p\,\tau + \alpha' w\,\sigma + \text{oscillators}X=x0​+α′pτ+α′wσ+oscillators, with ppp multiplying τ\tauτ and www multiplying σ\sigmaσ in exactly parallel roles.

    Winding energy: the stretch is the mass

    Here is why winding is momentum's partner, made quantitative. The string has tension T0=1/(2πα′)T_0 = 1/(2\pi\alpha')T0​=1/(2πα′) — energy per unit length (natural units, ℏ=c=1\hbar=c=1ℏ=c=1). A string wound ∣m∣|m|∣m∣ times has length ∣m∣ 2πR|m|\,2\pi R∣m∣2πR, and its rest energy is tension times length: M = T_0 \cdot |m|\,2\pi R = \frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\,|m|\,2\pi R = \frac{|m|R}{\alpha'} = |w|. \tag{4} A wound string is a stretched rubber band, and the energy stored in the stretch is its rest mass. Momentum gave M=∣p∣∝1/RM = |p| \propto 1/RM=∣p∣∝1/R; winding gives M=∣w∣∝RM = |w| \propto RM=∣w∣∝R — the opposite dependence on the radius, exactly as promised.

    This line is also the safest way to remember the factors in (2)(2)(2): the 2π2\pi2π in the circumference cancels the 2π2\pi2π in the tension, leaving precisely mR/α′mR/\alpha'mR/α′. No stray 2π2\pi2π survives in www.

    Why www is quantized

    Two views, genuinely different in what they assume.

    Topological. mmm counts wrappings, so it is an integer by construction. Then (2)(2)(2) makes www automatically quantized in units of R/α′R/\alpha'R/α′. Nothing quantum is used — this is pure topology of the map.

    Operator. Impose the periodicity (1)(1)(1) not as a classical identity but as a condition on the allowed quantum states. In the mode expansion the only σ\sigmaσ-dependent zero-mode piece is α′w σ\alpha' w\,\sigmaα′wσ, so demanding (1)(1)(1) hold as an operator equation forces \alpha'\, w\,(2\pi) = m\,(2\pi R) \;\Longrightarrow\; w = \frac{mR}{\alpha'}, \qquad m\in\mathbb{Z}. \tag{5} This is the exact mirror of momentum quantization. There, single-valuedness of the wavefunction on the spacetime circle forced p=n/Rp = n/Rp=n/R (see Momentum modes on a circle). Here, single-valuedness of the string map into the target circle forces the integer mmm, and with it the momentum-like www. Two quantizations, one principle: going once around a circle must close up.

    The "double strike"

    Turning a line into a circle does two opposite things to the spectrum:

    • Gains states: winding modes with m≠0m\ne 0m=0, which did not exist before. Only a string can wind.
    • Loses states: momentum becomes discrete, p=n/Rp = n/Rp=n/R, so the momentum continuum is gone.

    A particle on a circle only loses states — it cannot wind. So the gain is genuinely stringy, and it is the source of everything distinctive about strings on compact spaces.

    Why this is the seed of T-duality

    A particle on a circle is dull: shrink the circle and its momentum levels just spread apart. A string carries a second charge — how many times it is looped around — and that charge costs stretch energy that grows with the circle. So the string has two ways to feel the radius, and they pull in opposite directions. Momentum says small circle, expensive; winding says small circle, cheap. Because the string carries both at once, you cannot tell a small circle from a big one by watching its energy levels alone. That single statement is what T-duality turns into a theorem.

    Open questions

    • Every operator in the expansion of XXX commutes with www, so naively www looks like a fixed label picking out a superselection sector. The more useful picture — where www has its own conjugate zero mode q0q_0q0​, on equal footing with (x0,p)(x_0, p)(x0​,p) — is what makes T-duality an operator map X=XL+XR→X~=XL−XRX = X_L + X_R \to \tilde X = X_L - X_RX=XL​+XR​→X~=XL​−XR​; see Spectrum invariance under R to alpha-prime over R.
    • Open strings on a space-filling brane have no topological winding: their endpoints slide, so the loop can always open and shrink. The "winding" that appears after T-duality is instead a Dirichlet stretch between a brane and its image on the covering space; see Dp to D(p minus 1) under T-duality.

    Exercise

    The winding energy (4)(4)(4), M=∣w∣=∣m∣R/α′M = |w| = |m|R/\alpha'M=∣w∣=∣m∣R/α′, was read off from tension times length. Confirm it a different way, from the mass formula, and pin down where the factor of tension hides.
