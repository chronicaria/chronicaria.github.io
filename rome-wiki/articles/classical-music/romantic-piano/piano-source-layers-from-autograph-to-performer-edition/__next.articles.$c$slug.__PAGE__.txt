<h2 id="a-source-ladder">A source ladder</h2>
<p>An autograph may precede revision; an authorized copy may transmit corrections; a proof may contain composer changes; parallel first editions may descend from different exemplars; a later impression may silently repair or introduce errors. A modern critical edition argues among these witnesses through an editorial policy and report. A performer edition adds fingering, pedaling, dynamics, phrasing, or redistribution that may document reception but is not automatically composer text.</p>
<p>The ladder is not a simple ranking. Authority depends on the question and the work's transmission. For pitch or rhythm, compare stemmata and corrections; for pedaling or fingering, ask whether a mark is autograph, publisher, editor, or performer; for listening history, a famous performer edition may be primary evidence of later practice even when textually secondary.</p>
<h2 id="place-in-the-map">Place in the map</h2>
<p>Use this method before <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/classical-music/romantic-piano/reading-chopin-early-editions/" class="wiki-link">Reading Chopin early editions</a> and work-specific edition laboratories. It prevents “Urtext” from meaning unmediated intention and gives every audible variant a traceable textual layer.</p>
