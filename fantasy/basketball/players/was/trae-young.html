Role and baseline

  • 2025-26: 15 games, 25.6 MPG across Atlanta and Washington. A right MCL sprain, quadriceps contusion/re-injury, knee management and low-back irritation limited him to 10 Hawks games and five Wizards games.
  • Transaction/security: acquired January 9 and re-signed in July for four years and about $212 million. There is no role ambiguity when healthy.
  • 2026-27 base role: starting point guard, primary pick-and-roll creator and highest-assist player; 32 of the team's exact 240 base minutes.

Correction 2026-07-18: base GP lowered from 62 to 55. The injury ledger already stated that "a 15-game season demands a GP discount," and the projection did not apply one. Young played 15 games in 2025-26 — ten with Atlanta, five with Washington after a March 5 debut — by far the fewest of his career against a prior floor of 54, and Washington ruled him out indefinitely on March 21 with a recurrent right-quadriceps contusion and lower-back irritation. Both were treated conservatively rather than surgically, which means no procedure date anchors the recovery and his July press-conference claim that he is ready to play is a self-report, not a medical bulletin. 55 keeps him well clear of a shutdown assumption while pricing in a rebuilding team's willingness to rest a guard it has just paid.

Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: projected top 25-35 per game. Assists, FT% volume and threes can anchor a build; FG%, turnovers and blocks are structural weaknesses.
  • Points: top-25 rate upside because most formats reward points and assists while softening the turnover penalty.
  • Dynasty: roughly top 35-45: still only 28, but small-guard defense, recent lower-body injuries and a developing supporting cast temper the peak-Atlanta valuation.
  • Best pairing: efficient low-turnover wings/bigs and strong blocks. Davis and Sarr can cover defense, but neither fixes Young's missed-game risk.

Floor, median and ceiling

  • Floor: another 45-55-game season, reduced scoring next to Dybantsa/Davis and top-60 totals value.
  • Median: 62 games with approximately 22 points and 10 assists; an early-round category specialist.
  • Ceiling: 70 healthy games, restored scoring efficiency and top-15 overall value.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Oklahoma, one season (2017-18). He played 32 games for 27.4 points, 8.7 assists and 3.9 rebounds, becoming the only player in NCAA Division I history to lead the country in both points per game and assists per game in the same season (848 points, 271 assists, 48.6 percent assist rate). Consensus first-team All-American. College shooting splits are not verified here. Draft: 2018, first round, No. 5, Dallas Mavericks — traded on draft night to Atlanta with a protected future first-round pick for the rights to No. 3 pick Luka Dončić.

The 2025-26 row is a two-team total: ten games with Atlanta before the January 9, 2026 trade that sent him to Washington for CJ McCollum and Corey Kispert, and five games with Washington after a March 5 debut, at 15.2 points, 3.0 rebounds and 6.2 assists in restricted minutes. Treat the whole 2025-26 line as a small, minutes-capped, injury-contaminated sample rather than a true baseline; the 25.6 MPG and 2.6 turnovers are artifacts of load management, not a new profile.

Fantasy history: Season-end nine-category ranks are not verifiable from a rater source here and are not estimated. The category shape has been consistent for seven years: elite assists, high free-throw volume at a strong percentage, plenty of threes and real points, offset by a field-goal percentage that has been a negative in every season except 2021-22 and by four-plus turnovers a night — the highest recurring turnover cost of any guard in this tier. That combination made him a reliable early-round per-game asset in his peak Atlanta years while keeping him out of the true top tier, and it makes him strictly a builder's piece: he requires a roster that punts nothing he supplies and covers field-goal percentage and blocks elsewhere. Points leagues have consistently rated him higher than category leagues, since points and assists both count fully and turnovers are typically a single small deduction. The 2025-26 season had no meaningful fantasy value at 15 games regardless of format.

Durability: GP by season: 81, 60, 63, 76, 73, 54, 76, 15. Two of those are partly contextual — the 2019-20 and 2020-21 team seasons ran 67 and 72 games — but the pattern of one compromised year every second or third season is real. The 2023-24 drop to 54 was a torn ligament in his left pinkie finger, repaired surgically on February 27, 2024, which cost 23 games. The 2025-26 season was effectively lost: a right MCL sprain suffered October 29, 2025 against Brooklyn cost 22-23 games through mid-December, the knee remained a management issue after his return, and following the trade he did not debut for Washington until March 5 before a quadriceps injury and low-back irritation ended his season with the team announcing he was out indefinitely and that neither injury required surgery. He is coming off a season in which he played 15 games and finished on the shelf; that is the dominant fact in any 2026-27 projection.

Trajectory: Young's leap came immediately — the jump from 19.1 to 29.6 points as a second-year player was a usage and green-light change on a team built entirely around him, and the scoring level he reached at 21 has never been exceeded since. What has actually improved over time is the passing: 8.1 assists to 11.6 across seven seasons, with the 2024-25 mark a career high at age 26, which reflects genuine growth in reading coverages rather than more minutes. Working against him, field-goal percentage has trended down for three straight healthy seasons (.429, .430, .411) as defenses have learned to live with the deep pull-up, and the turnover rate has climbed alongside the assists. He is 27 turning 28, which is nominally the middle of the prime, but a small guard whose game depends on change of direction coming off a knee sprain plus quad and back problems is not a normal aging-curve case. The four-year, $212 million commitment guarantees the role; nothing guarantees the games. The honest read for 2026-27 is that the per-game production should return close to his 2024-25 shape with somewhat lower scoring next to Washington's young creators, and that games played is the entire distribution.
