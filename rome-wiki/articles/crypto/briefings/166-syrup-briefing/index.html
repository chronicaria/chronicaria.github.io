    Thesis

    Syrup (SYRUP) is a top-200 snapshot asset at rank 166. Current call: BEST_CREDIT_WATCH__NO_BUY. Main blocker: Holder capture is SSF/treasury-mediated, the forward buyback mandate is unresolved, and flagship products are not cleanly U.S.-available.

    Token-Specific Evidence

    • Maple/Syrup is a real credit platform with protocol revenue and normal U.S. spot-token liquidity.
    • The holder-capture route is SSF/treasury-mediated rather than a hard passive-holder claim.
    • Flagship product access is not cleanly U.S.-available, so spot-token access does not automatically make the economic route usable.
    • A standing buyback mandate, transaction-level accounting, and hard no-sell or burn treatment are the key proof gates.

    What Would Change The Verdict

    Standing buyback mandate, transaction-level SSF accounting, and hard sale constraint or burn.
