9:T18fba,<p>This is an orientation note, not a derivation of the Standard Model. Everything below rests on one idea: endpoint labels are gauge data. Once an endpoint behaves like a charge (<a href="/rome-wiki/articles/string-theory/part-iv-branes-and-duality/19-string-charge-and-gauge-fields/endpoint-charge/" class="wiki-link">Endpoint charge</a>), labeling <em>which</em> brane it touches becomes a gauge representation.</p>

<h2 id="honest-scope">Honest scope</h2>
<p>This is model-building orientation, not a derivation of the Standard Model from this vault. A realistic construction needs ingredients not developed here: compactification geometry; intersecting or orientifold branes to get <em>chiral</em> matter (a bare parallel stack gives left- and right-handed quarks with equal charges — non-chiral, wrong); the extra branes that fix hypercharge; anomaly cancellation; supersymmetry breaking.</p>
<p>The takeaway is narrower but strong enough for this course. A D-brane does not merely host a single Maxwell field: a <em>stack</em> hosts matrix-valued non-abelian gauge fields, and strings stretched between stacks are automatically charged matter in bifundamental representations. Both facts are forced by the one principle of this module — the endpoint carries a charge and remembers its brane — which is why D-branes became a serious language for building particle physics.</p>
