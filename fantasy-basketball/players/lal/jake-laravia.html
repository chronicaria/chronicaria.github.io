Projection logic

LaRavia averaged 8.2 points, 4.0 rebounds, 1.8 assists, 1.3 steals, 0.5 blocks, and 1.0 three in 25.1 minutes while appearing in all 82 games in 2025-26. The desk preserves his useful defensive-event rates but cuts the workload: Quentin Grimes, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Ziaire Williams, Cameron Carr, and Jarred Vanderbilt all draw from the same wing/forward pool. His ability to defend multiple spots still gives him the steadiest availability projection among the forwards. ESPN player statistics

Redraft, dynasty, and scenarios

  • Median: A real-life rotation player who contributes about one three and one defensive event without commanding the ball.
  • Ceiling: He holds the starting power-forward job outright, reaching 27 minutes and deep-league top-150 relevance.
  • Floor: Carr develops quickly and Vanderbilt is healthy, leaving LaRavia below 15 minutes on some nights.
  • Format note: More useful in 14- and 16-team nine-category formats than in points leagues because low turnovers and steals preserve value.
  • Waiver trigger: Add if his rolling five-game workload reaches 26 minutes; the per-minute profile is broad enough to matter there.

Confidence

  • Roster: High — on the official roster.
  • Minutes: Medium — the forward competition is unusually dense.
  • Rates/availability: High — the full 82-game 2025-26 season supplies a strong baseline.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Indiana State 2019-21 (9.4 PPG/5.9 RPG as a freshman, 12.3/6.3 and second-team All-MVC as a sophomore), then Wake Forest 2021-22: 14.6 PTS, 6.6 REB, 3.7 AST, second-team All-ACC. Draft: 2022, Round 1, Pick 19, Minnesota — rights traded to Memphis on draft night.

Durability: 35-game seasons in each of his first two years (Memphis's crowded wing room plus assorted minor injuries and assignments), 66 in the split MEM/SAC year (47 Memphis, 19 Sacramento), then all 82 in 2025-26 — the best availability signal on the Lakers' forward depth chart.

Trajectory: The record shows a connector whose defensive events have climbed every year — 0.3, 0.8, 0.9, then 1.3 steals per game — while usage stays low and efficiency has settled in the mid-.40s. In 2026-27 he competes with Mamukelashvili, Ziaire Williams, and Vanderbilt for the starting-forward minutes next to the Lakers' core. He becomes fantasy-relevant if he holds 26-plus minutes as the starter, where the steals-threes-low-TO package reaches deep-league top-150 value.
