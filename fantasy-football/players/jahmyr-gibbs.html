A three-down bell cow for Detroit who projects as the single highest scorer on the board, and is nonetheless not the pick at 1.01, because he is the only candidate in that tier flagged as risky.

The role

He stays on the field on every down and takes both the handoffs and the throws, which is the most valuable job in fantasy football because it puts two income streams on one roster spot. He is also one of the four NFL names you already know — see The four players you already know — so be careful that familiarity is not doing the arguing. The label, not the name, is the reason to draft him.

The number

367.4 projected points, from the proj_points column of Data/players.csv — first of 243 rows, by half a point over the second name. It is a projection: FFToday stat lines dated 2026-07-23, scored under this league's rules, assuming all 17 games. A half-point margin across a full season is not a ranking, it is a tie.

The risk

His risk flag is med, the only one in the 1.01 tier that is not low. What visibly drives it is a teammate: Detroit also carries an early-down committee back projected for 122 carries, so a share of the workload that makes Gibbs valuable is a coaching decision that may not survive the August 30 roster cutdown. Weeks 1–14 require a top-six finish before this league's payout rewards any variance at all, so rounds 1–4 are the wrong place to absorb that. The share is not hypothetical either: he was on the field for two-thirds of Detroit's plays in 2025, and the projection asks for 36 more carries than he actually took.

The context

He is 24.5 at the September 9 opener and went twelfth overall in 2023 to Detroit, out of Alabama. First-round capital usually means the team intends to use him — see Draft capital dominance. He has played 15, 17 and 17 games since 2023, missing two; the official injury report listed him Out once and Questionable once, named a hamstring twice, and never covered one of the two absences. The 2025 usage is where the committee stops being a guess: 67.0 percent of Detroit's offensive snaps — plays, see Fantasy football glossary — the lowest of the three bell cows atop the board, with 243 carries and 77 catches on 94 targets, a 17.1 percent target share. The projected 279 carries is 36 more than he got. Rookie deal, about $4.5 million a year, free agent 2028.
