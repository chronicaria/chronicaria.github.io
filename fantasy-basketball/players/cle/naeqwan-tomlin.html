Tomlin played 64 games at 15.7 minutes in 2025-26, producing 5.8 points, 2.9 rebounds, 0.8 assists, 0.6 steals, and 0.5 blocks with low turnovers. Cleveland converted him to a standard contract February 11. 2025-26 Cavaliers statistics · Cavaliers postseason guide

Fantasy profile

  • Role: reserve PF/small-ball C, projected for 11 minutes at PF and four at C on a healthy night.
  • 9-cat: modest REB and about 1.2 combined stocks with low TO; insufficient PTS/3PM/AST for standard leagues.
  • Points leagues: deep-league injury substitute; low usage limits per-minute scoring.
  • Dynasty: Hashtag ranked him 336th. His practical value is a controllable rotation role, not star upside. Hashtag dynasty rankings

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: squeezed below 10 minutes by Mobley/Allen/Bryant; no fantasy use.
  • Base: 15-minute reserve with stocks streamer appeal in 16-team leagues.
  • Ceiling: top 130-160 if an Allen or Mobley absence creates 24-plus minutes and the stocks scale.

Role uncertainty

The departures of Dean Wade and Larry Nance Jr. create a cleaner path, but Thomas Bryant re-signed and Evan Mobley can cover center minutes. Tomlin's ability to defend both frontcourt spots is more important to his minutes than his scoring.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Long JUCO-to-power-conference road — Monroe CC and Chipola College, then Kansas State 2022-23 (36 GP, 27.2 MPG, 10.4 PTS, 5.9 REB, 1.2 AST, 1.2 STL, 1.0 BLK on the Elite Eight team) and Memphis 2023-24 (21 GP, 26.7 MPG, 14.1 PTS, 5.6 REB, 1.1 BLK). Undrafted in 2024; spent most of 2024-25 with the G-League Cleveland Charge before a 10-day contract (Feb 20, 2025) became a two-way (Mar 2, 2025). Draft: undrafted (2024).

Durability: GP totals track contract status, not health — 5 games in 2024-25 came after a late-February call-up, and the 64 games in 2025-26 reflect rotation-fringe DNPs rather than injury. No significant injury history on record. Trajectory: The record shows a late-blooming energy forward (turns 26 in December 2026) who defends both frontcourt spots and posts a combined 1.1 stocks in under 16 minutes, but shoots 23% from three on minimal volume. In 2026-27 he competes for the first-reserve power forward job in Cleveland on a standard contract, which is his structural advantage over camp bodies. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if a Mobley or Allen absence pushes him past roughly 24 minutes, at which point the stocks scale into deep-league value; the jumper is unlikely to add a second path.
