The role

He is the three-down bell cow in Cincinnati: on the field on first and second down for the carries, and still out there on third down instead of ceding the job to a passing-down specialist. That second part is the whole point. Two income streams on one roster spot is the scarcest structure in fantasy football, and it is why a back like this is priced above better-known runners who leave the field when the ball goes in the air. He plays in an offense that also contains an alpha X receiver, so the passing game is not being forced through him by default.

The number

57 catches, from the promoted 2026-08-02 row—a model projection, not something observed. At one point per reception that is 57 points before a yard is gained, roughly three and a half a game; current role evidence is still needed before treating that outcome as certain.

The risk

The risk column says low, and the thing that would break the case is a committee. There is a backup back on the same roster carrying a high risk flag, and the split between them is a coaching decision, not a settled fact — if third-down work moves, the archetype falls back to early-down committee back and the receiving projection goes with it. Roles are not resolved until the August 30 roster cutdown. Availability is not the objection here — the games he has missed since 2023 were overwhelmingly a rookie who had not yet won the job, not a player who broke.

The context

He is 26.5 on the opener and was the 163rd pick of the 2023 draft — fifth-round capital, so by the logic of Draft capital dominance Cincinnati never planned this for him; he took the job. He has played 12, 16 and 17 games across 2023–2025. Of the six missed, five fall in his rookie year and never appear on the official injury report, so those absences were not medical; one week across the three years has him listed Out, and he was Questionable or Doubtful three times (hamstring twice, quadricep, ankle). In 2025 he played 66.6% of snaps, took 232 carries and caught 69 of 88 targets — 14.5% of Cincinnati's throws. The 57-catch projection sits below what he already caught. He is on a $4.1 million rookie deal, free agent 2027.
