Nine-category: top 150-215 per game; threes/rebounds with modest assists are useful only when efficiency cooperates. Points: top 160-220. Dynasty: top 130-190, well above his redraft value because he is 23 and Siakam/Oubre are older.

Why the line looks like this

Walker played 76 games, started 41 and averaged 11.6/5.1/2.5 in 25.7 minutes. The full-season 41.9% FG is the main caution, but after January 1 he produced 13.8/5.7/3.1 on 46.2% FG and 41.7% from three. The model preserves part of that growth while removing opportunity for Haliburton, Nesmith, Siakam and Oubre. Pacers player review

Hashtag ranked him 191st in its July crowd and 184th in dynasty. The encyclopedia's dynasty band gives slightly more credit to the late-season improvement. Hashtag crowd · Hashtag dynasty

Redraft, dynasty, and scenarios

  • Redraft: final-round upside pick only in 14-team leagues; in 12-team leagues, make him the first wing add if Siakam/Oubre/Nesmith misses time.
  • Dynasty: hold or buy below top 150; the bet is a future 28-minute two-way connector, not an immediate star.
  • Floor: 17-19 minutes, 7 points and damaging FG% if he loses the second-unit order.
  • Ceiling: 27 minutes, 12/5/3 with two threes after winning the closing role; top-110 stretches.

Durability: No injury red flags — the rookie-year 33 GP was DNPs and G League assignment, not health, and he has answered the bell 75 and 76 times the last two seasons. Trajectory: The record is a textbook slow-build: minutes have climbed 10.3 → 15.8 → 25.7 and production has scaled with them, while the college block rate (1.3 per game) has never translated (0.3 across three NBA seasons). The unresolved question is efficiency — .472 FG% in 2024-25, .419 in the larger 2025-26 role, with the post-January .462/.417 stretch suggesting the higher-usage version can work. In 2026-27 he competes for the 22-minute multiposition reserve role behind Siakam, Nesmith and Oubre, with closing-lineup upside. He becomes fantasy-relevant when either a starter's absence pushes him to 26+ minutes or the late-2025-26 efficiency proves real, at which point the threes-boards-assists package plays in 12-team leagues.
