Sharife Cooper

Sharife Cooper is no longer under contract with Washington — an unrestricted free agent after the Wizards declined his qualifying offer — and carries no 2026-27 projection unless he is re-signed.

Role, baseline, and status

  • 2025-26: 41 games, 17.1 MPG; 8.1 points and 3.0 assists, on a two-way signed September 29 2025.
  • Contract status: not under contract for 2026-27. Washington declined to tender a qualifying offer on June 29 2026, making him an unrestricted free agent. He does not appear on the Wizards' 2026-27 cap table, and he does not appear in the club's June-July 2026 transaction log. All three two-way slots are now filled by Watkins, Reese and Okpara, so the two-way path back is blocked; a return would require the single open standard roster spot.
  • 2026-27 projection: none. Any line would be conditional on a signing that has not occurred.

Correction 2026-07-18: the previous 8-minute conditional projection and the "official offseason roster listing" status were removed. Cooper was carried as a roster member on the strength of a stale offseason roster page; he has been an unrestricted free agent since the June 29 qualifying-offer decline. Fantasy dossier

  • All formats: undraftable. He is not on an NBA roster at the cutoff.
  • If re-signed by Washington: a deep assists streamer only, and only with Young and Carrington both unavailable. Almost no blocks and low rebounding and three-point volume narrow the contribution even then.
  • Dynasty: very deep leagues only; Young's four-year deal blocks the principal path regardless of where he signs.
  • Watch trigger: Washington has no third pure point guard under contract, which is the most plausible use of its one open standard spot. That makes a reunion possible but in no way reported.

Career record

Between NBA stops (2022-25): Cleveland Charge in the G League, a 2024 CBA title with Liaoning (22 points and 9 assists in the clincher), then Aris Thessaloniki in Greece and JDA Dijon in France before the September 2025 Wizards two-way.

Durability: No significant injury record; every gap in the file is contractual, not physical.

Trajectory: The record shows a former elite college playmaker who spent three seasons rebuilding his stock through the G League, China, Greece and France, then delivered a real NBA answer in 2025-26 — 8.1 points and 3.0 assists on .514 FG with a respectable 38.2% from three, the shooting being the skill that had been missing. Washington still declined the qualifying offer, so he enters 2026-27 as an unrestricted free agent with no roster. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he signs somewhere with a thin point-guard depth chart and injuries hand him 20+ minutes, at which point he is a deep-league assists streamer and nothing wider.
