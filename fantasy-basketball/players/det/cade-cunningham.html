Nine-cat: first-round ceiling, second-round median; strongest in assists, points, and guard stocks. Points: A+, projected top five. Dynasty: 9.5/10, foundational top-ten asset.

Role and evidence

Cunningham's 2025-26 Basketball-Reference line was 23.9 points, 5.5 rebounds, 9.9 assists, 1.4 steals, 0.8 blocks, and 3.7 turnovers in 33.9 minutes across 64 games. Detroit named him first-team All-NBA, and the NBA's early 2026-27 fantasy rankings placed him fifth overall.

The roster added shooting but did not add a proven co-primary creator. That protects Cunningham's usage and assist ceiling while keeping his turnovers high. Duren returning would preserve his most efficient lob partnership; Okorie becoming ready would modestly reduce the exhausting creation load without displacing the offense.

The official April 6 gamebook listed Cunningham out with a left-lung pneumothorax. He subsequently returned and produced 32 points and 12 assists in playoff Game 7 on May 3, direct evidence that the event should not carry an active injury tag. The 66-game median is therefore an ordinary availability discount, not a forecast of recurrence. AP game report, 2026-05-03

Range

  • Ceiling: 72 games, 25/6/10 with roughly 2.3 stocks and top-five value in every common points format.
  • Floor: another 55-60-game season, slightly lower assists if Duren leaves, and late-second-round category value because turnovers and volume FT misses matter.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Oklahoma State, one season (2020-21). He played 27 games at 35.4 minutes for 20.1 points, 6.2 rebounds, 3.5 assists, 1.6 steals and 0.8 blocks on .438/.400/.846 splits, taking Big 12 Player of the Year and Big 12 Freshman of the Year in the same season and earning consensus first-team All-American honors plus the Wayman Tisdale Award. The 40 percent three-point mark on real volume as a freshman is the number that separated him from the rest of that draft class. Draft: 2021, first round, first overall, Detroit Pistons; no draft-night trade.

Fantasy history: Season-end nine-category finishes from a rater source (Basketball Monster, Hashtag, ESPN Player Rater final standings) could not be verified for any season, so no rank is asserted here. What the per-game record supports: 2021-22 and 2022-23 were not startable at cost — sub-.420 shooting on high volume with 3.3-3.7 turnovers is a category anchor regardless of counting stats. The 2023-24 season was the first genuinely positive one, driven by the jump to .449 from the field and .869 from the line. 2024-25 and 2025-26 are clearly first- or second-round profiles: elite assists, strong points, positive steals, neutral-to-positive percentages, with turnovers as the one consistently negative category. Mid-season ESPN rest-of-season lists during 2025-26 placed him fifth in points formats and outside the roto top nine, which is the correct shape — the points-league value materially exceeds the category value because points formats do not punish 3.7 turnovers and reward his assist and usage volume directly.

Durability: 64, 12, 62, 70, 64. The 2021-22 shortfall was a preseason ankle sprain in early October that cost him all of preseason and the first five regular-season games, with scattered later absences. The 2022-23 season effectively did not happen: a left tibia stress fracture led to surgery in December 2022 to stabilize the bone, ending his year at 12 games. In 2023-24 the largest single absence was a left knee strain that cost eight games around late January 2024. 2024-25 at 70 games is his healthiest season. In 2025-26 he suffered a left-lung pneumothorax diving for a loose ball in the first quarter against Washington on March 17, 2026, missed the remainder of the regular season, and returned for the playoffs. The relevant read is that only one of the four significant absences was a lower-body structural injury, and it was four seasons ago.

Trajectory: The leap is legible and it is not a minutes leap — minutes have sat between 32.6 and 35.0 for all five seasons while scoring went from 17.4 to a 26.1 peak and assists from 5.6 to 9.9. What actually changed was shot diet and efficiency: field goal percentage climbed from .416 to .469 across three seasons as Detroit surrounded him with spacing and he traded contested mid-range pull-ups for rim pressure. The 2025-26 line is not a decline despite the lower scoring; he shed 2.2 points and 0.7 turnovers while adding 0.8 assists and 0.4 steals, which is a more efficient version of the same player with the offense running through him at a higher rate. He turns 25 in September, which places him at the front edge of the prime window rather than in it, and the steal rate rising from 1.0 to 1.4 in his fifth season is the kind of late-arriving defensive improvement that usually holds. The one number that has not improved is free throw percentage, which fell to .812 in 2025-26 from a .846 career mark — worth watching but likely noise across 64 games. For 2026-27 the base case is a repeat of the 2025-26 shape with modest positive regression at the line; the honest risk is not skill but the games-played line, which has cleared 65 exactly once in five tries.
