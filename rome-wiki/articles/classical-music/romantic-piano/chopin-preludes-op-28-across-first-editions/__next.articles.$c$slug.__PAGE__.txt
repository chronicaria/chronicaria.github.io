<h2 id="a-bounded-edition-laboratory">A bounded edition laboratory</h2>
<p>Op. 28 appeared through different national publishers, with distinct dedications in the French/English and German issues. The English proofs were engraved from the French edition, while the wider source history includes manuscript and print relationships that cannot be reduced to publication date. The Online Chopin Variorum Edition identifies witnesses as FFE, GFE, and EFE and permits bar-level comparison.</p>
<p>The responsible laboratory starts with one numbered prelude and one parameter—pitch, rhythm, articulation, pedal, or layout. Record the exact copy and impression; compare images; identify shared errors or corrections; then consult a critical commentary. Until that work is done, a divergence is a variant, not proof that Chopin authorized two expressive solutions.</p>
<h2 id="place-in-the-map">Place in the map</h2>
<p>This applies <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/classical-music/romantic-piano/piano-source-layers-from-autograph-to-performer-edition/" class="wiki-link">Piano source layers from autograph to performer edition</a> to a concrete corpus and narrows <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/classical-music/romantic-piano/reading-chopin-early-editions/" class="wiki-link">Reading Chopin early editions</a> into reproducible comparisons. It precedes a measure-specific Prelude study.</p>
