Every competitive match in E11A4 that at least one of the four played — 65 of them. The chart is a running total of round win probability added.     Season totals

    These four numbers are not a leaderboard, and the page will not sort them. They cover different matches, different opponents, and exposure differing by more than ten to one. Trzzcko's rate is the clearest warning: five matches and ninety-three rounds, which is enough to produce a large number and nowhere near enough to mean one. Read each figure against the exposure printed beneath it.

    There is also a deeper reason they don't compare. Every non-cohort player in this dataset is re-pseudonymised in each match, so opponents and teammates cannot be linked across games. Lineup quality and player skill are therefore not separable — that limitation is structural, not a matter of collecting more matches.

    What this page is

    Four separate stories that occasionally overlap. All four have never been on the same server: the largest overlap is Martin and SN0RLAX at 27 matches, and TheMarias and Trzzcko have never played a single game together. Within any one match the players present faced the same opponents in the same rounds, so comparing them there is fair. Across the season they did not, so it isn't.
