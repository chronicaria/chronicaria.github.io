Career record

Pre-NBA: Three seasons at Iowa (Big Ten), breaking out as a junior in 2022-23 with 20.2 points, 7.9 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 1.0 steals and 1.2 blocks in 34.9 minutes on .476/.335/.729, earning first-team All-Big Ten and third-team AP All-American. Twin brother of Keegan Murray. Draft: 2023, first round, 23rd pick, Portland.

Durability: 62, 69 and 57 games across three seasons — he has never missed a large block of time, but has never played a full season either. The 2025-26 total came with 15 starts and a career-high 23.4 minutes, so the games missed were closer to rotation and rest decisions than to injury; specific causes are not verified here.

Trajectory: Three years in and the shooting has not arrived: 25.9% from three on 2.3 attempts per game for his career, and a 45.6% free throw season in 2024-25 that is hard to explain for a wing who shot 72.9% in college. The 2025-26 improvement to .467 from the field is real but came almost entirely inside the arc, which is the wrong place for a low-usage forward. What he does have is defensive versatility, 0.9 steals in 23 minutes, and low turnovers. Memphis acquired him in the Ja Morant trade and is expected to move him again; his fantasy path requires landing somewhere thin at forward and starting, and even then the historical rates cap him near 8 points and 5 rebounds with negative percentages.
