QR or QT — mostly not a decision
  • It is a forced choice at one firm out of 65. Virtu, which is one-application-only and posts a QR req titled "(Undergrad)". At HRT and Optiver a single requisition explicitly covers both. Everywhere else, filing both costs a form. Lead QR in how the résumé is written and where the prep goes; run QT in parallel; stop treating it as a strategy.
  • "You are not competing with PhDs for QR" is only half true, and an earlier version of this page asserted it flatly. Separate BS/MS-eligible QR requisitions do exist at Citadel Securities, Citadel, IMC, D. E. Shaw, DRW, Jump, Virtu, Akuna, AQR, Arrowstreet, Aquatic and Voloridge. They do not at Two Sigma, Jane Street or HRT, where one all-levels req puts undergraduates directly against PhDs — and at SIG and Five Rings the QR seat is PhD-only, so the undergraduate door there is the trading req.
  • QT has no degree gate, so the funnel is the largest in the market and the résumé stops mattering — but note the failure mode this creates. Applying to ten QT desks is not ten independent draws: the post-assessment rounds are the same games and the same person every time.
  • The timed arithmetic screen is the real gate, and it is the one part of this that is purely trainable. Be sceptical of the thresholds people circulate for the 80-in-8 (55, 56, 65 all get quoted) — none is employer-confirmed and Optiver publishes no figure. Drill it because it is a hard gate, not because someone attached a number to it.
  • Optiver is the one firm to hold back. One campus role at a time plus an eight-month global cooldown after a failed assessment — the only cooldown on the board. A cold attempt is genuinely expensive. Everywhere else, apply now: review is rolling and the desirable offices fill first.
Applying
  • Almost every prop shop reviews rolling. Early August beats late September with the same résumé. Banks are the exception — filter to Has a deadline and sort by deadline before doing anything else.
  • The online assessments are the real filter, not the résumé. Most are timed mental arithmetic and probability. Sequence the firms with a re-application cooldown last.
  • Apply on the firm's own board. Lensa, Jobright, ZipRecruiter reposts and BeBee re-host real reqs with stale windows and dead links, and they outrank the firms themselves in search. And nothing selling a "funded account" evaluation — FTMO, Apex, TopStep — is an employer; the CFTC sued MyForexFunds over that model in 2023.
