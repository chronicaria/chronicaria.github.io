Cutoff, scope, and confidence

This is an English-language public-web census researched through 2026-07-18, America/New_York. It covers directly discoverable YouTube channels, podcast feeds, and episode pages with 2026-27 relevance. It is broad, not literally closed-world: private Discord audio, deleted episodes, paywalled Patreon feeds, unindexed regional shows, live Spaces, and videos published after the cutoff remain gaps.

  • Fact means an episode title, host, publication time, duration, description, feed state, or channel state visible at the cutoff.
  • Analyst opinion means every sleeper, bust, rank, role, usage, or outcome claim inside a show unless independently corroborated.
  • Desk inference means the source-quality and routing judgments below.

No episode title is evidence that its basketball claim is true. A video becomes a dated analyst source only after its format, information cutoff, claim, and reasoning are captured.

Episode ingestion standard

Each retained episode needs:

Field Requirement
Identity Show, episode title, host and guests, canonical audio/video URL, feed GUID or YouTube ID
Time Publication timestamp with time zone, retrieval timestamp, episode runtime
Scope Redraft/dynasty/DFS/betting; category/points/platform; per-game/totals; league size if stated
Context Information cutoff, transactions already incorporated, recording versus publication date if different
Navigation Creator chapters, transcript timestamps, player/team/topic entities
Claim Short paraphrase, speaker, exact timestamp, confidence language, mechanism, reversal condition
Evidence Links or statistics cited by the speaker; official corroboration status
Lifecycle Current, aging, stale, superseded, retracted/corrected, unavailable/paywalled
Lineage Shared host, model, network, or upstream projection source

Never paste a full copyrighted transcript into the vault. Store short timestamped paraphrases and only brief quotations when necessary.

Deduplication and correlation rules

  1. Use RSS GUID, enclosure URL, YouTube ID, title, duration, and publication time to identify an episode.
  2. A simulcast on YouTube, Apple, Spotify, and a network site is one source observation.
  3. The same analyst repeating a take on two shows is two dated statements but one analyst family for consensus weighting.
  4. Guest appearances inherit both the guest's source lineage and the host show's context.
  5. Shorts clipped from a long-form show link back to the parent episode and do not add an independent vote.
  6. Cross-posted RSS items are assigned to the native show. The Punt Intended feed's Fantasy NBA Today item and the Bleav network feed's team shows show why feed title alone is insufficient.

Monitoring tiers

Daily during draft season: Locked On, Old Man Squad, Stuff It, Fantasy NBA Today.

Two or three times weekly: Full Court Press, RotoWire video, Bleav fantasy video, Angle, Punt Intended.

Weekly or event-driven: Know Ball Dynasty, Young Man Squad, Dizzle Dynasty multimedia, No Ceilings, KOC fantasy segments.

Archive-only until a new item appears: the long-tail channels whose last public-feed item predates the 2025-26 season.

Gaps and unavailable material

  • YouTube throttled normal page extraction intermittently; public oEmbed, page metadata, and Atom feeds supplied titles/dates, but captions and complete chapters were not uniformly available.
  • The user-supplied video had no public human chapter list in the inspected description; it still needs a timestamped claim pass.
  • Spotify pages, Apple storefronts, and podcast networks can display different release times, durations, or episode order because of time zones, ad insertion, and syndication.
  • Patreon-only Angle, Punt Intended/SportsEthos, Matt Lawson, and other dynasty audio was not audited beyond public descriptions.
  • Live YouTube streams, X Spaces, Discord stages, and deleted/private videos are not recoverable from standard RSS.
  • Feedspot's “30 channels” directory included stale and miscategorized channels and omitted at least two core current channels; it is discovery evidence only.
  • No public show supplies a fully documented, calibrated 2026-27 primitive projection distribution merely by publishing a rank episode.
  • Non-English fantasy basketball audio/video remains substantially undercounted.
