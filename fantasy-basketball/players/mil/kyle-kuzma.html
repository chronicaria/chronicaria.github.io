Fantasy case

Kuzma averaged 13.0 points, 4.5 rebounds, and 2.7 assists in 26.2 minutes across 69 games in 2025-26 while shooting .492. Giannis's departure creates frontcourt touches, but the rebuild also gives Milwaukee reasons to prioritize Kel'el Ware, Ousmane Dieng, Nate Ament, and Bogoljub Marković. Kuzma is therefore a classic trade-sensitive veteran rather than a stable mid-round asset.

Low case: 16-18 MPG, late-season shutdown, outside top 250. Base: 20 MPG and shallow-league streaming. High: a trade to a 27+ MPG role yields 15/6/3 and points-league usefulness. His points and rebounds help; poor FT%, low stocks, and moderate turnovers cap nine-cat value.

Durability: Genuinely durable: 61+ games in all nine seasons and 610 career games, with no major surgeries on record. The 2024-25 season totaled 65 games across the deadline trade from Washington to Milwaukee (the Khris Middleton deal). Trajectory: The arc is a clean rise and fade: All-Rookie First Team and a 2020 title role in Los Angeles, a 21-22 PPG featured peak in Washington (2022-24), then declining volume in Milwaukee even as his FG% hit a career-best .492 in a smaller 2025-26 role. The record says he is a scorer whose counting stats track minutes almost linearly, with FT% and turnovers as permanent nine-cat drags. In 2026-27 he competes for a nominal starting forward job on a rebuilding Bucks roster with every incentive to play its young frontcourt. He becomes fantasy-relevant again on a transaction: a trade or injury vacuum restoring 27+ minutes brings back a 15/6/3 points-league line.
