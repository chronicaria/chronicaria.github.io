<h2 id="voice-and-structure-every-note">Voice and structure (every note)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Directly under frontmatter: a <strong>bare one-to-two-sentence summary</strong> in plain prose. No "One-line:" prefix, no "One-line summary:", no whole-line bold, no course-scaffolding jargon ("Work Product #2").</li>
<li>Frontmatter key order: <code>title, created, source, status, tags, passes, finalized, published_pass</code>.</li>
<li>Lecturer's voice: declarative, precise, intuition-first. "We" for shared derivation steps; never first-person singular. One interpretive sentence after each nontrivial equation, as plain prose — no recurring bold gloss labels ("<strong>Reading:</strong>", "<strong>Interpretation:</strong>").</li>
<li><strong>Banned template scaffolds</strong> (keep the intuition, lose the boilerplate): headings or phrases "The picture in one breath", "The one-sentence picture", "The picture first", "The picture to hold onto", "The one picture to keep", "in one breath", "explain it to a friend", "say to a friend", "the punchline you should be able to say", "Exit line:", "note that", "it is worth noting", "the whole game". Open with prose or a heading specific to the note's content.</li>
<li>Equations: <code>\tag{n}</code> restarting per note, only on equations the note later references; <code>\boxed{}</code> for headline results. Checkmarks only as <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="normal">✓</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\checkmark</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6922em;"></span><span class="mord amsrm">✓</span></span></span></span> inside math, never ✓ in prose.</li>
<li>Typography: en dash in paired proper names (Nambu–Goto, Bekenstein–Hawking, Hardy–Ramanujan) in body text (file names keep their hyphens); <code>\mathrm{diag}</code>; American spelling; "worldsheet" and "spacetime" unhyphenated, "light-cone" hyphenated as adjective.</li>
<li>Wikilinks: short form <code>[[Title]]</code>/<code>[[Title|alias]]</code> unless the title is ambiguous vault-wide (duplicate basenames like "Boundary conditions as geometry", "Reparameterization invariance", every "00 — Overview") — those keep full-path links.</li>
<li>ASCII diagrams live inside triple-backtick fences.</li>
<li>Every content note ends <code>## Open questions</code> then <code>## Exercise</code> with one folded <code>> [!success]- Solution</code>.</li>
