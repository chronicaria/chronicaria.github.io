The role

Detroit uses him to run downfield. That is the field-stretching Z: fewer targets than a possession receiver (a target is a throw in his direction, counted whether or not he catches it — see Fantasy football glossary), but far more yards on each one, and a score that swings hard week to week — quiet one Sunday, enormous the next. He is the third option in that offense behind an alpha X, the clear first read, and a three-down bell cow who catches out of the backfield.

The number

1,088 receiving yards. From the projected stat line behind proj_points in Data/players.csv — a projection dated 2026-07-23, not an observation. At 0.1 a yard that is about 109 points from yardage alone, more than half his projected total, which is the signature of the archetype: the yards do the work, not the reception count that carries a big slot.

The risk

The CSV lists his risk as med, and the driver is variance rather than role loss. A receiver whose points come from long plays and touchdowns has a wide range of weekly outcomes, and the same profile that produces a 30-point Sunday produces three-point Sundays in between. Third in line for one team's throws sharpens that. The availability read, though, is better than his games-played line suggests — six of the seven games he has missed since 2023 are not weeks the official report listed him Out, and the projection is close to a repeat of a full 17-game season he has actually played.

The context

He is 25 on the September 9 opener, and Detroit took him twelfth overall in 2022 — first-round capital on the team that still employs him. His games-played line looks alarming at first: 12, 15 and 17 across the last three seasons, 44 of a possible 51. But the official injury report lists him Out only once and names an ankle twice, and one entry on it records a return from suspension rather than an injury. Most of what he has missed was not medical. In 2025 he was on the field for 90.2% of Detroit's snaps and drew 102 targets — 18.5% of the team's throws, second on the roster — for 65 catches and 1,117 yards. He is paid, at about $27 million a year, on a deal that voids in 2030.
