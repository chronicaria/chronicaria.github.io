Nine-category: top 65-105 per game, materially higher in punt-FT%, but a totals discount is mandatory. Points: strong double-double and block value if the platform rewards both. Dynasty: top-50-ish big-man path if the ankle stabilizes; availability and mobility create more downside than the rate line.

Range, strengths and risks

  • Strengths: elite rebounds, blocks and high-volume FG%; low need for offensive usage.
  • Liabilities: FT drag, turnovers for a low-assist center, no threes and game-flow sensitivity.
  • Floor: recovery limits his early workload or another lower-body issue occurs; 35-45 games near 20 minutes.
  • Base: starts when available but shares center with Stewart/Post, producing the table's double-double line.
  • Ceiling: 65-plus games at 29 minutes with 15/12 and 2.2 blocks—an early-mid-round category center.
  • Injury/transaction branches: Stewart and Post protect Memphis from forcing minutes; any camp restriction shifts Edey's workload down and their streaming value up.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Purdue, four seasons (2020-21 through 2023-24), a rare full-term college center in the modern draft. He grew from a 14.7-minute reserve as a freshman (8.7 points, 4.4 rebounds, 1.1 blocks, .597 FG, .714 FT) into the most decorated player in college basketball. 2021-22: 37 games, 19.0 minutes, 14.4 points, 7.7 rebounds, 1.2 blocks, .648 FG, .649 FT. 2022-23: 34 games, 31.7 minutes, 22.3 points, 12.9 rebounds, 1.5 assists, 2.1 blocks, .607 FG, .734 FT. 2023-24: 39 games, 32.0 minutes, 25.2 points, 12.2 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 2.2 blocks, .623 FG, .711 FT. He won consensus National Player of the Year and Big Ten Player of the Year in both 2023 and 2024, was a consensus first-team All-American twice, took the 2024 NCAA scoring title, and led Purdue to the 2024 national title game. Draft: 2024, first round, ninth overall, Memphis — no draft-night trade.

Fantasy history: 2024-25: outside the top 100 in 9-cat per-game value, though he was among the three best rookies in the format alongside Kel'el Ware and Donovan Clingan; 21.5 minutes and 1.3 blocks were the ceiling on it. 2025-26: an 11-game sample that graded far better per game — roughly top-60 shape on rate — but produced essentially no season-long value in any format. No season-end Basketball Monster or Hashtag rank was verifiable for either season, so treat those characterizations as rate-based reads rather than published finishes. Points leagues have rated him better than categories throughout: the FT drag and zero threes cost nothing there, while the double-doubles and high FG% volume carry.

Durability: 66 games (2024-25), 11 games (2025-26). He underwent left ankle surgery on June 10, 2025 to re-stabilize the joint and address excessive ligamentous laxity, with a four-month timetable, and that cost him the first 13 games of 2025-26. He played 11 games, last appearing December 7, 2025 against Portland, then was diagnosed with a stress reaction in the same left ankle. ESPN reported on March 1, 2026 that he would undergo a second operation on the left ankle to address ongoing discomfort and bone stress; he did not play again. Two surgeries on the same joint inside ten months, the second for bone stress in a 7-3, 305-pound frame, is the defining fact of his profile.

Trajectory: The 11 games he did play in 2025-26 were the most encouraging stretch of his career — points, rebounds, blocks, FG% and FT% all rose sharply on only 4.3 more minutes, which is genuine per-minute improvement rather than a workload artifact, and the .781 FT on real volume undercut the assumption that he would be a permanent punt-FT piece. The turnover rate nearly doubled to 2.4, the expected cost of a larger offensive role, and it remains the one category where he actively hurts. He is 24 during 2026-27 and only 77 NBA games in, so the skill curve should still point up. But the aging-curve question is irrelevant next to the load-bearing one: a body this size with a twice-operated ankle and a documented bone-stress reaction has a durability profile that no rate line can rescue, and any 2026-27 valuation has to price games played as the primary variable rather than production.
