Roddy Gayle Jr

Roddy Gayle Jr. is an Exhibit 10 camp invite with no NBA projection, listed here only because Detroit's two-way slots are full and he is the most plausible internal replacement if one opens.

Team: Detroit Pistons camp invite · Age: 22 · Position: SG · As of: 2026-07-18

2026-27 median projection

None. An Exhibit 10 is a non-guaranteed camp and preseason deal, not a roster spot, and no NBA statistical line is defensible until he converts to a two-way or standard contract. He holds zero minutes in the Detroit 240-minute allocation.

Nine-cat: no-draft. Points: no-draft. Dynasty: 1.5/10, and that is a bet on the contract converting rather than on the player.

Role and evidence

Gayle went undrafted in 2026 after four college seasons at Ohio State and Michigan and signed an Exhibit 10 with Detroit in late June (ClickOnDetroit). He was one of the named guards on Detroit's 2026 Summer League roster alongside Ebuka Okorie and Ugonna Onyenso.

His senior season was a rotation role on a dominant team rather than a lead role: 7.3 points, 3.3 rebounds, and 1.3 assists in 21.1 minutes across 39 games for a Michigan side that went 37-3, won the Big Ten at 19-1, and beat UConn 69-63 for the national title. He raised that to 10.3 points and 3.8 rebounds over the first four NCAA tournament games, which is the sample his camp case rests on. The profile is a downhill, defensively willing combo guard whose shooting is the open question — the same profile Detroit has repeatedly signed at the margins of its roster.

The relevance is structural rather than statistical. Detroit's three two-way slots are reported filled by Isaac Jones, Elijah Harkless, and Onyenso, and an Exhibit 10 is convertible to a two-way before opening night. Gayle is the queue behind that trio, not a competitor with the rotation.

Range

  • Ceiling: converts to a two-way, splits the season with the Motor City Cruise, and appears in scattered NBA garbage time. That is still no-draft in every standard format.
  • Floor: waived at the end of camp with an Exhibit 10 bonus and assigned to the Cruise, which is the base case.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four college seasons, 146 GP. Ohio State 2022-23 (35 GP, 4.6 PPG, .440/.429/.810 in 16.3 MPG) and 2023-24 (36 GP, 35 starts, 13.5 PPG, 4.6 RPG, 3.1 APG, .449/.284/.832 in 30.9 MPG — his lone lead-role year); Michigan 2024-25 (36 GP, 9.8 PPG, 3.4 RPG, 2.2 APG, .431/.232/.792) and 2025-26 (39 GP, 7.3 PPG, 3.3 RPG, 1.3 APG, .463/.318/.750 in 21.1 MPG on the 37-3 national-title team). College career: 8.8 PPG, 3.2 RPG, 1.9 APG, .446 FG%, .301 3P%, .799 FT%. Draft: undrafted (2026).

No NBA record — his most recent season is 2025-26 at Michigan; he holds only an Exhibit 10 camp deal with Detroit.

Durability: Reliable — 35, 36, 36 and 39 games across four seasons with no significant injury absences. Trajectory: The record shows a sophomore-year featured scorer who accepted shrinking roles on better teams: usage, minutes and scoring all declined for three straight years while his shooting efficiency ticked up in the smaller role. The good free-throw stroke (career .799) has never been matched by a reliable three (career .301). In 2026-27 he competes only for a two-way conversion behind Jones, Harkless and Onyenso, with a Motor City Cruise season as the base case. He becomes fantasy-relevant in no standard format this season; the only monitoring trigger is a two-way slot opening plus a genuine jump in his NBA-range shooting. Career sources: ESPN career stats, Wikipedia — Roddy Gayle Jr., Sports-Reference college page.
