Career record

Pre-NBA: Four years at Notre Dame, improving each season: 7.0 points and 4.4 rebounds as a freshman in 2011-12, then 8.9 and 4.7, then 13.8 and 7.1 in 2013-14, and 12.5 points with 7.4 rebounds in 37.2 minutes as a senior. He was also a pitcher, drafted by the Baltimore Orioles in the fourth round (121st overall) of the 2014 MLB draft and appearing for the Aberdeen IronBirds before returning to basketball. Draft: 2015, second round, 41st overall by Brooklyn; rights traded to Portland with Mason Plumlee on draft night.

He won a title with Milwaukee in 2021. Charlotte acquired him by trade on July 6, 2025, waived him on February 4, 2026, and re-signed him five days later to a two-year, $5.1 million deal carrying the 2026-27 team option Charlotte has since exercised.

Durability: Broadly available through the Milwaukee years — 61 to 82 games in six of seven seasons — with the recent drops driven by role rather than health. The 41 games in 2024-25 and 42 in 2025-26 reflect a shrinking rotation spot; the one substantial 2025-26 absence was a right-calf strain costing 11 games, plus three illness games.

Trajectory: A ten-year career that peaked in 2021-22 at 9.9 points and 2.2 threes in 26 minutes, then declined steadily as the shooting stopped carrying the minutes. At 34 he is a locker-room signing on non-guaranteed money, and the 7.1 minutes per game in 2025-26 is the honest read on his remaining role. He has no standalone fantasy case: the only path to relevance is surviving cutdown and then absorbing 15-plus minutes through injuries to Charlotte's wing rotation, and even that produces a threes-only line.
