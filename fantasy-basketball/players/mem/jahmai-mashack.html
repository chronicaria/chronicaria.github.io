Jahmai Mashack

Career record

Pre-NBA: Tennessee, four seasons (2021-25), 109 program wins; career 4.2 PPG as a defense-first wing. Senior year 2024-25: started all 38, 6.0 PTS / 4.2 REB / 1.5 AST / 1.7 STL on .454/.351, SEC All-Defensive, Naismith DPOY finalist, Elite Eight run. Draft: 2025, Round 2, Pick 59 (the draft's final pick) by Houston; rights moved to Memphis, where he signed a two-way on November 16, 2025.

Durability: No injury record; the 31-game total reflects the mid-season signing and two-way active-list limits, not health.

Trajectory: The record is unusually informative for a 59th pick: Memphis gave him real minutes (21.7 MPG, 7 starts) and the college scouting report reproduced exactly — elite defensive events (1.2 STL, 0.5 BLK from a guard) against genuinely poor shooting (.395/.253/.543). The senior-year .351 from three did not carry over. In 2026-27 he competes to keep a two-way slot and be Memphis's first defensive call-up. He becomes fantasy-relevant only as a deep-league steals stream during multiple Memphis guard absences; a standard-contract conversion would be the signal to watch.
