Allen Graves

Allen Graves is a high-activity rookie forward whose steals, blocks, rebounding and credible jumper give him a better fantasy pathway than his crowded initial role suggests.

Baseline and projection

At Santa Clara, Graves averaged 11.8 points, 6.5 rebounds, 1.9 assists and 1.9 steals in 22.7 minutes across 35 games on 51/41/75 shooting. NBA's profile describes a defense-first, multipositional forward with help instincts, offensive rebounding and a spacer/small-ball-five pathway. In Summer League he opened with 22 points and 13 boards; two games later he posted 15 points, five rebounds, six assists and three steals. Fantasy value

  • Nine-category / points: deep dynasty only at baseline; 13.6 simple FP/G.
  • Dynasty: rank 100-160, with category upside driven by stocks rather than scoring.
  • Ceiling: the Leonard deal removes Ingram and Dick, Graves earns 15-18 MPG and becomes a steals/blocks streamer by midseason.
  • Floor: rookie physical adjustment and Toronto's veteran depth send him through Raptors 905 for extended stretches.

Samson Folk and scout Josh Codinera projected a rotation 3-and-D floor and high-level starter ceiling, while explicitly calling star upside unlikely. That is attributed scouting. Raptors Republic / YouTube breakdown, 2026-07-09

Career record

Pre-NBA: Santa Clara — redshirted 2024-25, then one playing season (2025-26): 11.8 PTS, 6.5 REB, 1.9 AST, 1.9 STL in 22.7 MPG on .510/.410/.750 across 35 games, helping Santa Clara to its first NCAA Tournament since 1996. Third Bronco first-rounder in five years (after Jalen Williams and Brandin Podziemski). Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 19, Toronto Raptors.

No NBA record — 2026 first-round rookie with a single college season on file; the 2025-26 Santa Clara year above is the entire competitive record, plus 2026 Summer League flashes (22 points/13 rebounds in the opener; a 15/5/6 line with three steals two games later).

Durability: 35 of 35 games in his lone college season; the 2024-25 redshirt was developmental, and no injury history is on record. Trajectory: The record is one loud but thin season: elite defensive-event rates (1.9 steals plus blocks) and a 41% three on modest minutes, which is exactly the shape scouts project forward and exactly the sample size that demands caution — one WCC season at 22.7 MPG is the least evidence any 2026 first-rounder brings. In 2026-27 he competes for the fourth-forward role in Toronto, with 905 assignments the likely counterweight. He becomes fantasy-relevant if the frontcourt clears (e.g., a Leonard-deal outcome removing Ingram/Dick) and he holds 15-18 MPG, where the stocks-plus-threes mix would make him a deep-league streamer well before his scoring matters.
