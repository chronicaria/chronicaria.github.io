Riley Minix

Minix made nine NBA appearances for San Antonio and Cleveland in 2025-26, averaging 3.9 points in 6.4 minutes. In the G League, he averaged 19.3 points, 6.7 rebounds, 2.2 assists, and 1.3 steals in 31.6 minutes while shooting .556 from the field and .457 from three. Cavaliers roster news · G League two-way release

Status and fantasy profile

  • Contract: Cleveland signed him to a two-way contract February 14, with a reported second year. Hoops Rumors reported in June that he was nearing a deal with ASVEL. Until resolved, the NBA line is conditional and becomes 0/0 for Cleveland if he leaves. Hoops Rumors, June 2026
  • 9-cat: future hooks are 3PM, REB, and potentially efficient low-volume scoring; he lacks an NBA role.
  • Points leagues: G League production does not translate into rosterable NBA volume in the base case.
  • Dynasty: deep speculative hold only if he remains in the organization; Europe may improve development but removes near-term NBA availability.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: signs with ASVEL and produces zero Cleveland games.
  • Base: Charge starter plus occasional seven-minute call-ups; no redraft value.
  • Ceiling: wins 15-18 reserve-forward minutes after a roster move and becomes a threes/rebounds deep-league stream.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four seasons at NAIA Southeastern University 2019-23 (14.6 PTS / 7.6 REB as a freshman, then 22.7 and 25.4 PPG in his last two years; two-time NAIA First-team All-American, two-time Sun Conference Player of the Year), then one NCAA season at Morehead State 2023-24: 20.9 PTS / 9.7 REB over 35 games with 18 double-doubles, 2024 OVC Player of the Year. Draft: undrafted (2024).

Durability: Shoulder surgery ended his 2024-25 G League season with Austin after a strong Tip-Off Tournament (20.5 PPG); healthy through 2025-26. Missed most of his 2020-21 NAIA season to injury.

Trajectory: The record is a classic small-school scorer's climb — NAIA All-American, one dominant mid-major year, undrafted, then two-way stints in San Antonio (waived December 12, 2025) and Cleveland, where his G League shooting stayed elite. Ten NBA games is no sample; the G League line is the real signal. For 2026-27 he competes for Cleveland's two-way forward depth behind Tomlin and Tyson — if the reported ASVEL move completes, the NBA projection zeroes out. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he stays, converts to a standard deal, and wins 15-plus reserve minutes as a threes/rebounds stream.
