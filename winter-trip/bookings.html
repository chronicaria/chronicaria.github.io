Reservation calendar

Most of this cannot be bought yet. The order below follows the release dates of the institutions themselves.

July–September

  • Buy the flight and Last Supper; hold refundable lodging.
  • Check the Florence Duomo official calendar for a 12:45 dome slot on 27 December.
  • Begin a weekly rail check in September, but do not worry if inventory is absent; the timetable changes on 13 December.
  • Around 29 September, begin checking Pisa for 28 December. One official Pisa page says online tickets can open 90 days ahead, while the FAQ currently says 15 days. Because the official pages conflict, start early and set a second hard reminder for 13 December. Official purchase advice · official FAQ

October–early November

  • 8 October: buy three adjacent Swan Lake seats for 31 December at 18:00. Target €48 balcony rows 2–3 or €35 first gallery; skip the €580-per-person ballet-and-hotel-dinner package. Official performance · official prices · sale-date announcement
  • Reserve the 24 December dinner, 25 December lunch, 1 January lunch, and a late 31 December dinner as soon as written menus appear. Do not book the post-opera table until the theatre publishes runtime or the restaurant agrees to hold it flexibly.
  • Book one Florence bistecca dinner and one small Venetian seafood osteria.
  • Check Uffizi, Accademia and Vatican inventory weekly; those institutions had not published a dependable public opening interval for these dates.
  • Begin weekly checks for the official Lake Como winter ferry PDF in late October.

November

  • Around 10–15 November: winter rail schedules should be substantially clearer. Buy the three direct trains and verify them against the final timetable milestone.
  • Rebuild the 23 December Varenna–Bellagio–Milan chain and require at least 90 minutes before the evening Venice departure. See the Lake Como winter day.
  • Confirm Seta or Nando for Bellagio lunch at 12:30 in writing.
  • Around mid-November: buy the 29 December Pantheon slot.
  • Reconfirm every holiday meal in writing: date, time, fixed-menu price, wine/water/service inclusion, deposit, cancellation, dietary needs, and whether the table continues to midnight on New Year's Eve.

December

  • 1 December: buy the 08:30 Colosseum ticket for 31 December when its 30-day window opens. Named tickets and photo ID are required. Official ticket portal · hours and rules
  • 10 December: first full holiday audit—municipal transit, attraction notices, ferry service, papal calendar, Rome's public New Year's event, and state-museum extraordinary openings.
  • 13 December: hard Pisa reminder if tickets were not already released.
  • 13 December: re-run every exact 23 December train/ferry pairing after the timetable change; save the official PDFs.
  • 22 December: final audit—Lake weather/ferries, rail engineering, meal confirmations, Venice ACTV holiday service, Rome New Year's transport, We Run Rome closures, and any security/container rules.

Ticket rules that matter

  • Carry a passport or government photo ID when a ticket is named.
  • Carry a current physical student card. The Vatican's student reduction through age 25 is not limited to EU citizens, but many Italian state-museum youth reductions are; U.S. visitors should budget the full price unless the official rule clearly applies.
  • The Last Supper admits the group for only 15 minutes and requires arrival 30 minutes early.
  • The Brunelleschi Dome has 463 stairs, no lift, a strict named time, and only a short grace period.
  • The three-day Brunelleschi Pass also covers Giotto's Bell Tower; use it on 29 December rather than stacking the two Florence climbs.
  • Pisa requires tower bags in its cloakroom and refuses late arrivals.
  • Lake ferries can suspend for wind/waves and ordinary tickets do not guarantee capacity on one specific sailing; reach the dock 20 minutes early.
  • Colosseum tickets open exactly 30 days ahead and use passport-exact names.
  • High-speed rail tickets are assigned-train reservations; regional tickets follow separate validation rules.

Holiday meal checklist

Ask each restaurant in writing:

  • Is the meal à la carte or fixed menu?
  • What is the exact per-person price?
  • Are water, wine, cover/service, midnight toast and entertainment included?
  • What is the deposit and cancellation policy?
  • For 31 December, will the restaurant seat the group after the opera and keep the table through midnight?
  • Can dietary restrictions be accommodated?
  • What is the dress code?

Buy breakfast supplies, water, fruit and emergency sandwiches before late afternoon on 24 December and 31 December.
