Duke Brennan

Duke Brennan is a powerful rebounding center on Brooklyn's camp/G League fringe, with a disputed Exhibit 10 label and no dependable NBA fantasy role yet.

  • Team / role: Brooklyn Nets Summer League center; reported Exhibit 10 by one undrafted-free-agent tracker, but not independently confirmed in the signing coverage found.

Fantasy translation

  • Redraft: not draftable; even a two-way conversion would initially make him an injury-only rebound streamer.
  • Points: no present value without a confirmed contract and rotation role.
  • Dynasty: watch only in 30-team or G League-aware formats because rebounding and interior finishing can translate into a reserve-center niche.

Role, floor and ceiling

Brennan is behind Day'Ron Sharpe, Moritz Wagner, Danny Wolf and several small-ball centers. Floor: Summer League-only relationship followed by a different professional destination. Ceiling: Exhibit 10 conversion, Long Island starting center and emergency Brooklyn appearances.

Risk monitor

No Ceilings labeled Brennan an Exhibit 10, but Villanova/local reporting described a Summer League audition and the independent Nets signing report named only Dion Brown and Ben Humrichous. Until a transaction or camp roster confirms the deal, the contract bucket is unresolved. No Ceilings, 2026-06-29 · Villanova-area report, 2026-06-28 · Nets signing report, 2026-06-26

His college 15.1% offensive-rebound rate is the carrying skill, while limited vertical pop, weak ball-screen defense and a 1.9% block rate constrain the defensive projection. No Ceilings, 2026-06-29

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four college seasons across three programs. Arizona State 2022-23 (36 GP, 9.1 MPG, 2.4 PTS, 2.3 REB, 51.9 FG%), Grand Canyon 2023-24 (35 GP, 22.9 MPG, 7.0 PTS, 6.7 REB, 58.3 FG%) and 2024-25 (30 GP, 26.6 MPG, 10.4 PTS, 9.2 REB, 64.6 FG%), then Villanova 2025-26 (32 GP, 31.0 MPG, 12.4 PTS, 10.3 REB, 2.0 AST, 0.8 STL, 0.5 BLK, 65.8 FG%, 61.3 FT%). Career college line 133 GP, 21.9 MPG, 7.8 PTS, 6.9 REB on 62.2% shooting with essentially no three-point volume. The 2025-26 season produced 14 double-doubles and the first 10-plus rebounding average by a Villanova player in roughly four decades. Draft: undrafted (2026).

Brennan has not appeared in an NBA regular-season game. His most recent competitive season is the Villanova 2025-26 line above; his only professional exposure to date is the 2026 Summer League, where he posted 10 points, 12 rebounds, two assists, one steal and one block in 23 minutes against Milwaukee.

Durability: Missed five games at Grand Canyon in 2024-25 (30 of 35); otherwise a full-season player, including all 36 at Arizona State as a freshman and 32 starts at Villanova. No reported major injury.

Trajectory: A four-year college big whose value is concentrated in offensive rebounding and interior finishing at high efficiency, with no perimeter shot and modest shot-blocking. He is competing for a two-way or Long Island roster spot rather than an NBA rotation role in 2026-27. Fantasy relevance requires a chain of events: an Exhibit 10 converting to a two-way, then simultaneous injuries to the bigs ahead of him, at which point he would be a short-term rebounding streamer only.
