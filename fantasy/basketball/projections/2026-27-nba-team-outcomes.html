Fantasy interpretation

  • High team strength can stabilize closing roles and reduce late-season shutdown incentives, but it can also create blowout and workload-management risk.
  • Wide win bands or rating standard errors flag roster, rookie, health, or minute uncertainty that should propagate into player projections.
  • A poor team does not imply poor fantasy value. Concentrated creation and permissive minutes can make weak real teams productive fantasy environments.
  • Team dossiers may challenge this quantitative prior with newer transactions or mechanism-specific evidence; disagreements stay visible rather than being silently averaged.
