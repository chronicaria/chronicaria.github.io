<p>The amplitude is soft in the UV. Because a string is a fuzzy extended object, not a point, it dies exponentially at high energy — the Fourier transform of a smooth blob, not the power-law of a point charge. This is the first glimpse of why string theory is UV-finite.</p>
<p>This is the formula string theory started with: Veneziano wrote it down in 1968 to fit strong-interaction data, years before anyone knew a string produced it.</p>
