2025-26 baseline

Orlando's official game notes list Penda at 59 games and 12.8 minutes, averaging 3.8 points, 3.2 rebounds, and 1.2 assists. In two starts he averaged 7.0 points, 6.5 rebounds, 1.5 assists, 1.0 steal, and 1.0 block.

That broad stat shape—rebounding, passing, and defensive events rather than pure scoring—gives him more fantasy pathways than a one-dimensional reserve. The 2026-27 healthy base sits slightly below his 2025-26 minutes because a healthy Wagner restores the forward queue, but he is inside the rotation rather than behind it.

Role and pathway

Penda is the backup power forward behind Banchero, sharing second-unit forward minutes with da Silva and situationally Isaac. He is also among the first names to absorb wing absences. A multiweek Wagner or Banchero absence can create 20-25 minutes; a fully healthy roster holds him near ten. Sweeney's stated emphasis on physicality and offensive-glass crashing is a direct fit for his rebounding.

Category shape

  • Potential strengths: rebounds, steals, blocks, secondary assists.
  • Needs proof: three-point shooting and FT.
  • Risk: low scoring efficiency and uncertain role can erase the versatile counting line.

Career record

Pre-NBA: French, born January 7, 2005. Two seasons at JA Vichy-Clermont in LNB Pro B, the French second division — 3.9 points in 37 games as a 17-year-old in 2022-23, then 9.5 points and 5.9 rebounds in 37 games in 2023-24, earning Pro B Best Young Player. He moved up to Le Mans Sarthe Basket in the LNB Élite, France's top division, for 2024-25 and started at 19: 10.3 points, 5.3 rebounds and 2.8 assists, winning Pro A Best Young Player. Also an LNB All-Star and Young Star Game MVP in 2025, with bronze at the 2022 FIBA U17 World Cup and gold at the 2024 FIBA U20 EuroBasket (11.7 points in seven games). Draft: 2025, second round, 32nd overall, Boston; Orlando acquired his rights on draft night. He spent part of 2025-26 on assignment with the Osceola Magic.

Durability: 59 of 82 games as a rookie, with the absences a mix of rotation decisions and G League assignment rather than a documented injury. No availability concern in the record.

Trajectory: Two years of European first-division production followed by a rookie season that translated the shape but not the efficiency — the rebounding, passing and low-turnover profile all carried, while .404 from the field and .323 from three did not. He was already a starter in a top European league at 19, which is the reason to be patient. He enters 2026-27 as the backup power forward behind Banchero, sharing second-unit forward minutes with da Silva and Isaac, and is among the first replacements for a wing absence. He becomes fantasy-relevant if a multiweek Banchero or Wagner absence pushes him past 20 minutes, where the across-the-board counting profile — rebounds, secondary assists, steals and blocks together — produces deep-league value that his scoring alone never would.
