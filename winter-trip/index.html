Italy, north to south

Milan, Venice, Florence, Rome — four hotel bases, three high-speed trains and eight distinct places, sequenced so that nothing worth seeing is shut on the day we arrive.

The sun rises at 07:47 and sets at 16:31. That single fact does more to shape this itinerary than any preference we have: it is why the Lake Como boats are caught before nine, why every climb is a morning climb, and why the long reserved lunch — not the afternoon — is where the day's leisure lives. Why north to south

Fly JFK → Milan overnight on Sunday 20 December, travel south by rail, and fly Rome → JFK on Saturday 2 January. The direction is not sentimental. It is what the holiday calendar allows.

  • There is no city change on 25 December or 1 January, when transport and attractions are most constrained.
  • Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve are already in the city where we sleep — no risky late transfer.
  • Milan's Last Supper falls on a Tuesday rather than its Monday closure.
  • Florence's museum-closing Monday becomes the natural Pisa–Lucca day.
  • The Vatican and Colosseum land on fully operational midweek dates, and 1 January is reserved for one gentle outdoor loop.
  • The likely afternoon nonstop out of Rome is more forgiving than Milan's typical morning departure.

This is the ambitious-but-controlled version. Naples and Genoa are excellent; a fifth base over this calendar would turn roughly ten usable sightseeing days into a transfer-heavy trip. That reasoning is laid out in what we cut.

First five actions

  1. Reprice and buy Delta DL184 + DL231 as one multi-city ticket if the three-person carry-on total holds near $3,660. Use the $1,105 American/Aer Lingus combination only if the saving matters more than a nonstop return.
  2. Buy the official 09:30 English guided Last Supper visit on 22 December if three seats remain. Standard admission currently has no block of three.
  3. Hold one refundable legal triple or three-person apartment in each city. The live value set is about $2,419–2,439 including estimated taxes.
  4. Put 8 October on the calendar for three adjacent Swan Lake seats at Teatro Costanzi on New Year's Eve.
  5. Monitor the 2026/27 Lake Como ferry timetable and winter rail schedules from late October through mid-November, then lock the 23 December chain.
