Justin Edwards

Justin Edwards is Philadelphia's first reserve wing, but Jaylen Brown's arrival turns a useful real-life role into a low-ceiling fantasy profile unless injuries restore 25-plus minutes.

Edwards played 64 games at 15.3 minutes in 2025-26, producing 6.0 points, 1.5 rebounds, 1.3 assists, 0.8 steals, and 0.2 blocks on .447 FG, .372 from three, and .846 FT. That is a good low-turnover reserve line but too little volume for standard leagues. Sixers game notes, May 2026

Role and fantasy outlook

  • Brown takes the starting 3 and the highest-value wing possessions. Edwards can still cover both forward spots and is the first replacement when Brown or Dean Wade sits.
  • 9-cat: steals, one three, decent percentages, and low turnovers are the useful pieces. Low rebounds and scoring prevent standard relevance at 14 minutes.
  • Points: waiver/call-up option only. His projection lacks the volume or peripheral spikes required by most formulas.
  • Dynasty: deep hold in 20-team formats. Philadelphia controls a 2027-28 team option, but Brown/Edgecombe block a near-term breakout. RealGM roster, accessed July 18, 2026

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: single-digit minutes or DNPs when Philadelphia uses three guards and Barlow at the 4.
  • Base: 14 minutes and waiver-wire status.
  • Ceiling: 25-28 minutes after a Brown/Wade injury, with enough 3-and-D production for top-150-to-190 stretches.

Health and uncertainty

Edwards carried no public July restriction. The risk is opportunity rather than health. RealGM's automated chart still lists him as a starter, but the official Brown acquisition and independent fantasy analysis make a reserve role much more likely. Career record

Pre-NBA: Kentucky 2023-24 (one-and-done): 8.8 PTS, 3.4 REB in 32 games (30 starts) — a consensus five-star who at one point ranked No. 1 in the ESPN 2023 class, and whose freshman year underdelivered on that billing. Draft: undrafted (2024); two-way with Philadelphia 2024-07-04, converted to standard 2025-02-09, re-signed 2025-06-27 for three years, $7.1M.

Durability: No significant injuries on record. The 44-game 2024-25 was contract-driven (two-way until February), and 2025-26's 64 games came with a healthy roster shrinking his minutes, not medical absence.

Trajectory: The rookie-year 26-minute audition (10.1 PTS, 1.6 3PM, 1.0 STL on a tanking roster) is the more informative sample; 2025-26 showed the same per-minute 3-and-D profile compressed into a 15-minute reserve role on a contender. In 2026-27 he is the first wing replacement behind Jaylen Brown and Dean Wade. He becomes fantasy-relevant only through injury above him: a Brown or Wade absence restoring 25-plus minutes brings back the top-150-to-190 stretches his rookie year already demonstrated.
