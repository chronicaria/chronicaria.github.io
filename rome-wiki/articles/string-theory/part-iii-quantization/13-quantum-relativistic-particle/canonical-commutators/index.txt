<h2 id="the-postulated-commutators">The postulated commutators</h2>

<p><span class="katex-error" title="ParseError: KaTeX parse error: \tag works only in display equations" style="color:#cc0000"> \boxed{\;[\,x^I,\,p^J\,] = i\,\eta^{IJ} = i\,\delta^{IJ},\qquad [\,x_0^-,\,p^+\,] = i\,\eta^{-+} = -\,i\;}\tag{2}</span></p>
