3:T26837,<h2 id="an-infinite-stack-of-oscillators">An infinite stack of oscillators</h2>
<p>A guitar string, quantum-mechanically, is an infinite collection of harmonic oscillators, one for each vibrational overtone. Every quantum oscillator keeps an irreducible <strong>zero-point energy</strong> even in its ground state: it can never sit perfectly still. Add up the zero-point energies of all the overtones and the total is formally infinite. That infinity is not a disaster. It is the same object as the <strong>Casimir energy</strong> — the small attractive force between two uncharged metal plates in vacuum, which is real and measured — and in both cases the physically meaningful quantity is finite and comes out <strong>negative</strong>.</p>

<h2 id="why-the-cutoff-answer-is-trustworthy">Why the cutoff answer is trustworthy</h2>
