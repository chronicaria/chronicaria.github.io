Golden tests

The page's geometry kernel and classifier are ports. A port that has drifted is worse than no port at all, because the reader would have no way to know. Every assertion below runs in your browser, against a reference value the solver produced, and prints its own verdict.

Reference values come from the solver's own figure definitions and from four rows of its synthetic-classifier output. The μ → 0 reduction and the polar gradient are asserted at tolerance 0 — bit-exact, not merely close — because the constructions are built to be exact there and a near miss would mean a real bug. The break-up radius is asserted at 1e−12 rather than exactly, because the exact trigonometric root lands one unit in the last place away from 3/2.
