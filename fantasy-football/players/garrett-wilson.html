The role

He is an alpha X: his team's unambiguous first option, lined up wide, thrown to whether or not the matchup favours him. High target volume — throws in his direction, counted whether or not he catches them (Fantasy football glossary) — is the most stable quantity in fantasy football, more stable than efficiency, which is why alphas are the safest picks on the board that are not running backs. His is a concentrated version of the role: the New York Jets have no second receiver anywhere near him on the board.

The number

100 catches, from the note column of Data/players.csv (the stat line behind his 245.5 proj_points). This is a projection dated 2026-07-23, not an observation. A hundred catches is a hundred points before a single yard, about six a game across a 17-game season, and it is the highest catch projection of anyone available at picks 24 and 25.

The risk

The CSV flags him med, and the driver is the offense around him rather than the player. The Jets' projected quarterback is a pocket QB with the lowest point projection of any starting quarterback on the six teams clustered at this turn — a receiver cannot score on throws that are never made, and volume in a weak passing offense is the version of volume most likely to be revised downward. The volume question now sits on top of an availability one: he played 7 games in 2025, so a 100-catch projection is assuming a full season he last completed in 2024 (Availability and injury risk).

The context

He is 26 on the September 9 opener, and the Jets took him 10th overall in 2022 — top-ten capital, and they have kept feeding him (Draft capital dominance). Then 2025 happened. He played 17 games, then 17, then 7 — 41 of a possible 51, with every absence coming last season. The report listed him Out two weeks and Questionable or Doubtful three (knee four times, elbow once); the other eight missed games never appeared on it as injury designations. When he played he took 87 percent of the Jets' plays but drew only 59 targets and 36 catches, a 12.5 percent share of the team's season. The projected 100 catches is nearly triple that, and most of the gap is the ten games back. He is paid $32.5 million a year, a free agent in 2031.

Open questions

  • Whether 100 catches survives contact with the quarterback situation. A concentrated target share in a bad offense and a diluted one in a good offense can land in the same place, and the projection does not distinguish them.
  • Bye week 13, which clashes with Zay Flowers — the other receiver flagged as a substitute at this turn — so the two should not be paired.
