3:Tbd9,<h2 id="the-headline-metric-acc-of-500-hpa-height">The headline metric: ACC of 500 hPa height</h2>
<p>The 500 hPa geopotential height field is the standard "mid-tropospheric flow" diagnostic. The <strong>anomaly correlation coefficient</strong> measures how well the forecast's departures-from-climatology correlate with the observed departures. It runs from +1 (perfect) through ~0 (no value) to −1 (anti-phase).</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>ACC ≈ 0.6</strong> — the "useful forecast" threshold: below it, synoptic features are no longer positioned well enough to trust.</li>
<li><strong>ACC ≈ 0.8</strong> — ECMWF's official <em>headline</em> score for a very good forecast.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>ECMWF HRES skill horizon</strong> (Northern Hemisphere, recent ~2023–24):</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Threshold</th>
<th>Reached at</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>0.8 (headline)</td>
<td>~day 7</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>0.6 (useful)</td>
<td>~day 9–10</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>The ensemble mean typically reaches 0.6 about a day later than HRES. These crossings are where deterministic skill fades into the <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/atmospheric-predictability-limit/" class="wiki-link">~2-week ceiling</a>.</p>
<h2 id="the-one-day-per-decade-rule">The "one day per decade" rule</h2>
<p><strong>Bauer, Thorpe &#x26; Brunet (2015)</strong> quantified the long, quiet improvement of NWP. Their exact framing: <em>"the skill of deterministic weather forecasts in the range from 3 to 10 days ahead has been improving by about one day per decade — that is, today's 6-day forecast is as accurate as the 5-day forecast ten years ago."</em></p>
<p>The gains came from better models, higher resolution, better <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/ensemble-forecasting/" class="wiki-link">data assimilation</a>, the satellite observing system, and raw compute — not one breakthrough. (The underlying result traces to Simmons &#x26; Hollingsworth 2002.)</p>
<p>As of 2026 the "best deterministic global model" framing is contested: ML models (GraphCast, FuXi, ECMWF's AIFS) now match or slightly beat HRES on Z500 ACC at these lead times.</p>

</ul>
