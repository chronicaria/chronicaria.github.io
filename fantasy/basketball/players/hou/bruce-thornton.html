Bruce Thornton

Bruce Thornton is Houston's polished rookie guard and best deep-roster stash, but four strong Summer League games do not solve the VanVleet-Sheppard-Smart-Bogdanović logjam.

Nine-cat: redraft watch list, not a draft target. Reference points: 2.0 FP/G, grade E. Dynasty: 5.0/10; top-200/deep-stash range.

Role and evidence

Houston acquired the 31st pick and drafted Thornton on June 24. The NBA prospect profile credits his final two Ohio State seasons with 18.8 points, 4.3 rebounds, 4.3 assists, and 1.1 steals on 53/40/84 shooting (NBA Draft profile); Ohio State identifies him as its all-time scoring leader and a four-time captain.

Through four Las Vegas games he averaged 21.2 points, 3.8 rebounds, 3.8 assists, and 3.0 steals in 31.5 minutes (RealGM Summer League log). NBA recaps recorded 27 points and three steals on July 10 and 23 points on July 16. The sample is encouraging skill evidence, not an NBA rate projection; a Rockets community thread on July 15 made the same correlation warning.

Range, confidence, and reversals

  • Ceiling: a consolidation trade or VanVleet setback creates 16-20 minutes and a path to threes, assists, and steals.
  • Floor: mostly G League/development work with brief garbage-time appearances.
  • Confidence: roster high; minutes very low; rookie rates low; availability high.
  • Dynasty trigger: his ability to defend NBA guards and keep turnovers low matters more than another large Summer League scoring night.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Ohio State, four seasons (2022-26), four-year starter and four-time captain — 2022-23: 10.6 PTS/2.7 REB/2.6 AST on .456/.375/.729 (35 GP); 2023-24: 15.7/3.7/4.8, 1.2 STL on .427/.333/.850 (35 GP); 2024-25: 17.7/3.4/4.6, 1.1 STL on .501/.424/.853 (32 GP); 2025-26: 19.9/5.1/3.9, 1.1 STL on .554/.400/.829 (34 GP). Finished as Ohio State's all-time leading scorer (2,164 points); one of two Big Ten players ever with 2,000+ points, 500+ rebounds, and 500+ assists. All-Big Ten Third Team 2024, Second Team 2025 and 2026. Draft: 2026, Round 2, Pick 31 — selected by New York and traded on draft night to Houston.

No NBA record — 2026 second-round rookie. Most recent competitive samples: the 2025-26 Ohio State senior season above, plus 2026 Las Vegas Summer League (4 GP: 21.2 PTS, 3.8 REB, 3.8 AST, 3.0 STL in 31.5 MPG).

Durability: 136 games in four college seasons (35, 35, 32, 34) — never missed extended time; a durable, high-minute lead guard. Trajectory: The record shows steady year-over-year growth in both volume and efficiency, ending at 55/40/83 on real usage — the profile of a finished, older (23) rookie rather than a projection bet. In 2026-27 he competes for third-guard scraps behind VanVleet, Sheppard, Smart, and Bogdanović, so the median outcome is G League reps plus garbage time. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if an injury or consolidation trade hands him 16-20 NBA minutes, at which point the threes/assists/steals mix plays in deep leagues almost immediately. Career sources: Wikipedia · Sports-Reference college page · Ohio State bio · ESPN
