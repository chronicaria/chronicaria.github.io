He sat out 2023-24 after transferring to Grambling State, then broke out at Jacksonville State as 2025 Conference USA Player of the Year and first-team all-conference, including a 36-point game against Middle Tennessee that set the school home record. The SMU year traded volume for efficiency — scoring down four points but field-goal percentage up nearly four points against better competition. Draft: 2026, second round, 58th overall by New Orleans; he is the first New Orleans native the Pelicans have drafted.

NBA record: None. Pierre has not appeared in an NBA regular-season game and, as of July 18, 2026, has not signed a standard or two-way contract — the Pelicans hold his draft rights and he played the 2026 Summer League on that basis. His most recent competitive season is the 2025-26 SMU line above.

Durability: No injury history of note; he played 24 to 36 games in each active college season. The 2023-24 blank is a transfer year, not a medical one.

Trajectory: Two strong late-college seasons after three ordinary ones, which is the profile of a player who improved genuinely and also benefited from a step down in competition before the SMU year re-tested him. He turned 24 in July 2026, old for a rookie, and that compresses the development runway a 58th pick normally gets. The immediate question is not minutes but a contract: until he signs a two-way, his 2026-27 Pelicans games are zero. Even after signing, the base case is Birmingham with short call-ups, and fantasy relevance would require a wing trade plus efficient G League scoring forcing a standard conversion.
