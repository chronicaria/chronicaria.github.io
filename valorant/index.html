Where somebody sat out, their line stops and resumes when they return: the running total carries, the stroke does not, because a connecting slope would draw performance in rounds nobody played.

The walk

    Same model as before, not re-solved: these are the same cross-fitted, out-of-fold credits from a 5,137-match corpus, re-sliced to whoever was on the server. A line only begins once that player passes 20 rounds of exposure — below that a rate is noise, and the release suppresses it rather than drawing it. Expect a line to swing hard just after it starts and settle as exposure grows: that early movement is the denominator filling in, not form. Click any match to open its rounds.

    Season rates

    These are not a leaderboard, and the page will not sort them. They cover different matches, different opponents, and exposure differing by more than ten to one. Trzzcko's rate is the clearest warning: five matches and ninety-three rounds is enough to produce a large number and nowhere near enough to mean one.

    There is a deeper reason they don't compare. Every non-cohort player in this dataset is re-pseudonymised in each match, so opponents and teammates cannot be linked across games. Lineup quality and player skill are therefore not separable — a structural limit, not something more matches would fix.
