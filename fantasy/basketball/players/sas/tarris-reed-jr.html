Role case

San Antonio signed Reed to a first-round rookie-scale contract after selecting him in 2026. NBA transactions, accessed 2026-07-18 His Summer League included 14-point, 10-rebound and 12-point, 12-rebound games, reinforcing the immediate rebounding and interior-efficiency case. NBA player game log, accessed 2026-07-18

Luke Kornet opens as the safer backup to Victor Wembanyama, but Reed's physicality and development priority create a real battle. Jayden Quaintance's knee surgery removes one competitor early. The San Antonio Spurs healthy map gives Reed three minutes, with a much larger season average possible if he passes Kornet or covers rest games.

Ceiling and floor

  • Ceiling: wins 14 backup-center minutes and offers deep-league FG%, rebounds and blocks.
  • Floor: spends most of the season in Austin and appears only in spot duty.
  • Best use: deep dynasty or Wembanyama handcuff watchlist, not standard redraft.
  • Category caution: limited passing, threes and uncertain free throws narrow his route to rank value.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four college seasons. Michigan 2022-23: 3.4 PTS / 3.9 REB / 0.9 BLK on .517 FG (34 GP, reserve); 2023-24: 9.0 PTS / 7.2 REB / 1.4 BLK on .519 FG but .586 FT (31 starts). UConn 2024-25: 9.6 PTS / 7.3 REB / 1.6 BLK on .670 FG, .644 FT — Big East Sixth Man of the Year. UConn 2025-26: 14.7 PTS / 9.0 REB / 2.0 BLK with a conference-leading 60.7 eFG% in 35 starts — First-team All-Big East, All-NCAA Tournament, East Region Most Outstanding Player (19.5 PTS / 13.2 REB across UConn's six-game tournament run). Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 26 (selected by Denver, traded to San Antonio for pick 35 plus 2028 and 2031 second-rounders).

No NBA record; most recent season is UConn 2025-26 above, followed by July 2026 Summer League games of 14/10 and 12/12.

Durability: 136 college games in four years with no missed-season injury history — a genuinely durable big.

Trajectory: The record shows a slow-developing physical center who improved every single year — from end-of-bench freshman to conference-leading interior efficiency and a March-carrying senior season — with the classic limitations (career .563 college FT through his junior year, no three-point range, limited passing). In 2026-27 he competes with Luke Kornet for the backup-center minutes behind Wembanyama, with Austin G League time in the base case. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he wins the backup job outright or covers Wembanyama rest/injury games, where his rebounding, FG% and blocks would play in deep leagues immediately; the FT% is the standing category tax.
