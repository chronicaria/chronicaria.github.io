The role

He is the three-down bell cow in Indianapolis: on the field on every down, absorbing the carries (running plays handed to him) and staying out there on passing downs rather than yielding to a specialist. A team has a fixed number of touches to give — see The conserved team touch budget — and this is a back projected to take almost all of the running half of it. His quarterback is Daniel Jones, one of the four names you already know, listed here as a pocket QB.

The number

299 carries, from the note column of the promoted Data/players.csv — a projection built from FFToday's complete stat-line snapshot updated 2026-07-30, not a record of anything played. Only one back in the file projects for more. His projected catch total is modest by bell-cow standards, so the case rests on that carry count: in full PPR the receiving work is what separates the top backs, and his edge is volume on the ground rather than a second income stream through the air.

The risk

The risk column says low, and the pressure point is the receiving half of the role. If Indianapolis gives the passing-down work to a change-of-pace back, the archetype quietly degrades toward early-down committee back while the carry projection stays intact — same yards, fewer points in a league that pays a full point per catch. The backup listed behind him carries a high risk flag of his own, which is the usual shape of a handcuff situation. The second pressure point is arithmetic the low flag ignores: the projection assumes seventeen games, and he has played seventeen once in the last three years — see Availability and injury risk.

The context

He is 27.6 on the opener and was the 41st pick of the 2020 draft, second-round capital Indianapolis has kept paying, which is the pattern described in Draft capital dominance. Availability is the correction this note needed: 10, 14 and 17 games across 2023–2025, 41 of a possible 51. Seven weeks appear on the official injury report — six listed Out and one more Questionable or Doubtful — naming an ankle four times and a thumb three; four of the missed games do not appear there at all, so those absences were not medical. His modelled availability is 0.809, under the 0.823 running back mean — a MODEL, shrunk toward that mean, not an observation. In 2025 he played 82.6% of snaps, took 323 carries and caught 46 of 55 targets, so the 299-carry, 36-catch projection is a haircut on what he just did. He is paid $42 million, $26.5 million guaranteed, free agent 2027.
