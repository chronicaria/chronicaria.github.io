Two flags

Full PPR. Every reception is a full point. Every published non-PPR and half-PPR ranking is therefore the wrong board and may not be used unadjusted. See 2026 draft board.

The waiver order resets weekly. Priority is not saved and does not roll over — a claim not spent this week expires worthless, because next week's order is recomputed from the standings regardless. See Waivers.
