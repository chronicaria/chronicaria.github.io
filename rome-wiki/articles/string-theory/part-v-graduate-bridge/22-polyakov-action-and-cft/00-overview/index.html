    22 — Polyakov Action and CFT (Overview)

    The Polyakov action swaps the ugly square root of Nambu–Goto for an auxiliary worldsheet metric, and that one swap turns the worldsheet into a 2D conformal field theory whose symmetry algebra is the Virasoro algebra — the language every modern string text speaks.

    From area to a fake ruler

    Think of the string's worldsheet as a rubber sheet swept out in spacetime. Nambu–Goto measures its area, and area comes with a square root (−det⁡γ\sqrt{-\det \gamma}−detγ​) that is miserable to quantize. Polyakov's move is the same trick that tames the relativistic point particle with an einbein: stop measuring the sheet with its own induced geometry, and hand it a separate "ruler" metric hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ that you vary independently. The ruler is fake — it has no dynamics of its own — but introducing it makes the action polynomial in the fields. Then three facts cascade:

    • The ruler is pure gauge. A symmetric 2×22\times22×2 metric has 3 components; the theory has exactly 3 local symmetries (2 coordinate reparametrizations + 1 Weyl rescaling) to eat them. So hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ carries zero physical information and can be flattened to ηαβ\eta_{\alpha\beta}ηαβ​ — conformal gauge.
    • The constraints stop being an axiom. In light-cone gauge we imposed (X˙±X′)2=0(\dot X\pm X')^2=0(X˙±X′)2=0 by hand. Here they are simply the equation of motion of the ruler: vary hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ and you get Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0, the worldsheet stress tensor set to zero. The constraints fall out; we never assumed them.
    • What survives is a CFT. The flattened theory is DDD free scalar fields XμX^\muXμ in two dimensions — the simplest CFT there is. Its stress tensor T(z)T(z)T(z), expanded in modes LnL_nLn​, generates the Virasoro algebra, and the entire spectrum becomes representation theory of that one algebra.

    The worldsheet metric is thus a disposable scaffold. Setting it up costs nothing (it is pure gauge), knocking it down for free gives the constraints, and what remains standing is a conformal field theory.

    Why this rebuild is worth it

    We already quantized the string once, in light-cone gauge: pick a clever frame, solve the constraints by hand, read off the spectrum. That works but hides Lorentz invariance and is welded to one gauge choice. The Polyakov/CFT route buys three things the light-cone route obscures:

    • Manifest covariance. No preferred frame; all DDD coordinates XμX^\muXμ are kept.
    • The constraints for free. (X˙±X′)2=0(\dot X\pm X')^2=0(X˙±X′)2=0 are the metric EOM, not a postulate — see 22.1 Independent worldsheet metric and 22.2 Virasoro constraints.
    • One algebra runs everything. Primaries, descendants, the mass formula — all become Virasoro representation theory. The famous critical dimension D=26D=26D=26 turns into a single clean statement: the total central charge must vanish. The bosons contribute c=Dc=Dc=D; the gauge-fixing ghosts contribute c=−26c=-26c=−26; consistency forces D=26D=26D=26. The old open-string intercept (L0−1L_0-1L0​−1) is the same zero-point/Casimir bookkeeping wearing CFT clothes, not a separate magic number.

    The hinge: the stress tensor

    Two vocabularies are braided through this module, and getting lost means confusing them:

    • Geometry language (continuation of Parts I–II): actions, metrics, gauge symmetries, constraints.
    • CFT language (the new graduate vocabulary): local operators, OPEs, conformal weights, primaries, descendants, Virasoro modes.

    The stress tensor TαβT_{\alpha\beta}Tαβ​ is the single hinge connecting them. Read it three ways and the whole module unfolds:

    1. Vary the Polyakov action by the metric → Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0 (the constraints).
    2. Write its holomorphic part T(z)T(z)T(z) as a Laurent series → the operators LnL_nLn​.
    3. Compute the short-distance product T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w) → the central charge ccc.

    Whenever a CFT statement feels abstract, ask: what did the stress tensor do in the geometry story?

    Exercise

    No-notes self-test for the whole module. Answer all three cold, then check yourself.

    1. Why does an independent worldsheet metric simplify the theory? State the Polyakov action and give the three payoffs of making hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ an independent field.
    2. State the Virasoro algebra and name every piece. Which term is the classical (Witt) algebra, and which is the quantum central extension?
    3. What does the central charge ccc mean? Give at least two independent readings, then explain how ccc fixes D=26D=26D=26.
    Solution

    1 — Independent metric. The action is SP=−14πα′∫d2σ −h hαβ ∂αX⋅∂βX,S_P=-\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma\,\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}\,\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X,SP​=−4πα′1​∫d2σ−h​hαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X, with hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ varied separately from XμX^\muXμ. Three payoffs: (a) No square root — the action is quadratic in ∂X\partial X∂X, so the EOM is a linear wave equation and the XXX path integral is Gaussian. (b) Constraints for free — because hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ appears with no derivatives, its EOM is algebraic: Tαβ=∂αX⋅∂βX−12hαβhγδ∂γX⋅∂δX=0T_{\alpha\beta}=\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X=0Tαβ​=∂α​X⋅∂β​X−21​hαβ​hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X=0. In conformal gauge this is exactly (X˙±X′)2=0(\dot X\pm X')^2=0(X˙±X′)2=0, the Virasoro constraints — now derived, not imposed. (c) Gauge counting — a symmetric 2×22\times22×2 hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ has 3 components; the 3 local symmetries (2 reparametrizations + 1 Weyl) eat all 3. So hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ has no propagating degrees of freedom and can be flattened to ηαβ\eta_{\alpha\beta}ηαβ​ (conformal gauge), leaving a free CFT.

    2 — The algebra. [Lm,Ln]=(m−n) Lm+n+c12 m(m2−1) δm+n,0.[L_m,L_n]=(m-n)\,L_{m+n}+\frac{c}{12}\,m(m^2-1)\,\delta_{m+n,0}.[Lm​,Ln​]=(m−n)Lm+n​+12c​m(m2−1)δm+n,0​.

    • (m−n)Lm+n(m-n)L_{m+n}(m−n)Lm+n​ is the Witt algebra, the classical algebra of 2D conformal transformations (the modes ℓn=−zn+1∂z\ell_n=-z^{n+1}\partial_zℓn​=−zn+1∂z​ obey [ℓm,ℓn]=(m−n)ℓm+n[\ell_m,\ell_n]=(m-n)\ell_{m+n}[ℓm​,ℓn​]=(m−n)ℓm+n​). This term is the entire classical content.
    • c12m(m2−1)δm+n,0\frac{c}{12}m(m^2-1)\delta_{m+n,0}12c​m(m2−1)δm+n,0​ is the central extension: a c-number (proportional to the identity), nonzero only when m+n=0m+n=0m+n=0, arising purely from normal-ordering the operators inside TTT — a quantum effect. Its coefficient ccc is the central charge. Note it vanishes for m∈{−1,0,1}m\in\{-1,0,1\}m∈{−1,0,1}, so {L−1,L0,L1}\{L_{-1},L_0,L_1\}{L−1​,L0​,L1​} (the global SL(2,C)SL(2,\mathbb C)SL(2,C) subalgebra) closes without anomaly.

    3 — Meaning of ccc, and D=26D=26D=26. Any two of: (i) Degree-of-freedom count — one free boson gives c=1c=1c=1, so DDD bosons give c=Dc=Dc=D; ccc measures "how much stuff" the CFT contains (made precise by the c-theorem: ccc decreases along RG flow). (ii) Anomaly in TTT — c/2c/2c/2 is the coefficient of the (z−w)−4(z-w)^{-4}(z−w)−4 pole in the T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w) OPE; equivalently the Schwarzian obstruction to TTT transforming as a tensor. (iii) Physical energy — the Casimir energy on the cylinder is E=−2π(c+cˉ)24E=-\tfrac{2\pi(c+\bar c)}{24}E=−242π(c+cˉ)​, and on a curved worldsheet the trace anomaly is ⟨Tαα⟩=−c12R\langle T^\alpha{}_\alpha\rangle=-\tfrac{c}{12}R⟨Tαα​⟩=−12c​R.

    Weyl invariance of the full gauge-fixed string requires the trace anomaly to cancel, i.e. total c=0c=0c=0. The DDD embedding bosons give c=Dc=Dc=D; the diffeomorphism (bcbcbc) ghosts give c=−26c=-26c=−26. Hence ctotal=D−26=0⇒D=26c_{\text{total}}=D-26=0\Rightarrow D=26ctotal​=D−26=0⇒D=26. (The ghost value c=−26c=-26c=−26 is the one piece not derived in this module — it needs Faddeev–Popov quantization.)
