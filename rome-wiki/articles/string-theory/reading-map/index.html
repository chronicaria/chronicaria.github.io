    Why this exists. The syllabus lists readings by book chapter, but (a) PDF page ≠ printed page, and (b) your is the 1st edition (23 chapters, ends at Ch. 23 "Loop amplitudes", ~571 pp), while the syllabus was written against the 2nd edition (26 chapters). So the syllabus's later citations — "Z Ch. 24", "Z §22.1–22.7", "Z §25.x" — are 2nd-edition chapter numbers that don't exist in your file. This map converts every reading to your 1st-edition pages, matched by topic . When a syllabus chapter number disagrees with the topic, trust the topic/page here.
