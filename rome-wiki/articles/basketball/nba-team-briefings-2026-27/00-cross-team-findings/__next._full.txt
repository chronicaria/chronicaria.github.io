<h2 id="biggest-model-high-challenges">Biggest Model-High Challenges</h2>
<p>GSW (-4.0), TOR (-3.3), CHA (-2.6), HOU (-2.6), BOS (-2.5), SAC (-1.7)</p>
<p>The strongest downward challenges generally came from teams where the public prior assumes a high regular-season floor but the agents found fragile minutes or availability mechanisms: older stars carrying large usage, post-injury workload uncertainty, aggressive role-player valuations, or crowded rotations where the best defensive lineup and best spacing lineup may not be the same.</p>
<h2 id="biggest-model-low-challenges">Biggest Model-Low Challenges</h2>
<p>CHI (+5.0), PHI (+4.1), DET (+3.2), LAL (+3.2), WAS (+3.1), UTA (+3.1), ATL (+2.8), DEN (+2.6), SAS (+2.5), IND (+2.4)</p>
<p>The strongest upward challenges generally came from teams where the public prior is pessimistic relative to added talent, young-player development, or a healthier rotation path. These are not model replacements: the agents framed the deltas as judgmental research adjustments, usually tied to specific players and lineup mechanisms.</p>
<h2 id="teams-where-the-model-looks-most-robust">Teams Where The Model Looks Most Robust</h2>
<p>MIN (+0.5), POR (-0.5), BKN (+0.8), NYK (+1.0), DAL (-1.0), LAC (+1.0), MEM (-1.0), OKC (-2.0)</p>
<p>The about-right teams tended to have offsetting mechanisms: real upside, but already priced playoff odds; or roster weaknesses that were visible in the public prior and not strong enough to justify a large directional move.</p>
<h2 id="common-minutes-assumptions-challenged">Common Minutes Assumptions Challenged</h2>
<ul>
<li>Several agents challenged whether high-minute starter allocations survive training camp once public depth charts and recent transactions are reconciled.</li>
<li>Multiple reports flagged crowded rotations where players are individually useful but hard to combine without sacrificing spacing, rim protection, or ballhandling.</li>
<li>Veteran-heavy teams drew questions about whether regular-season MPG should be managed down even if the player remains highly effective per minute.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="common-rookiecold-start-issues">Common Rookie/Cold-Start Issues</h2>
<ul>
<li>Agents repeatedly treated <code>n_cold_start</code> as a warning label, not a direct downgrade. The key question was whether rookies/cold rows are real rotation pieces or merely deep-bench placeholders.</li>
<li>Teams with several cold-start players often had wider thesis uncertainty because public reporting can confirm draft status and roster placement but not NBA translation.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="common-aginginjury-issues">Common Aging/Injury Issues</h2>
<ul>
<li>Availability was the most common downward mechanism: post-surgery returns, older stars, recurring soft-tissue histories, and teams whose depth collapses if one top player misses time.</li>
<li>Some agents also argued the prior was already conservative where expected games were visibly low, producing smaller or no negative delta.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="teams-dependent-on-one-or-two-uncertain-players">Teams Dependent On One Or Two Uncertain Players</h2>
<p>Several briefings highlighted concentrated dependence on star health or a single high-leverage role: BOS with Tatum/George and the center hierarchy, HOU with Durant/VanVleet, SAS with Wembanyama, GSW with Curry/Butler, and WAS with Davis/Young. These teams should be revisited after training-camp availability and rotation news.</p>
<h2 id="recommended-next-modeldata-improvements">Recommended Next Model/Data Improvements</h2>
<ul>
<li>Keep <code>private-model-workspace/andrewjparkus.github.io/projection_2026_27.json</code> as the explicit source of truth in every boss packet.</li>
<li>Mark stale team-detail artifacts as roster-context-only until regenerated from the tuned public prior.</li>
<li>Export a single reconciled team packet per franchise containing public prior, roster rows, depth chart rows, and source timestamps.</li>
<li>Add machine-readable fields for agent stance, deltas, confidence, and cited sources after each briefing is accepted.</li>
<li>Run a follow-up pass after training-camp depth charts and preseason injury reports are available.</li>
