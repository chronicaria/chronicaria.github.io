    The counting question

    A quantum string is a stack of harmonic oscillators — one for each vibrational overtone, in each transverse direction. So the whole subject reduces to a bookkeeping question you can ask about any pile of oscillators:

    Fix a total energy. In how many distinct ways can the oscillators share it?

    That number, the degeneracy Ω(E)\Omega(E)Ω(E), is everything. Its growth rate controls whether a hot gas of strings behaves like ordinary matter or hits a wall — the Hagedorn temperature — and much later the same count reappears as black-hole entropy. This module builds the counting machine from nothing.

    Two ways to count, and why you need both

    There are two languages for "how many states at energy EEE," and this module is the dictionary between them.

    • Microcanonical — count directly. Fix the energy, list the states, get Ω(E)\Omega(E)Ω(E). Conceptually primary, but the constraint that the occupied modes' energies add up to exactly EEE makes it a hard combinatorics problem.
    • Canonical — count with a generating function. Relax the hard constraint: weight every state by e−βEe^{-\beta E}e−βE, where β=1/(kT)\beta=1/(kT)β=1/(kT) is the inverse temperature, and add them all up, Z(β)=∑αe−βEαZ(\beta)=\sum_\alpha e^{-\beta E_\alpha}Z(β)=∑α​e−βEα​. This is easy, because for independent oscillators the sum factorizes into a product.

    The trick: for a spectrum E=ℏω0NE = \hbar\omega_0 NE=ℏω0​N with integer NNN, set q=e−βℏω0q=e^{-\beta\hbar\omega_0}q=e−βℏω0​ and ZZZ becomes an ordinary power series,

    Z(q)=∑N≥0gN qN.Z(q)=\sum_{N\ge 0}g_N\,q^N.Z(q)=∑N≥0​gN​qN.

    The coefficient of qNq^NqN is exactly the count you wanted. The physical parameter β\betaβ (inverse temperature) and the formal variable qqq (bookkeeping) are the same knob. Compute ZZZ the easy way, then read off the degeneracy gNg_NgN​ as a Taylor coefficient. This is the applied-math heart of the module: a partition function is a generating function that happens to have a thermometer attached.

    Three rungs of the count

    One oscillator is boring. Frequency ω\omegaω, levels E=nℏωE=n\hbar\omegaE=nℏω: exactly one state per energy. Z1=(1−e−βℏω)−1Z_1=(1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega})^{-1}Z1​=(1−e−βℏω)−1, a geometric series.

    A tower is not. Give the string its overtones ω0,2ω0,3ω0,…\omega_0,2\omega_0,3\omega_0,\dotsω0​,2ω0​,3ω0​,…. Independent factors multiply, so

    Z(q)=∏ℓ=1∞11−qℓ=∑N=0∞p(N) qN.Z(q)=\prod_{\ell=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{1-q^{\ell}}=\sum_{N=0}^{\infty}p(N)\,q^{N}.Z(q)=∏ℓ=1∞​1−qℓ1​=∑N=0∞​p(N)qN.

    The coefficient p(N)p(N)p(N) is the number of ways to write NNN as a sum of positive integers — the integer partitions of NNN. Choosing nℓn_\ellnℓ​ quanta in the mode of frequency ℓω0\ell\omega_0ℓω0​ is the same data as putting nℓn_\ellnℓ​ copies of ℓ\ellℓ into a partition of N=∑ℓℓ nℓN=\sum_\ell \ell\,n_\ellN=∑ℓ​ℓnℓ​. Physics counting is number-theory counting.

    And it explodes. A short thermodynamic calculation (do Z→Z\toZ→ energy →\to→ entropy, eliminate temperature) gives

    ln⁡p(N)  ≃  2πN6.\ln p(N)\;\simeq\; 2\pi\sqrt{\tfrac{N}{6}}.lnp(N)≃2π6N​​.

    The π2/6\pi^2/6π2/6 hiding in the prefactor is ζ(2)=∑m≥1m−2\zeta(2)=\sum_{m\ge1}m^{-2}ζ(2)=∑m≥1​m−2, an ordinary convergent sum — no divergent-series regularization anywhere in this module. The point is the shape: ln⁡Ω∼N\ln\Omega\sim\sqrt NlnΩ∼N​, so Ω∼ecN\Omega\sim e^{c\sqrt N}Ω∼ecN​ grows faster than any polynomial. Concretely p(100)≈1.9×108p(100)\approx1.9\times10^{8}p(100)≈1.9×108 and p(104)≈3.6×10106p(10^4)\approx3.6\times10^{106}p(104)≈3.6×10106.

    Why this seeds Hagedorn

    A non-relativistic oscillator tower gives Ω∼ecE\Omega\sim e^{c\sqrt E}Ω∼ecE​ — fast, but tame. A relativistic string does qualitatively more, and one extra fact is responsible: its mass formula ties level to energy quadratically. For an open string at high level (natural units ℏ=c=1\hbar=c=1ℏ=c=1 for the string formulas),

    α′M2=N⊥−1  ≃  N⊥⟹N⊥≃α′E2(E=M at rest).\alpha' M^2 = N^\perp - 1 \;\simeq\; N^\perp \quad\Longrightarrow\quad N^\perp\simeq \alpha' E^2 \quad(E=M\text{ at rest}).α′M2=N⊥−1≃N⊥⟹N⊥≃α′E2(E=M at rest).

    So N⊥≃α′ E\sqrt{N^\perp}\simeq\sqrt{\alpha'}\,EN⊥​≃α′​E. The string vibrates in b=24b=24b=24 transverse directions, each with its own tower; independent towers multiply ZZZ, which pushes N⊥/6N^\perp/6N⊥/6 to b N⊥/6b\,N^\perp/6bN⊥/6 under the square root, so ln⁡Ω≃2π24 N⊥/6=4πN⊥\ln\Omega \simeq 2\pi\sqrt{24\,N^\perp/6}=4\pi\sqrt{N^\perp}lnΩ≃2π24N⊥/6​=4πN⊥​. Substituting the mass formula turns the harmless N⊥\sqrt{N^\perp}N⊥​ into a term linear in EEE:

    S(E)=k 4πα′ E⟹Ω(E)∼eβHE,kTH=1βH=14πα′.S(E)=k\,4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}\,E \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Omega(E)\sim e^{\beta_H E},\qquad kT_H=\frac{1}{\beta_H}=\frac{1}{4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}}.S(E)=k4πα′​E⟹Ω(E)∼eβH​E,kTH​=βH​1​=4πα′​1​.

    A density of states exponential in EEE decides everything: the thermal sum ∑EΩ(E)e−βE\sum_E\Omega(E)e^{-\beta E}∑E​Ω(E)e−βE stops converging once β\betaβ drops to βH\beta_HβH​. There is a maximum temperature. The module's spine is the growth ladder

    Ω=1⏟1 oscillator  →  Ω∼Nr−1⏟finite stack  →  Ω∼ecN⏟infinite tower  →  Ω∼ecE⏟relativistic string  →  TH⏟a wall.\underbrace{\Omega=1}_{\text{1 oscillator}}\;\to\;\underbrace{\Omega\sim N^{r-1}}_{\text{finite stack}}\;\to\;\underbrace{\Omega\sim e^{c\sqrt N}}_{\text{infinite tower}}\;\to\;\underbrace{\Omega\sim e^{cE}}_{\text{relativistic string}}\;\to\;\underbrace{T_H}_{\text{a wall}}.1 oscillatorΩ=1​​→finite stackΩ∼Nr−1​​→infinite towerΩ∼ecN​​​→relativistic stringΩ∼ecE​​→a wallTH​​​.

    The module is complete when every arrow is obvious.

    (Closed strings hit the same wall: their left- and right-moving towers each contribute the full 24-species count, doubling ln⁡Ω\ln\OmegalnΩ, while the closed-string mass formula gives 2N⊥≃α′E2\sqrt{N^\perp}\simeq\sqrt{\alpha'}E2N⊥​≃α′​E — half the N⊥\sqrt{N^\perp}N⊥​ per unit energy. The two factors of 222 cancel, and S=k 4πα′ ES=k\,4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}\,ES=k4πα′​E again.)

    Open questions

    • Dropping the oscillators' zero-point energy is harmless here — it only shifts the zero of energy and never touches S=−∂F/∂TS=-\partial F/\partial TS=−∂F/∂T. But the infinite tower's 12ℏω0∑ℓℓ\tfrac12\hbar\omega_0\sum_\ell \ell21​ℏω0​∑ℓ​ℓ is divergent; the quantization modules must regularize it honestly (a cutoff yielding ζ(−1)=−112\zeta(-1)=-\tfrac1{12}ζ(−1)=−121​, never the naive divergent sum) before it enters the mass spectrum.
    • Canonical/microcanonical equivalence is a large-system, sharp-saddle statement. Strings near THT_HTH​ are exactly the case where it breaks (the canonical ZZZ diverges while Ω(E)\Omega(E)Ω(E) stays finite), so Hagedorn physics is discussed through entropy and density of states, not ZZZ. Treated later.

    Exercise

    This is the no-notes self-test for the whole module. Close everything and reproduce, in order:

    1. One oscillator. From Z1=∑ne−βEnZ_1=\sum_n e^{-\beta E_n}Z1​=∑n​e−βEn​ with En=nℏωE_n=n\hbar\omegaEn​=nℏω, recognize the geometric series and get Z1Z_1Z1​ in closed form. Say what changes if you keep En=ℏω(n+12)E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)En​=ℏω(n+21​).
    2. Many oscillators. For rrr independent oscillators, explain why a sum of Hamiltonians makes ZZZ factor, and write Zr=∏iZ1(ωi)Z_r=\prod_i Z_1(\omega_i)Zr​=∏i​Z1​(ωi​).
    3. Product to counting. For the tower ωℓ=ℓω0\omega_\ell=\ell\omega_0ωℓ​=ℓω0​, show Z(q)=∏ℓ≥1(1−qℓ)−1Z(q)=\prod_{\ell\ge1}(1-q^\ell)^{-1}Z(q)=∏ℓ≥1​(1−qℓ)−1 and explain why the coefficient of qNq^NqN counts partitions of NNN.
    4. Bridge to Hagedorn. Explain in words why ln⁡p(N)∼N\ln p(N)\sim\sqrt Nlnp(N)∼N​ becomes ln⁡Ω(E)∼E\ln\Omega(E)\sim ElnΩ(E)∼E for a relativistic string, using N⊥≃α′E2N^\perp\simeq\alpha' E^2N⊥≃α′E2.
    Solution

    1. One oscillator. With En=nℏωE_n=n\hbar\omegaEn​=nℏω and x≡e−βℏω∈(0,1)x\equiv e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}\in(0,1)x≡e−βℏω∈(0,1), Z1=∑n=0∞xn=11−x=11−e−βℏω.Z_1=\sum_{n=0}^\infty x^n=\frac{1}{1-x}=\frac{1}{1-e^{-\beta\hbar\omega}}.Z1​=∑n=0∞​xn=1−x1​=1−e−βℏω1​. Keeping the zero-point energy, En=ℏω(n+12)E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)En​=ℏω(n+21​), pulls out a constant factor: Z1→e−βℏω/2Z1=[2sinh⁡(βℏω/2)]−1Z_1\to e^{-\beta\hbar\omega/2}Z_1=[2\sinh(\beta\hbar\omega/2)]^{-1}Z1​→e−βℏω/2Z1​=[2sinh(βℏω/2)]−1. This shifts FFF and ⟨E⟩\langle E\rangle⟨E⟩ up by the constant 12ℏω\tfrac12\hbar\omega21​ℏω but leaves S=−∂F/∂TS=-\partial F/\partial TS=−∂F/∂T unchanged — the entropy does not care where you put the zero of energy.

    2. Many oscillators. Independence means H=∑iHiH=\sum_i H_iH=∑i​Hi​ with no coupling, so a microstate is a list (n1,…,nr)(n_1,\dots,n_r)(n1​,…,nr​) with E=∑iniℏωiE=\sum_i n_i\hbar\omega_iE=∑i​ni​ℏωi​. The Boltzmann weight of a sum is a product of weights, e−β∑iniℏωi=∏ie−βniℏωie^{-\beta\sum_i n_i\hbar\omega_i}=\prod_i e^{-\beta n_i\hbar\omega_i}e−β∑i​ni​ℏωi​=∏i​e−βni​ℏωi​, and each factor depends on one index only, so the multiple sum splits: Zr=∑n1,…,nr∏ie−βniℏωi=∏i=1r(∑ni≥0e−βniℏωi)=∏i=1rZ1(ωi).Z_r=\sum_{n_1,\dots,n_r}\prod_i e^{-\beta n_i\hbar\omega_i}=\prod_{i=1}^r\Big(\sum_{n_i\ge0}e^{-\beta n_i\hbar\omega_i}\Big)=\prod_{i=1}^r Z_1(\omega_i).Zr​=∑n1​,…,nr​​∏i​e−βni​ℏωi​=∏i=1r​(∑ni​≥0​e−βni​ℏωi​)=∏i=1r​Z1​(ωi​). Independent subsystems ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ partition functions multiply (ln⁡Z\ln ZlnZ adds). This is the single most-used fact in the module.

    3. Product to counting. Put ωℓ=ℓω0\omega_\ell=\ell\omega_0ωℓ​=ℓω0​ and q=e−βℏω0q=e^{-\beta\hbar\omega_0}q=e−βℏω0​: Z(q)=∏ℓ=1∞11−qℓ=∏ℓ=1∞(1+qℓ+q2ℓ+⋯).Z(q)=\prod_{\ell=1}^\infty\frac{1}{1-q^\ell}=\prod_{\ell=1}^\infty\big(1+q^\ell+q^{2\ell}+\cdots\big).Z(q)=∏ℓ=1∞​1−qℓ1​=∏ℓ=1∞​(1+qℓ+q2ℓ+⋯). Choosing the term qℓnℓq^{\ell n_\ell}qℓnℓ​ from the ℓ\ellℓ-th factor means "put nℓn_\ellnℓ​ quanta in mode ℓ\ellℓ." Multiplying the choices gives q∑ℓℓnℓq^{\sum_\ell \ell n_\ell}q∑ℓ​ℓnℓ​, so the coefficient of qNq^NqN counts occupation lists with ∑ℓℓnℓ=N\sum_\ell \ell n_\ell=N∑ℓ​ℓnℓ​=N — precisely the partitions of NNN, hence p(N)p(N)p(N).

    4. Bridge to Hagedorn. The oscillator count alone gives ln⁡p(N)∼N\ln p(N)\sim\sqrt Nlnp(N)∼N​: the log-count grows like the square root of the level. For a relativistic string the level is not the energy — the mass formula ties them quadratically, N⊥≃α′E2N^\perp\simeq\alpha' E^2N⊥≃α′E2, so N⊥≃α′ E\sqrt{N^\perp}\simeq\sqrt{\alpha'}\,EN⊥​≃α′​E. Substituting turns ln⁡Ω∼N⊥\ln\Omega\sim\sqrt{N^\perp}lnΩ∼N⊥​ into ln⁡Ω∼E\ln\Omega\sim ElnΩ∼E: a density of states exponential in energy. Since Ω(E)∼eβHE\Omega(E)\sim e^{\beta_H E}Ω(E)∼eβH​E, the canonical sum ∑EΩ(E)e−βE\sum_E\Omega(E)e^{-\beta E}∑E​Ω(E)e−βE diverges once β≤βH\beta\le\beta_Hβ≤βH​ — a maximum temperature kTH=1/(4πα′)kT_H=1/(4\pi\sqrt{\alpha'})kTH​=1/(4πα′​).
