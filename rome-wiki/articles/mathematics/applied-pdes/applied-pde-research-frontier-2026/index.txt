<h2 id="current-frontier">Current frontier</h2>
<p>The neural-operator element method combines finite-element structure with reusable learned local operators and publishes code and data. Separate work on free-boundary problems develops neural-operator approximation results and numerical examples for domains whose boundary is part of the solution.</p>
<p>The shared research question is not whether learning “solves PDEs,” but which stability, consistency, approximation, and generalization properties survive after a learned component enters a classical numerical pipeline.</p>
<p><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/neural-operator-topology-priors-for-pde-constrained-inverse-design/" class="wiki-link">Neural-operator topology priors for PDE-constrained inverse design</a> adds a July 8 preprint in which a DeepONet compresses design topology and CMA-ES searches the latent space while a classical PDE solver evaluates candidates. Its reported gains are benchmark evidence, not yet a guarantee that optimal designs survive the compression.</p>
<p>Thirty-two canonical tutorials now supply the spine needed to judge that frontier. The newest layer turns learned-PDE evaluation into controlled experimental design in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/scientific-machine-learning-benchmark-design/" class="wiki-link">Scientific machine learning benchmark design</a>, derives coupled additive order and stability requirements in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/imex-time-integration/" class="wiki-link">IMEX time integration</a>, makes admissibility a fully discrete contract in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/positivity-preserving-discretizations/" class="wiki-link">Positivity-preserving discretizations</a>, and connects moving coordinates to the geometric conservation law in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/arbitrary-lagrangian-eulerian-methods/" class="wiki-link">Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian methods</a>. The preceding layer derives evolving-surface transport and implicit geometry in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/surface-conservation-laws-and-level-set-geometry/" class="wiki-link">Surface conservation laws and level-set geometry</a>, builds the certified parametric workflow of <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/reduced-basis-methods/" class="wiki-link">Reduced basis methods</a>, separates nonlinear sampling cost from projection in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/hyper-reduction-for-nonlinear-pde-models/" class="wiki-link">Hyper-reduction for nonlinear PDE models</a>, and identifies unresolved feedback, memory, and rollout validation in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/closure-error-in-reduced-pde-models/" class="wiki-link">Closure error in reduced PDE models</a>. Earlier layers explain learned closures, operator splitting, goal-oriented estimation, neural operators, reduced models, uncertainty, inverse problems, structure preservation, classification, well-posedness, convergence, conservation, DG, AMR, weak solutions, finite volumes, and adjoints through the linked tutorials below.</p>
