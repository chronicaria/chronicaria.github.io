How to read the ledger

This note separates evidence from modeling rather than hiding both in one sentence.

  • Fact: an official transaction, surgery, dated medical update, games-played record, contract state or reported procedural hold. The evidence/recheck column points to the team desk and, for the highest-leverage items, the primary or stable source.
  • Inference: every projected GP/MPG, rank movement, rest policy, starter, closer, trade likelihood, return workload and branch consequence. Base lines come from 2026-27 fantasy basketball player projections and the 30 team desks; they are not consensus ADP.
  • S sensitivity: can change a first-three-round player, move one player by roughly 50-plus ranks, or rewire three or more standard-league players.
  • A sensitivity: can move a player roughly 25-50 ranks or change a clear draft/waiver decision.
  • B sensitivity: usually 10-25 ranks, streaming value, or one narrow category specialist.
  • C sensitivity: mainly deep redraft, two-way or long-horizon dynasty liquidity.

The labels measure decision leverage, not the probability that the downside occurs. A low-probability transaction can still be S if it would invalidate two entire depth charts.
