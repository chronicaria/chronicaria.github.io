<h2 id="a-useful-reading-order">A useful reading order</h2>
<ol>
<li>Begin with the cut-point method and its assumptions.</li>
<li>Read the level-set geometry note to see how a change in shape enters a conservation law.</li>
<li>Read the shallow-water note for the main dynamical model.</li>
<li>Use the verification ladder before treating any numerical result as scientific evidence.</li>
<li>End with the program-status note, which separates package capability, research implementations, working manuscripts, and open claims.</li>
</ol>
