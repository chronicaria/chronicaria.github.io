Snapshot

  • Role: projected 17-minute power forward — and, with Chris Finch declining to slide McDaniels to the four, the only veteran actually under contract at the position. That makes him a live candidate to open as the starter, not merely the backup.
  • Transaction: agreed to terms on approximately 2026-07-03 and signed July 11. It is a one-year veteran minimum worth close to $3.9M, which is the relevant fact for role security: Minnesota owes him nothing beyond this season and can replace him from either open roster slot without cost.
  • Recent baseline: 12.5 PTS, 4.7 REB, and 1.6 AST in 20.8 MPG across 74 Real Madrid games; 13.3/4.3/1.6 in 20.9 minutes across 36 EuroLeague games, shooting 42% from three.

Fantasy translation

Role, floor and ceiling

Lyles is the only veteran frontcourt bridge between Jaden McDaniels, Rudy Gobert, and the young centers. Floor: 12 minutes if Minnesota signs another big. Ceiling: 24 minutes, 10 points, five rebounds, and two threes when Gobert or McDaniels sits.

Risk monitor

Returning from Europe creates translation uncertainty, and his weak defensive-stat profile limits fantasy value even when he plays. The roster's two open standard slots could bring direct competition before camp.

2025-26 (Real Madrid): No NBA games. Signed a one-year deal with Real Madrid on September 10, 2025 and played the season in Spain. EuroLeague's own player page lists 42 appearances at 13.5 points, 4.5 rebounds and 1.5 assists with a 15.1 PIR, including a 29-point career high at Barcelona on November 7 and 24 points in the EuroLeague Final loss to Olympiacos on May 24. Liga ACB: roughly 28 games, with reported totals of 327 points and 141 rebounds. The all-competition figure quoted in Minnesota's signing release — 12.5 points, 4.7 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 20.8 minutes across 74 games — is consistent with 42 EuroLeague plus 28 ACB plus cup play.

Durability: Nine NBA seasons, seven of them at 63 games or more, and no significant injury history — the outliers are 2020-21 (23 games in San Antonio, a rotation decision as much as anything) and 2023-24 (58). He has never had a season-ending injury. Availability is one of the few things reliably in his favor.

Trajectory: A ten-year stretch four whose production has been remarkably stable and remarkably modest — 7.6 points and 4.3 rebounds in 18 minutes over 650 games, with a career .347 from three that has ranged from .255 to .387 depending on the year. The Real Madrid detour raised his usage and confidence without changing what he is. He returns as Minnesota's only veteran power forward under contract, which is a real path to 17-plus minutes and possibly the opening-night start given Finch's stated reluctance to slide McDaniels to the four. He is a threes-and-rebounds streamer in deep leagues, nothing more: the steal and block rates have been at or below 0.5 for his entire career, which caps him regardless of minutes. He becomes worth rostering if Rudy Gobert or Jaden McDaniels misses extended time and his minutes reach the mid-20s; he becomes irrelevant the moment Minnesota fills either open standard slot with a bigger name.
