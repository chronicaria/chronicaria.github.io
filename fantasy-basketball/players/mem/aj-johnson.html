Durability: No injury flags in two seasons — the low game counts are role-driven (rookie DNPs in Milwaukee, tanking-team auditions in Washington, bench spot in Dallas). Trajectory: The record shows a rare early opportunity already spent: Washington gave him 27 minutes and 11 starts as a 20-year-old post-Kuzma-trade rookie (9.1 PPG, 3.1 APG on .381 FG), then his shooting collapsed to .324 across Washington and Dallas in 2025-26 and the role shrank to spot minutes. Now on his fourth organization in three years, he enters 2026-27 as a leading Memphis cut/trade candidate with a zero-minute base role. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in dynasty, and only if a patient team retains him and league-average finishing or three-point efficiency finally appears alongside 16+ minutes.
