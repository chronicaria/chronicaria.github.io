Fantasy thesis

George's leap was supported by better shooting and enormous free-throw volume, not merely empty late-season minutes. The risk is usage distribution: Peterson needs lead-guard repetitions, Isaiah Collier averaged 7.2 assists, and Markkanen/Jackson return. ESPN ranked George 42nd in April points; Dynatyze ranks him 52nd; Hashtag ranks him 44th in analyst dynasty and 49th in its crowd market. ESPN, 2026-04-14 · Dynatyze, updated 2026-07-03 · Hashtag dynasty, accessed 2026-07-18

CBS describes Peterson as another high-level creator who may improve George's shot quality while making his fantasy value more efficiency-dependent. CBS player news, accessed 2026-07-18

Ceiling: repeat efficiency plus seven assists produces top-30 to top-40 value. Floor: usage and assists fall while turnovers stay near three, pushing him outside the top 80.

Health checkpoint

George sustained a Grade 2 right-hamstring strain March 12 and missed Utah's final 16 games. The offseason provides substantial recovery time, but no explicit full-clearance bulletin was located by the cutoff. The 68-game median assumes an unrestricted camp; a setback moves creation toward Darryn Peterson and Isaiah Collier. KSL Sports, 2026-03-12 · official Jazz game notes

Career record

Pre-NBA: Baylor, one season (2022-23). 33 games, 28.6 MPG: 15.3 PTS, 4.2 REB, 2.8 AST, 1.1 STL, 0.2 BLK, 2.3 3PM on .376 FG / .338 3PT / .793 FT. Big 12 Freshman of the Year. The efficiency was poor even by one-and-done standards — sub-38 percent from the field on high volume — and that inefficiency followed him into the NBA for two full seasons before breaking. Draft: 2023, first round, 16th overall, Utah Jazz.

Fantasy history: No season-end nine-category finish is verifiable from available sources for any of his three seasons, so none is asserted here. What the raw lines imply is unambiguous: 2023-24 and 2024-25 were negative-value seasons in category leagues despite the assists and threes, because .391 shooting on 13-14 field goal attempts plus 2.5-2.7 turnovers is a two-category anchor that scoring alone cannot offset. The 2025-26 profile inverts that — .456 FG and .892 FT with 1.1 steals is a positive contributor in every category except turnovers. His points-league value has consistently outrun his category value, and the dossier's cited 2026-27 rankings (ESPN 42, Hashtag 44, Dynatyze 52) reflect that gap rather than resolve it.

Durability: 75 / 67 / 54 games played, 2023-24 through 2025-26 — a straight decline, and the trend is the concern. Absences by season, per the Fox Sports injury log: 2023-24, foot issues November 7 and December 13 plus an illness March 5; 2024-25, foot November 8, ankle December 20, heel January 5, illness March 5, foot March 10; 2025-26, illnesses November 30, December 29 and December 31, a forearm injury January 21 after a career-high 43 points against Minnesota, ankle sprains January 31 and February 7 that cost him most of a month, injury management March 5, illness March 10, and then the Grade 2 right-hamstring strain suffered against New York on March 12, 2026 that ended his season. Sources differ on whether the hamstring cost him the final 15 or 16 games. Note that these are almost all soft-tissue and lower-extremity events rather than one structural problem, and that 2025-26's 28 missed games came from roughly five separate causes, not one.

Trajectory: The 2025-26 leap is the largest in his cohort and it was efficiency-driven, not minutes-driven — MPG rose only 1.6 while scoring jumped 6.8 points and FG% climbed 6.5 points. That is the specific shape of a real improvement rather than a volume artifact, and the free-throw profile corroborates it: .892 on genuine volume is elite touch and is the least fluky skill in his line. The counterargument is sample. Fifty-four games is thin, his two prior seasons were both .391 from the field, and a shooter who moves from 39 to 46 percent in one year usually gives some of it back. He also turns 23 in November 2026, meaning the leap arrived early enough on the aging curve that partial retention is the reasonable base case, not full regression. For 2026-27 the binding constraint is not skill but touches: Darryn Peterson arrives needing lead-guard reps, Isaiah Collier is still on the roster, and George's usage has only ever gone up on a bad team. A healthy Jazz with more mouths to feed is the environment in which his assists and volume compress even if the shooting holds.
