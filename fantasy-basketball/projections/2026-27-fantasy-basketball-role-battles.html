Reading the board

  • F means the underlying roster, contract, transaction or medical state is sourced as a fact through the cutoff.
  • I means the ordering, lineup, minute split or fantasy consequence is this desk's inference.
  • F→I means a sourced fact creates an inferred role consequence. Every minute split below is a projection, even when the trigger is factual.
  • Baseline split is the healthy-night allocation in the team note unless explicitly labeled season-weighted, conditional or trade branch. A 0 means outside the base rotation, not zero expected appearances.
  • Confidence grades the current ordering, not the player's talent: High is robust without a new event, Medium has a real challenger, and Low is a genuine camp, health or transaction fork.
  • Beneficiary names the player whose fantasy draft price moves most if the catalyst hits; it is not necessarily the projected winner today.

Monitoring protocol

  1. Transactions first: re-run the affected team's exact 240-minute table after every signing, match, trade, waiver or conversion; do not merely add the newcomer on top.
  2. Medical ladder: upgrade only on concrete progression—running, non-contact, contact, five-on-five, preseason appearance, unrestricted games—rather than optimistic target dates.
  3. Camp evidence: log starters, first substitutions and repeated closing groups. One lineup is weak evidence; three or more consistent uses are actionable.
  4. Role evidence: distinguish who starts from who closes, and active-night minutes from season-weighted minutes. Fantasy value follows minutes, usage and category rates, not the lineup card alone.
  5. Reconciliation: after a battle resolves, update the linked team note and every affected player dossier before changing consensus ranks.
