Career record

Pre-NBA: Virginia Tech 2011-12: 6.3 points and 7.0 rebounds in 33 games, ACC All-Freshman. Transferred to Florida, sat 2012-13, then played three seasons in Gainesville — SEC Sixth Man of the Year in 2013-14 and second-team All-SEC in 2015 and 2016. He was the first Florida player to reach 1,000 career points after transferring in mid-tenure. Draft: undrafted (2016); signed with Dallas on July 8, 2016.

Durability: Broadly available through his Dallas prime — 81, 81, 71, 60 and 80 games in five of his first six seasons. The exceptions bracket the career. In 2017-18 left knee quadriceps tendinitis cost him 51 straight games; he returned on March 10, 2018 having last played November 12, 2017. Ankle surgery in June 2025, addressing an injury carried through 2024-25, pushed back his Houston debut and held him to 37 games in 2025-26.

Trajectory: For six seasons this was one of the league's more reliable three-and-D wings — a 39-40% shooter from deep across 2019-22 while defending across three or four positions. The 2025-26 collapse was total rather than gradual: minutes halved to 16.8, field-goal percentage fell to .333 and the three-point rate to .270, both career lows by a wide margin, in his age-32 season and first year off ankle surgery. Charlotte took him purely as the price of collecting three second-round picks, and local reporting says he is unlikely to play a game for them. He matters in fantasy only if he is bought out, lands somewhere needing wing defense, and the shot returns toward even his 2023-24 level — three conditions, none of them yet met.
