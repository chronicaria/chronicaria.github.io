18:T3593d,<p>Ordinary spacetime axes measure "how much time" and "how much space" separately. Light-cone axes instead measure position <em>along the two light rays</em> — the directions in which time and space cancel to zero interval. Aligning the coordinates with the rays trades the mixed signs of the interval for a single cross-term, and everything downstream — a square-root-free energy, the tractable quantization of the string — is the payoff of this one change of basis.</p>
