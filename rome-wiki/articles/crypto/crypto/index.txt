<h2 id="market-mechanics-and-risk">Market mechanics and risk</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/from-protocol-fees-to-tokenholder-value/" class="wiki-link">From protocol fees to tokenholder value</a> — trace user payments through suppliers, treasuries, governance, and the exact holder instrument.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/layer-2-tokens-and-the-economics-of-sequencers/" class="wiki-link">Layer-2 tokens and the economics of sequencers</a> — separate rollup activity and operator surplus from enforceable tokenholder capture.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/token-emissions-and-real-yield/" class="wiki-link">Token emissions and real yield</a> — separate external income from dilution, circular incentives, and risk compensation.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-float-market-cap-and-fully-diluted-value/" class="wiki-link">Crypto float market cap and fully diluted value</a> — distinguish circulating value, tradable float, future dilution, and thin-price extrapolation.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-reserve-and-redemption-risk/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin reserve and redemption risk</a> — analyze assets, liabilities, legal eligibility, settlement, and run dynamics together.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-yield-is-a-balance-sheet-claim/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin yield is a balance-sheet claim</a> — identify the payer, legal claim, redemption path, and risks behind a quoted stablecoin yield.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/liquid-staking-tokens-are-claims-on-validators/" class="wiki-link">Liquid staking tokens are claims on validators</a> — trace staking rewards through validator, withdrawal, slashing, custody, and secondary-liquidity layers.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/wrapped-assets-and-duplicate-crypto-exposure/" class="wiki-link">Wrapped assets and duplicate crypto exposure</a> — preserve each representation while consolidating shared underlying exposure.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/tvl-without-double-counting/" class="wiki-link">TVL without double counting</a> — separate local gross inventory from recursive claims, price effects, liabilities, and unique backing.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/perpetual-funding-and-crowded-leverage/" class="wiki-link">Perpetual funding and crowded leverage</a> — read venue funding rules as one positioning signal rather than a directional oracle.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-liquidation-cascades/" class="wiki-link">Crypto liquidation cascades</a> — trace maintenance-margin breaches through forced flow, disappearing depth, collateral reflexivity, and cross-venue propagation.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/spot-futures-basis-in-crypto/" class="wiki-link">Spot futures basis in crypto</a> — measure maturity-matched carry as a balance-sheet price, with explicit benchmarks, annualization, execution costs, and failure tests.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/cross-venue-fragmentation-in-crypto/" class="wiki-link">Cross-venue fragmentation in crypto</a> — treat liquidity as a network of local balance sheets connected by costly, failure-prone settlement and arbitrage links.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-etf-flows-and-market-impact/" class="wiki-link">Crypto ETF flows and market impact</a> — separate secondary turnover, primary creations, holdings, and AUM before making any price-impact claim.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/governance-capture-and-admin-key-risk/" class="wiki-link">Governance capture and admin-key risk</a> — map the shortest authority path through votes, keys, timelocks, guardians, multisigs, and legal control.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/tokenized-treasury-products-in-crypto-rankings/" class="wiki-link">Tokenized fixed-income products in crypto rankings</a> — classify YLDS debt and JAAA fund shares by their legal claims instead of treating provider capitalization as protocol value.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/unlock-overhang-and-circulating-supply/" class="wiki-link">Unlock overhang and circulating supply</a> — trace scheduled vesting through availability, holder incentives, venue access, market depth, and realized dilution without turning calendars into forecasts.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/oracle-bridge-and-sequencer-risk/" class="wiki-link">Oracle bridge and sequencer risk</a> — model how data, cross-chain messaging, and ordering/liveness dependencies compose into protocol loss and recovery paths.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-liquidity-regime/" class="wiki-link">Crypto liquidity regime</a> — distinguish marked asset stocks, gross flows, executable depth, funding capacity, leverage, and contingent forced flow.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-correlations-with-us-financial-conditions/" class="wiki-link">Crypto correlations with US financial conditions</a> — measure changing co-movement with equities, rates, the dollar, and volatility using aligned clocks and regime-aware tests.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-supply-as-a-market-signal/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin supply as a market signal</a> — reconcile issuer liabilities, treasury inventory, chain migration, wrappers, ownership, and use before interpreting supply as liquidity.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/reading-an-onchain-incident-postmortem/" class="wiki-link">Reading an onchain incident postmortem</a> — reconstruct invariants, deployed state, timelines, losses, response, and remediation without accepting a project narrative as the complete record.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/mev-and-order-flow-auctions/" class="wiki-link">MEV and order-flow auctions</a> — trace ordering surplus through builders, proposers, solvers, users, and token-value gates rather than treating gross MEV as protocol revenue.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/custody-rehypothecation-and-counterparty-risk/" class="wiki-link">Custody rehypothecation and counterparty risk</a> — map technical control, legal title, records, reuse, and insolvency claims across intermediary chains.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/automated-market-maker-loss-versus-rebalancing/" class="wiki-link">Automated market maker loss versus rebalancing</a> — isolate stale-quote adverse selection from impermanent loss, fees, inventory risk, and benchmark choice.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/bridge-finality-and-settlement-assumptions/" class="wiki-link">Bridge finality and settlement assumptions</a> — trace source finality, verification, relaying, destination execution, and economic redemption as separate layers.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-depeg-transmission/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin depeg transmission</a> — map how redemption uncertainty moves through pools, oracles, collateral links, and forced deleveraging.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/token-governance-delegation-markets/" class="wiki-link">Token governance delegation markets</a> — treat delegated voting power as an agency market with expertise, concentration, compensation, and capture tradeoffs.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/token-buybacks-and-holder-value/" class="wiki-link">Token buybacks and holder value</a> — audit funding, authorization, execution, disposition, net supply, governance, and enforceability before treating repurchases as holder return.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/validator-economics-and-staking-yield/" class="wiki-link">Validator economics and staking yield</a> — decompose issuance, fees, MEV, costs, penalties, dilution, and concentration behind a quoted staking rate.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/restaking-risk-is-correlated-leverage/" class="wiki-link">Restaking risk is correlated leverage</a> — consolidate shared collateral, operator, contract, liquidity, and slashing dependencies across services.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/decentralized-exchange-volume-quality/" class="wiki-link">Decentralized exchange volume quality</a> — reconstruct pool-leg and user-intent volume while testing routing duplication, incentives, self-trading, arbitrage, and price conversion.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/onchain-lending-liquidation-mechanics/" class="wiki-link">Onchain lending liquidation mechanics</a> — trace an unsafe loan from oracle eligibility through execution, collateral conversion, and the final bad-debt waterfall.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/protocol-treasury-management-and-runway/" class="wiki-link">Protocol treasury management and runway</a> — separate unrestricted liquid resources, commitments, native-token marks, revenue, and governance authority before estimating survival time.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/cross-chain-token-supply-accounting/" class="wiki-link">Cross-chain token supply accounting</a> — consolidate burn-and-mint, lock-and-mint, native issuance, inventory transfers, wrappers, and in-flight claims without double counting.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-insurance-and-risk-transfer/" class="wiki-link">Crypto insurance and risk transfer</a> — test cover wording, trigger evidence, claims governance, correlated capital, settlement liquidity, and the identity of the ultimate loss bearer.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/token-holder-rights-are-not-equity-rights/" class="wiki-link">Token holder rights are not equity rights</a> — inventory possession, use, governance, cash-flow, remedy, and insolvency powers instead of importing a stock analogy.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/protocol-fee-switches-and-tokenholder-claims/" class="wiki-link">Protocol fee switches and tokenholder claims</a> — separate the ability to vote on fee routing from an activated, eligible, durable, and enforceable holder benefit.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/crypto-bankruptcy-claims-and-recovery-waterfalls/" class="wiki-link">Crypto bankruptcy claims and recovery waterfalls</a> — distinguish property return from creditor recovery, then trace entity, priority, setoff, avoidance, and distribution rules.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/blockchain-fee-revenue-and-economic-security-budgets/" class="wiki-link">Blockchain fee revenue and economic security budgets</a> — reconcile fees, issuance, burn, MEV, provider costs, and attack economics without calling every flow revenue.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-bankruptcy-remoteness/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin bankruptcy remoteness</a> — test segregation, estate exclusion, priority, redemption mechanics, and intermediary exposure instead of treating reserve backing as automatic legal protection.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/protocol-bad-debt-and-insolvency/" class="wiki-link">Protocol bad debt and insolvency</a> — distinguish unhealthy accounts, economic shortfalls, recognized deficits, and the contractual waterfall that allocates loss.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/application-revenue-and-blockchain-settlement-costs/" class="wiki-link">Application revenue and blockchain settlement costs</a> — separate payment volume, pass-through fees, retained revenue, settlement expense, contribution profit, and tokenholder capture.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/stablecoin-liquidity-fragmentation/" class="wiki-link">Stablecoin liquidity fragmentation</a> — measure dollar-token liquidity by exact issuer, contract, chain, venue, route capacity, latency, and redemption access rather than aggregate market cap.</li>
</ul>
