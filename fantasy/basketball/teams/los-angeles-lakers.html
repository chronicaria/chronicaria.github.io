Team Desk · Pacific Division

Los Angeles Lakers

Los Angeles begins the post-LeBron era with a concentrated fantasy core: Luka Dončić supplies first-round creation, Walker Kessler supplies scarce blocks and FG%, and nearly every other role depends on how JJ Redick balances shooting against perimeter defense.

The projected starters—Dončić, Reaves, Grimes, Mamukelashvili, and Kessler—are an inference, and the beat is genuinely split on the fifth spot. Grimes is the cleanest point-of-attack defender among the high-minute guards, while Mamukelashvili's shooting gives Kessler room to screen and roll; that is the Lakers Daily projection. Yahoo's post-Ziaire depth chart instead lists LaRavia first among power forwards and expects him to fill the starting forward role with Ziaire as the backup 3-and-D wing. A defense-first version starts Vanderbilt or Ziaire there instead. The desk treats LaRavia and Mamukelashvili as close to co-favourites and prices neither as a secure starter. Transaction capacity. All 15 standard spots are filled, so any addition requires a trade or a two-way conversion, not a signing above the minimum. The sign-and-trade for Kessler hard-capped Los Angeles at the first apron: it sat roughly $13M below that line before the Ziaire Williams deal and roughly $10M below it after, meaning any in-season addition needs matched salary going out. The tradeable first-round inventory is also depleted — unprotected 2031 and 2033 firsts and 2028/2030 swaps were conveyed to Utah. Reporting on the Kuminga pursuit states plainly that these constraints have prevented a competitive offer; a sign-and-trade built around Vanderbilt plus a swap is the structure discussed, which makes Vanderbilt, LaRavia, and Knecht the minutes most at risk if it ever happens. Every consolidation row on this page should be read at a materially lower probability than the rest of the table. Silver Screen and Roll cap sheet · Lakers Daily, Kuminga and the hard cap

Correction 2026-07-18: the desk previously invoked a consolidation trade three times without pricing it. With the roster full at 15, a first-apron hard cap roughly $10M away, and the 2031/2033 firsts and 2028/2030 swaps already conveyed for Kessler, those rows are downgraded from live scenarios to tail cases.

Kessler underwent November 2025 surgery for a left shoulder labral tear after five games. He said in July that he was 100% cleared; the base projection accepts clearance but not a 75-game certainty. Dončić had two left-hamstring absences before a Grade 2 strain ended his regular season, while Reaves missed 19 games with a calf strain and then suffered a Grade 2 oblique strain.
