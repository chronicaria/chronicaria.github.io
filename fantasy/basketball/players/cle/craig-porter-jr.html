Fantasy profile

  • Role: third guard behind James Harden, Donovan Mitchell, and Dennis Schröder, holding 5 healthy-night minutes in the base 240 as the off-guard alongside Schröder in the second unit. The 11 MPG projection is per appearance and assumes the rest comes from rest nights and absences.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: regular DNPs and G League-level fantasy irrelevance.
  • Base: spot duty at 10 minutes per appearance; monitor rather than draft.
  • Ceiling: top 110-140 over a multiweek 24-minute injury role because of well-rounded rebounds/assists/stocks.

Health and role uncertainty

Porter had hamstring and groin absences in 2025-26. No current limitation was publicly reported by the cutoff. His path improves dramatically if Harden does not sign or Schröder is traded, and disappears if Cleveland adds another veteran organizer.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Vincennes JUCO 2018-20 (62-7 combined record, 2019 NJCAA national champion), then Wichita State 2020-23; senior year (2022-23): 13.5 PTS, 6.2 REB, 4.9 AST per game, third-team All-AAC. Draft: undrafted (2023); signed a two-way with Cleveland on 2023-07-07, converted to a standard deal in February 2024.

Durability: 51, 51, and 64 games across three seasons — the gaps are role-driven (two-way year, G League assignments to the Cleveland Charge, DNPs) more than medical, though 2025-26 included hamstring and groin absences. No reported structural injuries.

Trajectory: Three seasons of the same shape: efficient inside the arc, well-rounded rebounds/assists/stocks for a guard, weak FT% and minimal three-point volume. The 2025-26 bump to 17.9 MPG showed the per-minute defensive stats (0.9 STL, 0.6 BLK) are real. In 2026-27 he competes for the third-guard role behind Donovan Mitchell and Dennis Schröder; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if two of Cleveland's ball handlers are out simultaneously and he holds a 20-plus-minute organizer role for multiple weeks.
