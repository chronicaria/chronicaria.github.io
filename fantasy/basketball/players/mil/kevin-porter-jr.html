Fantasy case

Before right-knee arthroscopic surgery ended his 2025-26 season, Porter averaged 17.4 points, 5.2 rebounds, 7.4 assists, and 2.2 steals in 33.2 minutes across 38 games. Those rates are elite fantasy signals. They are also attached to a player returning from surgery on a team that now has Ryan Rollins, Tyler Herro, and Kasparas Jakučionis.

Low case: delayed return, 8-10 MPG, or roster exit. Base: 12 managed minutes with bench creation. High: full health plus a trade clearing the guard room produces 25+ minutes, 6+ assists, and top-75 per-game value. Strengths are assists, steals, rebounds, and FT%; liabilities are turnovers, availability, and role volatility.

Learning notes

Porter is not an ordinary rank-band player; he is a health-and-minutes option. Put him on a watch list, then act when clearance and a stable 20-minute role arrive together.

Durability: Never played 62 games in a season. The 2023-24 gap is not injury — a September 2023 arrest led to Houston sidelining him, an October trade to OKC, and an immediate waiver; he finished that year with PAOK in Greece before the Clippers signed him July 2024. His 2025-26 ended at 38 games with April right-knee arthroscopy. Earlier seasons mixed minor injuries with off-court disruptions (mid-2020-21 exit from Cleveland). Trajectory: The record shows a genuine per-minute fantasy producer — three separate seasons of 6-plus assists with strong steals, and a 2025-26 line (17.4/5.2/7.4/2.2 steals) that is top-50 material when active — undermined every year by availability, turnovers, or circumstance. In 2026-27 he competes with Rollins, Herro, and Jakučionis for Milwaukee's guard minutes while returning from knee surgery. He becomes fantasy-relevant the moment full clearance and a stable 20-plus-minute role coincide; his history says treat that as an in-season event to react to, not a draft-day bet.
