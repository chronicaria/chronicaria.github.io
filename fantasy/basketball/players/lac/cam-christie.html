Cam Christie

Cam Christie is a still-young shooting prospect who needs a major role jump before his NBA statistics become fantasy-relevant.

Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: rank 240+; not draftable outside very deep formats.
  • Points: G League/deep-bench prospect until a 20-minute NBA path appears.
  • Dynasty: end-roster stash only in leagues with 250-plus players; age is the argument, not current production.

Role and scenarios

  • Low: remains behind Wagler, Sanders, Jordan Miller and Dunn and spends extended time in San Diego.
  • Base: spot minutes and 48 appearances, mostly through rest/injury paths.
  • High: a wing-clearing trade produces 18 minutes and a threes-and-steals watchlist role.

Career record

Pre-NBA: One season at Minnesota (2023-24), born July 24, 2005 and a full year young for the class. Started 26 of 33 games and averaged 11.3 points, 3.6 rebounds and 2.2 assists in 30.1 minutes on .403/.391/.791 — a high-volume freshman shooter on a bad Big Ten team, with the three-point rate the entire draft case. Younger brother of Max Christie. Draft: 2024, second round, 46th overall, LA Clippers.

Durability: No injury story. The 13 games as a rookie and 55 in year two reflect assignment and rotation decisions — he was sent to the San Diego Clippers repeatedly in both seasons — rather than missed time. Availability is not the constraint on him.

Trajectory: Two seasons, 68 games, 7.9 minutes a night, and the one skill he was drafted for has not appeared: .391 from three on real college volume has become .226 on 68 NBA games, with 0.2 makes a game. The counting stats are too small to evaluate anything else, and the field-goal jump from .292 to .381 is the only positive signal in the record. He turns 21 in July 2026 and age remains the whole dynasty argument. Fantasy relevance requires two things in sequence — a wing-clearing trade or injury that opens 18-plus minutes, and a three-point rate that recovers toward league average. Neither has happened, and until the second does, extra minutes are unlikely to be offered.
