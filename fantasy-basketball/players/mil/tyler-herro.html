Fantasy case

The Giannis trade makes Herro the best half-court scorer on the roster. The NBA's July 15 transition analysis gives him the “green light” framing, while Ryan Rollins, Jaime Jaquez Jr, and Kevin Porter Jr can absorb enough initiation to keep him working off the ball. His strongest categories are points, threes, and free-throw percentage; rebounds and assists are helpful for a scoring guard. Low steals and blocks limit his nine-category ceiling.

Herro played only 33 games in 2025-26 but still averaged 20.5 points, 4.8 rebounds, 4.1 assists, 2.5 threes, and shot .480/.917 in 31.3 minutes. The projection deliberately cuts minutes and games to reflect toe/foot and rib concerns, not lost offensive primacy.

Learning notes

Herro is a safer bet for usage than for games played. Drafting him is a wager that elite shooting and a first-option workload outweigh the low defensive-stat profile and Milwaukee's incentive to manage or trade veterans.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Kentucky, one season (2018-19). He started all 37 games and averaged 14.0 points, 4.5 rebounds and 2.5 assists in 32.6 minutes on .462 FG, .355 from three and .935 from the line — the free-throw stroke that has defined his NBA profile was already the best thing about him as a freshman. SEC All-Freshman team. Draft: 2019, first round, 13th overall, Miami — no draft-night trade. He spent his first seven NBA seasons with the Heat and was traded to Milwaukee on July 6, 2026, alongside Jaime Jaquez Jr, Kasparas Jakučionis, Kel'el Ware, the rights to Nate Ament and four future picks, for Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bobby Portis.

Fantasy history: The shape has been stable for five years: a points-and-threes guard carried in categories by an elite FT% on real volume and dragged down by 0.7 steals, 0.2 blocks and 2.2 turnovers. 2021-22 and 2022-23 were roughly mid-round category seasons; 2023-24 was a per-game top-70-ish line wrecked by 42 games; 2024-25 was his best, an All-Star year that graded near the top 40 in 9-cat and materially higher in points leagues; 2025-26 was a strong per-game line across a 33-game sample with no season-long value. Published season-end Basketball Monster or Hashtag ranks were not verifiable for any of these years, so read those as band estimates rather than finishes. The points-league shape is consistently 15 to 25 spots better than the category shape, because scoring volume and three-point makes are his entire case and standard points formats do not punish the missing defensive stats.

Durability: 55, 54, 66, 67, 42, 77, 33 games. Two seasons define the risk. In 2023-24 he lost 40 games to a right ankle sprain suffered against Memphis that cost 18 straight, then a foot problem that emerged around the All-Star break, required PRP treatment and cost another 17, plus a hyperextended left knee. In 2025-26 he had surgery on September 19, 2025 for posterior impingement syndrome in the left ankle, missed the first 17 games, returned November 24, then sustained a right costochondral rib injury around January 17, 2026 that cost over a month; he returned in late February and dealt with left quad soreness in March. The one counterexample is 2024-25, when he played 77 games. The pattern is lower-body — ankle and foot on the same side — recurring across three separate seasons.

Trajectory: The scoring leap came in 2021-22 when Miami handed him the sixth-man volume role, and the second one came in 2024-25 when Jimmy Butler's departure made him the outright first option: usage, minutes and assists all rose together and he posted career highs in points, assists and FG%. What matters for 2026-27 is that the efficiency has kept climbing while the volume fell — .439 in 2022-23 to .472 to .480, and .917 from the line last season — which is real shot-quality improvement rather than a minutes artifact. He is 26 entering the season, which is the flat top of a scoring guard's curve, so the production is not the question. The question is games. He has cleared 70 once in seven years, the ankle and foot history is chronic rather than incidental, and he now lands on a rebuilding Milwaukee team with both the incentive to manage his workload and a live reason to trade him before February. Price the rate line as real and the availability as the whole risk.
