Which table to use

This note now carries two ranked boards. They are not interchangeable, and picking the wrong one is a real draft error, not a rounding difference.

The two boards disagree meaningfully: 126 of the 253 ranked players move five or more slots between them, and 12 move fifteen or more. ESPN's −1 per field-goal attempt is the driver, and it is a genuine change in player value rather than a display artifact. If the platform is unknown, resolve it before drafting rather than averaging the boards.

Model, not consensus

The statistical inputs are the 30 independent team desks' median 2026-27 player cards, normalized in 2026-27 fantasy basketball player projections and Data/player-projections-2026-07-18.csv. The eligible comparison pool contains 261 players projected for at least 20 games and 15 minutes per appearance with complete counting-stat inputs. This note applies one formula mechanically and displays the top 200.

No market smoothing was added. A surprising rank therefore means “the projection plus this formula produced it,” not “the public agrees.” The current public-source audit found no trustworthy complete post-draft 2026-27 consensus, ADP sample, and projection set to substitute for a model. See Major fantasy media, Fantrax, and Hashtag Basketball.

Neutral illustrative scoring

\[ \mathrm{FP/G}=\mathrm{PTS}+1.2(\mathrm{REB})+1.5(\mathrm{AST})+3(\mathrm{STL})+3(\mathrm{BLK})-\mathrm{TO} \]

This is neutral only in the practical sense that it is simple, transparent, percentage-free, and broadly familiar. It exactly matches Yahoo's public default points weights at the cutoff and also matches the disclosed formula used by FantraxHQ's prior-season points board; it is not a universal fantasy standard. Yahoo default settings, accessed 2026-07-18 · FantraxHQ's disclosed 2025 points formula

  • Season FP is projected FP/G × GP.
  • Draft index, which determines the displayed order, is FP/G × sqrt(GP/82). It is the geometric mean of per-game value and 82-game-normalized season value, so availability matters without turning the list into a pure totals board.
  • Exact GP remains visible. A medical flag does not create a second penalty beyond the GP forecast.
  • Ties and tiny gaps are false precision; tier and settings fit matter more than one slot.

Bands and flags

The PG band and Total band columns translate rank within the 261-player comparison pool into 12-team draft shorthand: R1 = 1–12, R2 = 13–24, R3–4 = 25–48, R5–7 = 49–84, R8–10 = 85–120, R11–13 = 121–156, R14–16 = 157–192, and R17+ = 193 or later.

Avail is only the projected GP band: High = 70+, Med = 62–69, Low = 50–61, VLow = below 50. It is not a medical probability.

Flags are compact audit prompts, not automatic fades:

  • M — named medical, rehab, recurrence, or material rest-risk case in 2026-27 fantasy basketball injury and availability ledger.
  • R — role/minute sensitivity; projected at 25 MPG or less.
  • N — 2026 NBA rookie/first-year translation risk.
  • T — unresolved transaction, contract, or team-placement risk in the dossier.
  • No flag — none of these specific flags; ordinary uncertainty still applies.

ESPN default top 100

ESPN's default head-to-head points scoring is not a variant of the Yahoo formula; it is a different question. It scores five shot events the Yahoo default ignores entirely, and the −1 on every field-goal attempt means a missed shot costs a full point. Netted out, a made three is worth 5 (3 points, +1 3PM, +2 FGM, −1 FGA), a made two is worth 3, a made free throw is worth 1, and every miss is −1 — ESPN's own explainer states these three totals. Volume is no longer free, and efficiency is priced directly rather than through a percentage category.

\[ \mathrm{FP/G}=\mathrm{PTS}+\mathrm{3PM}+2(\mathrm{FGM})-\mathrm{FGA}+\mathrm{FTM}-\mathrm{FTA}+\mathrm{REB}+2(\mathrm{AST})+4(\mathrm{STL})+4(\mathrm{BLK})-2(\mathrm{TO}) \]

How the shot volumes were obtained, and how far to trust them. The team-desk dossiers state FGA and FTA for only 54 of the 262 pooled players. For the rest, Data/player-projections-corrected.csv derives them: projected FTA scales each player's 2025-26 free-throw rate by projected minutes, and projected FGA is then solved from the scoring identity PTS = 2(FGM) + 3PM + FTM. The volumes are therefore consistent with the projected points by construction rather than guessed — across the pool the implied points reproduce the projected points to a mean error of 0.07 and a maximum of 1.01. That is an internal-consistency guarantee, not an accuracy guarantee: 194 players use minute-scaled 2025-26 rates and 14 rookies use a pool-median rate, so a player whose efficiency or shot diet genuinely changes will be mispriced. This is why the ESPN board stops at 100 while the Yahoo board runs to 200, and why ESPN ranks deserve less deference than Yahoo ranks at equal distance from the top.

Ordering uses the same draft index as the Yahoo board — ESPN FP/G × sqrt(GP/82) — so the two are directly comparable. How to use the board

  1. Copy the league's exact scoring weights before looking at rank, then pick the matching board — Yahoo-default settings use Top 200, ESPN-default settings use ESPN default top 100. If the weights match neither, re-run the formula rather than reading across.
  2. Start from FP/G; use Season FP, Avail, and the medical flag to decide how much missed-time risk the roster can absorb.
  3. Treat tier crossings as meaningful only when the underlying dossier, role, and settings agree. Do not trade value for a one-slot model edge.
  4. Re-run after any camp clearance, surgery update, starting-lineup change, trade, or projection revision. The player links are the audit trail.
  5. Once a real 2026-27 ADP sample exists, compare price to this model rather than replacing projection value with popularity.
