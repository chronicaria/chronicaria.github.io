    Snapshot

    July 6, 2026 snapshot: about 0.174price,0.174 price, 0.174price,205M market cap, 215MFDV,215M FDV, 215MFDV,18.5M 24h volume, and roughly 1.19B circulating SYRUP.

    Score: 71/100. Upside: 6.5/10. Kill risk: 7/10.

    Evidence

    Maple has visible usage: about 2.36BTVL,2.36B TVL, 2.36BTVL,1.90B active loans, 7.58M30dborrowerfees/interest,about7.58M 30d borrower fees/interest, about 7.58M30dborrowerfees/interest,about914K 30d retained revenue, and about $229K 30d holder-revenue/buyback allocation. Maple's own site cites much larger lifetime borrowing and loan counts.

    Token capture is real but not final. Maple states that 25% of protocol revenue funds SYRUP buybacks, but the H2 2026 continuation needs confirmation. The prior MPL-to-SYRUP migration and supply reporting discrepancies create governance and trust surfaces.

    Credit risk is the central issue. Maple's 2022 Orthogonal default was about $36M and exposed how institutional underwriting can fail when borrower/counterparty losses are hidden.

    Bull case

    Maple becomes the credit and yield layer for stablecoin distribution across fintech, exchanges, and DeFi, driving AUM above 5Bandretainedrevenuehighenoughforbuybackstomatterversusaroughly5B and retained revenue high enough for buybacks to matter versus a roughly 5Bandretainedrevenuehighenoughforbuybackstomatterversusaroughly200M market cap.

    Bear case

    AUM is hot-money yield chasing; crypto collateral and borrower quality remain correlated; a market shock creates impairments or withdrawal queues; and governance reduces or delays buybacks.

    Gates

    Promote if active loans stay above 2.3B,TVLabove2.3B, TVL above 2.3B,TVLabove3.0B, 30d retained revenue above 1.5M,buybacksabove1.5M, buybacks above 1.5M,buybacksabove375K 30d, H2 2026 buyback allocation is formally extended at at least 25%, and supply reporting reconciles within 2%.

    Demote if active loans fall below 1.25Bfor30days,retainedrevenuestaysbelow1.25B for 30 days, retained revenue stays below 1.25Bfor30days,retainedrevenuestaysbelow500K 30d for two months, lender losses exceed 0.25% TVL or 1% active loans, secured-pool collateral ratio falls below 120%, or governance cuts buybacks below 15%.
