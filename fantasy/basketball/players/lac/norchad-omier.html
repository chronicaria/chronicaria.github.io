Norchad Omier

Career record

Pre-NBA: Born August 28, 2001 in Bluefields, Nicaragua; listed 6'7". Five college seasons across three schools — Arkansas State (12.6 points, 12.3 rebounds as a freshman, Sun Belt Freshman of the Year and first-team all-conference; 17.9 and 12.2 as a sophomore), Miami (13.1/10.0, then 17.0/10.0), and Baylor in 2024-25 (15.7 points, 10.8 rebounds on .560 FG / .290 3P / .750 FT). Career college line across 155 games: 15.3 points, 10.9 rebounds, 1.2 assists on .572 FG. Draft: undrafted (2025). He signed an Exhibit 10 with Cleveland, was waived, and joined the Cleveland Charge, where his G League rookie season — 27 games, 19.3 points, 12.3 rebounds, 2.7 assists, 1.3 steals, 0.7 blocks in 31.0 minutes on roughly 59% shooting — earned All-NBA G League Rookie Team honors and a third-place Rookie of the Year finish. The Clippers signed him to a two-way contract during the season.

Six games at four minutes is not a record to draw conclusions from. His March 2, 2026 debut made him the first Nicaraguan-born player to appear in an NBA game.

Durability: No injury history to report. The six-game NBA total reflects two-way assignment rules and a late-season signing, not availability.

Trajectory: The G League production is genuinely elite — a 19-and-12 rookie season with steals and assists attached — and the college record backs it up across three levels of competition. The obstacle is the same one that made him undrafted: a 6'7" center who does not block shots and does not shoot threes, which is a difficult NBA archetype regardless of rebounding. He is unsigned at this cutoff, with the Clippers' two two-way slots held by Sean Pedulla and Nick Martinelli and a third open. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he takes that third slot and injuries hit LA's frontcourt hard enough to force an 18-minute role, in which case he streams as rebounds and field-goal percentage with no blocks.
