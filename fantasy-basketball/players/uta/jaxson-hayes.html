Fantasy thesis

Utah signed Hayes to a two-year standard contract July 9. Axios describes his athleticism as a complementary contrast to Nurkic's craft, but Filipowski, Bamba, and Jackson all compete for the same minutes. NBA movement data, accessed 2026-07-18 · Axios roster overview, 2026-07-13

Ceiling: a Nurkic absence gives Hayes 22 minutes and FG%/blocks streaming value. Floor: loses the backup battle and plays only in selected matchups.

Career record

Pre-NBA: One season at Texas in 2018-19, averaging 10.0 points and 5.0 rebounds on .728 shooting, named Big 12 Freshman of the Year, second-team All-Big 12 and Big 12 All-Defensive. He hurt his left knee in the Big 12 tournament and declared for the draft in April 2019. Draft: 2019, first round, 8th overall by Atlanta; rights traded immediately to New Orleans in the package that sent De'Andre Hunter to the Hawks.

Seven seasons, never more than 20 minutes a game in any of them. The defining number is efficiency: .665 for his career and above .720 in each of the three Lakers seasons, because essentially every shot is a dunk or layup. He abandoned the three-point attempt entirely after 2021-22. Utah signed him on July 10, 2026 to a two-year, $12 million deal with a team option on the second year.

Durability: 64, 60, 70, 47, 70, 56, 66 games. He has never missed a season and has cleared 60 games in six of seven years, but has also never played a full one — the pattern is a reliably available reserve who loses 10 to 20 games a year to assorted minor absences. The one significant dip, 47 games in 2022-23, coincided with his smallest role in New Orleans.

Trajectory: A former top-ten pick who settled, by his mid-twenties, into a stable and narrow identity: a vertical rim-runner who finishes everything, blocks under one shot a game, rebounds adequately, and shoots poorly enough from the line that his FG% is only half a category asset. Nothing in seven seasons suggests untapped upside; 2025-26 was essentially his 2019-20 rookie line at better efficiency. In Utah he is behind Nurkic and contesting backup-five minutes with Filipowski, Bamba and Jackson, which makes his fantasy case entirely conditional — a Nurkic absence lifts him to roughly 22 minutes and turns him into a FG%-and-blocks streamer, and nothing short of that puts him on a standard-league roster.
