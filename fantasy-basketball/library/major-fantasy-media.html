Scope and reading rules

  • Accessed: 2026-07-18, America/New_York.
  • Coverage: Publicly accessible rankings, mocks, rookie boards, draft-market pages, projections, and fantasy news from five major publishers. Paywalled or authenticated-only data is recorded as a gap rather than inferred.
  • Observed means a directly visible page field, table, byline, timestamp, or rank.
  • Opinion means an analyst's projection, value judgment, role expectation, or recommendation; it is attributed rather than converted into fact.
  • Historical baseline means a genuine 2026-27 article that was reasonable when published but predates material draft, trade, injury, or free-agency information.
  • Stale / mislabelled means the underlying season or payload is older even if dynamic team metadata makes the page look current.
  • Evidence grades: specialist editorial rankings and analysis are normally C; platform-hosted tables can be B/C for what the table actually displays, but not for an unstated season or methodology; news summaries remain secondary until checked against an official release or primary report.
  • Rankings are never merged without preserving scoring system, league size, date, and whether the list is a rank, mock selection, ADP, projection, or article order.

Executive verdict

As of July 18, none of these five publishers offered a trustworthy, complete, post-draft public 2026-27 overall board and a current full projection set and real 2026-27 ADP. The usable public record is instead a layered timeline:

  1. April/May way-too-early overall ranks and mocks establish a pre-draft baseline.
  2. June/July draft, trade, and Summer League articles update particular players and situations.
  3. Current news feeds supply leads, not authoritative roster or health facts.
  4. Several evergreen tools still expose 2025-26 ranks, ADP, or projections under current roster labels.

The strongest early cross-format signal is a top tier of Nikola Jokic, Victor Wembanyama, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, and Luka Doncic, with Tyrese Maxey, Cade Cunningham, Jalen Johnson, and Anthony Edwards repeatedly appearing in the early first round. That is an inference from dated lists—not a new consensus ranking.

Draft-room and syndication traps

  • Yahoo draft analysis / ADP route and Yahoo Fantasy Basketball draft route did not expose a responsibly citable 2026-27 dataset on 2026-07-18; the public route returned an unavailable/not-found experience during direct access.
  • Search-engine remnants can surface prior-season Yahoo draft averages. They are not an adequate substitute for a dated, sampled, live 2026-27 table.
  • Many pages on the Yahoo domain are syndicated. For example, “Fantasy Basketball: 2026-27 rookies to target in drafts” identifies Athlon Sports as the original publisher. Domain alone must not be treated as Yahoo analyst consensus.
  • No complete, public, post-draft Yahoo 2026-27 overall or dynasty board was located by July 18. Titus's July 14 article says a full dynasty ranking is still to come, so that future artifact should supersede these partial boards once published.

Safe ingestion rules for the encyclopedia

  1. Store each rank with source, author, published_at, accessed_at, format, league_size, rank_type, and universe_size.
  2. Give an actual mock draft a mock_pick field, never an ADP or expert_rank field.
  3. Treat article order as unordered unless the author explicitly calls it a ranking.
  4. Preserve preseason and prior-season ADP labels exactly. Never relabel a comparison input as 2026-27 market data.
  5. Separate current roster metadata from the timestamp of the rank/projection payload.
  6. For news feeds, follow the attribution chain to the primary reporter or official announcement before updating player/team facts.
  7. Preserve analyst language as attributed scenario statements: “Titus expects…” or “Hanshew identifies…,” not “Player X will start.”
  8. Attach a freshness state to every article: current, aging, historical baseline, or stale/mislabelled.
  9. Re-audit all five publishers after draft rooms open and again after the first clearly labelled 2026-27 projection release.
