Exact switch rules

Puka → Bijan (only-Bijan-clears branch)

Select Bijan Robinson only when, within 48 hours of the decision, both are true and Gibbs has not also cleared his gate:

  1. A Falcons or NFL primary report says he returned to full team practice or has no injury/participation restriction; and
  2. A separate named national or local beat-reporting source independently confirms active first-team/team-period participation with no injury designation.

If a current role report instead shows Brian Robinson repeatedly taking Bijan's first-team goal-line or two-minute work, do not assume Bijan's 280-carry/84-catch forecast still holds. Obtain first preseason first-team usage or two consecutive named-practice reports before treating that role split as real.

Puka → Gibbs (only-Gibbs-clears branch)

Select Jahmyr Gibbs only if Bijan has not cleared and the same two-source test establishes that Gibbs has returned to full team work with no designation and resolves the reported back issue as non-limiting. A generic "day to day" statement, an unexplained missed practice, PUP/NFI/IR, or a hold-in theory is not a clearance.

Stay on Puka / reopen the card

  • Stay on Puka if neither lead back meets its gate. Do not turn unresolved news into a medical conclusion.
  • Reopen rather than auto-substitute if Nacua misses practice or is designated, or if Stafford's status escalates; confirm either event through a club/NFL source and an independent reporting family.
  • Reopen McCaffrey if preseason first-team usage contradicts the announced workload reduction; routine camp participation alone does not restore a 279/82 workload forecast.
  • Reopen Chase when a second current source establishes full team participation without a limitation. His August 1 team-media appearance alone is not that confirmation.

Why the gate outranks a tiny projection edge

Gibbs leads Bijan by 0.5 projected points; Bijan leads Puka by 27.6. Those totals provide a useful prior, but cannot settle a present camp absence. The lead backs have the better raw full-PPR projection case; Puka has the only cleared current availability case. The decision therefore separates projection from status instead of silently discounting a player or pretending a photo, pre-camp preview, or contract rumor is medical clearance.
