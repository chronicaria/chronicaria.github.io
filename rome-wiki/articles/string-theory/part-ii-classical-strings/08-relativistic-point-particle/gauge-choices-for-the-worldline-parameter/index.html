    Gauge choices for the worldline parameter

    Because the action is reparameterization invariant , the label \tau on the worldline is pure redundancy, so we may fix a gauge by choosing a convenient \tau — and the three standard choices (proper length, lab time, light-cone time) each buy a simpler equation at a different price.

    A blank ruler along the worldline

    The worldline is a curve in spacetime — a set of points, a bare wire. To describe it we lay a ruler alongside it and read off xμ(τ)x^\mu(\tau)xμ(τ). But the ruler starts blank: reparameterization invariance says any smooth monotone relabeling τ→f(τ)\tau\to f(\tau)τ→f(τ) gives the same curve, the same action, the same physics. We get to paint the tick marks wherever we like. That freedom is a gauge redundancy, not a symmetry: a symmetry relates different physical situations (a boost relates two observers), a redundancy relates two descriptions of the same situation. Fixing a gauge just picks one representative from the equivalence class of relabelings — it deletes bookkeeping, never physics.

    In applied-math terms: the physical object is the equivalence class of parameterized curves under precomposition with a monotone bijection, and a gauge condition is a rule that selects one representative, at least locally.

    For a point particle this is a luxury — the Free particle equation of motion is already trivial. For the string it is survival: the Nambu-Goto action is intractable until we spend the reparameterization freedom, all the way to the Light-cone gauge on which quantization is built. The point particle is the rehearsal, where each move is visible with one dimension instead of two.

    Legal ruler. A relabeling must stay one-to-one along the curve, so dτ′/dτ≠0d\tau'/d\tau\neq 0dτ′/dτ=0; we take it strictly increasing, dτ′/dτ>0d\tau'/d\tau>0dτ′/dτ>0, so the parameter still runs from the initial endpoint to the final one.

    Below, the same freedom is spent three ways. Read each as: what did we set the ruler to, and what did that buy us?

    Gauge 1 — Proper-length gauge: τ=s\tau = sτ=s

    Set the ruler to the particle's own clock: paint the marks so equal spacing means equal proper time elapsed on a clock the particle carries. In this vault the parameter is the proper-length variable sss, with physical proper time τproper=s/c\tau_{\text{proper}}=s/cτproper​=s/c (see Proper length and proper time); the two differ only by the constant ccc, which is why the literature names the identical choice the proper-time gauge. Two names, one gauge — anywhere else in the vault, "proper-time gauge" means exactly this τ=s\tau=sτ=s.

    • Defining condition. With τ=s\tau=sτ=s the tangent is a unit vector (mostly-plus signature, ccc kept explicit throughout this module), −ημνdxμdsdxνds=1,-\eta_{\mu\nu}\frac{dx^\mu}{ds}\frac{dx^\nu}{ds}=1,−ημν​dsdxμ​dsdxν​=1, which is just ds2=−ημν dxμdxνds^2=-\eta_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nuds2=−ημν​dxμdxν divided by ds2ds^2ds2 — the definition of sss, not dynamics. In time units, Uμ=dxμ/dτproper=c dxμ/dsU^\mu=dx^\mu/d\tau_{\text{proper}}=c\,dx^\mu/dsUμ=dxμ/dτproper​=cdxμ/ds obeys UμUμ=−c2U^\mu U_\mu=-c^2UμUμ​=−c2.
    • Payoff. The equation of motion dpμ/dτ=0dp_\mu/d\tau=0dpμ​/dτ=0 collapses to its cleanest form,   d2xμds2=0.  \boxed{\;\frac{d^2 x^\mu}{ds^2} = 0.\;}ds2d2xμ​=0.​ Because pμ=mc dxμ/dsp^\mu=mc\,dx^\mu/dspμ=mcdxμ/ds is constant and mmm is constant, the tangent is constant: the worldline is literally straight, advancing by equal four-vector steps per unit of proper length — equivalently, per tick of the particle's own clock. This is Newton's first law in covariant dress. (In time units the same statement is d2xμ/dτproper2=0d^2x^\mu/d\tau_{\text{proper}}^2=0d2xμ/dτproper2​=0.)
    • Cost. Only exists for massive particles. A massless particle moves on a null line where ds=0ds=0ds=0: its clock never advances, so there is no ruler to paint. The gauge simply does not exist for it.

    Gauge 2 — Static/time gauge: τ=t\tau = tτ=t

    Set the ruler to the lab clock: paint the marks so τ\tauτ equals coordinate time in one chosen frame, x0(τ)=c τx^0(\tau)=c\,\taux0(τ)=cτ, i.e. τ=t\tau=tτ=t. This is the everyday habit — "position as a function of time" — now recognized as one gauge among many.

    • Defining condition. x0(τ)=c τx^0(\tau)=c\,\taux0(τ)=cτ, equivalently τ=t\tau=tτ=t.
    • Payoff. The action becomes an ordinary time integral, S=-mc^2\int dt\,\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}, \tag{1} from which the familiar Lagrangian L=−mc21−v2/c2L=-mc^2\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}L=−mc21−v2/c2​, momentum p⃗=γmv⃗\vec p=\gamma m\vec vp​=γmv, and energy E=γmc2E=\gamma mc^2E=γmc2 fall out (see Proper-time action). This is the gauge that reconnects to Newtonian intuition.
    • Cost. Lorentz covariance is no longer manifest: we singled out one frame's time. The physics is still invariant — it just is not obvious from the formulas anymore.

    Gauge 3 — Light-cone gauge: τ∝x+\tau \propto x^+τ∝x+

    Set the ruler to a light-front clock: use a null combination of time and one space direction as "time." Define light-cone coordinates x^\pm \equiv \frac{1}{\sqrt2}\big(x^0 \pm x^1\big), \tag{2} and tie the parameter to light-cone time: x+=β τx^+ = \beta\,\taux+=βτ for constant β\betaβ.

    • Payoff. In these coordinates the mass-shell constraint solves linearly for the dependent momentum p−p^-p− (shown below), instead of the usual quadratic E=p⃗2c2+m2c4E=\sqrt{\vec p^2c^2+m^2c^4}E=p​2c2+m2c4​. That one algebraic simplification is what makes string quantization tractable in Light-cone gauge (see also Mass-shell relation).
    • Cost. Covariance is broken hardest of all: a specific spatial axis x1x^1x1 is singled out on top of the time direction. In exchange, every surviving variable is physical — no leftover gauge redundancy to quotient out.

    Point-particle version, worked. The momentum component along x+x^+x+ is p+=mc dx+ds,p^+ = mc\,\frac{dx^+}{ds},p+=mcdsdx+​, constant by the equation of motion dpμ/dτ=0dp_\mu/d\tau=0dpμ​/dτ=0, and automatically positive for a massive, future-directed particle (since ∣p1∣<p0|p^1|<p^0∣p1∣<p0). So x+x^+x+ increases monotonically with sss, and x+x^+x+ is a legal ruler. Now the mass shell: with the convention (2), the inner product in light-cone components is a⋅b=−a+b−−a−b++aIbIa\cdot b=-a^+b^- - a^-b^+ + a^Ib^Ia⋅b=−a+b−−a−b++aIbI, where the transverse index I=2,…,D−1I=2,\dots,D-1I=2,…,D−1 runs over the untouched directions. So p⋅p=−2p+p−+pIpIp\cdot p=-2p^+p^-+p^Ip^Ip⋅p=−2p+p−+pIpI, and setting this to −m2c2-m^2c^2−m2c2, −2p+p−+pIpI=−m2c2⟹  p−=pIpI+m2c22p+.  -2p^+p^-+p^Ip^I=-m^2c^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\;p^-=\frac{p^Ip^I+m^2c^2}{2p^+}.\;}−2p+p−+pIpI=−m2c2⟹p−=2p+pIpI+m2c2​.​ The dependent component p−p^-p− is now an explicit function of the independent data (p+,pI)(p^+,p^I)(p+,pI), no square root. This is the exact prototype of the string move: choose X+X^+X+ as time, then solve the constraint for the minus-component rather than carrying it as an independent degree of freedom.

    The pattern

    Gauge Ruler set to Payoff What's lost
    Proper length particle's own clock, τ=s\tau=sτ=s (=c τproper=c\,\tau_{\text{proper}}=cτproper​) d2xμ/ds2=0d^2x^\mu/ds^2=0d2xμ/ds2=0, straightest form undefined for m=0m=0m=0
    Static/time lab clock, τ=t\tau=tτ=t, x0=cτx^0=c\taux0=cτ Newtonian LLL, p⃗\vec pp​, EEE manifest covariance
    Light-cone light-front clock, x+=βτx^+=\beta\taux+=βτ p−p^-p− solved linearly a spatial axis singled out

    Every row spends the same freedom — the reparameterization redundancy — and buys a different simplification. No row changes the physics; that is guaranteed by Reparameterization invariance. The parameter is a blank ruler, and a gauge choice is a decision about where to paint the marks so the equation you care about looks simplest.

    Open questions

    • Static gauge x0=cτx^0=c\taux0=cτ is global only for future-directed timelike worldlines whose coordinate time is monotonic in the chosen frame; a worldline that "doubles back" in ttt breaks it.
    • The massless light-cone gauge needs the einbein/constraint formulation, since S=−mc∫dsS=-mc\int dsS=−mc∫ds degenerates at m=0m=0m=0 while the constraint p2=−m2c2p^2=-m^2c^2p2=−m2c2 survives.
    • After fixing τ=s\tau=sτ=s, the only residual freedom is a constant shift s→s+s0s\to s+s_0s→s+s0​ (choice of origin), once endpoint labels are matched.

    Exercise

    Show that the static gauge τ=t\tau=tτ=t and the proper-length gauge τ=s\tau=sτ=s are related by a legal reparameterization for a massive particle, and find ds/dtds/dtds/dt explicitly. Then explain in one sentence why the massless case blocks this.

    Solution

    Both are legal rulers on the same timelike worldline, so they must be connected by a monotone relabeling s(t)s(t)s(t). From the definition of proper length, ds2=c2dt2−dx⃗ 2ds^2=c^2dt^2-d\vec x^{\,2}ds2=c2dt2−dx2, so dsdt=c2−v⃗ 2=c1−v2/c2=cγ.\frac{ds}{dt}=\sqrt{c^2-\vec v^{\,2}}=c\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}=\frac{c}{\gamma}.dtds​=c2−v2​=c1−v2/c2​=γc​. This is strictly positive for v<cv<cv<c, hence ds/dt>0ds/dt>0ds/dt>0: the relabeling is monotone increasing, a legal reparameterization. It is exactly the Jacobian that converts S=−mc∫dsS=-mc\int dsS=−mc∫ds (proper-length gauge) into S=−mc2∫dt1−v2/c2S=-mc^2\int dt\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}S=−mc2∫dt1−v2/c2​ (static gauge, eq. (1)), since mc ds=mc⋅cγ dt=mc21−v2/c2 dtmc\,ds = mc\cdot\frac{c}{\gamma}\,dt = mc^2\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}\,dtmcds=mc⋅γc​dt=mc21−v2/c2​dt.

    Massless case: a photon has v=cv=cv=c, so ds/dt=c1−1=0ds/dt=c\sqrt{1-1}=0ds/dt=c1−1​=0. The ruler sss collapses to a point — sss is constant along the whole worldline — so it is not one-to-one and cannot serve as a parameter. There is no reparameterization to the τ=s\tau=sτ=s gauge because that gauge does not exist for m=0m=0m=0. (This is the same degeneracy that kills S=−mc∫dsS=-mc\int dsS=−mc∫ds at m=0m=0m=0; the light-cone gauge, using x+x^+x+ instead of sss, is the escape.)
