Dennis Schröder

Dennis Schröder is a contingent assists streamer: buried behind Cleveland's star guards in the base case, but immediately useful if James Harden leaves, an initiator is injured, or a trade opens a larger role.

Across 70 games in 2025-26, Schröder averaged 10.8 points and 4.9 assists in 24.2 minutes on .405 FG. In 17 Cleveland games his role fell to 15.9 minutes, 5.5 points, and 2.4 assists. The projection assumes Harden returns and trims that role again. Basketball-Reference Cavaliers season

Fantasy profile

  • Role: second-unit organizer in a 12-minute healthy allocation. He can absorb primary point-guard duties when Harden or Mitchell sits.
  • 9-cat: AST and some FT%/STL, but a damaging FG% and low-volume everything else in the base role. Undrafted in standard formats.
  • Points leagues: injury streamer; more useful than his 9-cat rank when assists are heavily rewarded.
  • Dynasty: Hashtag ranked him 310th in dynasty and 652nd in keeper. He is a short-term role asset whose next destination matters more than Cleveland's current depth slot. Hashtag dynasty rankings

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: under 10 minutes or traded as salary; no fantasy value.
  • Base: 12 minutes and 2.2 assists, with occasional streaming starts.
  • Ceiling: top 100-130 in a post-trade 25-minute role or during a multiweek Harden absence.

Transaction and health watch

July reporting identified Schröder as a possible outgoing salary in a LeBron structure. That is not a completed deal. A trade to a team needing a lead guard could improve his fantasy projection even while removing him from this page's rotation.

He had no major current injury publicly listed at the cutoff. Role—not health—is the principal uncertainty.

2025-26 split: 40 games at 26.4 MPG / 12.8 PTS / 5.3 AST for Sacramento, then 30 games at 21.4 MPG / 8.2 PTS / 4.3 AST after the trade to Cleveland.

Durability: Remarkably durable for a 13-year guard — 61+ games every season after his rookie year, with no major injury absence on record; the low rookie GP was a role decision, not health.

Trajectory: The record is a full arc: Atlanta starter peaking at 19.4 PPG in 2017-18, Sixth Man of the Year runner-up in OKC (2019-20), then a decade-long slide into plug-in lead-guard work across eleven teams, with assists (4-6 per game) the one constant at any stop giving him 25+ minutes. In 2026-27 he competes for Cleveland's backup point-guard minutes behind Donovan Mitchell and, if he returns, James Harden. He becomes fantasy-relevant under one condition: a Harden departure, an initiator injury, or a trade that restores a ~25-minute lead-guard role — the Sacramento stretch of 2025-26 (12.8/5.3) shows exactly what that looks like.
