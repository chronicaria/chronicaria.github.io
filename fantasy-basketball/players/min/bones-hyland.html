Bones Hyland

Bones Hyland is a microwave-scoring reserve guard with an established Minnesota rotation role, squeezed but not erased by an offseason of backcourt additions.

Team
Minnesota Timberwolves

Outside the 262-player projection pool — see the dossier for status.

Snapshot

  • Role: fourth guard behind LaMelo Ball, Anthony Edwards, and Ayo Dosunmu.
  • 2025-26 baseline: 71 games for this same team at 16.6 MPG with 8.5 PTS, 2.6 AST and 1.8 REB on .453 FG, .388 from three and .830 at the line.
  • Healthy-night share: approximately 11 minutes while Donte DiVincenzo is unavailable.
  • Fantasy use: points and threes streamer when the lead guards sit, not a standing roster hold.

Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: undrafted; a threes-and-assists streamer in deep formats.
  • Points: 180+; a spot starter or high-usage injury streamer.
  • Dynasty: 450+; no stable development pathway on this roster at 26.

Floor and ceiling

Floor: the perimeter additions squeeze him to genuine emergency status, with DNP-CDs and fewer than 40 games. Ceiling: he holds something near the 16-17 minutes he just played, and a Ball absence pushes him to 24 with double-digit points and two threes. His low rebounds and defensive stats keep even that ceiling format-sensitive.

Risk monitor

Minnesota can fill backup creation through Dosunmu and Edwards, so Hyland does not own a protected role, and he is on a one-year minimum with no guarantee past the deadline. Zyon Pullin gives the team a two-way point-guard alternative, though Pullin's 50-game two-way cap limits how much of Hyland's role he could actually absorb.

Durability: No significant injury ledger; the thin GP seasons (37 in 2023-24, 24 in 2024-25) were rotation exile in LA — traded to Atlanta at the 2025 deadline, waived, then rescued on a Minnesota two-way — not health. 71 games in 2025-26 is the availability signal.

Trajectory: The record shows a bench microwave who scored double digits per game as a Denver first-rounder (All-Rookie 2nd Team in 2022), washed out of the Clippers rotation on sub-.40 shooting, and rebuilt his value in Minnesota with the most efficient season of his career (.453 FG, .388 3P in 16.6 MPG over 71 games). In 2026-27 he competes for the fourth-guard minutes behind Ball, Edwards and Dosunmu, with roughly 11-13 healthy-night minutes while DiVincenzo is out. He becomes fantasy-relevant only during a lead-guard absence, when a 24-minute run makes him a points-and-threes streamer; he holds no standing roster value.
