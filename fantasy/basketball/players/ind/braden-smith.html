Why the line looks like this

Smith's Purdue senior line was 14.3 points and an NCAA-leading 8.9 assists; Summer League included games of nine assists and an 8/6/8 closeout. Redraft, dynasty, and scenarios

  • Redraft: no value; even an official two-way leaves three veteran point guards ahead of him.
  • Dynasty: stash in 30-team or 200-plus-prospect formats for passing feel; size and scoring translation cap upside.
  • Floor: contract resolution is delayed or another organization acquires his rights.
  • Ceiling: wins the two-way, dominates G League assists and earns a late-season 12-minute NBA audition.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Purdue, four seasons (2022-26): 9.7/4.2/4.4 as a freshman starter, 12.0/5.8/7.5 in the 2024 national-runner-up year, 15.8/4.5/8.7 with 2.2 steals as a junior (2025 Bob Cousy Award, Big Ten Player of the Year), 14.3/3.5/8.8 with 1.7 steals on .440/.362/.825 as a senior (2026 Big Ten Tournament MVP). Career: 13.0 PTS / 4.5 REB / 7.4 AST / 1.7 STL over 149 games; two-time consensus first-team All-American; NCAA Division I all-time career assist leader (1,103, past Bobby Hurley's 1,076). Draft: 2026, Round 2, Pick 38, Chicago Bulls; rights traded to Indiana.

No NBA record. Most recent season: 2025-26 at Purdue (39 GP, 34.5 MPG, 14.3 PTS, 8.8 AST).

Durability: Never missed meaningful time at Purdue — 149 games in four seasons, 35-39 every year.

Trajectory: The record is four years of escalating elite playmaking with strong steals and free-throw shooting, capped by the all-time NCAA assist record — but at a listed 6-foot with modest scoring translation, which is why a two-time consensus All-American fell to pick 38. In 2026-27 he competes for a reported two-way slot and the Noblesville Boom lead-guard job, with no healthy-night NBA minutes behind Indiana's veteran guards. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in a deep-dynasty sense: if the two-way becomes official, he dominates G League assists, and a Pacers guard injury opens a 12-plus-minute audition where his assist rate could make him a deep-league AST/STL stream.

Career sources: Wikipedia, Sports-Reference college page, Purdue athletics profile.
