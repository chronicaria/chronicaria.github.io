Carter Bryant

Role case

Bryant averaged 4.2 points, 2.5 rebounds and 0.7 threes in 11.5 minutes across 71 games as a rookie, shooting 40.8%. The full-season line understates where he finished: over the final 34 games he averaged 6.1 points and 3.4 rebounds on 37.3% from three in 15.3 minutes, and he appeared in 22 playoff games at 8.5 minutes. That trajectory, not the season average, is the right base for year two of a No. 14 pick. ESPN stats, accessed 2026-07-18 · Roundtable season recap, accessed 2026-07-18

His 2026 Summer League included a 19-point performance, useful evidence of on-ball growth. Harrison Barnes no longer sits ahead of him.

Ceiling and floor

  • Ceiling: overtakes Johnson outright or benefits from a Keldon trade, reaching 20-plus minutes and standard-league value on threes and stocks.
  • Floor: stalls at 8-10 minutes behind the veterans and stays a dynasty-only hold.
  • Best use: dynasty hold with real redraft upside in 14-team and deeper formats; still a late flier in 12-team.
  • Dynasty tension: prospect-market rank is far more optimistic than the immediate role, creating both patience upside and liquidity risk.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Arizona, one season (2024-25): 37 GP, 19.3 MPG, 6.5 PTS, 4.1 REB, 1.0 AST, 0.9 STL, 1.0 BLK on .460/.371/.695 — a low-usage, high-tools bench wing whose draft stock ran ahead of his production. Draft: 2025, Round 1, Pick 14, San Antonio Spurs.

One NBA season; the rookie row is the career record. He added 22 playoff appearances (2.6 PPG on .500 FG in 8.5 minutes) in San Antonio's deep run.

Durability: 71 of 82 rookie games plus a full playoff run — no injury flags in college or year one.

Trajectory: The record repeats his college pattern one level up: trusted defensive minutes, modest usage, and a three-point stroke (.335 as a rookie, .373 over his final 34 games) that decides everything. The closing stretch — 6.1 points, 3.4 rebounds, 15.3 minutes — is the honest baseline for year two, not the season average. In 2026-27 he contests Keldon Johnson's wing minutes behind Vassell, Champagnie and Harris. He becomes fantasy-relevant if he takes that job outright or Johnson is traded: at 20+ minutes his threes-plus-stocks profile plays in 14-team formats, while below that he stays a dynasty hold whose market rank (top-120 keeper crowd) already prices in the growth.
