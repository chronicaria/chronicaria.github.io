<h2 id="valuation-foundations">Valuation foundations</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-replacement-level/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball replacement level</a> — value begins with the best realistically available alternative in the actual league.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-category-scarcity/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball category scarcity</a> — scarcity is a marginal market relationship, not the raw rarity of a box-score statistic.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/category-covariance-in-fantasy-basketball-roster-construction/" class="wiki-link">Category covariance in fantasy basketball roster construction</a> — model how roles and shared events move category outcomes together instead of treating each column as independent.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/games-played-versus-per-game-value-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Games played versus per-game value in fantasy basketball</a> — scheduled games create value only when they become active-lineup appearances.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/points-league-volume-and-efficiency/" class="wiki-link">Points-league volume and efficiency</a> — translate minutes, role, and box-score events through the league's exact scoring formula.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/percentage-categories-and-volume/" class="wiki-link">Percentage categories and volume</a> — model shooting ratios through makes and attempts rather than averaging player percentages.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/category-floors-and-ceilings-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Category floors and ceilings in fantasy basketball</a> — define category-specific quantiles and coherent role scenarios instead of vague safe/upside labels.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/robust-punt-builds-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Robust punt builds in fantasy basketball</a> — stress-test a deliberate category concession against projection error, correlated failure, replacement supply, and changing majority paths.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/scarce-category-specialists-and-roster-fragility/" class="wiki-link">Scarce-category specialists and roster fragility</a> — separate a specialist's marginal category value from the roster's concentrated dependency and recovery risk.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/using-projection-disagreement-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Using projection disagreement in fantasy basketball</a> — normalize competing forecasts, locate contested assumptions, and combine them without mistaking spread for calibrated uncertainty.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/adp-uncertainty-and-player-uncertainty/" class="wiki-link">ADP uncertainty and player uncertainty</a> — separate uncertainty about draft acquisition timing from uncertainty about future player outcomes.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="opportunity-and-availability">Opportunity and availability</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-role-change-checklist/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball role-change checklist</a> — test whether opportunity, responsibility, and sustainability moved together.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/how-nba-injury-reports-should-change-fantasy-decisions/" class="wiki-link">How NBA injury reports should change fantasy decisions</a> — treat each status as timestamped evidence rather than a prognosis or transaction command.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/late-season-shutdown-and-rest-risk-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Late-season shutdown and rest risk in fantasy basketball</a> — price correlated late-season availability branches without turning incentives into medical claims.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/rookie-roles-that-matter-for-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Rookie roles that matter for fantasy basketball</a> — project the interaction of opportunity, skill translation, and actual responsibility rather than draft pedigree alone.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="transactions-and-weekly-planning">Transactions and weekly planning</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/consolidation-trades-and-roster-spots/" class="wiki-link">Consolidation trades and roster spots</a> — compare complete post-trade rosters, including the legal use and risk of capacity created by an uneven deal.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/two-for-one-trade-valuation-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Two-for-one trade valuation in fantasy basketball</a> — audit a specific uneven trade through its replacement path, playable lineups, category fit, and concentrated risk.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/trade-evaluation-under-role-uncertainty/" class="wiki-link">Trade evaluation under role uncertainty</a> — compare complete roster paths across correlated role states, reversal thresholds, and the option to wait for evidence.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/lineup-congestion-and-playable-games/" class="wiki-link">Lineup congestion and playable games</a> — model each rostered game as a dated assignment competing for eligible lineup capacity.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/positional-flexibility-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Positional flexibility in fantasy basketball</a> — value eligibility by the marginal legal assignments it unlocks rather than by counting position labels.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-streaming-on-low-volume-days/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball streaming on low-volume days</a> — optimize playable games after lineup congestion and acquisition costs.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-streaming-break-even-thresholds/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball streaming break-even thresholds</a> — require the stream's marginal lineup value to clear drop, transaction, and option costs.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/multi-move-streaming-paths/" class="wiki-link">Multi-move streaming paths</a> — treat a sequence of pickups as a contingent policy whose early moves preserve or destroy later legal options.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/faab-and-waiver-priority-as-option-value/" class="wiki-link">FAAB and waiver priority as option value</a> — spending a scarce claim resource sacrifices future flexibility.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/weekly-lineup-locks-and-injury-risk/" class="wiki-link">Weekly lineup locks and injury risk</a> — price irreversible weekly assignments with probability-weighted availability, workload, and playable games.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/il-slots-as-roster-flexibility/" class="wiki-link">IL slots as roster flexibility</a> — value contingent roster capacity net of eligibility timing, transaction restrictions, and the activation squeeze.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/roster-slot-liquidity-in-fantasy-basketball/" class="wiki-link">Roster-slot liquidity in fantasy basketball</a> — price how quickly and cheaply a roster position can be converted into a better legal lineup path.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/bench-slots-and-latent-lineup-value/" class="wiki-link">Bench slots and latent lineup value</a> — value retained reserves as state-contingent options over future legal lineups rather than as inactive box scores.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/fantasy-basketball-playoff-schedule-value/" class="wiki-link">Fantasy basketball playoff schedule value</a> — value playable marginal production across conditional rounds, not raw scheduled games.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/schedule-value-under-availability-uncertainty/" class="wiki-link">Schedule value under availability uncertainty</a> — propagate correlated participation, workload, and recoverability states through the calendar.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/conditional-playoff-schedule-value/" class="wiki-link">Conditional playoff schedule value</a> — weight later-round production by alternative-specific survival and opponent paths.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/transaction-limits-and-streaming-option-value/" class="wiki-link">Transaction limits and streaming option value</a> — treat every acquisition as both current production and a state-dependent future control.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/turnovers-as-an-opportunity-cost/" class="wiki-link">Turnovers as an opportunity cost</a> — judge turnovers as the format-dependent cost of a complete opportunity bundle rather than rewarding inactivity.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/fantasy-basketball/keeper-value-versus-redraft-value/" class="wiki-link">Keeper value versus redraft value</a> — separate current production from rule-created future surplus, keeper-slot opportunity cost, and roster friction.</li>
</ul>
