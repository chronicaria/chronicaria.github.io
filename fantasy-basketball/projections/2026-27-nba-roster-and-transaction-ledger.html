Completed trades since the July 18 baseline

2026-07-19 — three-team trade (OKC / ATL / DAL), completed. Oklahoma City sent Luguentz Dort to Atlanta; Dallas sent Ryan Nembhard to Atlanta; Atlanta sent Zaccharie Risacher to Dallas. Oklahoma City received Chicago's 2027 second-round pick (via Dallas) plus the two most favorable of Atlanta's 2031 and 2032 second-rounders, moving comfortably under the second tax apron — the stated motive — and creating a $17.7M trade exception. Dallas absorbed Risacher into an existing trade exception. The three team desks, dossiers, and 240-minute models were updated the same day. HoopsRumors · NBA.com · Yahoo

Update rule

A reported agreement stays conditional until an official team or league transaction confirms it. A roster-page listing does not by itself prove a current contract, and a completed transaction does not by itself settle the player's minutes.
