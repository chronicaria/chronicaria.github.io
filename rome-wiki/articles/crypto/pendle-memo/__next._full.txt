That is real usage, but the current run-rate is well below the 2025 peak, so this is a cyclical DeFi-yield asset rather than a straight-line growth story.</p>
<p>Token capture is better than most crypto. Pendle docs describe YT/yield fees and swap fees, with a large share routed to PENDLE buybacks and sPENDLE rewards. Team/investor cliffs appear largely past, but supply is not finished because 2% terminal inflation and liquidity incentives remain.</p>
<p>Security posture is above average with multiple audits and a Cantina bounty, but privileged roles and upgrade/pause surfaces still matter. An Aave risk review of Pendle PT architecture found guardian/admin controls without full trust minimization.</p>
<h2 id="bull-case">Bull case</h2>
<p>Pendle becomes the default onchain rates venue for stablecoins, RWAs, LST/LRTs, Ethena-like assets, and funding-rate markets; fees recover toward prior highs; buybacks turn usage into recurring token demand.</p>
<h2 id="bear-case">Bear case</h2>
<p>Demand was mostly yield/points seasonality. Revenue remains sub-$1M/month, emissions offset buybacks, and underlying-protocol or maturity-liquidity risk damages trust.</p>
