Fantasy case

Green averaged 10.4 points, 3.0 threes, 2.7 rebounds, and 1.9 assists in 29.1 minutes across 78 games in 2025-26, shooting .424 overall and .419 on 7.1 three-point attempts a night. That volume produced 232 makes, the most in franchise history, past Ray Allen's 229. The skill is bankable; the minutes are not. Tyler Herro, Ryan Rollins, Brayden Burries, Gary Trent Jr, Caris LeVert, and Kevin Porter Jr all occupy adjacent roles, and beat coverage has framed Green as a trade chip to declutter the guard room. His low turnovers preserve category utility, but tiny rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks make him almost entirely a threes stream.

Low case: roster consolidation drops him to 10-12 MPG or he is traded into a deeper rotation. Base: 18 MPG and 2.0 makes, rosterable in 14-team and three-point-hungry builds. High: veteran trades restore 26+ MPG and 2.8+ threes, pushing him toward top-110 category value.

Learning notes

Green's waiver signal is simple: 22 projected minutes in a schedule week. His fantasy skill is obvious enough that managers need not speculate before that workload appears.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Northern Iowa (2018-22): 17.9 PTS, 3.2 REB, 2.5 AST on .411/.378 with .900 FT over 99 games (1,769 points — checksum clean: 589 FGM, 266 3PM, 325 FTM). Two-time MVC Player of the Year (2020, 2022); lost essentially all of 2020-21 to surgeries on both hips (torn labrum/fracture). Draft: undrafted (2022); signed a two-way with Milwaukee.

Durability: GP climb of 35 → 56 → 73 → 78 tracks role, not health — no notable NBA injuries. The college hip surgeries (2020-21) have produced no recurrence in four pro seasons. Trajectory: Four straight seasons at essentially the same FG% (.423-.429) with steadily rising minutes and three-point volume, capped by 232 makes at .419 in 2025-26 — the franchise record. This is a one-skill specialist whose skill has never wavered; every fantasy question about him is a minutes question. In 2026-27 he competes with Herro, Rollins, Trent, LeVert, and Porter for wing minutes in a decluttering guard room. He is fantasy-relevant in category leagues whenever his projected workload clears about 22 MPG — via trade, injury, or roster consolidation — because at that threshold 2.5+ threes with elite FT% and near-zero turnovers arrives almost automatically.
