Isaac Jones

Isaac Jones demolished the G League with efficient scoring and nearly two blocks per game, making him a worthwhile deep-dynasty watch despite a two-way contract and no healthy Detroit rotation minutes.

Role and evidence

Jones made the 2025-26 All-NBA G League First Team after averaging 22.2 points, 8.9 rebounds, 2.6 assists, 1.0 steal, and 1.9 blocks in 30.0 minutes while shooting 61.6%. The G League player page lists him on a Detroit two-way contract; an NBA player update records the two-year two-way signing on February 10 after a brief waiver transaction.

He has an NBA-ready physical production profile but little spacing and a deep frontcourt ahead of him. His route is a Duren/Reed/Collins absence, followed by proving the G League rim finishing and block rate survive against NBA size.

Range

  • Ceiling: wins a standard deal and reaches 15 minutes, producing useful FG%, rebounds, and one-plus block.
  • Floor: remains an elite G League player with only scattered NBA call-ups.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Three seasons at Wenatchee Valley College (JUCO), then Idaho 2022-23 (19.4 PPG, 7.8 RPG, 1.7 APG — Big Sky Newcomer of the Year, Second-Team All-Big Sky) and Washington State 2023-24 (15.3 PPG, 7.6 RPG, 1.5 APG — First-Team All-Pac-12). Draft: undrafted (2024); signed a two-way with Sacramento on July 3, 2024.

Durability: No injury history of note. The thin NBA game counts are transactional: converted to a standard deal by Sacramento in March 2025, waived November 4, 2025, claimed by Detroit November 6, waived February 4, 2026, then re-signed to the two-year two-way on February 10. Trajectory: The NBA record is 47 games of elite-efficiency garbage time (.633 career FG%), while the 2025-26 G League record (22.2 PPG, 8.9 RPG, 1.9 BPG, .616 FG%, All-NBA G League First Team) shows a finisher-rim protector who has outgrown that level at 26. In 2026-27 he competes for the Motor City Cruise lead-big role and the first frontcourt call-up behind Duren, Reed, and Collins. He becomes fantasy-relevant only via a standard-contract conversion plus a Detroit frontcourt absence that hands him 15+ minutes, at which point he is a deep-league FG%/rebounds/blocks play.
