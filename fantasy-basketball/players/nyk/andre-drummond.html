Nine-cat: top 180-260 neutral; top-120 potential in punt-FT builds. Points: C-, with injury-away upside. Dynasty: 2.0/10.

Role and evidence

Drummond averaged 6.4 points, 8.4 rebounds, 1.3 assists, 0.6 steals, and 0.8 blocks in 19.5 minutes across 63 games in 2025-26, shooting .472 from the field and .631 at the line (Hashtag Basketball). New York officially signed him July 6 after Mitchell Robinson's departure (NBA.com).

The roster has no contracted third center as of July 18, so Drummond's healthy 17 minutes are structurally secure. Towns' shooting creates clean bench-unit spacing, but Drummond is unlikely to close except when rebounds or size dictate.

Role branches

  • Ceiling: Towns misses time and Drummond reaches 25 minutes, producing 11 rebounds, one steal, and one block.
  • Median: 17 minutes with strong rebounds/FG% and material FT damage.
  • Floor: defensive matchup issues force small-ball; minutes fall near 12 and he becomes a category-only streamer.

Market and confidence

Hashtag placed him 309th in analyst dynasty and 591st on the crowd keeper board. Rebound-rate confidence is high; role is medium-high because of roster construction; games and defensive usability are medium-low.

Durability: Iron-man early career — seven straight Detroit seasons of 78-82 games. The dips since: 57 GP in the pandemic-shortened 2019-20, 46 in the 2020-21 Cleveland shutdown-then-buyout year, and 40 in 2024-25 (in-and-out of the Philadelphia rotation with lower-leg issues). 63 games in 2025-26 at a steady backup load. For a 33-year-old center, availability is a relative strength.

Trajectory: Two clean phases. 2013-20: a four-time rebounding champion, two-time All-Star and All-NBA Third Team center peaking at 17.7 and 15.2. 2021-present: a per-minute-elite backup who still rebounds like a starter (8.4 in 19.5 minutes last year) and, new in 2025-26, shot 32-of-90 (.356) from three — the first real perimeter wrinkle of his career, worth noting against this file's 0.0 3PM projection. Career .489 FT is the permanent tax. In 2026-27 he holds New York's only conventional backup-center job behind Towns; he is fantasy-relevant now as a punt-FT rebounds/FG% specialist at 17 minutes, and becomes a must-add if a Towns absence pushes him to 25-plus, where his history says double-digit rebounds arrive immediately.
