Justin Champagnie

Role, baseline, and projection

  • 2025-26: 69 games, 20.0 MPG; 8.7 points and 5.6 rebounds on .502 FG. That was the most durable and most efficient wing-forward production on the roster.
  • Contract: $2,667,944 for 2026-27, non-guaranteed until January 10 2027, with a $3.01M team option for 2027-28. The non-guarantee is a real cut risk before that date, not a retention guarantee.
  • 2026-27 healthy-roster base: 10 allocated minutes at power forward. Dybantsa, George, Coulibaly, Johnson, Riley and Middleton absorb the rest of the modeled wing/four minutes.
  • Conditional role: 20 active-game minutes when a wing or power forward is unavailable.

Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: deep-league rebounds/FG%/stocks streamer with enough across-the-board production to matter at 22-plus minutes.
  • Points: waiver option only when injuries create a clear workload.
  • Dynasty: limited upside relative to Washington's younger wings.
  • Floor/ceiling: the January 10 non-guarantee is the floor mechanism — a cut is live if the roster crunch resolves against him; top-160-ish bursts if he reaches the mid-20s.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Two seasons at Pittsburgh — a 12.7-point freshman year, then a 2020-21 sophomore season of 18.0 PPG and 11.1 RPG, the only major-conference double-double average in the country; All-ACC First Team and AP All-America Honorable Mention. Draft: undrafted (2021); signed a two-way with Toronto in August 2021.

Durability: The gaps in the record are contractual, not physical — waived by Toronto in February 2023 with only 10-day Boston stints after, then a G League road back before Washington signed him late in 2023-24. Since earning the standard deal he has been steady: 62 and 69 games the last two seasons. Trajectory: A classic undrafted-rebounder survival arc: elite college board work, two years on the roster margins, then two consistent Washington seasons at essentially the same line — roughly 9 points, 5.6 boards, a steal and half a block on .50+ shooting in 20 minutes. The per-minute rebounding and stocks are real and repeatable; the assists and three-point volume are not growing. In 2026-27 he competes for the 10-minute reserve-four role against Riley and Middleton with a January 10 non-guarantee hanging over it. He becomes fantasy-relevant when a Washington wing or four goes down and he returns to 20+ minutes, where he is an immediate deep-league rebounds/FG%/stocks stream — the 2024-25 season is the proof of concept. Career sources: StatMuse career by season, Basketball-Reference, Wikipedia — Justin Champagnie, Sports-Reference college page.
