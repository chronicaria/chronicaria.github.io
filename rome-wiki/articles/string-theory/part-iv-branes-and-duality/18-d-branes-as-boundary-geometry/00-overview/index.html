    The one idea

    A boundary condition is a geometry. Fix a ppp-dimensional flat surface in space. Declare that open-string endpoints must live on it. That declaration is the Dppp-brane — there is no extra object, no new force. The surface is just the set of allowed landing points.

    Everything in this module is the consequence of taking that rule seriously and quantizing. The payoff is a chain that turns a boundary-value problem into physics:

    This is the first place string theory produces something recognizable as ordinary field theory. Stated plainly: the gauge fields of the Standard Model can be read off a stack of surfaces where strings end.

    Two boundary conditions, direction by direction

    A string at one instant is a little arc, and its two endpoints are beads that must stay on the allowed surface. In each spacetime direction the bead does one of two things:

    • Along the brane the bead is free to slide. Nothing pins it, so the endpoint force in that direction must vanish — the slope X′X'X′ goes to zero. This is a Neumann condition. Slogan: Neumann = "along the brane."
    • Normal to the brane the bead cannot leave the surface, so its coordinate is frozen at the brane's location xˉa\bar x^axˉa. This is a Dirichlet condition — the "D" in D-brane. Slogan: Dirichlet = "on the brane."

    From this one dictionary the rest is bookkeeping:

    • One brane, one string: both beads on the same surface. Quantizing gives a photon living on the brane plus one scalar per normal direction — and those scalars turn out to be the brane's own wobble modes, the first hint the brane is a real dynamical thing.
    • Two branes: each bead can land on brane 1 or brane 2, so a string carries a sector label [ij][ij][ij] — think of it as a directed edge from node iii to node jjj. A [12][12][12] string has its ends pinned to different surfaces, so it is a taut segment stretched across the gap; that tension costs energy, and the energy shows up as extra mass.
    • NNN coincident branes: the edge labels [ij][ij][ij] become the N×NN\times NN×N matrix indices of a gauge field. Joining string ends multiplies the matrices. Coincident ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ all N2N^2N2 strings are massless ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ the photon is promoted to a full U(N)U(N)U(N) Yang–Mills field.

    That last bullet is the module in miniature. Separating the branes gives the off-diagonal strings a length and hence a mass — a stringy Higgs mechanism — leaving only the diagonal U(1)NU(1)^NU(1)N light.

    1. 18.1 Definition of a Dp-brane — what a Dppp-brane is: ppp spatial dimensions, a (p+1)(p+1)(p+1)-dimensional world-volume, located by fixing its (d−p)(d-p)(d−p) normal coordinates. (The +1+1+1 is time; do not lose it.)
    2. 18.2 Boundary conditions as geometry — the central derivation: varying the string action produces a boundary term, and killing it forces either Neumann (X′=0X'=0X′=0, tangent) or Dirichlet (δX=0\delta X=0δX=0, normal). This note is the "boundary condition = geometry" claim, worked out.

    The load-bearing numbers

    Three facts do most of the work; if you remember only these, you can reconstruct the module.

    • Stretched-string mass: M2=(∣Δxˉ∣/(2πα′))2+1α′(N⊥−1)M^2 = \big(|\Delta\bar x|/(2\pi\alpha')\big)^2 + \tfrac1{\alpha'}(N^\perp-1)M2=(∣Δxˉ∣/(2πα′))2+α′1​(N⊥−1). The first term is (tension×length)2(\text{tension}\times\text{length})^2(tension×length)2, since the tension is T0=1/(2πα′)T_0 = 1/(2\pi\alpha')T0​=1/(2πα′).     • Critical separation: the [12][12][12] ground state (a scalar, M2=−1/α′+(∣Δxˉ∣/(2πα′))2M^2 = -1/\alpha' + \big(|\Delta\bar x|/(2\pi\alpha')\big)^2M2=−1/α′+(∣Δxˉ∣/(2πα′))2) is tachyonic when close, massless at ∣Δxˉ∣=2πα′|\Delta\bar x| = 2\pi\sqrt{\alpha'}∣Δxˉ∣=2πα′​, massive beyond.
    • Coincidence gives symmetry: NNN coincident branes ⇒\Rightarrow⇒ U(N)U(N)U(N) gauge symmetry, because all N2N^2N2 off-diagonal strings drop to zero length and hence zero mass.

    Open questions (module-level)

    • Open-string quantization suggests that fields live on the brane, but it does not prove the brane is dynamical. The proof needs closed strings scattering off it — flagged in 18.3 Open strings on one brane.
    • Where do the stretched [12][12][12] fields actually live? Not cleanly on either brane; the honest answer points toward noncommutative geometry, beyond this first pass.
    • Intersecting D-branes (the route to Standard-Model-like fermions) are structurally related but belong to a later model-building layer, not this boundary-geometry module.

    Exercise

    Close the notes and answer all three aloud. Each targets one pillar of the module; if all three come out clean, bump status on the sublessons.

    1. Why does a Dppp-brane have ppp spatial Neumann directions and (d−p)(d-p)(d−p) transverse Dirichlet directions?
    2. Describe a string stretched between two parallel branes — its shape, its zero mode, and its mass. Explain why the mass term is (tension × length)2^22.
    3. Why do NNN coincident branes give U(N)U(N)U(N) rather than NNN unrelated photons?
    Solution

    1. The N/D split. The brane is a ppp-dimensional spatial hyperplane, so locating it takes (d−p)(d-p)(d−p) conditions — one per normal direction. Endpoints must lie on the brane. In a normal direction, "on the brane" means "frozen at the brane's position xˉa\bar x^axˉa" — a Dirichlet condition; that gives (d−p)(d-p)(d−p) of them. In a tangent direction nothing pins the endpoint, and killing the boundary term in δS\delta SδS forces X′=0X'=0X′=0 — a Neumann condition; that gives the ppp spatial ones, plus the always-Neumann time direction x0x^0x0 (strings are never pinned in time), for p+1p+1p+1 NN coordinates total. One line: Dirichlet = "on the brane," Neumann = "along the brane."

    2. The stretched string. Its two endpoints are Dirichlet-pinned to different branes in the normal directions, so classically it is a straight taut segment of length ∣Δxˉ∣|\Delta\bar x|∣Δxˉ∣ from brane 1 (at σ=0\sigma=0σ=0) to brane 2 (at σ=π\sigma=\piσ=π), plus sin⁡nσ\sin n\sigmasinnσ oscillations that leave the ends fixed. It carries a nonzero zero mode 2α′ α0a=1πΔxˉa\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,\alpha_0^a = \tfrac1\pi\Delta\bar x^a2α′​α0a​=π1​Δxˉa but no normal momentum (α0a\alpha_0^aα0a​ appears in the slope X′aX'^aX′a, never in the velocity X˙a\dot X^aX˙a). Its mass is M2=(∣Δxˉ∣2πα′)2+1α′(N⊥−1).M^2 = \left(\frac{|\Delta\bar x|}{2\pi\alpha'}\right)^2 + \frac1{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp - 1\big).M2=(2πα′∣Δxˉ∣​)2+α′1​(N⊥−1). The first term is (tension × length)2^22: with T0=1/(2πα′)T_0 = 1/(2\pi\alpha')T0​=1/(2πα′) it equals (T0 ∣Δxˉ∣)2(T_0\,|\Delta\bar x|)^2(T0​∣Δxˉ∣)2, the squared rest energy of a static taut string. It vanishes only when the branes coincide.

    3. Coincidence gives U(N)U(N)U(N). Each endpoint can land on any of the NNN branes, so a massless vector carries two labels i,j=1,…,Ni,j=1,\dots,Ni,j=1,…,N: it is a matrix entry AmijA_m{}^i{}_jAm​ij​, with N2N^2N2 of them. When the branes coincide, every off-diagonal string has zero stretched length and is therefore massless, so all N2N^2N2 vectors are light and assemble into one matrix-valued gauge field — the U(N)U(N)U(N) Yang–Mills connection (joining string ends realizes matrix multiplication, [ij]∗[jk]=[ik][ij]*[jk]=[ik][ij]∗[jk]=[ik]). Separate the branes and the off-diagonal strings gain mass (∣Δxij∣/(2πα′))2\big(|\Delta x_{ij}|/(2\pi\alpha')\big)^2(∣Δxij​∣/(2πα′))2, leaving only the diagonal U(1)NU(1)^NU(1)N light — the Higgs mechanism, geometrically.
