    Worldsheet stress tensor

    The worldsheet stress tensor T_{\alpha\beta} is what the Polyakov action pushes back with when you deform the worldsheet metric. Because that metric is auxiliary, its own equation of motion is simply T_{\alpha\beta}=0 — and that equation is the Virasoro constraints . It is automatically traceless (the algebraic fingerprint of Weyl invariance ), so in complex coordinates it splits into a holomorphic T(z) and an antiholomorphic \bar T(\bar z) .

    Why the response must vanish

    A stress tensor records the flow of energy and momentum. TττT_{\tau\tau}Tττ​ is energy density, TτσT_{\tau\sigma}Tτσ​ is momentum flowing along the string, and "vary the metric" asks: if we locally stretch or shear the worldsheet, how much does the action care? The Polyakov action is built to be reparameterization invariant — it does not depend on how we draw the coordinate grid — so a pure change of grid leaves it unchanged and the response to it vanishes. The twist unique to the Polyakov string is that the metric is not a fixed backdrop but a field we vary, so "the action doesn't care" is not a theorem checked afterwards; it is the equation of motion of that field. The allowed string configurations are exactly the ones with zero worldsheet stress.

    The one statement to carry forward is that Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0 is not an extra rule imposed on the string; it is what the auxiliary metric demands. Everything below makes that precise and reads off the normalization.

    Deriving TαβT_{\alpha\beta}Tαβ​ by varying the metric

    Start from the Polyakov action (mostly-plus signature, ημν=diag(−1,+,…,+)\eta_{\mu\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(-1,+,\dots,+)ημν​=diag(−1,+,…,+) in spacetime, hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ the worldsheet metric): S_P=-\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-h}\;h^{\alpha\beta}\,\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X. \tag{1} We vary hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ and demand δS=0\delta S=0δS=0. Two small identities do all the work.

    (i) Varying the determinant. For any matrix, δdet⁡A=(det⁡A) Tr(A−1δA)\delta\det A=(\det A)\,\mathrm{Tr}(A^{-1}\delta A)δdetA=(detA)Tr(A−1δA) (Jacobi's formula: the first-order change of a determinant is the trace of the inverse times the change). Applied to h≡det⁡hαβh\equiv\det h_{\alpha\beta}h≡dethαβ​, \delta h = h\,h^{\alpha\beta}\,\delta h_{\alpha\beta}. \tag{2}

    (ii) Trading δhαβ\delta h_{\alpha\beta}δhαβ​ for δhαβ\delta h^{\alpha\beta}δhαβ. Since hαβhβγ=δγαh^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\gamma}=\delta^\alpha_\gammahαβhβγ​=δγα​ is fixed, varying it gives hαβδhβγ=−hβγ δhαβh^{\alpha\beta}\delta h_{\beta\gamma}=-h_{\beta\gamma}\,\delta h^{\alpha\beta}hαβδhβγ​=−hβγ​δhαβ. Feed that into (2): \delta h=-h\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,\delta h^{\alpha\beta} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \delta\sqrt{-h}=-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\delta h}{\sqrt{-h}}=-\tfrac12\sqrt{-h}\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,\delta h^{\alpha\beta}. \tag{3} This −12−h hαβ-\tfrac12\sqrt{-h}\,h_{\alpha\beta}−21​−h​hαβ​ is the universal "measure piece" you get whenever you vary −h\sqrt{-h}−h​; it is the source of the −12hαβ(… )-\tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}(\dots)−21​hαβ​(…) trace-subtraction in every stress tensor.

    Assemble. Varying (1) hits both the explicit hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ (giving ∂αX⋅∂βX\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X∂α​X⋅∂β​X) and the −h\sqrt{-h}−h​ (giving (3)): \delta S=-\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-h}\;\delta h^{\alpha\beta}\left(\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}\,h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X\right). \tag{4} Demanding δS=0\delta S=0δS=0 for arbitrary δhαβ\delta h^{\alpha\beta}δhαβ kills the parenthesis pointwise. That parenthesis is the stress tensor, and its vanishing is the metric's equation of motion: \boxed{\;T_{\alpha\beta}\;\propto\;\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}\,h^{\gamma\delta}\,\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X=0\;} \tag{5} The first term is the induced metric γαβ=∂αX⋅∂βX\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta Xγαβ​=∂α​X⋅∂β​X (cf. Independent worldsheet metric) — the geometry the string actually carries; the second term subtracts off its trace part. The EOM says the induced metric must equal its own trace part, i.e. the worldsheet is "on shell" as a geometry.

    Fixing the normalization

    Equation (5) leaves an overall constant free. The clean way to pin it is the general-relativity definition of a stress tensor: it is the response of the action to the inverse metric, \boxed{\;T_{\alpha\beta}=-\frac{4\pi}{\sqrt{g}}\,\frac{\partial S}{\partial g^{\alpha\beta}}\;} \tag{6} where gαβg_{\alpha\beta}gαβ​ is now the Euclidean worldsheet metric (−h→g\sqrt{-h}\to\sqrt{g}−h​→g​) and the 4π4\pi4π is the conventional string-theory normalization. Reading (6) off the Euclidean free-boson action S=14πα′∫d2σ g gαβ∂αX⋅∂βXS=\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma\,\sqrt g\,g^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta XS=4πα′1​∫d2σg​gαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X (no minus sign in Euclidean signature): the ∂S/∂gαβ\partial S/\partial g^{\alpha\beta}∂S/∂gαβ produces exactly the parenthesis of (5) times g4πα′\frac{\sqrt g}{4\pi\alpha'}4πα′g​​, and the −4π/g-4\pi/\sqrt g−4π/g​ prefactor cancels the g\sqrt gg​ and the 4π4\pi4π, leaving \boxed{\;T_{\alpha\beta}=-\frac{1}{\alpha'}\left(\partial_\alpha X\,\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12\,\delta_{\alpha\beta}\,(\partial X)^2\right)\;} \tag{7} So the proportionality constant in (5) is −1/α′-1/\alpha'−1/α′. (The Lorentzian variation and this Euclidean definition are the same computation up to a Wick rotation — see Complex coordinates on the worldsheet; only the sign of ±h\sqrt{\pm h}±h​ and the flat metric η→δ\eta\to\deltaη→δ change. The local equation (7) is what every later OPE uses.)

    Tracelessness — the conformal fingerprint

    Trace (5) with hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ, using hαβhαβ=2h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}=2hαβhαβ​=2 in two dimensions: hαβ∂αX⋅∂βX−12(hαβhαβ)⏟=2 hγδ∂γX⋅∂δX=hγδ∂γX⋅∂δX−hγδ∂γX⋅∂δX=0.h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12\underbrace{(h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta})}_{=2}\,h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X = h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X-h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X=0.hαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X−21​=2(hαβhαβ​)​​hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X=hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X−hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X=0. The trace vanishes identically — before using any equation of motion, purely because the 12\tfrac1221​ and the factor 222 from the dimension conspire. This is the algebraic shadow of Weyl invariance: rescaling hαβ→Ω2hαβh_{\alpha\beta}\to\Omega^2 h_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​→Ω2hαβ​ leaves the action alone, and the derivative of the action along that rescaling is TααT^\alpha{}_\alphaTαα​, so Tαα=0T^\alpha{}_\alpha=0Tαα​=0. A traceless stress tensor is the defining local feature of a conformal theory; that is why the gauge-fixed worldsheet is a CFT.

    Complex coordinates: T(z)T(z)T(z) and Tˉ(zˉ)\bar T(\bar z)Tˉ(zˉ)

    In the coordinates of Complex coordinates on the worldsheet (z=σ1+iσ2z=\sigma^1+i\sigma^2z=σ1+iσ2), tracelessness Tαα=0T^\alpha{}_\alpha=0Tαα​=0 reads Tzzˉ=0T_{z\bar z}=0Tzzˉ​=0, and conservation ∂αTαβ=0\partial^\alpha T_{\alpha\beta}=0∂αTαβ​=0 becomes ∂ˉTzz=0\bar\partial T_{zz}=0∂ˉTzz​=0, ∂Tzˉzˉ=0\partial T_{\bar z\bar z}=0∂Tzˉzˉ​=0. A function killed by ∂ˉ\bar\partial∂ˉ is holomorphic, so T(z)\equiv T_{zz}=-\frac{1}{\alpha'}\,\partial X\,\partial X,\qquad \bar T(\bar z)\equiv T_{\bar z\bar z}=-\frac{1}{\alpha'}\,\bar\partial X\,\bar\partial X. \tag{8} The stress tensor of one free boson is just (minus, over α′\alpha'α′) the square of the holomorphic current ∂X\partial X∂X — the simplest possible composite operator, and the seed of every computation in Free boson OPEs. Conservation + tracelessness having forced TTT to be holomorphic is precisely why its modes can be extracted by a contour integral, which is what makes the Virasoro algebra computable.

    Conformal gauge: the Virasoro constraints reappear

    Fix −h hαβ=ηαβ\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}=\eta^{\alpha\beta}−h​hαβ=ηαβ (conformal gauge, Independent worldsheet metric) and write ξ1=τ, ξ2=σ\xi^1=\tau,\ \xi^2=\sigmaξ1=τ, ξ2=σ, X˙=∂τX\dot X=\partial_\tau XX˙=∂τ​X, X′=∂σXX'=\partial_\sigma XX′=∂σ​X. The Weyl factor drops out of (5), and component by component it gives T_{\tau\tau}=T_{\sigma\sigma}\propto \dot X^2+X'^2=0,\qquad T_{\tau\sigma}\propto\dot X\cdot X'=0, \tag{9} equivalently (X˙±X′)2=0(\dot X\pm X')^2=0(X˙±X′)2=0 — the Virasoro constraints. (The third component is redundant: symmetric 2×22\times22×2 has three entries but tracelessness removes one, matching the two constraints.) So "Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0" and "Virasoro constraints" are literally the same statement — the conditions we imposed by hand in light-cone gauge are now the free equation of motion of the metric. Expanding T(z)T(z)T(z) in Laurent modes gives the covariant generators L_n=\tfrac12\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\alpha_{n-p}\cdot\alpha_p, \tag{10} whose algebra is worked out in Virasoro algebra and central charge. Here the dot runs over all DDD spacetime indices (including X0X^0X0), which is why covariant quantization must work to remove negative-norm states.

    Quantum subtlety: normal ordering

    Classically T=−1α′∂X ∂XT=-\tfrac{1}{\alpha'}\partial X\,\partial XT=−α′1​∂X∂X is a product of fields at the same point, which is singular quantum-mechanically — ⟨∂X(z)∂X(w)⟩∼(z−w)−2\langle\partial X(z)\partial X(w)\rangle\sim(z-w)^{-2}⟨∂X(z)∂X(w)⟩∼(z−w)−2 blows up as z→wz\to wz→w. The fix is to normal-order, subtracting the divergent self-contraction: T=-\frac{1}{\alpha'}:\!\partial X\,\partial X\!:\;\equiv-\frac{1}{\alpha'}\lim_{z\to w}\Big(\partial X(z)\partial X(w)-\langle\partial X(z)\partial X(w)\rangle\Big), \tag{11} so that ⟨T⟩=0\langle T\rangle=0⟨T⟩=0 by construction. This finite composite operator is what lets the T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w)T(z)T(w) OPE develop a central term: the ccc in the Virasoro algebra is a genuine quantum short-distance effect, of the same origin as the normal-ordering constant a=−1a=-1a=−1 in the open-string mass shell at D=26D=26D=26.

    Open questions

    • Wick-rotation bookkeeping. The Lorentzian variation (giving (5)) and the Euclidean definition (giving (7)) agree after the standard Wick rotation; a full sign-by-sign proof is worthwhile but unnecessary for the local equations used here.
    • Quantum trace anomaly. This note proves classical tracelessness. On a curved background the trace no longer vanishes: ⟨Tαα⟩=−c12R\langle T^\alpha{}_\alpha\rangle=-\tfrac{c}{12}R⟨Tαα​⟩=−12c​R, derived from the same central charge in Weyl invariance and Virasoro algebra and central charge.

    Exercise

    Repeat the metric variation of the Polyakov action (1) on a hypothetical ddd-dimensional worldsheet (same action, α,β=1,…,d\alpha,\beta=1,\dots,dα,β=1,…,d), and show the stress tensor comes out as Tαβ∝∂αX⋅∂βX−12 hαβ hγδ∂γX⋅∂δX,T_{\alpha\beta}\propto\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X,Tαβ​∝∂α​X⋅∂β​X−21​hαβ​hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X, with the same 12\tfrac1221​ for every ddd. Then take its trace and find the one dimension in which Tαα=0T^\alpha{}_\alpha=0Tαα​=0. Why is that the worldsheet dimension of string theory?

    Solution

    Where the 12\tfrac1221​ comes from. In the assembly step (4), the subtraction term is the measure piece (3): δ−h=−12−h hαβ δhαβ\delta\sqrt{-h}=-\tfrac12\sqrt{-h}\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,\delta h^{\alpha\beta}δ−h​=−21​−h​hαβ​δhαβ. That 12\tfrac1221​ is the derivative of a square root, ddh−h\tfrac{d}{dh}\sqrt{-h}dhd​−h​ — it has nothing to do with the worldsheet dimension. So on a ddd-dimensional worldsheet the variation gives exactly the boxed form above, 12\tfrac1221​ and all.

    The trace. Write P≡hγδ∂γX⋅∂δXP\equiv h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta XP≡hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X and use hαβhαβ=dh^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}=dhαβhαβ​=d: Tαα=hαβ∂αX⋅∂βX−12 (hαβhαβ) P=P−d2 P=(1−d2) P.T^\alpha{}_\alpha = h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X-\tfrac12\,(h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta})\,P = P-\tfrac{d}{2}\,P=\Big(1-\tfrac{d}{2}\Big)\,P.Tαα​=hαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X−21​(hαβhαβ​)P=P−2d​P=(1−2d​)P. This vanishes for all configurations (P≠0P\neq0P=0 generically) iff d=2d=2d=2.

    So the Polyakov stress tensor is traceless only in two dimensions, because a fixed coefficient 12\tfrac1221​ meets the dimension-dependent requirement d2=1\tfrac{d}{2}=12d​=1 only at d=2d=2d=2. This is the same fact seen from the trace side that Weyl invariance sees from the action side: −h hαβ\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}−h​hαβ is Weyl-invariant only in d=2d=2d=2, so only a 2D worldsheet is conformal. The stringy worldsheet is 2D precisely so that this tracelessness — the CFT structure the whole module rests on — actually holds.
