<p>A particle's spin is not chosen; its momentum chooses. Massive means it can be stopped, so all the rotations of space act and the multiplet is fat; massless means it cannot, so only the rotations transverse to its flight survive and the multiplet is exactly two components leaner, the missing modes being what gauge invariance throws away. Count the transverse indices on a string state and you have read off which particle it is.</p>
