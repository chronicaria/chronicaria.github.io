Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: 130-175 overall; rises further in punt-FT% builds and in blocks scarcity. At 21 minutes this is a real late-round target rather than a watchlist name, though the free-throw line remains disqualifying in FT%-sensitive builds.
  • Points: 150-200; scoring and playmaking still do not carry a bench-usage center in most points settings.
  • Dynasty: 90-140; a useful young rim-running center, though Derik Queen owns the superior offensive ceiling.

Role, floor and ceiling

Missi starts games because his rim protection better complements Zion Williamson, then yields closing minutes to Queen. Floor: 14–16 minutes as the third active center if Karlo Matković is healthy and stretching the floor, or if New Orleans fills its open standard roster spot with a veteran center — the reporting is explicit that the remaining hole is at the five, which is the live threat to this projection. Ceiling: 26–28 minutes, 8/8 and 1.9 blocks if Mosley leans as hard on defense as his Orlando record suggests and Matković's back limits him.

Injury and transaction monitor

Missi was cleared from a calf strain on February 28 and suffered a right-hand sprain in April. FantasyPros, 2026-02-28 · CBS/RotoWire, 2026-04-07 Camp should confirm both are fully resolved.

Durability: 73 GP as a rookie; 66 GP in 2025-26 around a calf strain (cleared February 28) and a late-season right-hand sprain. No structural red flags. Trajectory: The record is a rookie-year workhorse (26.8 MPG, near-double-double pace) demoted to a reserve in year two — yet his block rate rose on fewer minutes while FG% held steady, so the defensive skill is real and the decline was situational, not developmental. In 2026-27 he competes to keep the nominal starting-center job against Queen's offensive ceiling and Matković, under a defense-first coach whose system favors him. He is fantasy-relevant as a late-round blocks/FG% specialist whenever he holds 20+ MPG; the sub-.600 FT% (and worsening: .623 to .559) caps him to punt-FT% and deep category builds unless the line rebounds.
