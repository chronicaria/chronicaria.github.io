The proof gate is not the financing press release; it is clean delivery, installation, facility closing, revenue commencement, and customer payment evidence in Q2/Q3 filings.</p>
<h2 id="bear-case">Bear case</h2>
<p>The stock may be capitalizing press-release EBITDA before operations exist at scale. The unnamed end customer, related-party revenue history, preferred/warrant complexity, and repeated equity issuance make the forward contract less bankable than it first looks.</p>
<h2 id="falsifiers">Falsifiers</h2>
<ul>
<li>Q2/Q3 filings do not show GPU delivery, customer acceptance, and revenue ramp.</li>
<li>Another equity, preferred, or warrant financing arrives before clear cash generation.</li>
<li>Customer nonpayment, contract amendment, GPU impairment, or rail-business slippage appears.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="verdict">Verdict</h2>
<p>Active research. DUOT deserves the first full memo because the upside is directly tied to AI infrastructure, but it must be attacked through customer credit, GPU residual value, financing closure, and dilution.</p>
