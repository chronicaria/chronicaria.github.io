2026-07-19 — three-team Dort/Risacher/Nembhard trade (completed)

Oklahoma City traded Luguentz Dort to Atlanta; Dallas sent Ryan Nembhard to Atlanta; Atlanta sent Zaccharie Risacher to Dallas; OKC received three second-round picks (Chicago 2027 via Dallas, plus the two most favorable of Atlanta's 2031/2032 seconds) and got comfortably under the second tax apron. This is a completed transaction, not a report. Projection consequences applied same-day: Dort becomes Atlanta's starting defensive wing at 27 base minutes; Risacher gets a 22-minute Dallas wing runway next to Cooper Flagg; Nembhard drops to zero base minutes in a 17-contract Atlanta guard room; OKC's Mitchell/McCain/Wallace/Caruso all gained minutes and the fifth-starter battle narrowed to Wallace versus Mitchell.
