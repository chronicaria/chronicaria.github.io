Career record

Pre-NBA: Two seasons at Denver, then two at Baylor. As a senior in 2014-15 he averaged 10.1 points, 5.8 rebounds and 3.4 assists, finishing with 1,268 points across 134 Division I games. Draft: undrafted (2015). He spent 2015-16 with MHP Riesen Ludwigsburg in the German BBL (46 games, 8.4 points, 4.5 rebounds) and 2016-17 with Gran Canaria in the Spanish ACB (52 games, 8.3 points, 5.2 rebounds, 2.1 assists) before signing with Utah.

Durability: Exceptionally available — 678 games across nine seasons with no year below 69 played except the split 2023-24 campaign, in which he still appeared in 79 games between Brooklyn and Phoenix. There is no significant injury history to flag.

Trajectory: A nine-year career of the same job done at slowly increasing volume: low-usage forward, catch-and-shoot threes, positional rebounds, one steal, no self-creation. His 2025-26 line was his career best in points, threes and steals, but that came with a 28.4-minute role Phoenix gave him out of necessity, and Charlotte acquired him in the July 13 Miles Bridges trade as a veteran on a bench with younger competition. He is fantasy-relevant only if Charlotte hands him back a high-20s minute role — realistically requiring a Brandon Miller absence or a trade that clears the forward rotation — since at 18 minutes his counting stats fall below deep-league thresholds in every category.
