The charge <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>Q</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">Q</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">Q</span></span></span></span> is the count inside. <p>Current and charge live at different levels: the current is a field on the whole domain; the charge is one number at one instant. The rest of the note makes this precise, then builds the machine that manufactures a current from any symmetry.</p>
