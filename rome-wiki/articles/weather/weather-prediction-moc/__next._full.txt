17:Tc52,<h2 id="the-machinery">The machinery</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/mathematics/applied-pdes/data-assimilation-as-an-inverse-problem/" class="wiki-link">Data assimilation as an inverse problem</a> — inverse-problem foundations for forecast initialization and state estimation.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/numerical-weather-prediction/" class="wiki-link">Numerical weather prediction</a> — solving the atmosphere's fluid equations on a grid; the major global models (ECMWF, GFS, …) and data assimilation.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/ensemble-forecasting/" class="wiki-link">Ensemble forecasting</a> — running many perturbed forecasts so the <em>spread</em> becomes a flow-dependent uncertainty estimate.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="limits-and-skill">Limits and skill</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/atmospheric-predictability-limit/" class="wiki-link">Atmospheric predictability limit</a> — chaos, Lorenz, error growth, and the ~2-week ceiling on daily weather.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/forecast-skill/" class="wiki-link">Forecast skill</a> — how quality is scored (ACC of 500 hPa height) and the "one day per decade" improvement.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="the-practical-question">The practical question</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/temperature-forecast-confidence-intervals/" class="wiki-link">Temperature forecast confidence intervals</a> — <em>"I see 80 °F 3 days out — what's the 95% band?"</em> (≈ ±8 °F, widening with lead time, regime-dependent).</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/feels-like-temperature/" class="wiki-link">Feels-like temperature</a> — heat index (summer), wind chill (winter), and the year-round Steadman apparent temperature that Open-Meteo reports; how the three combine temperature, humidity and wind.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/urban-heat-island/" class="wiki-link">Urban heat island</a> — how much NYC temperature varies block-to-block (~2–8 °F air, mostly at night) and why forecasts can't see it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="build-it">Build it</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/weather-forecast-apis/" class="wiki-link">Weather forecast APIs</a> — where to get free forecast/ensemble data; only ensemble APIs give plottable spread.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/open-meteo-previous-runs-api/" class="wiki-link">Open-Meteo Previous Runs API</a> — free endpoint that serves <em>past forecasts at a fixed lead time</em>, so you can score "how good were recent forecasts?" client-side with no backend.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/nyc-weather-dashboard/" class="wiki-link">NYC weather dashboard</a> — project: a static page showing NYC hourly temperature with widening confidence bands + honest precip probability.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/weather/weather-dashboard-feature-roadmap/" class="wiki-link">Weather dashboard feature roadmap</a> — 50+ API-grounded ideas to grow the dashboard, tiered by effort (rain-timing, spaghetti view, AQI/smoke, "nicest right now" ranking, story-of-the-week, PWA…).</li>
