Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: top-85-to-125 range; materially better in punt-FT% builds. Rebounds, FG% and unusual center steals are the hooks.
  • Points: top-105-to-140, with a boost for offensive-rebound and double-double bonuses.
  • Dynasty: top-100-to-135 hold. He is 24, signed, and finally has a starting path, but defensive limitations could cap him near 25 minutes.

Role, floor and ceiling

Claxton's trade removes the incumbent, while the only veteran center addition is Moritz Wagner, signed for two years and $19 million. Reporting frames the job as a three-way competition between Sharpe, Wagner and Danny Wolf rather than a Sharpe coronation. Floor: 21 minutes, 9/7 with streaming value if Wagner or small-ball Randle closes. Ceiling: 28 minutes, 14/10 with one steal and one block, good enough for top-75 punt-build value.

Risk monitor

Sharpe required surgery for a left-thumb UCL tear and missed the end of 2025-26. Brooklyn Nets, 2026-03-12 Monitor hand clearance, foul rate and whether Brooklyn believes his rim protection can anchor starters.

Career record

Pre-NBA: North Carolina, one season (2020-21). 29 games, 19.2 MPG, 9.5 PTS, 7.6 REB, 1.4 AST, 0.9 BLK on .519 FG / .505 FT, with no three-point attempts of consequence; ACC All-Freshman Team. He was a bench big for Roy Williams' final Tar Heel team and led the ACC in offensive rebound rate, which has remained the defining skill of his professional career. Draft: 2021, first round, 29th overall by Phoenix; draft rights traded to Brooklyn on August 6, 2021 with Jevon Carter for Landry Shamet.

Fantasy history: Sharpe has never finished a season inside standard-league relevance in per-game nine-category terms, and I could not verify published season-end 9-cat ranks for any of his five years from Basketball Monster, Hashtag or the ESPN Player Rater, so no numeric finishes are stated here. The shape is consistent and can be read off the table: strong FG%, high rebounds for the minutes, weak-to-neutral FT%, effectively zero threes, and — since 2024-25 — a steals rate that is genuinely rare at center. Points leagues rate him lower than categories, because points formats pay for volume he has never had and do not reward the FG%-plus-steals combination that carries his category profile.

Durability: 32, 48, 61, 50, 62 games. 2021-22 and 2022-23 were rotation-depth seasons, not injury seasons — the missed games were coach's decisions behind Nic Claxton and Andre Drummond. 2023-24 included a left knee hyperextension suffered January 8, 2024. 2024-25 was the worst of it: a left hamstring strain in training camp cost him the first 21 games, and a right knee sprain ended his year roughly 10 games early. 2025-26 ended in March with surgery to repair a torn ulnar collateral ligament in the left thumb. Three consecutive seasons with a soft-tissue or structural absence is the pattern that matters, and none of the three injuries were to the same body part.

Trajectory: The per-game line has risen every year since 2022-23, but almost all of that is minutes rather than rate improvement — per 36, he scored roughly 16.2 in 2023-24, 15.7 in 2024-25 and 16.7 in 2025-26, while rebounds per 36 actually fell from about 15.3 to 12.9 as his role drifted from pure offensive-glass specialist toward a hub-passing big. That passing growth is the one real skill leap: 0.5 assists per game as a rookie to 2.3 in 2025-26, at roughly 4.4 per 36, which is the profile of a player Brooklyn now trusts to touch the ball at the elbow. The 2025-26 FG% spike to .601 is the second real signal, and it came with a shot diet that stayed almost entirely at the rim. The concerning line is blocks: 0.4 per game in 2025-26 was a career low in his career-high minutes, which argues his defensive value is coming from hands and positioning rather than rim protection and supports the dossier's doubt about anchoring a starting unit. He turns 25 in November, so this is the flat top of the athletic curve rather than a pre-leap age; expect the minutes to grow with Claxton gone and the rates to hold roughly where they are.
