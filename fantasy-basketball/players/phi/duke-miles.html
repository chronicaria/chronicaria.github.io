  • In his final Vanderbilt season, Miles averaged 16.1 points, 4.5 assists, 3.0 rebounds, and 2.6 steals in 28 games on 44/35/90 shooting. The official draft profile frames point-of-attack defense and mature guard play as his route. NBA draft profile

Fantasy outlook

  • 9-cat: steals and FT% are the interesting traits; low NBA volume and uncertain shooting suppress everything else.
  • Points: no redraft value. Monitor only if Philadelphia loses several guards and converts/signs him.
  • Dynasty: G League watch in very deep formats. An NBA contract would be the first value gate; minutes are the second.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: waived after camp and Delaware-only; zero NBA games.
  • Base: affiliate development plus a short late-season call-up, represented by the conditional 12-game line.
  • Ceiling: wins a two-way opening and becomes a defensive backup after injuries; still unlikely to clear 18 NBA minutes consistently.

Health and uncertainty

No current injury restriction was found. Contract uncertainty overwhelms statistical uncertainty. Miles should not be counted in the 14-standard-plus-two-two-way working roster or the healthy 240 unless his status changes.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Six-year, four-school college career (born February 3, 2002). Troy 2020-23: 49 games, 9.2 PTS, 3.1 AST, 1.8 STL. High Point 2023-24: 33 games, 17.5 PTS, 3.6 AST, 1.3 STL — Big South Newcomer of the Year and first-team All-Big South. Oklahoma 2024-25: 34 starts, 9.4 PTS, 2.0 AST, 1.4 STL in an SEC complementary role. Vanderbilt 2025-26: 16.1 PTS, 4.5 AST, 3.0 REB, 2.6 STL in 28 games on .440/.350/.900, with a 30-point SEC tournament quarterfinal win over Tennessee. Draft: undrafted (2026); agreed to an Exhibit 10 with Philadelphia on June 27, 2026.

No NBA record — his most recent competitive season is 2025-26 at Vanderbilt.

Durability: No injury flags on record; the 28-game Vanderbilt season is his shortest since Troy, and nothing in the file attributes that to health.

Trajectory: The record shows a steal-first guard who scored whenever the level allowed (17.5 at High Point, 16.1 at Vanderbilt) and defended at every stop, entering the NBA old for a rookie at 24. In 2026-27 he competes to survive Philadelphia's camp and anchor Delaware, with a two-way conversion as the realistic best case. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in very deep category formats, and only if he gets an NBA or two-way deal and guard injuries hand him minutes where his 2.6 college steals can translate — the .900 college FT% is the quiet secondary asset.
