Wednesday 23 December · Varenna & Bellagio

The lake, in winter

A committed Varenna–Bellagio day, followed by a buffered evening train from Milan to Venice. The day is fixed; the version of it is decided at seven in the morning.

Decision

Keep Lake Como in the trip, but treat the version — not the day itself — as weather-dependent. These are three parallel states, and exactly one of them is live by 07:00.

Normal plan

Varenna, ferry to Bellagio, a long indoor lunch, and an early return to Milan.

Ferries suspended

Suspended after reaching Varenna: enjoy Varenna, eat at a confirmed-open waterfront restaurant, and return early.

Severe weather

Severe wind, dense fog, snow/ice, or all-day heavy rain: substitute Como city, its cathedral and covered centre, a lakefront walk, and a long lunch. Use Brunate only if the funicular is operating and visibility is worthwhile.

The winter appeal is quiet villages and lake scenery, not villa collecting. Villa Monastero is closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays in December, Castello di Vezio is winter-weekend/holiday only, and the Bellagio villas are out of season.

The chain

Direct Milan–Varenna trains currently take about 64 minutes, run roughly hourly, and cost from €7.40 each way. Official Trenord route

The previous official winter timetable had 15-minute weekday Varenna–Bellagio crossings around 10:40/11:10 outbound and 14:55/15:40 inbound. That is evidence of feasibility, not a promise for 2026/27. The current adult fare is €5.50 each way, and the operator may suspend boats for wind or wave conditions. Prior winter timetable · official timetable and ticket portal

Lunch

First choice: Seta, Bellagio, 12:30. It is central, publishes a season through December 31, and currently lists perch risotto at €25. Budget €40–55 per person for a main, water, and shared wine, or €55–75 for two courses. Reconfirm Christmas-week service in writing in late November. Dining · menu

Value/robust alternative: Nando. Its published Tuesday/Wednesday lunch service is 11:45–16:00; budget about €25–40 per person. Reservation request

If the ferry stops after arrival in Varenna, Bar Il Molo publishes a winter operation and has a covered heated terrace; confirm the exact date before relying on it. Bar Il Molo winter information
