The role

He is the field-stretching Z: the deep threat, targeted less often than a team's alpha but for much longer throws. The scoring profile swings week to week — a quiet three-catch afternoon, then a long touchdown and a hundred-plus-yard game. That variance is a false friend to a fantasy basketball instinct: consistency is not the goal here. This league pays $500 for first and nothing for fourth, so a wide range of outcomes is an asset late in a roster — see How the league is won.

The number

1,186 receiving yards projected (note column, Data/players.csv: field-stretching Z; 79 catches for 1186 yards projected). That is the most yardage of any player in this band, produced on fewer catches than either alpha here — which is the whole archetype in one figure. It is a projection built from FFToday stat lines dated 2026-07-23, not an observation.

The risk

The CSV flags him low risk, so the concern is not his job but the shape of his scoring: yardage pays a tenth of a point a yard while a catch pays a full point, so a Z needs the yards to keep arriving to beat a high-catch receiver, and long plays are the least repeatable thing a receiver does. Houston's other receiver in the file projects far behind him, so nobody is credibly taking his targets. One caution the label does not carry: the projection is priced at a full seventeen games, and he has played 15, 12 and 15 in the last three seasons, so it assumes a season he has not had in three years.

The context

At 27.5 on the September 9 opener, he is the counterexample to the draft-capital rule (Draft capital dominance): Houston took him 89th overall in 2021, in the third round, and outgrew it. He has played 15, 12 and 15 games, 42 of a possible 51: Out twice, Questionable or Doubtful three times, naming calf, hamstring and concussion; seven of the nine games missed fall outside the report, so not medical. In 2025 he ran 75.9 percent of Houston's snaps — a snap is one play (Fantasy football glossary) — catching 71 of 120 targets, 21.7 percent of the throws. The projection asks for 79 catches and 1,186 yards, more than he just did. He is the best-paid of the six, 30 million a year, free agent in 2028.
