2025-26 baseline

Suggs played 57 games at 27.6 minutes, averaging 13.8 points, 3.9 rebounds, 5.5 assists, 1.8 steals, 0.7 blocks, 2.1 threes, and 2.7 turnovers. He shot .435 on 11.3 field goals and .855 on 2.1 free throws.

This was an unusually useful low-scoring fantasy line: the 2.5 combined steals and blocks, five-plus assists, and two threes overcame mediocre FG and turnovers. Yahoo's May exit interview placed him 54th in 9-cat and 71st in High Score, then suggested a top-75 next-season projection with explicit injury concern.

Role and pathway

Suggs is the provisional starting point guard and point-of-attack defender. He does not need Banchero-level usage because his value comes from defensive events, assists, and threes. The base nudges him to 29 minutes but holds scoring near the prior season, accounting for healthy Wagner and the growing Black.

His role has more substitution risk than Bane's but less than Carter's. If Sweeney prioritizes defense and ball pressure, Suggs closes. If the offense needs size and downhill creation, Black can close beside or instead of him. The most fantasy-friendly configuration uses both guards and lets Bane/Wagner/Banchero fill the frontcourt.

Category shape

  • Elite/strong: steals, blocks for a guard, assists, threes.
  • Helpful: FT percentage, rebounds for position.
  • Weak: FG percentage and turnovers.
  • Build fit: punt-FG or guard builds; managers seeking stocks without drafting a center.

The blocks are the differentiator. Even a regression to 0.5 would leave a strong guard profile; a loss of both steals and blocks would expose the modest scoring.

Injury and availability

Suggs underwent season-ending surgery after a 2024-25 left-knee trochlea injury. In 2025-26 he dealt with a right-knee MCL contusion, back strain, and hip contusion while reaching 57 games. The base is not a medical prediction; it recognizes that repeated lower-body and back interruptions justify a wide GP interval. Managers should watch camp participation, back-to-back plans, and whether Orlando describes a minutes ramp.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Gonzaga, one season (2020-21). 30 games, 14.4 points, 5.3 rebounds, 4.5 assists on .503 FG / .337 3P / .761 FT for a 31-1 team that lost the national championship game to Baylor. Consensus second-team All-American, first-team All-WCC, WCC Newcomer of the Year, WCC Tournament MVP; hit the 40-foot buzzer beater to beat UCLA in the 2021 Final Four. Draft: 2021, first round, fifth overall, Orlando Magic. No draft-night trade.

Fantasy history: 2023-24 finished 100th in 9-cat per game — the healthiest season but the lowest-event one, since the assists sat at 2.7. 2025-26 finished 54th in 9-cat and 71st in Yahoo High Score, the split that defines him. 2024-25 per-game rank is not verifiable from the sources checked; the 35-game sample was his best raw line (16.2 points, 2.2 threes, .882 FT) and would have graded well per game, but stating a number would be an estimate. 2021-22 and 2022-23 were outside standard-league value. The points-league shape is materially worse than the category shape in every season: his scoring has never cleared 17, and points formats do not pay for the 1.4-to-1.8 steals and 0.6-to-0.9 blocks that carry his category value.

Durability: 48, 53, 75, 35, 57 games — 268 of a possible 410, or 65 percent. 2021-22: fractured right thumb in November 2021 cost roughly 20 games; right ankle surgery followed the season. 2022-23: right ankle sprain suffered against Atlanta in late October 2022, out indefinitely, plus later absences. 2023-24: the clean year at 75 games. 2024-25: back spasms in January 2025, then a left-knee trochlea cartilage injury diagnosed 2025-02-28 and season-ending arthroscopic surgery in March. 2025-26: Grade 1 right-knee MCL contusion in early January 2026 costing eight games, then back spasms reclassified as a back strain in late February. Four of five seasons under 60 games, and the causes are unrelated to each other — thumb, ankle, knee cartilage, knee ligament, back — which argues against a single correctable fault and for a durable base-rate discount.

Trajectory: The 2023-24 season looks like the leap on the efficiency line (.471 FG, 2.0 threes) but it was the least valuable version of him in categories, because the role was off-ball spacer next to a high-usage Banchero. The actual leap is 2025-26, and it is a passing leap, not a scoring one: per-36 assists went 3.6 (2023-24) to 4.7 (2024-25) to 7.2 (2025-26) while per-36 points fell from 20.4 to 18.0. That is a genuine role change to primary initiator, not a minutes artifact — minutes were flat at 27 to 28 across all three years. The stocks have risen every season since 2021-22 and now sit at 2.5 combined, which at 24 years old is squarely in the athletic prime rather than in decline. What the history implies for 2026-27 is that the per-game profile is real and probably repeatable, and the only open variable is the denominator: he has cleared 60 games once in five tries, so any projection above about 60 is betting against his own record rather than reading it.
