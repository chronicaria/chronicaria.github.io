17:T1e8d,<p>The central object is not a box-score ranking. It is a daily, regularized lineup model built from possession-level play-by-play. A player's coefficient asks: after accounting for teammates, opponents, recency, and the rest of the design, how much did possessions involving this player move a particular scoring-value component? The public product then separates four layers that should never be confused:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Measurement:</strong> <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/rapm-lineup-model/" class="wiki-link">RAPM lineup model</a> estimates what the possession evidence says as of a date.</li>
<li><strong>Interpretation:</strong> <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/six-factor-rapm-decomposition/" class="wiki-link">Six-factor RAPM decomposition</a> splits net value into offensive and defensive shooting, turnover, and second-chance components.</li>
<li><strong>Comparison:</strong> <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/cross-league-rapm-translation/" class="wiki-link">Cross-league RAPM translation</a> maps feeder-league estimates onto an NBA-equivalent scale while preserving a large uncertainty penalty.</li>
<li><strong>Forecasting:</strong> <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/nba-player-forward-projection-model/" class="wiki-link">NBA player forward projection model</a> and <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/nba-team-season-projection-model/" class="wiki-link">NBA team season projection model</a> extrapolate measured ratings, minutes, availability, schedules, and game noise into future distributions.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="research-goal">Research goal</h2>
<p>The project is trying to build a coherent answer to several different questions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Who has added possession value, after adjusting for the other players on the court?</li>
<li>Which parts of that value look like shooting, ball security, second chances, shot defense, forced turnovers, or defensive rebounding value?</li>
<li>How has a player's estimate moved through a career rather than merely from season to season?</li>
<li>What does college or G League performance imply on an NBA scale?</li>
<li>What should a roster be expected to do next season, and how uncertain is that forecast?</li>
<li>Can every public claim be tied to a reproducible artifact, an honest baseline, and an explicit limitation?</li>
</ul>
<p>Those questions require different statistical objects. The daily RAPM curve is <strong>descriptive</strong>. A translated college rating is a noisy NBA-equivalent comparison, not a confident rookie forecast. The orange future trajectory and the 2026-27 standings are <strong>projections</strong>. Minutes and availability affect team wins without changing a player's per-100 rating. <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/rapm-evaluation-and-uncertainty/" class="wiki-link">RAPM evaluation and uncertainty</a> keeps these contracts separate.</p>
<h2 id="three-measured-views">Three measured views</h2>
<p>The player explorer can show three descriptions of the same game day:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pure RAPM:</strong> ridge coefficients shrink toward zero. This is the clean lineup-only reference.</li>
<li><strong>Prior-informed RAPM:</strong> the same possession model can shrink selected component coefficients toward a causal box/EPM-feature prior. Thin samples move more; established players are governed mainly by lineup evidence.</li>
<li><strong>Box prior:</strong> the prior estimate itself, displayed as its own daily curve rather than passed off as lineup evidence.</li>
</ul>
<p>The distinctions matter. A prior can make a rookie estimate more stable without proving that the box features add much net predictive power. The project's own testing finds the daily prior's net edge over pure RAPM small and regime-dependent; its clearer benefit is structural—daily, causal, multi-league, and inspectable.</p>

<ul>

<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/six-factor-rapm-decomposition/" class="wiki-link">Six-factor RAPM decomposition</a> — what the six components mean and why <code>net = sum6</code> is exact.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/rapm-regularization-and-identification/" class="wiki-link">RAPM regularization and identification</a> — what ridge makes estimable, what remains confounded, and what the error bars do not know.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/cross-league-rapm-translation/" class="wiki-link">Cross-league RAPM translation</a> — mover-based affine mappings and the irreducible league-jump variance.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/nba-player-forward-projection-model/" class="wiki-link">NBA player forward projection model</a> — one-to-five-year player trajectories, model gates, and re-decomposition.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/nba-team-season-projection-model/" class="wiki-link">NBA team season projection model</a> — rosters, minutes, availability, game model, and Monte Carlo season outcomes.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="project-boundary">Project boundary</h2>
<p><code>unified-rapm</code> is the source-of-truth research and build repository. <code>andrewjparkus.github.io</code> is a generated checkout containing the public pages and JSON artifacts. <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/basketball/nba-quantitative-modeling/daily-rapm-implementation-map/" class="wiki-link">Daily RAPM implementation map</a> records the boundary: the build may clear and reconstruct the public checkout, and publication occurs only after Andrew reviews, commits, and pushes it.</p>
<p>These notes summarize the working system inspected on July 9, 2026. Where older prose and executable code disagree, the live code path and generated artifact metadata are treated as the current behavior, and the mismatch is called out rather than silently harmonized.</p>
