Range and risks

  • Strengths: assists, pull-up threes and self-created scoring.
  • Liabilities: FG%, low stocks, declining minutes and turnovers.
  • Floor/base/ceiling: waived without a rotation role / zero Memphis minutes / joins a guard-needy team and returns to 24 minutes, roughly 13 points and five assists.

Split seasons: 2019-20 GSW 33 / MIN 12 (traded for Andrew Wiggins at the deadline); 2022-23 MIN 54 / LAL 17 (Westbrook three-teamer); 2024-25 LAL 29 / BKN 29. One All-Star selection (2019, Brooklyn).

Durability: Two knee operations mark the record — left-knee arthroscopy that capped 2017-18 at 48 games and another left-knee surgery in the 42-game 2020-21 season. From 2021-24 he was durable (65, 71, 76 GP). The 26-game 2025-26 in Dallas reflects a collapsed role as much as availability; he made only 3 starts.

Trajectory: The record shows a former No. 2 pick and one-time All-Star whose 18-23 point, 6-7 assist prime (2018-24) has decayed in three straight declining seasons — 12.6 then 10.2 PPG on sub-.410 shooting with vanishing steals. The pull-up three and passing survive; the burst and finishing do not. For 2026-27 he has no Memphis base role and is a leading cut/trade candidate; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if a guard-needy team hands him 20+ second-unit minutes, in which case the 2023-24 Lakers season is the proof-of-concept for an assists/threes streamer with FG% cost.
