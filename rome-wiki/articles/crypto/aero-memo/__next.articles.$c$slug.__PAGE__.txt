<p>Value capture only works through veAERO. veAERO voters direct emissions and receive fees plus external incentives from the pools they vote for. Liquid AERO is an option to lock, not a direct fee claim.</p>
<p>Supply risk is ongoing emissions, not a one-time unlock. Tail emissions around 21 bps/week imply roughly low-double-digit annualized dilution before rebases/team emissions. Base concentration is both moat and fragility.</p>
<h2 id="bull-case">Bull case</h2>
<p>Base keeps growing as a major consumer chain, Aerodrome remains its liquidity hub, fee growth outruns emissions, and AERO expansion creates a multi-chain liquidity layer.</p>
<h2 id="bear-case">Bear case</h2>
<p>Fee capture is emissions-reflexive. If bribes fade, Base share leaks, or another front-end/security event hurts trust, veAERO yield compresses and liquid AERO can reprice hard.</p>
