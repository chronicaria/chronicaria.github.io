Role and evidence

Crawford appears on the current NBA Rockets page but not the 13-player standard contract table. In 2025-26 he played 14 NBA games, averaging 2.0 points and 1.1 rebounds in 6.6 minutes (Basketball Reference).

Through four 2026 Summer League games he averaged 16.0 points, 6.2 rebounds, 2.8 assists, 1.0 steal, and 2.2 blocks in 29.8 minutes (RealGM); NBA recaps recorded 19 points on July 14 and 12 points, eight rebounds on July 16. Those games support a camp case, not a roster fact.

Range, confidence, and reversals

  • Ceiling: a new two-way deal and wing injuries create short NBA appearances with defensive-event upside.
  • Floor: another organization signs him or Houston uses the spot elsewhere.
  • Confidence: roster low; minutes very low; NBA rates low; Summer League defense intriguing but noisy.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Louisiana Tech, five seasons (2019-24) interrupted by two ACL tears in the same knee — 16 games in 2019-20 (8.3 PPG) before the first tear, 32 games in 2020-21 (11.8 PPG, 5.1 RPG, third-team All-C-USA), 3 games in 2021-22 before the second tear, then 13.7 PPG in 2022-23 and a 16.3 PPG, 6.2 RPG, 2.4 APG, 2.1 SPG, 1.7 BPG senior year on .485/.414/.728 that won both C-USA Player of the Year and Defensive Player of the Year. Draft: undrafted (2024); two-way with Sacramento July 3, 2024, then two-way with Houston July 26, 2025.

Durability: The two college ACL tears (2020, 2021, same knee) are the defining risk in the file; since returning in 2022-23 he has strung together four healthy seasons across college, the G League, and two-way NBA duty with no reported knee recurrences.

Trajectory: The record shows a two-way defensive wing whose G-League production has climbed each year (13.0 to 19.3 PPG with real efficiency) while his 29 NBA games remain garbage-time sized. In 2026-27 he competes for a new two-way or camp deal in Houston's wing depth chart — his July 2025 two-way was a one-year agreement, and no new contract is confirmed. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in very deep leagues, and only if he lands a standard or two-way deal and injuries open 15-plus NBA minutes where his steal-plus-block rate from college and Summer League (1.0 steal, 2.2 blocks per game in July 2026) can surface.
