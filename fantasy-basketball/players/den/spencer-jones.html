Spencer Jones

Spencer Jones gave Denver efficient low-usage wing minutes in 37 starts, but he remained a restricted free agent at the cutoff and has little fantasy ceiling unless his next roster gives him another 20-plus-minute role.

Reported baseline

Jones played 64 games, starting 37, and averaged 5.5 points, 3.3 rebounds, 0.8 assists, 0.9 threes, 0.8 steals and 0.5 blocks in 22.1 minutes. He shot 50.4% from the field but only 60.8% at the line.1 Denver converted his contract to a standard NBA deal in February, then issued the qualifying offer that made him a restricted free agent; he remained outside the July 13 signed-roster count.2 3 4

Fantasy profile

  • Helps: FG%, low turnovers, and a balanced low-volume mix of threes/steals/blocks.
  • Hurts: points and assists; poor FT accuracy adds risk if attempts ever grow.
  • Floor: signs elsewhere without a rotation spot or returns below 10 MPG.
  • Ceiling: repeats a 22-25 MPG role and nudges toward one three and one steal, useful only in deeper category leagues.

Role and market uncertainty

Jones' 37 starts were partly created by Denver's severe injury season, not proof of a locked 2026-27 starting path. If both he and Watson return, Denver's wing room becomes too crowded for every prior minute to survive. Roster confidence low, minutes low, rate medium, availability medium-high.
