Role case

Miller appeared in five NBA games in 2025-26, averaging 3.0 points, 0.8 assists and 0.4 steals in 6.6 minutes. The sample is too small to support a meaningful NBA fantasy baseline. ESPN stats, accessed 2026-07-18

San Antonio signed Miller to a two-way contract in February, and that deal remains the third two-way slot alongside Ja'Kobi Gillespie and Maliq Brown. Austin Spurs release, 2026-02-23 The San Antonio Spurs have Devin Vassell, Julian Champagnie, Keldon Johnson, Harrison Barnes and Carter Bryant ahead of him on the wing.

Ceiling and floor

  • Ceiling: earns injury call-ups and offers low-end points, threes and steals in a temporary 15-minute role.
  • Floor: remains in Austin and does not receive another NBA opportunity.
  • Best use: watchlist only in leagues rostering the full NBA/G League ecosystem.
  • Dynasty: no meaningful market value until a standard-contract opportunity appears.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Scarborough, Ontario native; five college seasons. Texas A&M (2019-21): 6.4 PTS/6.3 REB as a freshman, then a 16.2 PTS/8.2 REB sophomore leap. TCU (2021-24): 10.3/6.2, 12.3/6.5, and a senior year of 15.8 PTS, 6.1 REB, 2.6 AST, 1.1 STL on .486/.383/.815 in 34 games — Second-Team All-Big 12. Draft: undrafted (2024). Cut by Dallas in camp, Texas Legends (G League), Bulls two-way December 28, 2024; traded to Cleveland February 1, 2026, released, and signed San Antonio's two-way February 23, 2026.

Durability: No injury flags in college or the pros; the 11-game NBA total is entirely a contract-status artifact.

Trajectory: The record shows a strong-bodied college scorer whose NBA sample (11 garbage-time games across two seasons) proves nothing either way, and whose two-way journey — four organizations in two years — marks him as fringe in the league's own pricing. In 2026-27 he competes with Gillespie and Brown to be San Antonio's most-called-up two-way while five standard-contract wings sit above him. He becomes fantasy-relevant only via an injury wave plus a conversion to a standard deal; until an NBA team gives him 15 consistent minutes, he is watchlist-only in full-ecosystem leagues.
