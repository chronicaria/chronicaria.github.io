Role and evidence

Mogbo averaged 1.5 points, 1.7 rebounds, 0.6 assists, and 0.3 steals in 6.2 minutes over 40 games with Toronto in 2025-26, shooting 59.1% on very low volume (ESPN statistics).

Sacramento signed him to a two-way contract on July 2 and carried him on the official Summer League roster. ESPN lists him fifth at center, behind Sabonis, Raynaud, Cardwell, and Achiuwa, so his NBA minutes require multiple absences or a trade.

Range

  • Ceiling: 7 rebounds, 3 assists, and 1.2 steals in an 18-minute small-ball role.
  • Median: high-energy Stockton forward and situational NBA call-up.
  • Floor: spacing limitations keep him outside the NBA rotation.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four-stop college climb (born October 29, 2001): Independence CC 2020-21 (8.1 PPG, 5.9 RPG), Northeastern Oklahoma A&M 2021-22 (14.3 PPG, 10.3 RPG), Missouri State 2022-23 (8.0 PPG, 7.0 RPG on .589 FG), then San Francisco 2023-24: 14.2 PPG, 10.1 RPG, 3.6 APG on .636 FG — WCC Newcomer of the Year and first-team All-WCC. Draft: 2024, Round 2, Pick 31, Toronto.

Durability: No significant injury history; the drop from 63 games at 20.4 MPG to 40 games at 6.2 MPG was a rotation squeeze in Toronto, not a health story.

Trajectory: The record shows a rookie season that was genuinely interesting — 4.9 rebounds, 2.3 assists, and nearly a steal in 20 minutes, plus a 17-11-10 triple-double against Charlotte in April 2025 — followed by a second season in which Toronto stopped playing him and his role shrank to garbage time. Sacramento's two-way (July 2026) buys the do-everything forward skill set without the shooting. In 2026-27 he competes for call-up minutes fifth on the center depth chart behind Sabonis, Raynaud, Cardwell, and Achiuwa; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if multiple frontcourt absences hand him 18-plus minutes, where his rookie-year rebound-assist-steal shape is the template — the ceiling case the projection above already sketches.
