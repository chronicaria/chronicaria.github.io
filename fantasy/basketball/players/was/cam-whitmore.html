Durability: 47 and 51 games in Houston reflected rotation politics and G League assignments (Rio Grande Valley in both years) more than health. The serious entry is 2025-26: deep-vein thrombosis in the right shoulder diagnosed December 23, ruled out for the season January 15, 2026 — a medical situation, not an orthopedic one, with no public clearance found as of the July 18 cutoff. Trajectory: The record shows one of the best per-minute scorers of his draft class — 12.3 points in 18.7 minutes as a 19-year-old — who has never been given, or fully earned, a stable role: three seasons, three sub-20-minute averages, and assist and three-point numbers that went backward in Washington. In 2026-27 he competes for wing minutes in a crowded Wizards room, strictly conditional on blood-clot clearance. He becomes fantasy-relevant if cleared into 24-plus minutes, where the scoring and FG% scale immediately, most cleanly in points leagues; without clearance he is a zero, which is why the dossier holds him outside the base rotation.
