The role

He is the three-down bell cow: the back who stays on the field on every down, including passing downs, so he collects both carries (handoffs) and catches. In full PPR every reception is worth a full point before a yard is gained, which is why stacking those two income streams on one roster spot is worth more than any receiver's single stream. His note column reads 281 carries + 42 catches projected — carry volume at the very top of the position with a real receiving share attached.

The number

262.3 projected points (proj_points column, Data/players.csv). That is the highest figure among the six players clustered around picks 24 and 25, and it is a projection built from FFToday stat lines dated 2026-07-23 and scored under this league's rules — a model output, not something anyone observed.

The risk

The CSV flags him low risk, the mildest label in the file, and nothing in the data argues with that: Philadelphia's only other back in the file is Tank Bigsby, tagged handcuff, so there is no committee teammate credibly splitting the touches. The honest residual risk is the same one every note here carries — the touch split is a guess about a coaching plan, and the depth chart is not settled until the August 30 roster cutdown. What the availability record adds is not a health warning — 46 of 51 games is the second-best mark of these six — but an age one: he is the only player in this band on the wrong side of 29, and the projection is priced at a full seventeen games, a total he has not played in any of the last three seasons.
