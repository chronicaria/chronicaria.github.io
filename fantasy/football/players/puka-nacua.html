An alpha X for the Rams, the highest-projected receiver on the board and the first non-running-back in the 1.01 tier.

The role

He is his team's clear first option: he lines up wide, draws the opponent's best defender, and gets thrown at regardless of matchup. A target is a throw in his direction, counted whether or not he catches it (see Fantasy football glossary) — and target volume is the most stable input in fantasy football, more stable than efficiency, because it reflects a coaching decision that repeats every week rather than a good afternoon.

The number

116 catches, from the note column of Data/players.csv — tied for the second-most in the file. In full PPR that is 116 points before a single yard, about seven a game from receptions alone, which is the floor underneath everything else he does. This is a projection, built from FFToday stat lines dated 2026-07-23, not a record of anything that happened.

The risk

His risk flag is low, so the exposure is structural rather than personal: he sits about 28 points below the top of the board and carries a week 11 bye, the worst week of the 2026 calendar with six teams idle. Receivers are also charged less for scarcity than backs are — this league starts roughly 31 of them a week against 29 backs — so the board's ordering already reflects the format, not just the player. The personal exposure is availability, which low does not see: he has missed seven games in three seasons and spent seven further weeks listed Questionable or Doubtful, and the projection in front of you assumes seventeen games he has not played since 2023 — see Availability and injury risk.

The context

He is 25.3 at the September 9 opener and was the 177th pick of the 2023 draft, a fifth-rounder out of BYU — the counterexample to Draft capital dominance, a job won rather than handed over. Availability is the story: 17 games in 2023, 11 in 2024, 16 in 2025, seven missed, plus seven further weeks listed Questionable or Doubtful with a knee, an oblique and a hip named. Only one missed game has him listed Out; the other six never appear on the official report, so nothing calls them medical. In 2025 he played 70.5 percent of the Rams' snaps (plays) and caught 129 of 166 targets, 28.6 percent of the throws — thirteen more catches than the projection allows him over a full season. Still on the rookie contract, roughly $1 million a year, free agent 2027.
