Nine-cat: outside the standard-league top 150; threes/steals streamer. Points: C-. Dynasty: 5.0/10.

Role and evidence

In 25 games after joining Detroit, Huerter averaged 8.6 points, 2.8 rebounds, 2.5 assists, and 1.1 steals in 20.5 minutes, per Basketball-Reference. His 29.4% three-point mark in that sample was well below his 36.8% career baseline, so the projection expects shooting regression without restoring a large role.

The NBA offseason ledger lists him among Detroit's re-signings. That raises roster confidence, not minutes confidence: Joe and Robinson are stronger pure shooting bets, Thompson is the defensive closer, and Holland/Okorie need developmental reps. Huerter's passing and competent team defense are the route to staying ahead of a specialist-only role.

Range

  • Ceiling: 25 MPG, two threes, three assists, and one steal for top-130 value.
  • Floor: 14-16 MPG behind Joe/Robinson/Thompson, with no standard-league hold value.

Trade trail: dealt to Sacramento in 2022; to Chicago on February 3, 2025 (for Zach LaVine's outbound package — 43 SAC games at 7.9 PPG, then 26 CHI games at 13.2 PPG); to Detroit on February 3, 2026 with Dario Saric in a three-team Jaden Ivey deal (44 CHI games at 10.9 PPG on .314 3P, then 25 DET games at 8.6 PPG on .294 3P). Re-signed with Detroit on June 29, 2026 — three years, $27 million.

Durability: Mostly durable (69+ GP in six of eight seasons). 2023-24 ended at 64 games with a dislocated left shoulder and season-ending labrum surgery; 2019-20 (56 GP) also carried shoulder trouble. No chronic lower-body flags.

Trajectory: The record shows a 12-15 PPG starter-quality movement shooter at his 2019-23 peak (peaking at 15.2 PPG on .485/.402 in Sacramento) who has slipped into a 22-24 minute connector role with sub-career three-point shooting across two straight trade-season halves. The 2026-27 competition is Detroit's second-unit wing minutes against Joe, Robinson, and Holland; the new three-year deal secures the roster spot, not the role. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he reclaims a ~24-minute role and his 3P% regresses up toward the .368 career baseline — his 2022-23 season proves the top-100 ceiling still exists inside a bigger role.
