    On this page

    A short note, best read straight through.

    Create each as its own note (click the link to spawn it) when you're ready to write it:

    1. Capstone 1 — Action to Equations — derive the classical open-string EOM and boundary conditions.
    2. Capstone 2 — Mode Expansions + — compare open- and closed-string solutions.
    3. Capstone 3 — Light-Cone Gauge + — what's fixed, what remains, why transverse modes are physical.
    4. Capstone 4 — Open-String Spectrum + — build the first states; identify tachyon and photon.
    5. Capstone 5 — Closed-String Spectrum + — level matching; identify tachyon, graviton, BBB-field, dilaton.
    6. Capstone 6 — Critical Dimension + — the light-cone and CFT routes to D=26D=26D=26.
    7. Capstone 7 — D-Branes + — from boundary conditions; strings stretched between branes.
    8. Capstone 8 — T-Duality + — derive R↔α′/RR \leftrightarrow \alpha'/RR↔α′/R; brane dimension change.
    9. Capstone 9 — Worldsheet CFT + — OPEs, Virasoro generators, central charge, vertex operators.
    10. Capstone 10 — Interactions + — why perturbative amplitudes live on Riemann surfaces and moduli.

    Oral-defense prompts to rehearse: derive the open-string mass formula from oscillator data; explain the graviton's appearance in the closed spectrum; compare the two routes to D=26D=26D=26; explain T-duality without saying "small equals large."
