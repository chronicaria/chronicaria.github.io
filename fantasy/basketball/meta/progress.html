Pass 2 — adversarial verification and repair (Claude, 2026-07-18 evening). 39 audit agents, 41 career-profile agents, 24 shot-volume agents, 33 correction agents, 36 wave-two agents, 12 strategy agents — roughly 185 subagents and 15.4M subagent tokens.

Pass 3 — sweeps, news, and format parity (Claude, 2026-07-19). 29 agents: the July 19 Dort/Nembhard/Risacher three-team trade applied across nine files with all three 240-minute models re-verified; a league-wide news sweep (LeBron still unsigned, Cisse offer sheet matched by Dallas, Summer League wraps); a systematic games-played audit of the top 150 producing 19 corrections (Data/gp-overrides.csv) that now flow through both boards and the tiers; the final 185 career records (coverage now 555 of 555); and both remaining strategy notes rebuilt on corrected data. The HTML wiki (Encyclopedia wiki website) regenerates from the vault and passed a 61,000-link integrity check with zero breaks.

What pass 2 established

The factual foundation held. Every roster claim attacked survived independent checking: the Giannis trade to Miami, Trae Young to Washington, LaMelo Ball to Minnesota, the 2026 draft order, Khris Middleton's six-team sign-and-trade return to Washington, Dusty May's hiring in Dallas. Two auditor findings that alleged vault errors were themselves wrong and were rejected. The 2025-26 statistical baselines matched independent sources to the decimal in dozens of checks. A citation sweep of 1,195 of 2,958 unique URLs found exactly one dead link and no fabricated sources.

Working rules

  • Every time-sensitive claim carries an as-of date and a direct source where available.
  • Official roster, transaction, injury, and schedule evidence outranks commentary.
  • A sourced analyst opinion is attributed; it is not silently converted into fact.
  • Player rankings identify format and unit: per-game versus totals, categories versus points.
  • An audit finding is verified independently before it is applied. Rejecting a wrong finding counts as much as applying a right one.
  • A missing input propagates as a missing output, never as a silent default. This is the rule the original engine broke three times.

Known incomplete

  • Career records — complete as of 2026-07-19: 555 of 555 dossiers.
  • Strategy notes on defective data — both rebuilt and re-verified on the corrected export, 2026-07-19.
  • Games-played audit — systematic top-150 sweep completed 2026-07-19; 19 corrections applied through the boards. Below rank 150 the audit has not run.
  • Shot volume beyond the 262-player pool is deliberately not recovered. Players outside the pool fail the 20-game/15-minute gate and receive no rank at all, so proxy attempts in their dossiers cannot distort any board. This becomes worth revisiting only if the pool gate widens.
  • Atlanta must shed two standard contracts after the Dort trade (17 on roster); the ATL minute model's conditional swaps need revisiting when the exits happen.
  • The 2026-27 NBA schedule releases in August 2026 and nothing here reflects it. A pre-draft October re-verification pass remains mandatory.
