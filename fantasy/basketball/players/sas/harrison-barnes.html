Role case

Barnes started 52 of his 77 games in 2025-26 and fell to 9.9 points, 2.8 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 25.8 minutes, shooting 45.6% from the field and 38.8% from three. He still made 1.8 threes per game with low turnovers, but supplied only 0.8 combined steals and blocks. ESPN stats, accessed 2026-07-18

The Spurs brought him back on a reported one-year, $8M deal after adding Tobias Harris and re-signing Julian Champagnie, which is continuity money rather than a workload guarantee. Hoops Rumors, 2026-07-08 The San Antonio Spurs healthy map gives Barnes five power-forward minutes behind Champagnie and Harris, and post-free-agency depth-chart analysis calls him strictly a depth piece — a genuine demotion from 52 starts, and the reason his projection is a shape rather than a level.

Correction 2026-07-18: the assists figure here previously read 0.8; the correct 2025-26 number is 1.9. His 52 starts, which this file did not mention anywhere, are now stated. The projection also changes shape: 70 games at 7.0 minutes was internally inconsistent, since players at that workload appear in 40-50 games as DNP-CD candidates rather than 70. He is now modelled at 50 games and 10.0 minutes, which averages the two realistic states — roughly 18 minutes if a forward absence opens the rotation, roughly nothing if it does not.

Ceiling and floor

  • Ceiling: multiple forward absences restore 20-plus minutes and threes/points streaming value.
  • Floor: out of the normal rotation and relevant only as real-life insurance.
  • Best use: no draft investment outside very deep formats.
  • Dynasty: rosterable only in exceptionally deep contender leagues.

Durability: Elite — 1,070 games in fourteen seasons, four full 82-game years including a start-all-82 season in 2024-25. The only real absences: 16 straight games to a sprained left ankle in 2015-16 and 58-of-72 in the COVID-compressed 2020-21. 2012 No. 7 pick, 2015 champion with Golden State, 2016 Olympic gold medalist. Trajectory: The record shows a two-decade-defining pattern of graceful decline without collapse: a 19-point second option in Dallas at 24, a 15-16-point wing in Sacramento through 30, and now a sub-10-point spacing forward whose only surviving fantasy skills are 1.8 threes, solid percentages, and near-zero turnovers. In 2026-27 he is San Antonio's veteran fourth forward behind Champagnie and Harris on continuity money, a genuine demotion from 52 starts. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if multiple forward absences restore 20-plus minutes, and then strictly as a threes-and-efficiency streamer — the stocks have been absent for a decade.
