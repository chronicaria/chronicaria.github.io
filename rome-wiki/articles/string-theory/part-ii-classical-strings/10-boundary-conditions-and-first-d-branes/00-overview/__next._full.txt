<li><strong>An open string has two ends, and physics has to say something about them.</strong> Coordinate by coordinate, each end is either <em>free to slide</em> (Neumann) or <em>pinned</em> (Dirichlet). The pinned directions cut the endpoint down to a hyperplane; the free directions let it roam that plane. That hyperplane, swept through time, is a <strong>D-brane</strong> — the surface strings live on. You don't put it in by hand; the boundary conditions draw it for you.</li>
