Lajae Jones

Role and evidence

Golden State selected Jones 54th in the official 2026 draft, and both the NBA and ESPN current roster pages list him. A direct standard or two-way signing announcement was not located, so the dossier does not guess at the contract form.

Jones averaged 12.7 points, 5.7 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 1.2 steals, and 1.0 block in 29.5 minutes over 33 Florida State games, shooting .427 FG, .325 from three, and .763 FT (ESPN college statistics). Florida State described a 6-foot-10 wingspan, multi-position defense, and developing three-level offense (Seminoles bio).

The stocks can translate at low usage, but an NBA wing shooting .425 with fewer than one assist is not rosterable at eight minutes. Richard, Santos, Lendeborg, Melton, and eventually Butler/Moody all stand between Jones and a stable role.

Range

  • Ceiling: wins 16-18 minutes through defense, reaches 1.1 steals, and becomes a deep category stream.
  • Median: developmental appearances and substantial G League time.
  • Floor: two-way/camp status with few NBA games or a roster crunch after veteran signings.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four college stops. He barely played as a freshman at Tarleton State (10 games, 6.1 minutes, 3.6 points), spent 2023-24 at Barton Community College where he averaged 15.4 points and 9.2 rebounds and won a junior college national title, then produced as a full-time starter at St. Bonaventure in 2024-25 — 10.8 points, 5.8 rebounds, 1.5 steals and 0.8 blocks in 33.0 minutes on .456/.389/.800 — before transferring to Florida State for 2025-26: 12.7 points, 5.7 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 1.2 steals and 1.0 block in 29.5 minutes on .427/.325/.763. Across 77 Division I games he shot .446/.346/.771 with 1.2 steals and 0.8 blocks. Born May 21, 2004; his mother is Israeli, and he is one of very few Jewish players drafted in recent years. Draft: 2026, second round, 54th overall, Golden State.

Jones has not appeared in an NBA regular season game. His most recent competitive season is the Florida State line above; he played for Golden State in the 2026 Las Vegas Summer League.

Durability: No NBA record. He started 64 of 77 college games and played a full 33-game slate at Florida State, so there is no availability concern in the record.

Trajectory: The profile is consistent across two Division I schools: a 6-foot-7 wing with real defensive activity — better than a steal and close to a block per game at two different programs — attached to a jumper that regressed from .389 at St. Bonaventure to .325 at Florida State on higher-difficulty attempts. That shooting split is the entire question, because a low-usage wing who does not shoot has no NBA role. His contract form was still unsettled as of mid-July 2026, which itself signals Golden State sees him as a development project rather than a rotation addition. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in a narrow case: a two-way converted to standard, a catch-and-shoot percentage that stabilizes near league average, and injuries to the veteran wings ahead of him opening 16-plus minutes where his steals and blocks would carry deep-league streaming value.
