Scope and labels

Cutoff: 2026-07-18, America/New_York. “Offseason” here includes the June 23-24 draft through the cutoff. Only fantasy-relevant additions, departures, unresolved contracts, and role pressure are included; this is not a substitute for the league's complete end-roster transaction log.

  • Official: an NBA or team announcement, or an entry on the league's official trade/transaction tracker.
  • Reported: credible reporting that had not become an official transaction by the cutoff.
  • Inference: this encyclopedia's workload conclusion. It is not a reported lineup, trade discussion, or cut decision.
  • Roster crunch: a count above 15 standard contracts, a reported need to reduce the roster, or a pending contract decision that changes the count.
  • Role crunch: several players competing for the same finite minutes even when the contract count is legal.

The league-wide source spine is the NBA all-team offseason ledger, updated 2026-07-16, official trade tracker, updated 2026-07-17, official draft results, updated 2026-07-10, official options and qualifying-offer ledger, updated 2026-07-02, and NBA free-agent tracker, accessed 2026-07-18. Team sections link those sources again where the date or status is especially important.

Atlantic Division

Boston Celtics

  • Official by July 16: Boston acquired Paul George in the July 6 Jaylen Brown trade, signed Mike Conley and Mitchell Robinson, re-signed Neemias Queta and Ron Harper Jr., and lost Nikola Vučević. Boston also drafted Chris Cenac Jr. at No. 27 on June 23. NBA all-team ledger · official Brown-George trade · draft results
  • Roster pressure: the official transaction audit in Boston Celtics supports 14 standard players after Dalano Banton was waived July 9, leaving an open slot rather than a mandatory cut. Dillon Mitchell and camp-contract players remain unresolved at the fringe.
  • Inference — role change: Brown's departure consolidates more creation in Jayson Tatum, Derrick White, and George when healthy. Robinson and Queta project as a near-even center split; Cenac is the upside development big. Boston's larger squeeze is among its reserve wings, not at the top of the offense.
  • Trade-rumor status: no additional live Boston trade report was strong enough to include by the cutoff. George's health and the final roster slot are uncertainties, not evidence of another move.

Brooklyn Nets

  • Official / reported by July 16: Brooklyn acquired Julius Randle in the July 10 four-team trade, signed Keon Ellis, re-signed Day'Ron Sharpe and Josh Minott, and lost Nic Claxton and Ziaire Williams. Moritz Wagner's two-year agreement remained reported in the NBA ledger. Mikel Brown Jr. arrived at No. 6 in the June 23 draft. NBA all-team ledger · official four-team trade · draft results
  • Roster pressure: Brooklyn Nets reconciles 14 signed standard players plus the reported Wagner agreement, effectively a full 15 if completed. Five 2025 first-rounders plus Brown and other young guards create a development crunch even without an excess contract.
  • Reported: Michael Porter Jr. entered the final contract year and his agent was reported on July 17 to be planning extension talks. That supports both extension and trade branches; it does not establish that Brooklyn is shopping him. Chris Haynes report relayed in Nets discussion, 2026-07-17
  • Inference — role change: Sharpe has the clearest starting-center opening after Claxton's exit. Porter and Randle absorb veteran usage, so Brown, Egor Dëmin, Nolan Traoré, Ben Saraf, and Drake Powell cannot all receive development-star workloads simultaneously.

New York Knicks

  • Official by July 16: New York retained Jose Alvarado, Jordan Clarkson, Mohamed Diawara, and Landry Shamet, added Andre Drummond, and lost Mitchell Robinson and Ariel Hukporti. Its draft-night maneuvering produced Jack Kayil, Tyler Nickel, Nick Martinelli, and future assets rather than a first-round rotation player. NBA all-team ledger · official draft/trade results
  • Roster pressure: no mandatory standard-contract cut was established at the cutoff. The pressure is functional: Alvarado, Clarkson, Shamet, Miles McBride, and the incumbent starters divide a limited guard reserve pool, while Drummond becomes the conventional backup five.
  • Inference — role change: Robinson's exit removes the principal center-timeshare threat to Karl-Anthony Towns and moves Drummond into injury-streamer status. Alvarado adds steals and assists; Clarkson adds bench points but may compress McBride's and Shamet's minutes.
  • Trade-rumor status: no credible active Knicks trade report cleared the evidence threshold. A possible later roster restructuring is a cap/fit inference, not a reported July transaction.

NBA all-team ledger · official Brown-George trade · draft results
  • Roster pressure: Dominick Barlow's option was exercised, while Dalen Terry and Trendon Watford statuses remained part of the back-roster audit. No verified above-15 count was established, but the guard room is crowded.
  • Inference — role change: Brown becomes the high-minute wing beside Tyrese Maxey and Joel Embiid. Simons, Jared McCain, Philon, and the remaining guards create a significant touches crunch; Simons's value is more secure in threes and points than in assists. Hukporti inherits backup-center opportunity after Drummond's exit.
  • Trade-rumor status: no additional 76ers trade was verified. Embiid availability changes usage, but that is a medical branch rather than transaction evidence.

Toronto Raptors

  • Official: Toronto signed Kyle Anderson, re-signed Alijah Martin, drafted Allen Graves at No. 19, and lost Sandro Mamukelashvili. NBA all-team ledger · draft results
  • Reported and explicitly unfinished: Toronto and the Clippers agreed June 30 on Kawhi Leonard for Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, draft picks, seconds, and swaps. On July 9, Toronto announced the trade was on hold until the NBA investigation ends. As of July 18, Leonard remains a Clipper and Ingram/Dick remain Raptors for legal roster purposes. NBA/AP hold report, 2026-07-09

  • Inference — role change: if completed, Leonard becomes the first option and compresses Scottie Barnes/RJ Barrett usage while creating wing minutes after Ingram and Dick leave. If voided or delayed into camp, the pre-trade hierarchy remains. Graves is a development wing in either branch.

League-wide fantasy consequences

  1. The four true count crunches outrank coach quotes. Memphis, Charlotte, Milwaukee, and Atlanta must change their standard rosters; projections should preserve alternative branches rather than pretend every player survives.
