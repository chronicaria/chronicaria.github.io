  • Floor: DNP-CD heavy season.
  • Ceiling: 15 minutes with 5 rebounds and a steal/block blend if two frontcourt players are unavailable.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four seasons at Wyoming (Mountain West), 2011-12 through 2014-15: 11.3 points, 6.6 rebounds, 1.4 assists, 1.1 steals and 1.1 blocks in 123 games. Two-time All-Mountain West and two-time all-conference defense, Mountain West Defensive Player of the Year in 2014-15, and he took Wyoming to its first NCAA tournament since 2002. Draft: 2015, first round, 27th overall, Los Angeles Lakers.

Durability: Never above 67 games in eleven seasons, and below 50 in four of them. The specific causes are documented only for the most recent collapse: 2024-25 fell to 24 games after right hand surgery in December, then a nondisplaced fracture of the right medial femoral condyle suffered February 10 that cost him the rest of the season. The 2020-21 and 2021-22 shortfalls are not verified here as to cause. He returned for 35 games in 2025-26 but at roughly half his usual minutes.

Trajectory: The peak version — 2018-19 through 2020-21 in Cleveland, roughly 9-10 points, 7-8 rebounds, 2-3 assists and 1.5 steals with genuine passing for a big — is gone. The 2025-26 line is the sharpest decline of his career: minutes cut to 12.8, field-goal percentage down to .419 against a .535 career mark, and free-throw shooting at .462. That is the profile of a body that did not come back from the femoral condyle fracture. Indiana signed him July 9 as frontcourt insurance behind Zubac, Huff and small-ball Toppin, which is the fourth big on a healthy night. He is fantasy-relevant only if two of those three miss time simultaneously, and even then the bet is on rebounds and stocks at roughly 15 minutes, not on scoring.
