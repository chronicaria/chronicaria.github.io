<h2 id="the-puzzle-from-the-endpoint">The puzzle from the endpoint</h2>
<p><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/string-theory/part-iv-branes-and-duality/19-string-charge-and-gauge-fields/endpoint-charge/" class="wiki-link">Endpoint charge</a> left a puzzle: string charge flows down a string like current in a wire, but an open string <em>ends</em> on a D-brane, and current cannot just stop. The resolution is that the brane has its own electromagnetism and the endpoint is a point charge in it. The current does not vanish at the endpoint — it converts into brane electric flux and keeps flowing, now spread across the worldvolume. Picture a wire soldered to a metal sheet: the current fans out as a surface current on the sheet rather than disappearing at the joint.</p>
<p>Two facts do the work:</p>
<ol>
<li>The brane carries a photon, which drops out of quantizing the open string.</li>
<li>That photon's field strength is not gauge invariant on its own; only <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi mathvariant="script">F</mi><mo>=</mo><mi>F</mi><mo>+</mo><mi>B</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\mathcal F = F + B</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathcal" style="margin-right:0.0993em;">F</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7667em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1389em;">F</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">+</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0502em;">B</span></span></span></span> is. The <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>F</mi><mo>⋅</mo><mi>B</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">F\cdot B</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.1389em;">F</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span><span class="mbin">⋅</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2222em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0502em;">B</span></span></span></span> cross term in the brane Lagrangian is what ties electric field lines to string charge.</li>
