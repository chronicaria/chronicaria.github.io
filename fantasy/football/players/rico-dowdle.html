The role

He is an early-down committee back: he runs on first and second down and comes off the field when the offense throws. A carry is a handoff (Fantasy football glossary), and carries are handed out by a coach, not earned play by play — which is why this archetype's value is a guess about a depth chart rather than a judgement about a player. Pittsburgh has a second back doing the same job, and the file projects that teammate ahead of him on both carries and catches. See Jaylen Warren.

The number

186 carries projected — from the note column of Data/players.csv (early-down committee back; 186 carries + 30 catches projected). It is a projection dated 2026-07-23, not an observation. That is real early-down volume, close to a starter's workload, and it is the only reason to take him; strip the carries out and there is no receiving role underneath to catch him.

The risk

The CSV flags his risk as high, and the driver is the committee itself. Two backs on one roster projected for nearly identical carry counts is the least stable arrangement in fantasy football, because the split is a coaching decision that will not be legible until the August 30 roster cutdown — and one back losing that argument loses most of his value in a single week. Read that flag narrowly, though: it is about the split, not about the man, who has missed two games in three seasons and took fifty more carries in 2025 than the file projects for him now.

The context

He is twenty-eight on the September 9 opener and was never drafted — no team spent a pick on him in 2020, out of South Carolina. Draft capital usually tells you who a team hands a job to; he has none, so whatever role he holds he took. He is also the most available player in this price band: 16 games, then 16, then all 17 in 2025 — forty-nine of fifty-one, one week listed Out, three Questionable or Doubtful, and an ankle the only part named. In 2025 he played 54.9 percent of snaps (a snap is one play — Fantasy football glossary), took 236 carries for 1,076 yards and caught 39 passes. The projection of 186 carries is fifty fewer than he actually ran. His contract is modest and expires after 2027.
