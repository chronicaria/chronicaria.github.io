Fantasy case

Marković signed a multi-year Milwaukee contract after averaging 18.9 points, 8.9 rebounds, 2.6 assists, 1.0 steal, and 1.0 block in 31.8 minutes as the 2025-26 ABA League MVP. That production is more meaningful for dynasty than typical late-rookie summer output. He can score, rebound, and connect passes rather than functioning only as a rim runner.

The problem is access. Myles Turner, Kel'el Ware, Kyle Kuzma, Ousmane Dieng, Nate Ament, Jericho Sims, and Pete Nance all claim adjacent minutes, and Milwaukee must remove two standard contracts before opening night. A G League or overseas-style development cadence remains possible despite the NBA contract.

Outcomes and category fit

  • Low: outside the final standard roster or almost entirely developmental.
  • Base: brief NBA cameos; dynasty stash only.
  • High: frontcourt trades create 15+ MPG, exposing rebounds and a little of everything.
  • Strengths: rebound rate, touch, multi-category European résumé.
  • Liabilities: uncertain NBA defense, role, pace translation, and three-point volume.

Learning notes

Marković's ABA résumé argues for patience, but not for manufactured rookie minutes. He becomes actionable when Milwaukee's frontcourt count falls or he starts receiving consecutive 15-minute appearances.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Mega Basket / Mega Superbet (Serbia, ABA League) system product, born July 12, 2005. 2023-24: loaned to OKK Beograd, 17.9 PTS / 9.1 REB / 2.1 AST over 28 regular-season games. 2024-25 (Mega, ABA): 30 GP, 29.4 MPG, 13.7 PTS / 6.8 REB / 2.7 AST / 0.8 STL / 0.8 BLK — ABA Top Prospect. 2025-26 (Mega, ABA): 18.3 PTS / 9.1 REB (led league) / 2.5 AST with roughly a steal and a block per game and a league-best 23.8 PIR — ABA League MVP (Dejan Milojević Trophy), back-to-back Top Prospect, All-ABA First Team. 2023 FIBA U18 European Championship gold with Serbia. Draft: 2025, Round 2, Pick 47, Milwaukee Bucks (stashed one season, signed multi-year NBA deal July 1, 2026).

No NBA record — his most recent competitive season is 2025-26 with Mega Superbet in the ABA League.

Durability: No serious injury history on record; carried a 29-32 minute nightly load across back-to-back full ABA seasons.

Trajectory: The record shows a rare stash outcome: instead of plateauing after draft night, he jumped from good-prospect numbers (13.7/6.8) to league MVP at age 20 in a real professional league — scoring, leading the league in rebounding, and connecting passes rather than living on putbacks. That arc is the dynasty argument. In 2026-27 he competes for fifth-big minutes in a Milwaukee frontcourt holding Turner, Ware, Kuzma, Sims and others, with a G League cadence the likely default; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if frontcourt trades or injuries produce consecutive 15-plus-minute NBA appearances, at which point the rebound-plus-touch profile makes him an immediate deep-league add.
