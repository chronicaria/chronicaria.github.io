Team context: Dallas Mavericks · Research cutoff: July 19, 2026.

Correction 2026-07-19: Atlanta traded Risacher to Dallas in a three-team deal completed this morning — the Hawks acquired Luguentz Dort from Oklahoma City (plus Ryan Nembhard from Dallas), and OKC took back three second-round picks to get under the second tax apron. Dallas absorbed Risacher's $13.8M salary into an existing trade exception; his 2027-28 fourth-year option ($17.4M) is the next decision point. Masai Ujiri's front office framed it as a buy-low: under Dusty May he slots next to Cooper Flagg in a long, switchable wing group with PJ Washington and Max Christie, asked to defend and hit spot-up threes rather than create. The role and projection sections below were rebuilt for Dallas; the Atlanta wing-logjam framing is retired. HoopsRumors · NBA.com · Yahoo · FanSided rotation projection

Role and 2025-26 baseline

Risacher averaged 9.6 points, 3.8 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 0.9 steals, 0.5 blocks, and 1.4 threes in 22.4 minutes across 67 games, shooting 45.5% FG and 64.4% FT. His low assists, modest stocks, and poor FT% produced roughly waiver-level nine-category value despite sound real-life defense. Basketball-Reference · Hashtag player page

In Dallas he projects as the primary reserve wing behind Cooper Flagg and Naji Marshall, with a genuine path to closing lineups as a defender. The competition — Biberović, Caleb Martin, a possibly-departing Klay Thompson — is far softer than Atlanta's wing room was.

Fantasy fit and range

  • 9-cat: picks 130-170 in deep leagues; standard-league watch list with a real path this time.
  • Points: similar or slightly later because his defensive impact is undercounted.
  • Dynasty: top-120-to-160 hold and a modest buy-low window — the Hashtag July crowd rank of 150th predates the trade, and the No. 1-pick pedigree now has organizational sponsorship (Ujiri/Schmitz chose him) instead of a franchise that had moved on. Hashtag keeper ranks
  • Floor: 16-18 minutes as a spot-up-and-defend fourth wing if Biberović shoots his way past him.
  • Ceiling: 28 minutes, 13-14 points, two threes, and 1.5 stocks if Thompson is moved and Risacher claims the starting spot next to Flagg.
  • Health: a left-knee bone bruise/contusion cost him 11 games in 2025-26; camp health should be checked.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Signed his first professional contract with ASVEL in June 2023 and was immediately loaned to JL Bourg, also of the French first division. In 2023-24 he played 32 Betclic Elite regular-season games (23 starts) for Bourg, averaging 10.1 points and 3.8 rebounds in 21.9 minutes on .439 FG / .352 3P / .740 FT, and was named French League Best Young Player. He also played Bourg's run to the 2023-24 EuroCup Finals and won the EuroCup Rising Star award. Draft: 2024, round 1, pick 1, Atlanta Hawks.

Durability: 75 games as a rookie, 67 in 2025-26. He missed two games with an ankle injury sustained in the season opener, then lost roughly three weeks around late January to a left knee bone contusion, with Atlanta routing his return through a College Park rehab assignment.

Trajectory: The No. 1 pick has gone backwards on the box score — scoring, minutes and free-throw shooting all fell in year two — while the defensive profile that got him drafted held up. Correction 2026-07-19: the July 19 trade rewrites the third-year setup. Instead of fighting Wiggins, Kispert and Hield for Atlanta's reserve wing minutes, he arrives in Dallas as the buy-low reclamation of a new front office, on a rebuilding-adjacent roster where 22-plus minutes are available immediately and the ask — defend, run, hit spot-up threes next to Cooper Flagg — matches exactly what he already does. The mid-20s-minutes fantasy-relevance threshold the old projection gated behind an Atlanta trade is now his base case within reach.
