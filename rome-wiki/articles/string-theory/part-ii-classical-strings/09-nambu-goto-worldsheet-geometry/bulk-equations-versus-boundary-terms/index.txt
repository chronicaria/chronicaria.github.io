<p>The result cleanly separates two questions:</p>

<li><strong>Interior (bulk):</strong> what equation must the string satisfy at every point? Answer: a local conservation law for spacetime momentum.</li>

<h2 id="d-brane-preview">D-brane preview</h2>
<p>Dirichlet directions pin open-string endpoints to a surface. Module 10 turns this endpoint bookkeeping into geometry in <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/string-theory/part-ii-classical-strings/10-boundary-conditions-and-first-d-branes/neumann-versus-dirichlet-conditions/" class="wiki-link">Neumann versus Dirichlet conditions</a> and <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/string-theory/part-ii-classical-strings/10-boundary-conditions-and-first-d-branes/d-branes-first-appearance/" class="wiki-link">D-branes (first appearance)</a>.</p>
