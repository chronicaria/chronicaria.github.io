Fantasy thesis

Sensabaugh is extension-eligible and enters a contract season, yet Peterson and a healthy Bailey compress his usage. Hashtag ranks him 228th in analyst dynasty. Utah-specific July discussion likewise places him inside the likely top nine but behind the five starters and primary bigs; that is sentiment, not role confirmation. Ceiling: wins 24 minutes and sixth-man scoring volume, becoming a points/threes streamer. Floor: a single-digit healthy-night role and weak defensive stats make him waiver-only.

Career record

Pre-NBA: One season at Ohio State (2022-23), born October 30, 2003, listed at 6-6, 235. He averaged 16.3 points, 5.4 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 24.5 minutes across 33 games on .480/.405/.830, coming off the bench for much of the year, and was named third-team All-Big Ten and to the Big Ten All-Freshman Team. Florida Mr. Basketball in 2022. Draft: 2023, first round, 28th overall, Utah.

Durability: 32, 71 and 75 games. The rookie total was suppressed by rotation status and Salt Lake City assignments rather than a single injury, and availability has climbed since — 146 of a possible 164 games over the last two seasons.

Trajectory: A clean three-year scoring progression, and the efficiency moved with it: 39.0% from the field as a rookie, then 45.9% and 46.0%, with three-point volume settling at 2.2 makes a game and free-throw shooting in the mid-80s. He posted a 43-point game against Chicago on January 14, 2026. The problem for fantasy is the shape, not the scoring — 3.1 rebounds, 1.9 assists and 0.9 combined stocks in 23.5 minutes means the entire value is points, threes and FT%, and 1.7 turnovers eats into it. He enters a contract year as a rookie-scale extension candidate on a rebuilding roster that has added Darryn Peterson and expects a healthy Ace Bailey, both of whom want the same wing possessions. He is a points-league and threes streamer at the projected 18 minutes, and a genuine top-150 nine-cat piece only if he gets back above 25 minutes — which realistically requires Utah trading a wing or Bailey missing time again.
