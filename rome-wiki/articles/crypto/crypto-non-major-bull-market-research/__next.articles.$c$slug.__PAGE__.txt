<h2 id="research-question">Research question</h2>
<p>Which liquid non-major token combines real usage, defensible value capture, manageable supply risk, workable access, and asymmetric upside without requiring narrative-first reasoning?</p>
<h2 id="current-hierarchy">Current hierarchy</h2>
<ol>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/briefings/081-jup-briefing/" class="wiki-link">JUP</a> — the only tiny-only capital candidate today, but not eligible for size-up.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/briefings/148-pendle-briefing/" class="wiki-link">PENDLE</a> — the strongest product-economics lead if revenue, emissions, access, and admin gates align.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/briefings/166-syrup-briefing/" class="wiki-link">SYRUP</a> — the best credit/RWA watch if buyback accounting and borrower quality become sufficiently transparent.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/briefings/098-aero-briefing/" class="wiki-link">AERO</a> — a strong fee engine whose liquid-token economics remain inferior to active locked exposure.</li>
<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/briefings/092-pump-briefing/" class="wiki-link">PUMP</a> — an event watch with meaningful upside and equally meaningful unlock, legal, and market-structure risk.</li>
</ol>

<li><a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/aero-memo/" class="wiki-link">AERO memo</a> and <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/crypto/aero-deep-memo/" class="wiki-link">AERO deep memo</a></li>

<h2 id="method">Method</h2>
<p>The committee treats market screens as leads, not proof. It rejects token theses that confuse protocol success with passive-token capture, discounts active lock/stake mechanics when evaluating liquid ownership, and requires wallet-visible evidence for buyback, burn, or distribution claims.</p>
<h2 id="open-questions">Open questions</h2>
<ul>
<li>Which metrics should an hourly committee monitor without reacting to noise?</li>
<li>What evidence should automatically demote or retire a candidate?</li>
<li>When does an active strategy belong in a separate dossier from the liquid token?</li>
