Why the line looks like this

Furphy averaged 5.1 points, 4.4 rebounds and 1.2 assists on 47% FG before tearing his right ACL February 8; the diagnosis ended his season. A normal ACL range points to an in-season return, but no official July target or contact clearance existed. Oubre, Nesmith, Walker and Sheppard also crowd the recovery path. Trajectory: The record shows a young wing whose second-season role jump (7.6 to 18.4 MPG, with real rebounding for his size) was earning trust before the ACL tear cut it off; the ugly .486 FT% and modest three-point accuracy say the offensive skill is still raw. In 2026-27 he re-enters behind Oubre, Nesmith, Walker and Sheppard on a managed ramp, likely with G League conditioning stints. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he is cleared by early winter and injuries above him open an 18+ minute wing role, at which point he offers deep-league rebounds and scattered threes.
