Why a ledger and not a footnote

Everything in this encyclopedia decays at a different rate. League rules do not move. Projections move slowly. ADP moves weekly and then jumps. Depth charts resolve in a single day at the end of August. A note that mixes all four without saying which is which reads as uniformly authoritative when it is not.

So the rule is: every input gets a row, with a retrieval date and a next-check trigger. If a row cannot be filled in, that is itself the finding — see the not obtained rows below, which are as important as the ones that were.

The re-check calendar

Checks are hung on named events, not intervals, because football information does not arrive at a steady rate. It arrives in lumps.

Event Approximate date Why it matters
Padded practices / first preseason games Early August First useful role, route, third-down, and goal-line evidence; reconcile receipts
Second preseason game Mid-August The one where starters actually play a series or two. Watch who lines up with the first-team offense, not what they do
53-man cutdown 2026-08-30 The largest depth-chart resolution of the year. Every team cuts to its final roster in a day, repricing dozens of backup running backs and third receivers at once
Final preseason week Early September Depth-chart position and injury news only. Ignore the box scores entirely
Draft − 48h TBC Confirm exact date; pull fresh stat lines and 10-team ADP; rebuild data, board, tiers, and simulation

The August 30 fork changes the plan's shape. Drafting before the cutdown means a board built on depth charts that will churn immediately afterward — which promotes the in-season waiver layer to load-bearing and argues for late-round upside over insurance picks. Drafting after it means a stable board where the last rounds are worth spending on known backups.

One standing rule against the classic August trap: do not react to preseason statistics. Starters play one or two series. The leaders in preseason rushing yards are, disproportionately, players who will not survive the cutdown. What August produces is depth-chart position and injury news. It does not produce box scores worth reading.

What this ledger commits to

If a row above is stale at draft time and has not been re-pulled, the notes that depend on it are stale too, and the honest move is to say so rather than to draft from them. That is the same rule as everywhere else in this build: a missing input propagates as a missing output, never as a silent default — Evidence and confidence.
