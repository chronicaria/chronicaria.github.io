Career record

Pre-NBA: Joventut Badalona youth system from age 14 (2022-24, four senior-team appearances). Two NBL Next Stars seasons with the New Zealand Breakers: 2024-25 — 25 GP, 9.6 PTS, 4.7 REB as a 17-year-old (youngest player in NBL history to record a double-double); 2025-26 — 30 GP, 10.9 PTS, 5.5 REB, 1.6 AST, 1.0 BLK in 24.4 MPG per Memphis's official release (the NBL's own all-competition line runs higher: 11.9/6.1/1.9 with 1.2 STL in 25.6 MPG), with a career-high 32 points vs Melbourne United and an Ignite Cup title. His 358 points in 2025-26 were the most ever by a draft-eligible Next Stars player. Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 21, Detroit Pistons — rights traded to Memphis on draft night; first Mexican-born first-round pick in NBA history.

No NBA record. Most recent season: 2025-26 with the New Zealand Breakers (NBL).

Durability: 25 then 30 NBL games — no missed-time pattern before the July 2026 leg issue (conflicting calf/hamstring reports, no official diagnosis) that kept him out of Summer League.

Trajectory: Two NBL seasons show a teenager whose production climbed against grown professionals — scoring, rebounding and blocks all up year over year — which is the profile that historically translates. In 2026-27 he is a development forward with zero base-rotation minutes in a crowded Memphis frontcourt, likely splitting time with the G League affiliate. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in dynasty (patient stash) or if late-season frontcourt injuries hand him ~18 MPG, where threes-plus-blocks is the category shape to watch; the unresolved leg injury is the first checkpoint before any of that.
