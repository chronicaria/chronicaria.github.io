Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: top-105-to-150 upside, strongest in builds needing threes and assists without punting FT%.
  • Points: top-115-to-155; modest rebounding/stocks keep him below the best Year 2 breakouts.
  • Dynasty: top-85-to-130. His 6-foot-8 creation profile is more valuable long-term than his current box score.

Role, floor and ceiling

Brown's arrival shifts Dëmin toward a jumbo secondary-handler role, which can lower turnovers and raise catch-and-shoot quality while shaving assists. Floor: 24 minutes and a repeat of his rookie line. Ceiling: 31 minutes, 15/5/5 with 2.5 threes and top-90 category value.

Risk monitor

Brooklyn ended his rookie season because of increased left-foot plantar fasciitis. Official medical update, 2026-03 The Summer League return is encouraging but does not eliminate recurrence risk.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Real Madrid system, then BYU (one season). He appeared in one ACB game for Real Madrid's senior side in 2022-23 (0.9 minutes) and played mostly for Real Madrid B in 2023-24, averaging roughly 13.0 points and 5.1 rebounds over 21 games; he won the Euroleague Next Generation Tournament in 2023 and 2024. At BYU in 2024-25 he started all 33 games and averaged 10.6 points, 5.5 assists, 3.9 rebounds and 1.2 steals in 27.5 minutes on .412/.273/.695 shooting, making the Big 12 All-Freshman Team. The shooting split is the key pre-NBA datum: he was a 27.3% college three-point shooter who has since shot 38.5% in the NBA on far higher volume. Draft: 2025, first round, 8th overall, Brooklyn Nets.

Fantasy history: 2025-26 season-end nine-category rank is not verifiable from public season-end raters, and he would have been ineligible for most full-season leaderboards anyway at 52 games. On a per-game basis the shape is legible without a rank: elite three-point volume for a rookie (2.4 makes), strong FT% on low attempts, positive assists, and a FG% that is a net negative at .399. Points-league value trails category value because the profile leans on threes and percentages rather than counting stats, and his rebounding and blocks are thin for a 6-foot-8 player.

Durability: 2025-26: 52 GP (45 starts) of 82. The plantar fasciitis in his left foot was first diagnosed after the 2025 Las Vegas Summer League and recurred through the season; he last played February 27, 2026, and Brooklyn ruled him out for the remainder of the season on March 9, 2026, then had him undergo a non-surgical procedure. The absence was a single continuous shutdown rather than scattered games, which is the more concerning pattern for a chronic soft-tissue condition. He was a full participant in July 2026 Summer League.

Trajectory: The rookie year's headline is a shot-diet transformation that the college record did not predict — 27.3% on 2.6 threes a game at BYU became 38.5% on roughly 6 attempts a game in the NBA, including a rookie-record 34 straight games with a made three. That is either a genuine mechanical fix or the largest single-season shooting outlier in his file, and 52 games is not enough sample to settle it; treat the 2026-27 three-point projection as the widest error bar in his profile. The assist rate moved the other way: 5.5 per game at BYU on primary-handler usage fell to 3.3 in a role where he was mostly playing off Brooklyn's other creators, so his playmaking value is currently role-suppressed rather than skill-limited. At 21 he is on the steepest part of the curve and any minutes increase should compound, but the foot is the governing variable — plantar fasciitis that has already recurred once across a calendar year tends to recur again, and his 2026-27 value is best modeled as a strong per-game line discounted by a real chance of another 15-to-25-game absence.
