Scope and evidence legend

This map was checked on 2026-07-18 and focuses on publicly visible English-language X and Bluesky accounts relevant to redraft rankings, dynasty, rookies, projections, draft strategy, rotations, injuries, and transaction fallout. Post dates below are publication dates; Bluesky timestamps were exposed in UTC, while X displayed UTC through the public extraction layer.

Profile biographies are mutable and normally undated. A specialty inferred only from a bio is therefore recorded as self-description, not independently verified employment or expertise.

  • Verified fact: directly observable publication metadata, an official team/league statement, or a result that can be checked against a primary record.
  • Reported fact: attributed news from a reporter or news service; use provisionally until an official transaction, injury report, or team statement confirms it.
  • Analyst opinion: a ranking, sleeper, fade, player comparison, or interpretation.
  • Projection: a forward-looking numerical or role claim, including minutes, starts, availability, or games played.
  • Rumor/speculation: a possible transaction, landing spot, or role without confirmation.
  • Inference: a conclusion drawn in this research pass, such as an account's apparent emphasis or cross-platform activity pattern.
  • Stale/undated: useful historical context that should not be presented as current 2026-27 guidance.

The practical rule is simple: a social post can establish that an analyst held a view on a date; it cannot by itself establish that the view is true.

Executive map

  • X is the live draft room. On July 14-18, 2026 it exposed rough overall ranks, format-specific top 25s, auction values, expert mocks, sleepers, fades, rookie boards, and explicit work-in-progress projection updates.
  • Bluesky has a real but narrower active core. Josh Lloyd, SportsEthos, RotoWire NBA, Mark C, Raphielle Johnson, Adam Stock, and Athlon had usable 2026 activity. Many other analysts maintained recognizable profiles but had not posted current fantasy material there.
  • The ecosystem is highly collaborative. Two dated expert mocks connect most of the independent dynasty and redraft accounts; organizational networks such as RotoWire, SportsEthos, Basketball Monster/Locked On, Old Man Squad, Rotoworld, Yahoo, and RotoBaller supply the durable hubs.
  • Rankings are not yet consensus projections. Several July boards are explicitly rough, early, or personal. They are valuable for disagreement analysis, not as silent inputs to a single rank.
  • The alert layer and the analyst layer serve different jobs. BBMAlerts, Underdog NBA, FantasyLabs NBA, RotoWire NBA, SportsEthos blurbs, and beat reporters are useful for event detection. Rankings and minutes should be recomputed after the underlying event is verified.
  • Cross-platform presence does not imply cross-platform freshness. Alex Barutha, Adam King, NBA Fantasy Bible, Dizzle Dynasty, Noah Rubin, and FantasyBall365 were active on X but had older Bluesky fantasy samples.

Bots, mirrors, aggregators, and false-authority hazards

  • Shams Charania Tweets clearly describes itself as a bot. Its 2026-07-18 Moussa Cisse post is a useful accessibility mirror, but the source remains Shams/ESPN and the bot is not an official Bluesky identity.
  • Inside NBA News says it reposts Underdog and other sources. It is an active secondary wire, not primary reporting.
  • longtail-news.bsky.social, some-news.bsky.social, and rssfeedaccount.bsky.social surfaced many fantasy links in search. Their posts are automated syndication/discovery artifacts; cite the underlying publisher and byline.
  • NBA Injury Alerts had only a handful of posts and explicitly said on 2026-05-21 that it was testing for 2026-27. Its test messages included contradictory “out” and “back” states minutes apart. Do not ingest it as evidence.
  • rawnba.bsky.social and rawchili.bsky.social published many exact-match 2026-27 headlines. The social layer did not expose a stable analyst identity or methodology in this pass, so these are discovery-only until the underlying byline and sourcing are verified.
  • athlonsports.bsky.social produced unusually high volumes of brief fantasy headlines. That is not proof of low quality, but it makes byline, duplicate-content, and methodology checks mandatory before using any claim.
  • Two similar Bluesky handles appeared for Underdog NBA. underdognba.bsky.social had the relevant English alert history; underdog-nba.bsky.social presented as a Portuguese repost account. Similar names are not identity verification.
  • X display names and handles can change. Direct status URLs are more durable evidence than copied display names, but deleted or protected posts can still disappear.

Recommended monitoring lanes

For the encyclopedia, follow separate lanes rather than one undifferentiated feed:

  1. Primary events: NBA/team transaction logs, official injury reports, team PR, and official box scores.
  2. Original reporting: Shams Charania, Jake Fischer, Marc Stein, Michael Scotto, Brett Siegel, Keith Smith, and current team beats.
  3. Alerts: BBMAlerts, Underdog NBA, FantasyLabs NBA, RotoWire NBA, SportsEthos, and Rotoworld.
  4. Redraft ballots: Barutha, Lloyd, Titus, Adam King, NBA Fantasy Bible, FantasyBall365, Day To Day, Elite Fantasy Basketball, RotoWire, and SportsEthos.
  5. Dynasty/rookie ballots: Rubin, Lawson, Mark C, Dizzle, Angle, Lloyd, DynastyHoopsHQ, RotoBaller, and Court Consensus.
  6. Model/tool audits: Basketball Monster, Hashtag Basketball, Dynatyze, Breakout, CourtDepth, and any public projection files.
  7. Film and role context: Raphielle Johnson, beat reporters, prospect analysts, and team-specific film accounts.

Every imported take should retain author, platform, post date, format, unit, link, claim type, and superseded-by fields. That is the minimum needed to distinguish a July gut-check top 100 from a finalized category projection or a reporter's corrected injury update.

Access and indexing gaps

  • X logged-out access is incomplete. Direct X pages frequently returned no readable text. Public post/profile extraction and indexed search exposed many items, but cannot guarantee deleted posts, edits, quote-post context, replies, lists, Spaces, broadcasts, or protected accounts.
  • X search is not an archive. Results are ranked and paginated, and different queries surface different posts. “No match” means no match in the sampled public result set, not proof that an account never posted on the subject.
  • Private feeds were not readable. BBMAlerts on X is private; subscriber Discords, Patreon posts, paid rankings, and private FBI/SportsEthos/Basketball Monster discussion cannot be represented fully.
  • Images, polls, and broadcasts conceal data. Some rankings exist only in an attached image, live broadcast, or linked video. The social post can establish publication, but not the complete ordering or assumptions.
  • Bluesky identity is uneven. Most relevant profiles had no platform verification marker. Domain handles and cross-links help, but bios remain self-asserted. Unofficial mirrors were separated explicitly.
  • Cross-post lag is material. Several recognized analysts are active on X and dormant on Bluesky; Underdog showed the reverse problem of an apparently abandoned Bluesky mirror while X remained active.
  • Account renames and job changes create stale edges. Raphielle Johnson explicitly directs followers away from X; other network bios may lag current employment.
  • Following/follower graphs were sampled, not exhaustively crawled. Connections here are based mainly on explicit mentions, collaborations, organizational bios, and representative follow lists—not every reciprocal follow.
  • English-language bias remains. Turkish, French, Portuguese, Greek, Filipino, and other fantasy communities surfaced, but were not deeply audited for 2026-27 rankings or translation accuracy.
  • The calendar is early. July rankings precede training camp, finalized rosters, reliable public ADP, official depth information, and most full projection releases. Treat all role and minute statements as provisional.
