Fantasy value

  • Nine-category: deep-league steals watch; low creation and an uncertain contract keep him off ordinary redraft boards.
  • Points: defensive versatility does not translate as strongly unless the scoring system heavily rewards stocks.
  • Dynasty: late rookie-draft stash with a 3-and-D rotation ceiling.
  • Floor/base/ceiling: rights-only or affiliate development / intermittent call-ups / a rebuild-driven 18-minute wing role that supplies threes, rebounds, and one-plus combined stocks.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Trinidad-born pro since 16. Fuenlabrada, Spain (2021-23; 4.8 PTS / 3.5 REB in about 18 MPG in ACB 2022-23, including a 24-point game as an 18-year-old). Mexico City Capitanes, G League (2023-24; 50 GP, 8.3 PTS / 5.8 REB in 25-plus MPG). South East Melbourne Phoenix, NBL Next Star (2024-25: 6.8 PTS / 4.1 REB / 1.0 AST; 2025-26: 7.1 PTS / 4.3 REB / 2.2 AST / 1.2 STL / 0.6 BLK on 46-39-80 regular-season shooting). Draft: 2026, Round 2, Pick 60, Washington Wizards; rights traded to Milwaukee on draft night.

No NBA record. Most recent season: 2025-26 with South East Melbourne (NBL), roughly 7/4/2 with 1.2 steals in about 21 minutes.

Durability: Missed about a month of NBL26 with a pneumothorax and a hamstring injury; otherwise durable, including a 50-game G League season at 19.

Trajectory: The record shows a defense-first wing who has been playing pro basketball against grown competition for five years and is still only 21 — the shooting climb from ACB bench player to 39% NBL three-point shooter is the meaningful development. He has never carried offensive volume anywhere, so the fantasy case rests entirely on stocks plus threes. In 2026-27 he competes for a two-way or rights-stash outcome with Milwaukee, with zero healthy-rotation minutes. He becomes fantasy-relevant only in dynasty formats, and in redraft only if a rebuild-tilted Bucks season hands him roughly 18 wing minutes, where one-plus stocks with a corner three is the deep-league line.

Career sources: Wikipedia, NBL player page, NBA draft profile, Basketball.com.au Next Stars profile.
