Career record

Pre-NBA: Born February 10, 2005 in Rostov-on-Don; a 6'8" guard who came up through Lokomotiv Kuban's academy. In 2023-24 he played for the reserve side Lokomotiv Kuban-2, averaging 16.1 points, 7.0 rebounds and 4.3 assists, and was first-team All-VTB United Youth League after taking Youth League Supercup MVP in 2023. He joined the senior roster in September 2024. His 2025-26 VTB United League season is his only meaningful top-flight body of work: roughly 8.7 points, 4.6 rebounds, 2.0 assists and 1.3 steals in about 24 minutes, with sources listing 43 to 48 games and shooting splits in the range of .516-.528 FG / .451-.463 3P / .781-.801 FT. He was a VTB United League All-Star and the league's 2025-26 Young Player of the Year. Draft: 2026, round 2, pick 56, Chicago Bulls; rights traded immediately to Dallas via the Lakers.

Ishchenko has never appeared in an NBA regular-season game. His only Dallas action to date is the 2026 Summer League: 4 games, 28.0 minutes, 8.3 points, 4.8 rebounds, 2.0 assists, 1.3 steals, 0.8 blocks on 33.3% shooting.

Durability: Nothing to report. He played a full VTB season in 2025-26 with no publicly noted absences.

Trajectory: A 21-year-old big guard whose senior-level production is solid but unremarkable — the case rests on size, defensive activity and the efficient shooting line, not on volume. Dallas holds his rights rather than a contract, and the expectation is another season developing overseas rather than any 2026-27 NBA role. He becomes relevant to dynasty rosters only if he signs an NBA deal, and to fantasy only well beyond that; the near-term thing to watch is whether Dallas negotiates a buyout or leaves him in Krasnodar.
