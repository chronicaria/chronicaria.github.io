Cross-cutting lessons

The episode is most useful as a role-price map. It rarely argues that a player is universally good or bad. The recurring questions are whether a player starts, whether a prior surge came only through teammate injuries, whether per-minute production survives a larger role, and whether a specialist fits the drafter's category build.

Its strongest actionable thresholds are Day'Ron Sharpe near pick 100 before a confirmed 28-minute role, Coby White only after the top 70–80, Caleb Wilson around 80–100 as a rookie-upside bet, Jamal Murray outside first-round pricing, and De'Anthony Melton after pick 120. These are attributed July opinions, not observed ADP.
