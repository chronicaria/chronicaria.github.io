Role and evidence

Portland signed Williams to a multi-year extension June 30 after he averaged 6.7 points, 7.0 rebounds, 1.0 assist and 1.5 blocks in 17.1 minutes over 59 games. It was his highest games-played total since 2021-22. Trail Blazers, 2026-06-30

He backs up Donovan Clingan and is likely to sit some back-to-backs. Portland can preserve him because it also rosters Yang Hansen, Branden Carlson and Micah Potter. Hashtag ranks Williams No. 163 in dynasty. Hashtag Basketball, updated 2026-07-02

Fantasy value

  • 9-cat: top-100 per-game upside in punt-FT, but picks 125-160 in totals-conscious drafts.
  • Points: top 125-160.
  • Dynasty: top 140-180, contender specialist rather than a rebuilding asset.

Range

  • Ceiling: 18 minutes, 7/7 with 1.6 blocks and elite FG.
  • Floor: fewer than 40 games or 12 minutes behind a healthy Clingan.
  • Swing variables: back-to-back policy, knee/back status and whether Portland ever pairs its centers.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Texas A&M, two seasons (2016-17 and 2017-18), SEC. Freshman year 11.9 points, 8.2 rebounds, 2.5 blocks on .558 from the field and .590 from the line, winning SEC Defensive Player of the Year and second-team All-SEC. Sophomore year 10.4 points, 9.2 rebounds, 2.6 blocks on .668 from the field and .471 from the line, repeating as SEC Defensive Player of the Year and making the SEC All-Defensive team. The profile has never changed: elite rim finishing and shot-blocking, no shooting range, and a free-throw stroke that was already broken in college. Draft: 2018, first round, 27th overall, Boston. Traded: October 1, 2023 to Portland with Malcolm Brogdon and two future first-round picks for Jrue Holiday.

Fantasy history: season-end 9-category ranks are not verifiable from public sources for any season of his career and are not estimated here. The shape is unambiguous regardless of rank: a .720 career field-goal percentage and 1.6 blocks are two of the most valuable per-game categories in the format, and a .661 career free-throw percentage is one of the most damaging, which makes him a punt-FT specialist rather than a general-purpose asset. 2021-22 is the only season in which he was a startable standard-league player on a full-season basis. Hashtag currently ranks him 163rd in dynasty. In points leagues he is materially worse than his category profile, because blocks and field-goal efficiency are the two things points formats undervalue most and his raw scoring has never cleared 10.0.

Durability: 32 GP, 29, 52, 61, 35, 6, 20, 59. The named events: turf toe plus a sprained ankle in the 2021 playoffs; a torn left meniscus on March 28, 2022 with surgery and a 4-6 week timeline; a 2022-23 season begun late while still working back from that knee; a dislocated patella on November 5, 2023 that required surgery and ended his first Portland season after six games; a concussion evaluation on November 25, 2024; and left knee inflammation requiring arthroscopy on March 20, 2025, ruling him out another 4-6 weeks. The 2025-26 rebound to 59 games is the single most important fact in the file, and it came with an explicit cost — Portland did not play him in a single back-to-back all season. That is not a player who got healthy; that is a player whose availability is being purchased with a permanent 15-to-20-game haircut.

Trajectory: The peak is five years behind him. 2021-22 was the real season — 29.6 minutes, 10.0 and 9.6 with 2.2 blocks on .736 shooting, a top-tier defensive big playing starter minutes — and everything since has been the knees taking it back a piece at a time. The per-36 line tells the story cleanly: 12.2 points and 11.7 rebounds in 2021-22 against 14.1 and 14.7 in 2025-26, meaning the per-minute production is not merely intact but nominally better, while the minutes themselves have fallen from 29.6 to 17.1. He is still an excellent basketball player in the possessions he plays; he is simply not permitted to play many of them. At 29 in October 2026, behind Clingan, with Yang Hansen and two other centers on the roster and a front office that has just paid him $44 million over three years precisely to keep him fresh, there is no realistic path to a minutes increase. The new contract entrenches the management rather than loosening it. Treat the 59 games and 17 minutes as the ceiling of the role, not the floor.
