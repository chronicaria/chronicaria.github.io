Barnhizer has zero simultaneous minutes in the 240-minute healthy model. Redraft/points: no draft value. Dynasty: very deep hold only; a defensive/rebounding connector needs credible shooting to force conversion.

Why the line looks like this

Barnhizer appeared in 40 Thunder games as a rookie, averaging 1.7 points and 2.0 rebounds in 8.8 minutes while shooting 38.0% overall and 29.2% from three. His 21 Blue games were much richer: 18.2 points, 8.8 rebounds, 4.3 assists and 1.62 steals in 33.1 minutes. Oklahoma City re-signed him to a two-way July 3.

That G League breadth supports a future connector archetype; it does not move him ahead of Jalen Williams, Dort, Caruso or Kenrich Williams. Sources: official two-way release and Blue stats, 2025-26 Thunder stats.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: remains a Blue producer whose shot never translates.
  • Base: 18 NBA appearances at six appearance minutes.
  • Ceiling: wing injuries create 15 minutes and his rebounds/assists/steals make him a deep-league streamer.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Northwestern, four seasons (2021-25): 1.7 PPG in 11 games as a freshman → 7.6/4.9 → 14.6/7.5/2.6 as a junior starter → 17.1 PTS, 8.8 REB, 4.2 AST, 2.3 STL, 1.1 BLK in 17 games as a senior before a season-ending foot injury (January 2025). Career: 96 GP (51 starts), 11.1/6.1/2.2, 1,067 points; first Big Ten player since Michael Redd with 1,000+ points, 500+ rebounds and 200+ assists inside his first 96 games. Draft: 2025, Round 2, Pick 44, Oklahoma City Thunder.

G League (OKC Blue, 2025-26): 21 games, 33.1 MPG, 18.2 PTS, 8.8 REB, 4.3 AST, 1.6 STL. Re-signed to a two-way July 3, 2026.

Durability: The college red flag is the foot — a lingering injury aggravated in January 2025 ended his senior year at 17 games. He answered it as a rookie by staying available for 40 NBA and 21 Blue games with no reported recurrence.

Trajectory: The record is consistent at every level: broad box-score production (rebounds, assists, steals) with a shooting question that followed him from Evanston (.292 NBA 3P as a rookie). In 2026-27 he competes for the first wing call-up behind Jalen Williams, Caruso and Kenrich Williams — a queue that Dort's trade to Atlanta just shortened. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if wing injuries hand him ~15 NBA minutes and the three reaches league-average credibility; the stocks-and-glass base would do the rest in deep leagues.
