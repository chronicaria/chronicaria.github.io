Two steps, and what they produce

$definition

Every round produces a ledger: kill credit, death debit, plant, defuse, and a share of the clock drift, all of them in round win probability points. MWPA does two things to that ledger and nothing else.

Centring. A player's credit in a round has the mean credit of everyone else credited in that round subtracted from it. The number of players credited is whatever the round had, so a four-against-five round centres on four others rather than on an assumed nine. After centring, the credits in a round sum to zero exactly, which is the property that makes a lobby's surplus impossible to bank.

Leverage. The centred credit is then multiplied by the round's marginal match value: how much one round of win probability is worth to the match, evaluated at the score entering the round. A round at twelve apiece is worth several times a round at twelve to three, and the multiplication says so. $leverage_line

The product is in match win probability points. $unit_line That total is the numerator the headline above divides.

How to read an interval

$interval_line

An interval that covers zero means this exposure does not separate the number from no effect at all. It does not mean the player had no effect, and it does not mean the point estimate is wrong. It means the sample cannot tell the two apart. Every card and every breakdown cell on this site prints its interval next to its number for that reason.

Reading conventions

Sign
Carried three ways: the + or − the number itself is printed with, a colour, and the side of the zero line the bar sits on. Red and green are the one pair the common dichromacies cannot separate, so neither colour is ever asked to carry the sign alone. There were direction arrows here as a fourth channel and they are gone: they repeated the printed sign, and every signed field on this site is written to a format that prints one.
Em dash
Nothing unmeasured renders as a zero. A cell with no number carries an em dash and, on hover, the reason it is absent. A breakdown cell with no rounds is not in the payload at all and does not appear as a row.
Middot
A measured zero. In a round ledger a player who did not plant has a plant credit of exactly zero, which is a fact about the round rather than a missing value, and reads as a middot so it cannot be confused with either an em dash or a small number.
