The league — confirmed 2026-07-25

12 teams · ESPN · full PPR, fractional yardage · 9 starters (1QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/1FLEX/1D-ST/1K) + 7 bench + 1 IR · waivers reset weekly to inverse standings · 1.01 locked, snake · regular season weeks 1–14, six-team playoff weeks 15–17 · drafting late August 2026.

Money: $100 = $75 to the pool + $25 refundable participation deposit. Prizes $500 / $250 / $150 out of a $900 pool.

Three things that follow, and drive everything else

The payout is convex, so build for ceiling. Finishing 4th pays exactly what finishing 12th pays: nothing. Nine of twelve teams lose the same $75, and first place is worth more than second and third combined. Variance is rewarded — but in two stages: a high mean gets you into the six-team playoff, ceiling wins it once you are there. Don't sacrifice mean in rounds 1–4; prefer upside from round 7 on.

Waiver priority is use-it-or-lose-it. This league resets the order every week to the inverse of the standings rather than using rolling priority. So an unspent claim expires worthless, winning actively degrades your access to the wire, and the losing teams get first crack at every breakout. This inverts the fantasy-basketball instinct that waiver priority is option value.

Full PPR reshapes the board. A back who catches 60 passes gains 60 points he would not get in non-PPR. Pass-catching backs, slot receivers and elite tight ends all move up; every non-PPR or half-PPR ranking is the wrong board.

Data

The spreadsheet is truth and the notes are the rendering — the reverse of the basketball build's architecture.

Run order — the only ordering constraint in the build:

bash cd "Sports/NFL/Fantasy Football/Data" && ./fetch_nflverse.sh && python3 build_context.py && python3 build_board.py && python3 simulate_season.py

  • Data/players.csv — 243 rows: projections, ADP, archetype, risk. The only place a projected number is authored
  • Data/player_context.csv — 203 skill players: age, draft pedigree, three-year games played, official injury-report history, snap share, 2025 usage, live contract terms. All observed
  • Data/build_context.py — joins nflverse rosters, season stat lines, weekly injury reports and snap counts with OverTheCap's contract pages. Carries a join validator that catches wrong-player matches
  • Data/build_board.py — renders the board, the tier stacks and Availability and injury risk. --check runs its self-check
  • Data/simulate_season.py — the draft-and-season Monte Carlo behind When to start drafting for upside
  • Data/raw/ — the dated source pulls · Data/check_links.py — the link checker

Projected points are computed from published projected stat lines under this league's own scoring, not imported as someone else's PPR total. ADP is a Fantasy Football Calculator PPR proxy, not ESPN's own — ESPN's could not be extracted programmatically. Four things in the build are deliberately-labelled models: kicker points, D/ST points, Avail, and Adj VOR. Everything else is observed or cited.

The website

The whole vault also renders as a static HTML wiki at ~/Desktop/Code/fantasy-football-encyclopedia — same architecture as the fantasy basketball wiki, with an interactive Draft Room (sort, filter, position pills, availability bars) and player fact cards carrying the observed context data.

bash cd ~/Desktop/Code/fantasy-football-encyclopedia && python3 build.py && python3 serve.py

Why this is not a copy of the basketball build

2026-27 Fantasy Basketball Encyclopedia is 644 notes and roughly 852,000 words, written for someone already fluent. This is about 100 notes, for someone who does not watch NFL football.

  • One scoring format, one board — full PPR.
  • Position is the primary axis. The basketball tiers deliberately ignore position; here position scarcity, the tight-end cliff and bye weeks are the draft.
  • No projection engine. Consensus projections get scored under this league's rules; the effort goes into roster shape.
  • No categories, so no punt builds. Football is one summed number.
  • One 32-row table, not 32 team desks.
  • A literacy layer basketball never needed.
