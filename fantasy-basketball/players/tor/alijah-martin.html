Alijah Martin

Alijah Martin is a newly secured end-of-rotation guard whose defensive energy matters more to Toronto than his near-term fantasy line.

Team
Toronto Raptors

Baseline and projection

Martin played 23 NBA games at 6.3 MPG in 2025-26, averaging 2.2 points, 0.9 rebounds, 0.5 assists, 0.3 steals, 0.2 blocks, 0.2 threes and 0.3 turnovers on .320/.789 shooting. Toronto officially gave him a two-year deal after his two-way rookie season. Basketball-Reference, accessed 2026-07-18 · NBA offseason tracker, updated 2026-07-16

Ten minutes is close to the 6.3 MPG he played as a two-way rookie, adjusted for the standard two-year deal he has since signed.

Fantasy value

  • Nine-category / points: not draftable; 9.9 simple FP/G.
  • Dynasty: outside top 220, with slightly more value in deep points formats if the three develops.
  • Ceiling: injuries to Walter/Barrett create a 15-MPG defense-first role.
  • Floor: healthy-night DNPs and continued 905 assignments.

Martin and Chucky Hepburn both bring guard defense, but Martin's standard contract gives him a clearer NBA bench path. The knee inflammation is expected to resolve by the season rather than alter the long-term forecast. Raptors Republic, 2026-07-10

Career record

Pre-NBA: Five college seasons. FAU 2020-24, a four-year core piece of the 2023 Final Four team (peak 13.9 PTS / 5.3 REB on .400 3P in 2021-22; First-team All-C-USA and C-USA Tournament MVP 2023; Second-team All-AAC 2024). Transferred to Florida 2024-25: 14.4 PTS / 4.5 REB / 2.2 AST / 1.5 STL in 30.4 MPG over 38 games on .452/.350/.761, starting for the 2025 national champions. Draft: 2025, Round 2, Pick 39, Toronto Raptors.

The rookie year's real evidence is with Raptors 905: 18.5 PTS, 4.6 REB, 3.6 AST, and 1.4 STL in 30.7 MPG across 39 games (38 starts) on .485/.392/.720 — All-NBA G League Third Team, G League All-Defense, and G League All-Rookie.

Durability: Consistently available in college (33-38 games in each full season); the only current flag is the knee inflammation that kept him out of July 2026 Summer League, listed probable for the season.

Trajectory: The record shows a winner at every stop — Final Four, national title, decorated G League rookie season — whose NBA cameos (.320 FG in garbage-time minutes) say far less than the 905 line, where the .392 three-point mark on 227 attempts addressed his biggest question. In 2026-27, now on a two-year standard deal, he competes for the fourth-guard defensive role in Toronto. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if a Walter or Barrett absence hands him about 15+ MPG and the college/G League three-ball survives NBA distance; the steals would then make him a deep-league 9-cat specialist.
