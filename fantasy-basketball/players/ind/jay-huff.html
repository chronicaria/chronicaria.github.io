Why the line looks like this

Huff played all 82 games, started 47 and averaged 9.5 points, 4.0 rebounds and 1.9 blocks in 21 minutes; his 153 total blocks ranked second in the NBA. Zubac takes the starter workload, but the July 8 Micah Potter waiver removed the third center without a replacement signing, leaving Huff the only true backup five behind him — Toppin at 6'9" and the 33-year-old Nance are the alternatives. Published rotation projections list Huff as a core bench contributor near 17-18 minutes; this card sits slightly below that because Toppin retains some small-ball five work. The line scales Huff's observed rates rather than pretending a new skill level. Pacers player review · Pacers projected rotation

Correction 2026-07-18: base minutes raised from 8 to 14, and the nine-category band from 150-225 to 110-165. The prior eight-minute call put the NBA's second-ranked shot-blocker behind a 6'9" forward in the center pecking order, which no published projection supports and which the Potter waiver made harder still. Redraft, dynasty, and scenarios

  • Redraft: a late-round blocks-and-threes target in 12-team category leagues rather than a pure waiver watch; a priority add if he logs 18+ minutes twice.
  • Dynasty: cheap hold in center-premium/deep category leagues; ordinary points-league asset.
  • Floor: Carlisle goes small behind Zubac and Toppin absorbs the backup five, pushing Huff back toward 10 minutes.
  • Ceiling: 20 minutes, 9 points, two threes and 1.8 blocks during a Zubac absence; top-80 category specialist.

Durability: 82 of 82 in 2025-26 and 64 games the season before — no NBA injury history of note. The tiny early-career GP totals (4, 7, 20) were roster status, not health.

Trajectory: The record is a classic late-bloomer specialist arc: four fringe seasons, then Memphis proved the threes-plus-blocks pairing at scale (1.3 3PM, 0.9 BLK in under 12 minutes), and 2025-26 in Indiana confirmed it holds at 21 minutes and 47 starts with 1.9 blocks per game. Age 28 with only 177 career games means unusually low mileage. In 2026-27 he competes for the backup-five role behind Zubac with no true center behind him, and he is already fantasy-relevant in category formats at 14-plus minutes; any Zubac absence that pushes him back toward 20 minutes makes him a top-100 category specialist.
