<h2 id="design-thesis">Design Thesis</h2>
<p>The company should feel like a basketball front office, a betting syndicate, and a forecasting lab braided together.</p>
<p>The key split:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Research Department:</strong> 30 team subcommittees, each an expert on one NBA team.</li>
<li><strong>Prediction Department:</strong> model and agent forecasters consume team research without becoming attached to any team narrative.</li>
<li><strong>Aggregation Department:</strong> turns many forecasts into calibrated game and season probabilities.</li>
<li><strong>Finance Department:</strong> compares probabilities against sportsbooks, Vegas-style lines, Kalshi, Polymarket, and other markets.</li>
<li><strong>Backtesting Department:</strong> walks the system through the completed 2025-26 season as if it were live.</li>
<li><strong>Risk, Compliance &#x26; Audit:</strong> prevents leakage, illegal wagering, overbetting, and untracked model drift.</li>
</ul>
<p>The core principle is the same as <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/ai/designed-information-asymmetry-for-llm-forecasts/" class="wiki-link">Designed information asymmetry for LLM forecasts</a>: do not give every agent the same blob of context. Team committees should develop private, team-specific expertise. Prediction agents should receive structured packets from the relevant teams, plus league context, and then produce scoreable probability distributions.</p>
<h2 id="corporate-charter">Corporate Charter</h2>
<p><strong>Mission:</strong> produce probabilistic NBA forecasts and paper-bet recommendations through an auditable multi-agent company.</p>
<p><strong>Primary outputs:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>preseason win projections for all 30 teams.</li>
<li>playoff/play-in odds.</li>
<li>game moneyline probabilities.</li>
<li>spread cover probabilities.</li>
<li>total over/under probabilities.</li>
<li>player-availability-adjusted game simulations.</li>
<li>market-edge board versus sportsbook odds, Kalshi, and Polymarket.</li>
<li>backtested performance, calibration, and closing-line-value reports.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Default capital mode:</strong> paper betting. Real wagering is out of scope until the system has a leakage-free walk-forward backtest, legal/jurisdiction checks, stake limits, and a human approval gate.</p>
<p><strong>Primary backtest:</strong> the completed 2025-26 NBA season, walked forward date by date with only information available at each decision timestamp.</p>

<h3 id="bet-issuance-rules">Bet Issuance Rules</h3>
<ul>
<li>Default is paper betting.</li>
<li>Real betting requires human approval and legal eligibility.</li>
<li>No bet if edge is below the minimum threshold.</li>
<li>No bet if the line moved before ticket creation.</li>
<li>No bet if the market has unclear settlement rules.</li>
<li>No bet if the edge depends entirely on unconfirmed injury speculation.</li>
<li>No parlaying by default.</li>
<li>No player props until the game-level system is proven.</li>
</ul>

<p>This is not optional. The backtest department is the referee of the entire company.</p>
<h3 id="chief-backtesting-officer">Chief Backtesting Officer</h3>
<p>Owns the 2025-26 walk-forward simulation. Has authority to invalidate results for leakage.</p>
<h3 id="2025-26-walk-forward-protocol">2025-26 Walk-Forward Protocol</h3>
<p>For each game day in the 2025-26 season:</p>
<ol>
<li>Set the simulation clock to the decision timestamp.</li>
<li>Load only data available before that timestamp.</li>
<li>Build Team State Packets for all teams.</li>
<li>Activate team committees for that day's slate.</li>
<li>Produce market-blind game forecasts.</li>
<li>Aggregate house lines.</li>
<li>Load odds snapshots available at the same timestamp.</li>
<li>Issue paper bet tickets.</li>
<li>Advance to game result.</li>
<li>Score forecasts and bets.</li>
<li>Repeat.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="leakage-rules">Leakage Rules</h3>
<ul>
<li>No final-season EPM, DARKO, RAPM, or Basketball-Reference advanced stats may be used for earlier dates unless daily snapshots exist.</li>
<li>No final record, playoff outcome, or postgame box score may enter a pregame packet.</li>
<li>No closing line may enter a bet decision made earlier.</li>
<li>No official result may enter a same-day model update before the simulated game ends.</li>
<li>No agent may search the web live during historical backtest unless search results are time-filtered and archived.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="evaluation-metrics">Evaluation Metrics</h3>
<p>Forecast quality:</p>
<ul>
<li>Brier score for moneyline.</li>
<li>log loss for moneyline.</li>
<li>calibration curves by probability bucket.</li>
<li>spread MAE and RMSE.</li>
<li>total MAE and RMSE.</li>
<li>probability integral transform / distribution calibration.</li>
</ul>
<p>Betting quality:</p>
<ul>
<li>ROI by market.</li>
<li>closing-line value.</li>
<li>hit rate by edge bucket.</li>
<li>max drawdown.</li>
<li>Sharpe-like return over stake volatility.</li>
<li>profit by book/exchange.</li>
<li>profit by agent/committee driver.</li>
</ul>
<p>Process quality:</p>
<ul>
<li>packet completeness.</li>
<li>data freshness.</li>
<li>forecast dispersion.</li>
<li>effective forecaster count.</li>
<li>team committee accuracy.</li>
<li>injury scenario accuracy.</li>
<li>market movement after recommendation.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="risk-compliance--audit">Risk, Compliance &#x26; Audit</h2>
<p><strong>Purpose:</strong> keep the company legal, honest, and non-delusional.</p>
<h3 id="hard-vetoes">Hard Vetoes</h3>
<ul>
<li>real wagering without human approval.</li>
<li>wagering in an illegal jurisdiction or without meeting platform eligibility.</li>
<li>missing odds timestamp.</li>
<li>missing injury timestamp for an availability-sensitive forecast.</li>
<li>stale team packet.</li>
<li>unclear market settlement.</li>
<li>bet size above bankroll cap.</li>
<li>correlated exposure above slate cap.</li>
<li>backtest leakage.</li>
<li>prompt/model version not logged.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="public-website--story-desk">Public Website &#x26; Story Desk</h2>
<p><strong>Purpose:</strong> make the system visually understandable.</p>
<p>The website should not only show picks. It should show how the company thinks.</p>
<h3 id="core-views">Core Views</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Company org chart:</strong> live department and committee activity.</li>
<li><strong>30 team war rooms:</strong> each team's roster, health, advanced metrics, G League depth, schedule context.</li>
<li><strong>Slate board:</strong> all games, house probabilities, market odds, edge, confidence, status.</li>
<li><strong>Matchup room:</strong> two team committees' packet summaries side by side.</li>
<li><strong>Agent parliament:</strong> prediction pods grouped by game lean and rationale.</li>
<li><strong>Injury scenario tree:</strong> probability-weighted outcomes if questionable players play or sit.</li>
<li><strong>Market edge board:</strong> no-vig market probability vs house probability.</li>
<li><strong>Bet ticket ledger:</strong> paper bets, rejected bets, veto reasons, CLV.</li>
<li><strong>Backtest lab:</strong> 2025-26 walk-forward results, calibration, ROI, drawdown, mistakes.</li>
<li><strong>Committee scorecards:</strong> which team committees and prediction pods add value.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="daily-slate-cycle">Daily Slate Cycle</h2>
<h3 id="morning-cycle">Morning Cycle</h3>
<ol>
<li>Data Department freezes overnight stats and odds.</li>
<li>Research Department updates all 30 Team State Packets.</li>
<li>CEO activates only teams on the slate.</li>
<li>Prediction Department produces market-blind forecasts.</li>
<li>Aggregation Department creates early house lines.</li>
<li>Finance records current market deltas but does not issue final bets.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="injury-cycle">Injury Cycle</h3>
<ol>
<li>Injury Report Agent ingests official availability reports.</li>
<li>Health agents update team packets.</li>
<li>Player Availability &#x26; Minutes Committee produces scenario probabilities.</li>
<li>Prediction Department reruns affected games.</li>
<li>Aggregation publishes updated house lines.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="pregame-cycle">Pregame Cycle</h3>
<ol>
<li>Data QA checks odds and injury timestamps.</li>
<li>Finance identifies edges against current lines.</li>
<li>Risk reviews stake, correlation, and legality.</li>
<li>Paper bet tickets are issued.</li>
<li>Public website updates slate board and trade-court view.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="postgame-cycle">Postgame Cycle</h3>
<ol>
<li>Results and box scores are ingested.</li>
<li>Forecasts are scored.</li>
<li>Paper bets are settled.</li>
<li>CLV is updated.</li>
<li>Team committees receive postgame diagnostic tasks.</li>
<li>Backtesting logs are locked.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="preseason-cycle">Preseason Cycle</h2>
<p>The preseason product is different from the daily slate product.</p>
<h3 id="preseason-research-outputs">Preseason Research Outputs</h3>
<p>Each team committee writes:</p>
<ul>
<li>roster continuity report.</li>
<li>projected rotation.</li>
<li>player impact board.</li>
<li>age/development curve.</li>
<li>injury fragility report.</li>
<li>G League and two-way depth report.</li>
<li>coaching/system report.</li>
<li>schedule difficulty note.</li>
<li>win projection interval.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="season-simulation">Season Simulation</h3>
<p>The Season Forecast Committee runs an 82-game simulation:</p>
<ul>
<li>team strength distributions.</li>
<li>game-by-game win probabilities.</li>
<li>injury/rest uncertainty.</li>
<li>schedule effects.</li>
<li>trade-deadline uncertainty placeholder.</li>
<li>play-in/playoff simulations.</li>
</ul>
<p>Outputs:</p>
<ul>
<li>mean wins.</li>
<li>median wins.</li>
<li>10th/90th percentile wins.</li>
<li>division/conference seed probabilities.</li>
<li>playoff/play-in odds.</li>
<li>season win total edges versus market.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="versioned-build-plan">Versioned Build Plan</h2>
<h3 id="v0---static-company-blueprint">V0 - Static Company Blueprint</h3>
<ul>
<li>Build the website using static packets.</li>
<li>Show all 30 team committees.</li>
<li>No live data.</li>
<li>Goal: make the company structure compelling.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v1---data-backbone">V1 - Data Backbone</h3>
<ul>
<li>Ingest schedules, scores, box scores, NBA stats, G League stats, and odds.</li>
<li>Build entity mapping.</li>
<li>Store timestamped snapshots.</li>
<li>No LLM forecasts yet.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v2---30-team-research-committees">V2 - 30 Team Research Committees</h3>
<ul>
<li>One Team Chair plus two specialists per team.</li>
<li>Produce daily Team State Packets.</li>
<li>Add G League and injury modules.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v3---game-prediction-engine">V3 - Game Prediction Engine</h3>
<ul>
<li>Add market-blind game forecast packets.</li>
<li>Model moneyline, spread, and total distributions.</li>
<li>Add aggregation and calibration.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v4---finance-and-paper-betting">V4 - Finance and Paper Betting</h3>
<ul>
<li>Add odds comparison.</li>
<li>Add no-vig conversion.</li>
<li>Add paper bet tickets.</li>
<li>Add CLV and ROI dashboard.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v5---2025-26-walk-forward-backtest">V5 - 2025-26 Walk-Forward Backtest</h3>
<ul>
<li>Freeze simulated date.</li>
<li>Re-run the company through the full season.</li>
<li>Publish leakage audit, calibration, and betting performance.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="v6---full-agent-theater">V6 - Full Agent Theater</h3>
<ul>
<li>Expand to full 408-agent company.</li>
<li>Add committee scorecards and visual agent parliament.</li>
<li>Virtualize inactive agents so the full company can be displayed without paying for 408 fresh calls per slate.</li>
<li>Keep real betting disabled by default.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="recommended-technical-architecture">Recommended Technical Architecture</h2>
<pre><code class="language-text">Scheduler
  -> Data snapshot jobs
  -> Team state packet generation
  -> Slate activation
  -> Prediction fanout
  -> Aggregation
  -> Odds comparison
  -> Risk gate
  -> Paper bet ledger
  -> Postgame scorer
  -> Website publisher
</code></pre>
<p>Recommended stack:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Backend:</strong> Python FastAPI.</li>
<li><strong>Workflow:</strong> LangGraph for explicit state machines and supervisor routing.</li>
<li><strong>Storage:</strong> Postgres plus parquet files for historical snapshots.</li>
<li><strong>Modeling:</strong> Python, pandas, polars, scikit-learn/PyMC/Stan as needed.</li>
<li><strong>Agent orchestration:</strong> LLM calls only around packet interpretation, debate, and summaries; deterministic code for scoring, odds math, joins, and bet settlement.</li>
<li><strong>Frontend:</strong> Next.js or React dashboard with team pages, slate board, and backtest lab.</li>
</ul>
