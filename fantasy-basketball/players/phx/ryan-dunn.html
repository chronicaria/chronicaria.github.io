Ryan Dunn

Ryan Dunn is the clearest challenge to Phoenix's projected rotation: his defense and 70-game 2025-26 résumé merit playing time, but a severe wing logjam makes his 2026-27 role too uncertain to treat as a standard-league base.

Player card

  • Team / role: Phoenix Suns defensive forward and rotation challenger
  • Exact healthy-game base: 0 MPG; outside the projected 11
  • Conditional appearance projection: 45 GP, 12 MPG when used
  • 9-cat appearance line: .455 FG (1.4/3.1), .550 FT (0.2/0.4), 0.5 3PM, 3.5 PTS, 2.5 REB, 0.9 AST, 0.6 STL, 0.3 BLK, 0.5 TO
  • Projection confidence: Low
  • Formats: Defensive streamer if elevated; deep-dynasty hold; no points-league floor without minutes

Role and evidence

Dunn averaged 5.8 points, 4.2 rebounds, 1.5 assists, 0.9 steals, and 0.4 blocks in 19.4 minutes across 70 games. He shot .453 from the field but only .489 at the line, a major category weakness despite low volume. Source: Hashtag Basketball, updated 2026-07-02. Confidence: medium-high.

Phoenix then added Miles Bridges, re-signed Jordan Goodwin, signed Luke Kennard, drafted Koa Peat, and retained Rasheer Fleming; Haywood Highsmith was not a retention but a post-deadline buyout-market signing on 2026-02-18. The first post-transaction local rotation placed Dunn among the reserves rather than the first 11, though a competing depth chart published 2026-07-05 lists Dunn as the second-unit power forward — that disagreement is the whole case for him. Sources: Suns on SI rotation analysis, 2026-07-02, Bet Arizona depth chart, 2026-07-05. Confidence: medium-low; both are projections, not coach announcements.

Dunn's 20 points, 11 rebounds, and five assists in the April 12 finale show a broader statistical ceiling when given responsibility, but that short-handed game should not override the depth chart. Source: official NBA gamebook, 2026-04-12. Confidence: high on the game, low on extrapolation.

Category shape

  • Wins: Steals, rebounds, blocks from a wing; low turnovers.
  • Neutral: FG% can be acceptable on limited usage.
  • Costs: FT% and low points/threes/assists.
  • Build fit: Punt-FT% defensive builds and deep leagues.

Points and dynasty outlook

Hashtag ranked Dunn No. 250 in dynasty and its July 18 keeper crowd placed him No. 407. The wide skepticism reflects a role problem, not the disappearance of his defensive tools. Sources: Hashtag dynasty, updated 2026-07-02, Hashtag keeper, updated 2026-07-18. Confidence: low-medium as market context.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: DNP-heavy reserve role behind Bridges, Brooks, Fleming, Goodwin, and Kennard.
  • Base: 45 appearances at 12 minutes, supplying defense without fantasy volume.
  • Ceiling: Wins a 22-minute role, returns to four-plus rebounds and 1.3 stocks, and becomes a deep-category specialist.

Draft verdict

Dunn should be watched closely in camp because he is the zero-minute base most likely to make this team projection wrong. Do not draft him in ordinary redraft until a Jordan Ott rotation or preseason pattern places him clearly in the top 10.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Virginia, two seasons. 2022-23: 31 GP off the bench, 2.6 PTS / 2.9 REB / 1.1 BLK in 12.9 minutes. 2023-24: 34 GP / 34 GS, 27.5 MPG, 8.1 PTS / 6.9 REB / 1.3 STL / 2.3 BLK on .548 FG but .200 from three and .532 FT — ACC All-Defensive Team and a national defensive-player-of-the-year candidate. Draft: 2024, Round 1, Pick 28 (selected by Denver, traded on draft night to Phoenix with pick 56 and future seconds for pick 22).

Durability: 74 and 70 games in his two seasons — no availability concerns at all; the variance in his record is minutes, never health.

Trajectory: Two NBA seasons of essentially the same player: a 19-minute defensive forward whose counting stats inched sideways (points down, rebounds/assists/steals up) while the two college red flags — sub-50% free-throw shooting (.487, .489) and a shaky jumper — stayed exactly where Virginia left them. The defense that made him a first-rounder is real and NBA-proven; the offensive growth that would force a bigger role has not arrived on record. In 2026-27 he fights Bridges, Brooks, Fleming, Kennard and Peat for wing minutes from outside the projected top 11; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if he wins roughly 22-plus minutes in Jordan Ott's rotation, where his stocks-and-rebounds shape plays in punt-FT% builds — the April 12 finale (20/11/5) is the existence proof, not the expectation.
