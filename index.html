  • Model atmospheric flow on brown dwarfs, which spin fast enough to be visibly flattened. The literature solves these atmospheres on a sphere; I solve the deformed figure instead and measure what that assumption costs, finding a rotational signal thousands of times stronger than the spherical case predicts.

Sep 2021 – present

Rather than scoring a county on a single partisan axis, I fit an ideology vector for each and used the correlations between them to push national, state and local swings into races with no polling of their own.
