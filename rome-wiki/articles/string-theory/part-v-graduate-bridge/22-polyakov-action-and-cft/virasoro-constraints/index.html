    Virasoro constraints

    The Virasoro constraints are one equation, T_{\alpha\beta}=0 , written three ways: as "the worldsheet stress tensor vanishes," as (\dot X\pm X')^2=0 in conformal gauge, and as infinitely many conditions L_n=0 on the oscillator modes. Same content, three costumes.

    Why a metric variation gives a constraint

    The auxiliary worldsheet metric hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ is a fake degree of freedom — we put it into the Polyakov action by hand and it carries no dynamics of its own. Whenever a non-dynamical field sits in an action, its equation of motion is not a law of motion; it is a constraint on the other fields. Varying hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ produces that constraint, and it happens to be exactly Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0. So:

    Virasoro constraints = "the worldsheet stress tensor is zero." The name "Virasoro" only shows up later, when we Fourier-expand that zero and discover the modes obey the Virasoro algebra.

    Physically, what does Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0 say about the string? In conformal gauge it forces (∂+X)2=(∂−X)2=0(\partial_+X)^2=(\partial_-X)^2=0(∂+​X)2=(∂−​X)2=0: the two null tangent directions on the worldsheet map to null directions in spacetime. The string cannot store energy in "stretching along itself" — only transverse wiggles are physical. This is the same no-longitudinal-motion fact you solved by hand in light-cone gauge, now falling out of a metric variation instead of being imposed.

    From the metric equation of motion

    The Worldsheet stress tensor of the Polyakov string is

    Tαβ=∂αX⋅∂βX−12 hαβ hγδ ∂γX⋅∂δX,T_{\alpha\beta} =\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X -\tfrac12\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,h^{\gamma\delta}\,\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X,Tαβ​=∂α​X⋅∂β​X−21​hαβ​hγδ∂γ​X⋅∂δ​X,

    where A⋅B≡ημνAμBνA\cdot B\equiv \eta_{\mu\nu}A^\mu B^\nuA⋅B≡ημν​AμBν contracts spacetime indices with the mostly-plus metric ημν=diag(−1,+1,…,+1)\eta_{\mu\nu}=\mathrm{diag}(-1,+1,\dots,+1)ημν​=diag(−1,+1,…,+1). Because hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ enters the action with no derivatives, its Euler–Lagrange equation is purely algebraic:

     Tαβ=0. (1)\boxed{\,T_{\alpha\beta}=0.\,}\tag{1}Tαβ​=0.​(1)

    This is the covariant version of a familiar move in constrained mechanics: drop a non-propagating variable into the Lagrangian and its equation of motion deletes unphysical motion rather than evolving anything. (The point-particle einbein does the same job, turning S=−m∫−x˙2S=-m\int\sqrt{-\dot x^2}S=−m∫−x˙2​ into the mass-shell constraint p2+m2=0p^2+m^2=0p2+m2=0; see Independent worldsheet metric.)

    Conformal-gauge form

    Gauge-fix to conformal gauge hαβ=e2ωηαβh_{\alpha\beta}=e^{2\omega}\eta_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​=e2ωηαβ​ with ηαβ=diag(−1,+1)\eta_{\alpha\beta}=\mathrm{diag}(-1,+1)ηαβ​=diag(−1,+1) and coordinates (τ,σ)(\tau,\sigma)(τ,σ); write X˙=∂τX\dot X=\partial_\tau XX˙=∂τ​X, X′=∂σXX'=\partial_\sigma XX′=∂σ​X. The Weyl factor e2ωe^{2\omega}e2ω cancels between hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ and hγδh^{\gamma\delta}hγδ in TαβT_{\alpha\beta}Tαβ​, so it drops out entirely — the constraint is Weyl-blind. The components are

    Tττ=Tσσ=12(X˙2+X′2),Tτσ=X˙⋅X′.T_{\tau\tau}=T_{\sigma\sigma}=\tfrac12\big(\dot X^2+X'^2\big), \qquad T_{\tau\sigma}=\dot X\cdot X'.Tττ​=Tσσ​=21​(X˙2+X′2),Tτσ​=X˙⋅X′.

    (The equality Tττ=TσσT_{\tau\tau}=T_{\sigma\sigma}Tττ​=Tσσ​ is tracelessness Tαα=0T^\alpha{}_\alpha=0Tαα​=0, an identity in 2D — one of the three metric-EOM components is redundant, matching the count in Independent worldsheet metric.) Setting Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0 gives the two independent Virasoro constraints

    X˙2+X′2=0,X˙⋅X′=0.(2)\dot X^2+X'^2=0, \qquad \dot X\cdot X'=0.\tag{2}X˙2+X′2=0,X˙⋅X′=0.(2)

    Adding and subtracting them packages both into

    (X˙+X′)2=0,(X˙−X′)2=0,(3)(\dot X+X')^2=0, \qquad (\dot X-X')^2=0,\tag{3}(X˙+X′)2=0,(X˙−X′)2=0,(3)

    i.e. T++∝(∂+X)2=0T_{++}\propto(\partial_+X)^2=0T++​∝(∂+​X)2=0 and T−−∝(∂−X)2=0T_{--}\propto(\partial_-X)^2=0T−−​∝(∂−​X)2=0 with σ±=τ±σ\sigma^\pm=\tau\pm\sigmaσ±=τ±σ. This is the null-tangent statement: the left- and right-moving tangent vectors are lightlike in spacetime. The relation X˙2+X′2=0\dot X^2+X'^2=0X˙2+X′2=0 can hold with a mostly-plus metric precisely because X˙2\dot X^2X˙2 can be negative — the timelike coordinate X0X^0X0 is doing the cancelling.

    Mode language: why LnL_nLn​ appears

    The left- and right-movers decouple, so expand each constraint in Fourier modes along the string. Using the open-string oscillator expansion X˙μ±X′μ=2α′∑n∈Zαnμ e−in(τ±σ)\dot X^\mu\pm X'^\mu=\sqrt{2\alpha'}\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}\alpha^\mu_n\,e^{-in(\tau\pm\sigma)}X˙μ±X′μ=2α′​∑n∈Z​αnμ​e−in(τ±σ) (with α0μ=2α′ pμ\alpha^\mu_0=\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,p^\muα0μ​=2α′​pμ), squaring gives

    (X˙±X′)2=4α′∑n∈ZLn e−in(τ±σ), Ln=12∑p∈Zαn−p⋅αp .(4)(\dot X\pm X')^2 = 4\alpha'\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z} L_n\,e^{-in(\tau\pm\sigma)}, \qquad \boxed{\,L_n=\tfrac12\sum_{p\in\mathbb Z}\alpha_{n-p}\cdot\alpha_p\,.}\tag{4}(X˙±X′)2=4α′n∈Z∑​Ln​e−in(τ±σ),Ln​=21​p∈Z∑​αn−p​⋅αp​.​(4)

    So constraint (3) — "this function of σ\sigmaσ vanishes everywhere" — is equivalent to "every Fourier coefficient vanishes." Classically, therefore,

    (∂±X)2=0⟺Ln=0  for all n,(5)(\partial_\pm X)^2=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad L_n=0\ \ \text{for all }n,\tag{5}(∂±​X)2=0⟺Ln​=0  for all n,(5)

    For the closed string an independent barred tower Lˉn\bar L_nLˉn​ arises the same way from the right-movers, giving Lˉn=0\bar L_n=0Lˉn​=0 as well; for the open string the Neumann boundary conditions tie the two chiralities together, so both (X˙±X′)2(\dot X\pm X')^2(X˙±X′)2 are built from the same oscillators αnμ\alpha_n^\muαnμ​ and there is only the single tower LnL_nLn​. A single local equation has become infinitely many number-valued conditions — one per mode.

    Two facts about these LnL_nLn​ that decide the whole quantum story:

    • They sum over all spacetime directions, X0X^0X0 included. Unlike the transverse light-cone operators Ln⊥L_n^\perpLn⊥​ (which run over I=2,…,D−1I=2,\dots,D-1I=2,…,D−1 only), the covariant LnL_nLn​ in (4) contract with ημν\eta_{\mu\nu}ημν​, so the timelike oscillators appear. That extra baggage is exactly why covariant quantization must work to kill negative-norm ("ghost") states that light-cone gauge never even produces.
    • Equivalently on the plane, LnL_nLn​ is the Laurent mode of the holomorphic stress tensor, T(z)=∑nLn/zn+2T(z)=\sum_n L_n/z^{n+2}T(z)=∑n​Ln​/zn+2, i.e. Ln=12πi∮dz zn+1T(z)L_n=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint dz\,z^{n+1}T(z)Ln​=2πi1​∮dzzn+1T(z) (see Complex coordinates on the worldsheet). The contour integral is just coefficient extraction; it is the CFT-native route to the same operators.

    The quantum weakening — and why it is forced

    Classically, demanding Ln=0L_n=0Ln​=0 for every nnn is harmless. Quantum-mechanically it is impossible, and the reason is a short computation, not a slogan.

    Promote the LnL_nLn​ to operators. They no longer commute; they obey the Virasoro algebra

    [Lm,Ln]=(m−n)Lm+n+c12 m(m2−1) δm+n,0,c=D(6)[L_m,L_n]=(m-n)L_{m+n}+\frac{c}{12}\,m(m^2-1)\,\delta_{m+n,0}, \qquad c=D\tag{6}[Lm​,Ln​]=(m−n)Lm+n​+12c​m(m2−1)δm+n,0​,c=D(6)

    (derived in Virasoro algebra and central charge; c=Dc=Dc=D because each of the DDD free bosons contributes one unit to the central term). Suppose a physical state ∣ψ⟩|\psi\rangle∣ψ⟩ were annihilated by all LnL_nLn​. Take m=2, n=−2m=2,\,n=-2m=2,n=−2. The left side of (6) then kills ∣ψ⟩|\psi\rangle∣ψ⟩ automatically:

    [L2,L−2] ∣ψ⟩=L2L−2∣ψ⟩⏟0−L−2L2∣ψ⟩⏟0=0.[L_2,L_{-2}]\,|\psi\rangle = L_2\underbrace{L_{-2}|\psi\rangle}_{0}-L_{-2}\underbrace{L_2|\psi\rangle}_{0}=0.[L2​,L−2​]∣ψ⟩=L2​0L−2​∣ψ⟩​​−L−2​0L2​∣ψ⟩​​=0.

    But the right side of (6) at m=2m=2m=2 is [L2,L−2]=4L0+c12⋅2(4−1)=4L0+c2[L_2,L_{-2}]=4L_0+\tfrac{c}{12}\cdot 2(4-1)=4L_0+\tfrac{c}{2}[L2​,L−2​]=4L0​+12c​⋅2(4−1)=4L0​+2c​. Using the required mass-shell condition (L0−1)∣ψ⟩=0(L_0-1)|\psi\rangle=0(L0​−1)∣ψ⟩=0 (the "intercept," fixed below), this must also annihilate ∣ψ⟩|\psi\rangle∣ψ⟩:

    (4L0+c2)∣ψ⟩=(4+c2)∣ψ⟩=0.\Big(4L_0+\tfrac{c}{2}\Big)|\psi\rangle=\Big(4+\tfrac{c}{2}\Big)|\psi\rangle=0.(4L0​+2c​)∣ψ⟩=(4+2c​)∣ψ⟩=0.

    Since c=D>0c=D>0c=D>0, the prefactor 4+D/24+D/24+D/2 is a nonzero number, so ∣ψ⟩=0|\psi\rangle=0∣ψ⟩=0. Imposing all the constraints leaves only the zero state. The central term — the purely quantum piece of (6) — is the obstruction: it is a c-number that cannot be annihilated away.

    The escape is the same one used for the Lorenz condition ∂μAμ=0\partial_\mu A^\mu=0∂μ​Aμ=0 in photon quantization: impose only half the constraints. Keep the positive-mode ("annihilation-like") half,

     Ln∣ψ⟩=0  (n>0),(L0−1)∣ψ⟩=0 (7)\boxed{\,L_n|\psi\rangle=0\ \ (n>0), \qquad (L_0-1)|\psi\rangle=0\,}\tag{7}Ln​∣ψ⟩=0  (n>0),(L0​−1)∣ψ⟩=0​(7)

    (for the open bosonic string; the closed string adds the barred copy Lˉn∣ψ⟩=0\bar L_n|\psi\rangle=0Lˉn​∣ψ⟩=0, (Lˉ0−1)∣ψ⟩=0(\bar L_0-1)|\psi\rangle=0(Lˉ0​−1)∣ψ⟩=0, and level matching L0=Lˉ0L_0=\bar L_0L0​=Lˉ0​). The negative modes L−nL_{-n}L−n​ then build descendants rather than being set to zero, and the two "halves" only have to agree in expectation value, ⟨ψ∣Ln∣ψ⟩=0\langle\psi|L_n|\psi\rangle=0⟨ψ∣Ln​∣ψ⟩=0 for all n≠0n\neq0n=0, which (7) guarantees. It is a theorem that this half suffices to remove every negative-norm state.

    Why the shift by 111 in L0L_0L0​? The naive L0L_0L0​ is a divergent operator-ordered sum; normal-ordering it leaves a finite constant aaa, and matching the covariant spectrum to the light-cone answer fixes a=−1a=-1a=−1 for the bosonic string — an intercept −a=1-a=1−a=1 (the same −1/12-1/12−1/12-flavored Casimir shift that gives the tachyon; see Normal ordering constant). Concretely, (L0−1)∣ψ⟩=0(L_0-1)|\psi\rangle=0(L0​−1)∣ψ⟩=0 is the mass-shell relation M2=1α′(N−1)M^2=\tfrac{1}{\alpha'}(N-1)M2=α′1​(N−1) with NNN the number operator.

    States satisfying (7) are Virasoro primaries with the right L0L_0L0​ eigenvalue; the leftover null/gauge states are quotiented out, a story continued in Vertex operators and physical-state conditions.

    Two gauges, one physics

    Light-cone gauge and covariant quantization attack (2) from opposite ends:

    • Light-cone solves the constraints classically. Setting X+∝τX^+\propto\tauX+∝τ uses up the residual gauge freedom, and then (2) determines X−X^-X− algebraically in terms of the transverse XIX^IXI. Only the D−2D-2D−2 transverse oscillators survive — manifestly ghost-free, but Lorentz invariance is hidden.
    • Covariant keeps all XμX^\muXμ and never solves (2); it imposes (7) on states instead. Lorentz invariance is manifest, but ghosts must be exorcised by hand.

    The two must agree, and their agreement — the covariant spectrum matching the transverse one — is a linchpin of the consistency argument that culminates in Total central charge and D = 26.

    Open questions

    • The Virasoro algebra (6) and the origin of its central term c=Dc=Dc=D are derived in Virasoro algebra and central charge.
    • The ghost contribution c=−26c=-26c=−26, BRST refinement, and the resulting D=26D=26D=26 live in Total central charge and D = 26 and its siblings.
    • The claim that "half the constraints suffice to kill all negative-norm states" (the no-ghost theorem) is used here but proved only in outline; the full statement is the Goddard–Thorn / DDF result, beyond this module.

    The lesson: the anomaly bites exactly when the central term c12m(m2−1)\tfrac{c}{12}m(m^2-1)12c​m(m2−1) is nonzero, which requires ∣m∣≥2|m|\ge 2∣m∣≥2. For m∈{−1,0,1}m\in\{-1,0,1\}m∈{−1,0,1} the central term is zero, so {L−1,L0,L1}\{L_{-1},L_0,L_1\}{L−1​,L0​,L1​} close without anomaly (this holomorphic triplet generates the SL(2,R)SL(2,\mathbb{R})SL(2,R) Möbius subalgebra; together with the barred triplet {Lˉ−1,Lˉ0,Lˉ1}\{\bar L_{-1},\bar L_0,\bar L_1\}{Lˉ−1​,Lˉ0​,Lˉ1​} it gives the full global SL(2,C)SL(2,\mathbb{C})SL(2,C) conformal group of the plane).
