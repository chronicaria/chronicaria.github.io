a:T19d76,<h2 id="two-sectors-per-mover-four-sectors-per-string">Two sectors per mover, four sectors per string</h2>
<p>A closed string carries a <strong>left-moving</strong> copy and a <strong>right-moving</strong> copy of every field on one loop. The superstring puts worldsheet fermions on each copy, and each copy independently chooses a boundary condition: <strong>NS</strong> (antiperiodic fermion) or <strong>R</strong> (periodic). Two independent choices, four combinations — that is the entire architecture below.</p>

</ul>
