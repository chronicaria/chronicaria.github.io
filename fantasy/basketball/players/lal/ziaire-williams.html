Projection logic

Williams averaged 10.2 points, 2.4 rebounds, 1.1 assists, 1.4 steals, 0.4 blocks, and 1.5 threes in 22.9 minutes over 56 games in 2025-26, shooting .425 from the field. The steal rate is enticing, but the Lakers do not have 23 clean minutes for him without an injury or trade. Redraft, dynasty, and scenarios

  • Median: A twelve-minute energy wing with streaming value only in very deep leagues.
  • Ceiling: His defense wins 24 minutes and he repeats roughly 1.4 steals and 1.5 threes, creating top-160 category value.
  • Floor: Carr and LaRavia take the reserve-wing work, making Williams a frequent DNP-CD.
  • Draft stance: Watch list rather than a standard-league pick.
  • Dynasty: A low-cost hold only if roster depth allows; the contract does not provide long-term security.

Confidence

  • Roster: Medium-high — credible reported agreement and current ESPN listing, pending a Lakers transaction release at cutoff.
  • Minutes: Low — the forward hierarchy is unsettled.
  • Rates: Medium — last season's steal spike may regress in a smaller role.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Stanford, one season (2020-21): 20 GP, 10.7 PTS, 4.6 REB, 2.2 AST on .374/.291/.796 — a consensus five-star, top-ranked small forward in the 2020 class and McDonald's All-American selection whose lone college year underdelivered on the recruiting billing. Draft: 2021, Round 1, Pick 10, New Orleans Pelicans — traded on draft night to Memphis.

Durability: Has never reached 65 games in five seasons (62, 37, 51, 63, 56). A right knee injury cost him the first 24 games of 2022-23; ankle trouble and COVID protocols interrupted his rookie year; the other seasons mix minor injuries with rotation DNPs. Trajectory: The record shows a lottery pick who never became a scorer (career 8.7 PTS, .422 FG) but quietly rebuilt himself in Brooklyn as a 3-and-D wing, with 2025-26 the first year the steals (1.4) and threes (1.5) both landed at streamer-grade rates. In 2026-27 he competes for the Lakers' backup wing minutes behind LaRavia on a one-year, $3 million prove-it deal. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he wins roughly 24 minutes through injury or defensive trust and holds last season's defensive-event rates — the same profile at 12 minutes is waiver fodder.
