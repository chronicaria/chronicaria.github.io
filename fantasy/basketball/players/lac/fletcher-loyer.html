Fletcher Loyer

Fletcher Loyer is a training-camp shooting specialist on a non-guaranteed Exhibit 10 deal, competing for LA's open third two-way slot rather than for NBA minutes.

Cutoff/status: 2026-07-18; signed an Exhibit 10 contract with LAC on 2026-06-25 after going undrafted, age 22, 6-foot-4 guard out of Purdue. An Exhibit 10 is a one-year, non-guaranteed minimum deal with a camp invitation; it can be converted to a two-way, and LA has one two-way slot open behind Sean Pedulla and Nick Martinelli. His father, John Loyer, is the Clippers' director of pro scouting. Hoops Rumors, 2026-06-25 · Purdue athletics release · Los Angeles Clippers

Baseline and 2026-27 projection

Loyer led the Big Ten in three-point percentage as a senior at 43.2% and made 309 threes across four Purdue seasons at 41.1% for his career. The shooting is the entire case; size, athleticism and defensive projection are the reasons he was undrafted. Hoops Rumors

He finished with 1,829 career points (11th at Purdue) and 309 three-pointers, a school record. He led the Big Ten in three-point percentage as a junior at .444 and finished second as a senior at .432; his 112 threes in 2025-26 are the second-highest single-season total in program history, and he made multiple threes in 19 straight games. Three-time All-Big Ten honorable mention. Draft: undrafted (2026); signed an Exhibit 10 with the Clippers on June 25, 2026.

No NBA record. Loyer has never appeared in an NBA regular-season game. His most recent competitive season is the 2025-26 Purdue line above.

Durability: No missed-time issues to report. He appeared in 149 of a possible 149 games across four seasons and started all 39 as a senior.

Trajectory: A four-year starter whose case rests entirely on one elite, well-documented skill — 309 threes at 41.1 percent across 149 college games is about as much shooting evidence as a prospect can accumulate. The reasons he went undrafted are equally documented: 2.0 assists and 0.7 steals a game from a 6-4 guard describe a player with no secondary skill and no defensive projection. He is competing for the Clippers' third two-way slot against two players already holding two-ways, on a deal whose most likely resolution is a waiver plus the San Diego retention bonus. Fantasy relevance in 2026-27 is effectively off the table; the dynasty case is the narrow bet that shooting this good eventually finds 10 minutes somewhere.
