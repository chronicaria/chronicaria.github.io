    Polyakov vs Nambu-Goto

    Nambu–Goto measures the worldsheet's area directly, which buries a square root in the action. Polyakov hands the geometry to a separate metric field h_{\alpha\beta} instead; on shell h_{\alpha\beta} is forced back to the induced geometry, so the two actions describe the same classical string — but Polyakov is polynomial in the fields and therefore quantizable.

    The einbein trick, one dimension up

    Nambu–Goto is like defining a car's motion by the length of the road it traces — geometrically honest, but the arc-length formula ∫1+y′2 dx\int\sqrt{1+y'^2}\,dx∫1+y′2​dx has a square root you can't differentiate cleanly or path-integrate. The einbein trick for a point particle removes exactly this square root by adding a "ruler" field. Polyakov is that trick with one more dimension: add a worldsheet metric hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ that does the measuring, so the action becomes a plain quadratic ∫(∂X)2\int (\partial X)^2∫(∂X)2. The metric turns out to carry no physics of its own — its equation of motion just hands the geometry back — but along the way it converts an intractable action into a free 2D field theory. Everything downstream in this module is built on this one move.

    The problem with Nambu–Goto

    The Nambu-Goto action is (minus the tension times) the area of the worldsheet: SNG=−12πα′∫dτ dσ −γ,γ≡det⁡(γαβ),γαβ=∂αX⋅∂βX.S_{\mathrm{NG}} = -\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma},\qquad \gamma\equiv\det(\gamma_{\alpha\beta}),\quad \gamma_{\alpha\beta}=\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X.SNG​=−2πα′1​∫dτdσ−γ​,γ≡det(γαβ​),γαβ​=∂α​X⋅∂β​X. Here γαβ\gamma_{\alpha\beta}γαβ​ is the induced metric: the target-space Minkowski metric ημν\eta_{\mu\nu}ημν​ (mostly-plus, diag(−1,+1,…,+1)\mathrm{diag}(-1,+1,\dots,+1)diag(−1,+1,…,+1)) pulled back onto the worldsheet by the embedding Xμ(τ,σ)X^\mu(\tau,\sigma)Xμ(τ,σ), with A⋅B=ημνAμBνA\cdot B=\eta_{\mu\nu}A^\mu B^\nuA⋅B=ημν​AμBν. It is the geometry the string actually has, dictated entirely by where it sits in spacetime.

    This is beautiful and unusable. Writing out the 2×22\times22×2 determinant, γ=X˙2 X′2−(X˙⋅X′)2⟹SNG=−12πα′∫dτ dσ (X˙⋅X′)2−X˙2X′2,\gamma = \dot X^2\,X'^2 - (\dot X\cdot X')^2 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad S_{\mathrm{NG}}=-\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{(\dot X\cdot X')^2-\dot X^2 X'^2},γ=X˙2X′2−(X˙⋅X′)2⟹SNG​=−2πα′1​∫dτdσ(X˙⋅X′)2−X˙2X′2​, so −γ\sqrt{-\gamma}−γ​ is a nonpolynomial function of the velocities X˙μ\dot X^\muX˙μ and slopes X′μX'^\muX′μ (a timelike worldsheet has γ<0\gamma<0γ<0, so the radicand −γ=(X˙⋅X′)2−X˙2X′2-\gamma=(\dot X\cdot X')^2-\dot X^2 X'^2−γ=(X˙⋅X′)2−X˙2X′2 is positive). Three consequences, all fatal for quantization:

    • Momenta don't invert. The canonical momentum Pμτ=∂L/∂X˙μ\mathcal P^\tau_\mu=\partial\mathcal L/\partial\dot X^\muPμτ​=∂L/∂X˙μ comes out as a ratio with −γ\sqrt{-\gamma}−γ​ in the denominator, and you cannot algebraically solve back for X˙μ\dot X^\muX˙μ. This non-invertibility is the signature of a constrained system — the same pathology the relativistic point particle L=−m−x˙2L=-m\sqrt{-\dot x^2}L=−m−x˙2​ has.
    • The path integral chokes. A square root in the exponent of ∫DX eiSNG\int\mathcal D X\,e^{iS_{\mathrm{NG}}}∫DXeiSNG​ has no Gaussian handle; there is no standard way to do the integral.
    • Constraints must be added by hand. The mass-shell-like relations (X˙±X′)2=0(\dot X\pm X')^2=0(X˙±X′)2=0 are not produced by the action; you impose them after gauge-fixing (as in light-cone gauge).

    The fix: hand the geometry to an auxiliary field

    Polyakov's move is to stop letting the embedding dictate the metric. Introduce an independent worldsheet metric hαβ(τ,σ)h_{\alpha\beta}(\tau,\sigma)hαβ​(τ,σ) — see Independent worldsheet metric — a brand-new field varied separately from XμX^\muXμ, and write:  SP=−14πα′∫dτ dσ −h  hαβ ∂αXμ ∂βXν ημν \boxed{\,S_P = -\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-h}\;h^{\alpha\beta}\,\partial_\alpha X^\mu\,\partial_\beta X^\nu\,\eta_{\mu\nu}\,}SP​=−4πα′1​∫dτdσ−h​hαβ∂α​Xμ∂β​Xνημν​​ with h≡det⁡(hαβ)h\equiv\det(h_{\alpha\beta})h≡det(hαβ​) and hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ the matrix inverse, hαβhβγ=δγαh^{\alpha\beta}h_{\beta\gamma}=\delta^\alpha_\gammahαβhβγ​=δγα​. This is DDD scalar fields XμX^\muXμ living on a 2D worldsheet whose own metric is hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ — free scalars coupled to 2D gravity. The flat-gauge action S=−14πα′∫ηαβ∂αX⋅∂βXS=-\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int \eta^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta XS=−4πα′1​∫ηαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X is just SPS_PSP​ with −h hαβ\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}−h​hαβ frozen to ηαβ\eta^{\alpha\beta}ηαβ; Polyakov promotes that frozen background to a field.

    The single structural fact that everything rests on: no square root touches the XXX fields. The action is quadratic in ∂X\partial X∂X. So:

    • XμX^\muXμ obeys a clean wave-type equation, ∂α(−h hαβ∂βXμ)=0\partial_\alpha(\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\beta X^\mu)=0∂α​(−h​hαβ∂β​Xμ)=0 (derived in Polyakov action).
    • Once hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ is gauge-fixed, the XXX path integral is Gaussian — the reason the worldsheet becomes a tractable 2D conformal field theory.

    Why 1/(4πα′)1/(4\pi\alpha')1/(4πα′) and not 1/(2πα′)1/(2\pi\alpha')1/(2πα′)? The coefficient is forced, not chosen. When we eliminate hhh below we must land exactly on Nambu–Goto with its 1/(2πα′)1/(2\pi\alpha')1/(2πα′); the factor of 2 is supplied by the 2D identity hαβhαβ=2h^{\alpha\beta}h_{\alpha\beta}=2hαβhαβ​=2 (a 2D metric contracted with its inverse is δαα=2\delta^\alpha_\alpha=2δαα​=2). The equivalence check below closes this.

    Classical equivalence: hand the geometry back

    hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ is auxiliary: the action contains no derivatives of it, so its Euler–Lagrange equation is purely algebraic. We can solve it and substitute the solution straight back — "integrating out" a non-propagating field. Varying hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ gives (using δ−h=−12−h hαβ δhαβ\delta\sqrt{-h}=-\tfrac12\sqrt{-h}\,h_{\alpha\beta}\,\delta h^{\alpha\beta}δ−h​=−21​−h​hαβ​δhαβ; full derivation in Worldsheet stress tensor): \underbrace{\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X}_{=\,\gamma_{\alpha\beta}} - \tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}\,(h^{\gamma\delta}\partial_\gamma X\cdot\partial_\delta X) = 0. \tag{1} This is the worldsheet stress tensor set to zero, Tαβ=0T_{\alpha\beta}=0Tαβ​=0 — the constraints, now produced by the theory instead of imposed. The second term has no free indices (it's hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ times a scalar), so (1) says hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ is proportional to the induced metric at every point: h_{\alpha\beta} = f^2(\tau,\sigma)\,\gamma_{\alpha\beta}, \tag{2} with f2(τ,σ)f^2(\tau,\sigma)f2(τ,σ) an arbitrary positive function of position. The auxiliary metric is forced to be a local rescaling of the real induced geometry — it carries no information beyond that one free scale f2f^2f2.

    Now substitute (2) back. In 2D, scaling hαβ→f2γαβh_{\alpha\beta}\to f^2\gamma_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​→f2γαβ​ gives −h=f2−γ\sqrt{-h}=f^2\sqrt{-\gamma}−h​=f2−γ​ and hαβ=f−2γαβh^{\alpha\beta}=f^{-2}\gamma^{\alpha\beta}hαβ=f−2γαβ, so \sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta} = f^2\sqrt{-\gamma}\cdot f^{-2}\gamma^{\alpha\beta} = \sqrt{-\gamma}\,\gamma^{\alpha\beta} \tag{3} — the arbitrary factor f2f^2f2 cancels exactly. Then, using γαβγαβ=2\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=2γαβγαβ​=2, SP  ⟶  −14πα′∫dτ dσ −γ γαβγαβ⏟= 2  =  −12πα′∫dτ dσ −γ  =  SNG.S_P \;\longrightarrow\; -\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma}\,\underbrace{\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}}_{=\,2} \;=\; -\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma} \;=\; S_{\mathrm{NG}}.SP​⟶−4πα′1​∫dτdσ−γ​=2γαβγαβ​​​=−2πα′1​∫dτdσ−γ​=SNG​. So on shell for hhh, Polyakov = Nambu–Goto. The 14πα′⋅2=12πα′\tfrac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\cdot 2=\tfrac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}4πα′1​⋅2=2πα′1​ is the promised factor-of-2 bookkeeping: the boxed coefficient is exactly right.

    That f2f^2f2 dropped out of both the XXX equation (via (3)) and the action is not luck. The action's blindness to f2f^2f2 is Weyl symmetry — the extra local rescaling gauge symmetry Polyakov buys, and the thing that makes the worldsheet conformal.

    Why this is the better starting point

    Nambu–Goto Polyakov
    Fields XμX^\muXμ XμX^\muXμ and hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​
    XXX-dependence det⁡∂X∂X\sqrt{\det\partial X\partial X}det∂X∂X​ (nonpolynomial) quadratic in ∂X\partial X∂X
    Constraints imposed by hand fall out as the hhh equation of motion
    Path integral intractable Gaussian in XXX
    Gauge symmetry reparam. (2D diffeo) reparam. + Weyl

    Same classical string, then, but Polyakov's extra field and extra symmetry turn the worldsheet into a quantizable 2D field theory. Everything downstream — the Worldsheet stress tensor, Free boson OPEs, the Virasoro algebra and central charge — is built on SPS_PSP​, never on SNGS_{\mathrm{NG}}SNG​.

    Open questions

    • Quantum equivalence. Classically, eliminating hαβh_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​ gives Nambu–Goto exactly. Quantum mechanically, gauge-fixing hhh in the path integral introduces Faddeev–Popov ghosts and a potential Weyl anomaly, tamed only if the total central charge vanishes; developed in Weyl invariance and the ghost module.
    • Global metric data. The relation hαβ=f2γαβh_{\alpha\beta}=f^2\gamma_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​=f2γαβ​ is local. On higher-genus worldsheets a finite-dimensional space of moduli survives gauge-fixing — not a flaw in the action, but the integration variables of the later Riemann surface story in Moduli spaces.

    Exercise

    The boxed 1/(4πα′)1/(4\pi\alpha')1/(4πα′) looks like it disagrees with Nambu–Goto's 1/(2πα′)1/(2\pi\alpha')1/(2πα′) by a factor of 2. Show, without assuming the answer, that the coefficient is forced: starting from a general coefficient −λ∫−h hαβ∂αX⋅∂βX-\lambda\int\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}\partial_\alpha X\cdot\partial_\beta X−λ∫−h​hαβ∂α​X⋅∂β​X, carry out the hhh-elimination and determine the λ\lambdaλ that reproduces SNG=−12πα′∫−γS_{\mathrm{NG}}=-\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\int\sqrt{-\gamma}SNG​=−2πα′1​∫−γ​. Which 2D identity supplies the factor of 2, and where would it fail in d≠2d\neq2d=2 worldsheet dimensions?

    Solution

    The hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ equation of motion is independent of the overall constant λ\lambdaλ (it multiplies both terms of (1) equally), so it still gives hαβ=f2γαβh_{\alpha\beta}=f^2\gamma_{\alpha\beta}hαβ​=f2γαβ​. Substituting via (3), −h hαβ→−γ γαβ\sqrt{-h}\,h^{\alpha\beta}\to\sqrt{-\gamma}\,\gamma^{\alpha\beta}−h​hαβ→−γ​γαβ with f2f^2f2 cancelling, so S→−λ∫dτ dσ −γ γαβγαβ.S\to-\lambda\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma}\,\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}.S→−λ∫dτdσ−γ​γαβγαβ​. The factor of 2 comes from γαβγαβ=δαα=tr⁡(12×2)=2\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=\delta^\alpha_\alpha=\operatorname{tr}(\mathbb{1}_{2\times2})=2γαβγαβ​=δαα​=tr(12×2​)=2 — a metric contracted with its inverse gives the trace of the identity, which equals the worldsheet dimension. Thus S→−2λ∫dτ dσ −γ=!−12πα′∫dτ dσ −γ⟹λ=14πα′.S\to-2\lambda\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma}\stackrel{!}{=}-\frac{1}{2\pi\alpha'}\int d\tau\,d\sigma\,\sqrt{-\gamma}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \boxed{\lambda=\frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}.}S→−2λ∫dτdσ−γ​=!−2πα′1​∫dτdσ−γ​⟹λ=4πα′1​.​ The factor is not free; matching Nambu–Goto pins it. The d=2d=2d=2 specialness runs deeper than the numerology γαβγαβ=2\gamma^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=2γαβγαβ​=2. In a ddd-dimensional worldvolume the hhh-elimination itself breaks down: contract the hhh-equation of motion γαβ−12hαβ(hγδγγδ)=0\gamma_{\alpha\beta}-\tfrac12 h_{\alpha\beta}(h^{\gamma\delta}\gamma_{\gamma\delta})=0γαβ​−21​hαβ​(hγδγγδ​)=0 with hαβh^{\alpha\beta}hαβ to get (1−d2) hαβγαβ=0(1-\tfrac d2)\,h^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=0(1−2d​)hαβγαβ​=0. For d≠2d\neq2d=2 this forces hαβγαβ=0h^{\alpha\beta}\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=0hαβγαβ​=0, whereupon the equation of motion collapses to γαβ=0\gamma_{\alpha\beta}=0γαβ​=0 — the induced metric is driven to zero, not to f2γαβf^2\gamma_{\alpha\beta}f2γαβ​. So there is no finite λ\lambdaλ reproducing the area action away from d=2d=2d=2; the trick works at all only because d=2d=2d=2 makes the trace part of the constraint vacuous, leaving the Weyl factor f2f^2f2 free to cancel.
