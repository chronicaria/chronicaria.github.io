Joan Beringer

Snapshot

  • Role: backup center behind Rudy Gobert, and a live candidate for power-forward minutes beside him.
  • 2025-26 baseline: CBS listed 40 games with roughly 3.9 PTS, 2.3 REB, and 0.7 BLK in limited minutes.
  • Ceiling evidence: produced 24 points, 13 rebounds, and seven blocks on April 12.
  • Status: played Summer League game one — 18 points, 11 rebounds, four blocks in 25 minutes — and was held out of game two precautionarily with back tightness.

Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: 140-200; specialist stash whose value jumps in punt-FT%.
  • Points: 150-210; rebound and block bonuses matter.
  • Dynasty: 175-325. The range is wide because Gobert blocks the centre job but not the frontcourt minutes.

Role, floor and ceiling

Median role is 14-18 minutes across both frontcourt spots. Floor: back tightness recurs, foul trouble limits him to the low teens, and Minnesota signs a veteran four into one of its two open slots. Ceiling: 24 minutes, 10/7 with two blocks and elite FG%, which can produce top-100 specialist value.

Risk monitor

Back tightness must be rechecked in camp, though the Summer League absence was precautionary rather than diagnostic. Poor FT%, raw positioning, and foul risk make him build-dependent. Minnesota's lack of another standard-contract center gives him a clearer path than most developmental bigs, and the unsettled power forward slot gives him a second one — but the same two open roster slots are the direct threat to both.

Career record

Pre-NBA: French, born November 11, 2006 in Sélestat. He did not pick up a basketball until roughly age 15 — he played football until he could no longer find cleats in his size — which is the single most important fact about his profile. Came through Strasbourg Saint-Joseph and then SIG Strasbourg's youth system, jumping from 4.1 points in the U18 Espoirs league in 2022-23 to 17.4 in 2023-24. Signed a four-year deal with Cedevita Olimpija in November 2024 and was promoted straight to the senior team. In 2024-25 he played 33 ABA League games at 17.2 minutes: 4.8 points, 4.1 rebounds, 0.6 assists, 0.5 steals, 0.7 blocks on .604 from the field and .558 from the line, plus 19 EuroCup games. Won the Slovenian League and Slovenian Cup in 2025. Draft: 2025, round 1, pick 17, Minnesota Timberwolves.

One NBA season, and the sample is small enough that the per-game line understates the per-minute production. The regular-season finale against New Orleans is the ceiling evidence: 24 points, 13 rebounds, two assists and seven blocks in 31 minutes.

Durability: 40 of 82 games as a rookie, almost entirely coaching decision rather than injury — Gobert took the center minutes and Beringer was in and out of the rotation. The only flag is back tightness that kept him out of Summer League game two in July 2026, described as precautionary after he opened with 18 points, 11 rebounds and four blocks in 25 minutes. Nothing chronic is on record.

Trajectory: A 19-year-old with roughly four years of organized basketball behind him, whose NBA production so far is entirely rim-running and shot-blocking — .663 from the field, zero three-point attempts, 0.3 assists. The developmental curve is the asset, not the current line. He is the only other true center on Minnesota's standard roster behind Gobert, and the summer power-forward reps add a second route to minutes rather than making him wait on an injury. He becomes fantasy-relevant at 20-plus minutes, which arrives either through a Gobert absence, a Gobert trade, or the power-forward experiment surviving camp; at that volume the blocks and field-goal percentage carry specialist value in punt-FT% builds, and the free-throw percentage and foul rate cap everything else.
