San Antonio Spurs

San Antonio returns a 62-win Finals core, adds Tobias Harris, and presents one elite fantasy anchor plus an unusually difficult three-guard minute puzzle.

Snapshot

  • As of: 2026-07-18. This is an offseason projection, not an official depth chart.
  • 2025-26 result: 62-20, second in the West by NBA.com's conference display, Western Conference champion, and a 4-1 NBA Finals loss to New York. The team ranked third in scoring (119.8), second in rebounds (47.0), ninth in assists (28.1), and eighth in opponent scoring (111.5). NBA team page, accessed 2026-07-18
  • Coaching: Mitch Johnson remains head coach. NBA team page, accessed 2026-07-18
  • Base case: 57-25, a top-three West seed and legitimate championship contender.
  • Outcome range: 52-30 if health and guard-role friction cost regular-season efficiency; 64-18 if Victor Wembanyama reaches 32 minutes, the young guards improve as shooters, and the defense remains elite. The realistic postseason band runs from a second-round exit to a championship.

Fantasy hierarchy and format notes

  1. Wembanyama is the only format-proof target. Dan Titus ranked him second in his April 2026 early first round and explicitly endorsed 1.01; Hashtag Basketball ranks him dynasty No. 1. NBA/Yahoo early ranks, 2026-04-14 · Hashtag dynasty rankings, accessed 2026-07-18
  2. Castle is better in points than 9-cat. Seven-plus assists and strong rebounds are offset by high turnovers and below-average free throws. Hashtag's expert dynasty list ranks him No. 40, while its July crowdsourced keeper market is more bullish at No. 24.
  3. Harper is the dynasty-over-redraft gap. Hashtag places him No. 17 in expert dynasty and No. 14 crowdsourced, but acknowledges patience is required behind two established guards. His postseason role and strong rim efficiency support 27 minutes; a 30-minute starting role requires a Fox move or an unusually small three-guard alignment.
  4. Fox is a value only after the role discount. His 2025-26 minutes and scoring fell sharply, but efficient 19/6 with steals remains useful. He is a categorical buy only if drafters price the Finals narrative below his stable regular-season role.
  5. Vassell, Harris and Champagnie cannibalize each other. Vassell offers threes without many turnovers; Harris offers efficient low-volume balance; Champagnie offers threes, rebounds and availability. Champagnie is the one with the starting job and the 82-game history, but none of the three has a safe 30-minute path while the roster is intact.

Role battles

  • Third guard: Fox and Castle are projected starters; Harper is projected sixth man and closer. The three together absorb 87 healthy-night minutes, leaving little on-ball work for anyone else.
  • Starting four: Champagnie has continuity, 68 starts, the Finals start and the larger contract; Harris has positional logic and self-creation. The loser can still reach 21-24 minutes by backing up both forward spots, so the fantasy gap between the two outcomes is narrower than the starting-job language suggests.
  • Back-end wing: Keldon is the established scorer and Bryant is the development priority, and the public read is that Bryant may overtake him early in the season; Barnes is now a depth piece rather than a rotation lock. Their healthy-night allotment is 29 combined minutes, distributed differently than last season.
  • Backup center: Kornet's proven screening, rebounding and rim protection give him the floor. Reed's athletic rebounding creates an upside path. Quaintance is not a competitor for 2026-27 minutes at all; his meniscus recovery points to 2027.

Health and transaction watch

  • Jayden Quaintance: San Antonio announced successful right-knee surgery on July 15. The procedure was a meniscus repair, and it was a known follow-up to the ACL he tore in February 2025 at Arizona State — the Spurs drafted him 20th knowing it was coming, and complications from that tear had already limited him to four games at Kentucky. He will miss all of training camp and the start of the season; league sources said multiple teams expected the procedure to cost him most of 2026-27, and the early Spurs expectation is that he is not available until some point in 2027. Treat a full redshirt as the base case and do not draft him in redraft. Spurs injury update, 2026-07-15 · Hoops Rumors, 2026-07-16
  • De'Aaron Fox: the right high ankle sprain originated in Game 4 of the second round against Minnesota, when Ayo Dosunmu landed on his ankle. He played through it in Games 5 and 6, missed the first two Western Conference Finals games, then reaggravated it in Game 3 when Lu Dort dove for a loose ball. This was a multi-round, production-degrading injury rather than a two-game absence that cleared. San Antonio attributes his Finals collapse to it directly: 18.8 PPG on 46.2% over his first 11 playoff games before the injury, 12.0 PPG on 35.3% after, and 12.8 PPG in the Finals itself, against 18.6 on 48.6% in the regular season. Whether the ankle normalised over the summer is the primary Fox draft risk, not a footnote. NBA Fox report, 2026-05-19 · ESPN, 2026-05-20 · Hoops Rumors, 2026-07
  • Dylan Harper: sustained a right-adductor issue in West Finals Game 2, returned and was off the injury report by Game 4. This one is genuinely an offseason monitoring item rather than an active absence. NBA Harper report, 2026-05-21

Correction 2026-07-18: this desk previously grouped Fox with Harper as a resolved two-game West Finals absence and described the injury as "ankle soreness," while the Fox dossier called it a right high ankle sprain. The high ankle sprain is the correct diagnosis and the injury dates to the second round, not the conference finals; per the team it was still suppressing his shooting through the Finals. The health-risk weighting on his 70-GP/30-MPG projection is raised accordingly. - Roster slot: One standard vacancy leaves this entire depth chart exposed to one more veteran addition or a two-way conversion. - Extension/cap signal: Wembanyama signed through 2031-32 and accepted the 25% framework rather than a larger escalator, improving long-term flexibility but not changing his 2026-27 role.
