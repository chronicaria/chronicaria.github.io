2025-26 baseline

Carter's official career table records 53 games at 16.4 minutes in 2025-26, with 6.4 points, 1.7 rebounds, 1.6 assists, 0.8 steals, 0.2 blocks, and 1.5 threes. He shot .401 from the field and .789 from the line.

Orlando re-signed him July 1. The return provides veteran ball security and point-of-attack defense, not a guarantee that his prior 16-minute role survives a healthier Suggs, Black's growth, and Richardson's development.

Role and pathway

Carter is the first guard off the bench behind Suggs and Black, and the second unit's point-of-attack defender. Richardson may take developmental minutes from him on some nights, and Sweeney's blitzing scheme is a stylistic fit for exactly what Carter does, which is the argument for the role holding.

His fantasy pathway still requires absences. At 12 minutes he is a threes-and-steals specialist without the volume to matter in standard leagues; at 20-plus, with Suggs or Black out, he becomes a legitimate deep-league stream. The assist rate stays too modest for a classic backup-point-guard profile at any minute total.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four seasons at West Virginia (2014-18): 144 GP, 29.9 MPG, 12.2 PTS, 3.7 REB, 3.9 AST, 2.3 STL on .407 FG, .355 3P, .798 FT, peaking as a senior at 17.3 points, 6.6 assists and 3.0 steals in 35.5 minutes. Two-time NABC Defensive Player of the Year, two-time Big 12 Defensive Player of the Year, two-time Lefty Driesell Award winner, and the inaugural Naismith Defensive Player of the Year in 2018. Consensus second-team All-American as a senior. The defensive résumé is the reason he has lasted eight seasons. Draft: 2018, second round, 32nd overall by Memphis.

Career three-point percentage is .377 on roughly three attempts a game — the shooting has been the durable half of the profile, the .395 overall field-goal mark the persistent drag. The 2022-23 Milwaukee season (81 games, 39 starts, 22.4 MPG) remains his high-water mark and the only year he cleared standard-league relevance. ESPN's table carries a spurious one-game 2022-23 Boston row that pushes its career total to 466; the per-team rows sum to 465, which matches every other source, so it is excluded here.

Durability: 465 games across eight seasons. He has never had a season-ending injury on record; the low totals in 2023-24 through 2025-26 reflect coach's decisions and roster position rather than verified health absences. His Orlando half-season at 20.4 minutes across 30 games is the most he has played since Milwaukee.

Trajectory: Eight seasons of the same player — a point-of-attack defender who spaces the floor at a league-average rate and generates almost nothing else. The Orlando split is genuinely encouraging in isolation: 7.2 points, 2.3 assists, 0.9 steals and 1.5 threes in 20 minutes is a usable deep-league line, and Orlando re-signed him on July 1 specifically for that. But it was produced in a guard rotation thinned by absences. At 31 he is competing with a developing Jase Richardson for the same second-unit minutes, and the assist rate has never been high enough to make him a true backup point guard. He matters in fantasy only when Suggs or Black misses time and his minutes push past 18 — at which point stream him for threes and steals and drop him when they return.
