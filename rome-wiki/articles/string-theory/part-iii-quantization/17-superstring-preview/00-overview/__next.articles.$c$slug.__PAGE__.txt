3:Tf9c4,<h2 id="three-diseases-of-the-bosonic-string">Three diseases of the bosonic string</h2>
<p>You reached the end of Part III with a fully quantized <strong>bosonic</strong> string and its spectrum from the <a href="/rome-wiki/articles/string-theory/part-ii-classical-strings/08-relativistic-point-particle/mass-shell-relation/" class="wiki-link">Mass-shell relation</a>. It is beautiful and unphysical, for three independently fatal reasons:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Only bosons.</strong> Every state in the bosonic spectrum is a boson, but matter is made of fermions — electrons, quarks, neutrinos. A theory with no fermions cannot describe our world.</li>
<li><strong>A tachyon.</strong> The ground state has <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>M</mi><mn>2</mn></msup><mo>&#x3C;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">M^2 &#x3C; 0</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8532em;vertical-align:-0.0391em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.8141em;"><span style="top:-3.063em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight">2</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">&#x3C;</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span></span></span></span>. A negative mass-squared is not a fast particle; it is an <em>instability</em>. The vacuum you expanded around is a hilltop, not a valley, and the theory wants to roll off it — like a pencil balanced on its tip, where <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><msup><mi>M</mi><mn>2</mn></msup><mo>&#x3C;</mo><mn>0</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">M^2&#x3C;0</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8532em;vertical-align:-0.0391em;"></span><span class="mord"><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="msupsub"><span class="vlist-t"><span class="vlist-r"><span class="vlist" style="height:0.8141em;"><span style="top:-3.063em;margin-right:0.05em;"><span class="pstrut" style="height:2.7em;"></span><span class="sizing reset-size6 size3 mtight"><span class="mord mtight">2</span></span></span></span></span></span></span></span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">&#x3C;</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">0</span></span></span></span> says the wobble grows instead of oscillating.</li>
<li><strong>Twenty-six dimensions.</strong> Consistency forced <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>D</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>26</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">D=26</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6833em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">D</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span><span class="mrel">=</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.2778em;"></span></span><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.6444em;"></span><span class="mord">26</span></span></span></span>, far more than the world seems to want and a great deal to hide.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="honest-scope-what-this-preview-does-and-does-not-prove">Honest scope: what this preview does and does not prove</h2>
