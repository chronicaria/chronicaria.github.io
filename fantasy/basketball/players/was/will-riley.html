Will Riley

Will Riley is a young scoring stash with meaningful long-term upside but only eight healthy-roster minutes in Washington's current logjam.

Role and baseline

  • 2025-26: 74 games, 22.1 MPG.
  • July signal: averaged 25.0 points, 4.5 rebounds and 2.5 assists across two Summer League games, including a 32-point, six-three performance, before a rest shutdown. Translation weight is low.
  • 2026-27 base role: development wing/four; 8 base minutes (2 SF, 6 PF). Dybantsa, Coulibaly, Johnson, George, Middleton and Champagnie all compete for adjacent work.

Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: top 180-220 base; volume is too low for standard leagues, and the stocks line is weak.
  • Points: deep-league scoring stash because his offensive game translates better than his category scarcity.
  • Dynasty: top 80-110. Age, shot-making and a likely future veteran clearing give him more long-term than immediate value.
  • Best use: hold only in deep dynasty; use as a redraft watch-list name if Middleton or another wing leaves.

Floor, median and ceiling

  • Floor: spends long stretches outside the competitive rotation.
  • Median: 8 minutes and no standard-league value.
  • Ceiling: 25-plus minutes after roster changes and a top-120 scoring/threes profile.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Illinois, one season (2024-25): 12.6 PTS / 4.1 REB / 2.2 AST in 25.7 MPG over 35 games (9 starts) on .432/.326/.724 — Big Ten Sixth Man of the Year as an 18-year-old freshman. Canadian, from Kitchener, Ontario. Draft: 2025, Round 1, Pick 21, selected by Utah and traded to Washington.

The season strengthened as it went: an 18.2-point, 46.4%-from-three stretch in February, back-to-back 30-point games in April (first Wizards rookie to do so since Calbert Cheaney in 1994), and a 30-point/5-steal game that made him the youngest to hit that line since LeBron James in 2004.

Durability: 74 of 82 games as a rookie with no reported injury absences of note; the July 2026 Summer League exit was a rest shutdown, not an injury.

Trajectory: The record is a genuinely productive age-19 rookie season — 10 points on respectable efficiency with a late-season surge — which sits oddly against the 8-minute base role this dossier projects for 2026-27 after Washington's offseason wing additions (Dybantsa et al.). That tension is the whole fantasy question: his one season of history says he out-produces an end-of-rotation role the moment he gets minutes. He becomes fantasy-relevant if the rotation squeeze resolves toward him — via a Middleton or other veteran clearing — at which point 25+ minutes of scoring and threes with 80% FT would put him inside the top 120 in points formats quickly.
