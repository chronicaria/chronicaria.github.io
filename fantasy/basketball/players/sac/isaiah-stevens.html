Role and evidence

Stevens played three NBA games in 2025-26, averaging 3.3 points and 3.3 assists in 14.3 minutes; the sample is too small for rate projection (ESPN statistics). Before Sacramento signed him, he had averaged 12.3 points and 7.6 assists in 35.2 G League minutes according to the official two-way announcement.

The G League continues to list Stevens on a two-way contract. ESPN places him third at point guard behind Acuff and Monk. A right-ankle issue ended his 2025-26 NBA season, but he participated in 2026 Summer League and no current long-term restriction was found.

Range

  • Ceiling: 6 assists in 18 minutes if both lead guards are unavailable.
  • Median: Stockton lead guard and occasional NBA call-up.
  • Floor: no fantasy-relevant NBA appearances.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Five seasons at Colorado State, 2019 to 2024, where he left as the program's all-time leader in points (2,350), assists (863) and steals (158) — a four-time All-Mountain West selection. His senior year in 2023-24 brought 17.9 points, 6.7 assists and 3.4 rebounds, First-team All-Mountain West, the conference assist lead, a school-record 14-assist game in November 2023, and a Colorado State team that reached No. 13 in the AP poll, the highest ranking in program history. Draft: undrafted (2024). Signed with Miami, was waived, and spent 2024-25 with the Sioux Falls Skyforce before Sacramento signed him to a two-way contract in July 2025; he played 2025-26 with the Stockton Kings, averaging 14.1 points, 3.0 rebounds and 8.6 assists in the G League with a 15-assist game against Salt Lake City in January 2026.

Durability: Nothing to assess at the NBA level. The one relevant item is that he saw no action at either the NBA or G League level after March 21, 2026 because of a sore right ankle, which ended his season. He participated in 2026 Summer League and no ongoing restriction was found as of mid-July.

Trajectory: Stevens is a 25-year-old, 5-foot-11 two-way point guard whose one elite skill is passing — 8.6 assists a game in the G League, and a 3.3-assist NBA cameo in 14 minutes that at least did not contradict it. The problem is the rest of the profile: he has made zero NBA three-pointers, his size limits him defensively, and undersized pass-first guards without a jumper are the most replaceable archetype in the league. His median 2026-27 is lead guard in Stockton with scattered call-ups. He becomes relevant in an assist-premium deep dynasty format only if both Kings lead guards are unavailable simultaneously and he holds the third two-way slot through camp; the more likely thing to watch is whether the jumper appears at all, because without it there is no NBA role to grow into.
