Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: 50-75; assists and steals justify a middle-round price, while turnovers and post-injury uncertainty cap aggression.
  • Points: 55-80, with settings that reward double-doubles and steals pushing him upward.
  • Dynasty: 65-95; a veteran hold whose value can recover with 65 healthy games but is unlikely to regain a youthful premium.

Role, floor and ceiling

Murray should start and handle the first unit, while Jeremiah Fears remains a shared-creation partner rather than a pure backup. Floor: 24–26 minutes with rest management, roughly 14/5/5 and fewer than 55 games. Ceiling: 32 minutes, 18/6/8 and 1.7 steals if the Achilles is fully normalized and Jordan Poole moves out of the rotation or off the roster.

Injury and transaction monitor

Murray debuted February 25 after 13 months rehabbing the right Achilles rupture suffered January 31, 2025. He played 14 games and then missed the Pelicans' final five with a bruised left hand. NBA/AP return feature, 2026-02-24 His rehabilitation-period mentorship of Fears and public description of the rookie as part of the franchise's future support a two-guard coexistence model.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Washington (one season, 2015-16). Started all 34 games at 33.5 MPG for 16.1 points, 5.9 rebounds, 4.4 assists and 1.8 steals on .416 FG, .288 3PT and .663 FT; second-team All-Pac-12 and Pac-12 All-Freshman, and he led all conference freshmen in scoring, assists and steals while setting the Washington freshman assist record (151). Draft: 2016, first round, 29th overall, San Antonio. He was later traded to Atlanta in July 2022 and to New Orleans in July 2024.

Fantasy history: the peak was 2021-22 through 2023-24. Publicly cited season-end 8-category per-game finishes place him 23rd in 2022-23 and 20th in 2023-24; his 9-category rank sits meaningfully lower in both years because 2.2 and 2.6 turnovers are a real drag at that price. The 2021-22 season was his best category profile — 9.2 assists and 2.0 steals led the league in steals — but a precise 9-cat rank for it is not verified here. The 2024-25 and 2025-26 seasons are unrankable in any meaningful sense at 31 and 14 games. Points leagues have consistently treated him better than category leagues: the assist and steal volume plus low-value turnovers make him a firmer top-40 points asset than a top-40 9-cat asset.

Durability: 38, 81, 0, 66, 67, 68, 74, 78, 31, 14. Two catastrophic lower-body injuries bracket the career — a torn right ACL on October 8, 2018 that erased 2018-19, and a ruptured right Achilles on January 31, 2025 against Boston (noncontact, first quarter) that ended 2024-25 and cost all but the final stretch of 2025-26. He returned February 24, 2026 after roughly thirteen months, played 14 games, then lost time to a hand/finger problem. The 2019-20 through 2023-24 stretch is the honest read on his availability when healthy: 66 to 78 games a year, five straight seasons, no soft-tissue pattern.

Trajectory: The arc is a steady, minutes-driven climb from 8.5 MPG to 36.4 MPG across six seasons, with the genuine leap in 2021-22 — assists jumped from 5.4 to 9.2 on a near-identical field goal percentage, which is a usage and role change (he became San Antonio's sole initiator), not a skill jump. Atlanta traded scoring for playmaking: 2023-24 was his highest-scoring season at 22.5 on 2.6 threes, but assists were flat and steals dipped, because he shared the ball with Trae Young. The 2024-25 New Orleans sample is the one warning sign that predates the Achilles — .393 from the field and 3.4 turnovers, both career worsts, in a season where the shot diet got harder. He is entering his age-30 season on a repaired Achilles, the single worst injury for a guard whose value has always rested on defensive activity and downhill pressure; the 14-game 2025-26 line shows the passing and steals returned intact and the efficiency actually spiked, but 14 games at 27.8 minutes is not evidence about a 70-game workload. Treat the 2019-20 to 2020-21 version — mid-teens scoring, six-ish assists, 1.5 steals, near-.460 shooting — as the realistic 2026-27 center of gravity rather than the Atlanta peak.
