    Up: Crypto

    Research question

    Which liquid non-major token combines real usage, defensible value capture, manageable supply risk, workable access, and asymmetric upside without requiring narrative-first reasoning?

    Current hierarchy

    1. JUP — the only tiny-only capital candidate today, but not eligible for size-up.
    2. PENDLE — the strongest product-economics lead if revenue, emissions, access, and admin gates align.
    3. SYRUP — the best credit/RWA watch if buyback accounting and borrower quality become sufficiently transparent.
    4. AERO — a strong fee engine whose liquid-token economics remain inferior to active locked exposure.
    5. PUMP — an event watch with meaningful upside and equally meaningful unlock, legal, and market-structure risk.

    Deeper work

    • PENDLE memo and PENDLE deep memo
    • AERO memo and AERO deep memo
    • SYRUP memo and SYRUP deep memo

    Method

    The committee treats market screens as leads, not proof. It rejects token theses that confuse protocol success with passive-token capture, discounts active lock/stake mechanics when evaluating liquid ownership, and requires wallet-visible evidence for buyback, burn, or distribution claims.

    Open questions

    • Which metrics should an hourly committee monitor without reacting to noise?
    • What evidence should automatically demote or retire a candidate?
    • When does an active strategy belong in a separate dossier from the liquid token?
