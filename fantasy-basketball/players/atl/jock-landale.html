Jock Landale

Jock Landale is the favored backup center and a short-term streamer whenever Atlanta gives him 20-plus minutes.

Role and 2025-26 baseline

Landale averaged 10.6 points, 5.7 rebounds, 1.7 assists, 0.5 steals, 0.5 blocks, and 1.1 threes in 22.1 minutes across 68 total games. In 23 Atlanta games he produced 9.1 points, 4.1 rebounds, and 1.7 assists in 19.4 minutes while shooting 51.6% FG and 39.1% from three. Atlanta re-signed him for one year, strongly supporting the No. 2 center role. ESPN stats · Hawks re-signing release

Fantasy fit and range

  • 9-cat/points: picks 150-200 in deep leagues; standard-league stream when starting or projected above 22 minutes.
  • Dynasty: low-ceiling center depth; the one-year deal and incoming rookies reduce long-term security.
  • Floor: 12 minutes if Atlanta accelerates Zuby Ejiofor or plays Onyeka Okongwu longer.
  • Ceiling: 11/6/2 with one three in 22 minutes, useful in 14-team leagues.
  • Health: a high right-ankle sprain ended his regular season and kept him out of the playoffs. Re-signing implies optimism, but Atlanta has not published a formal full-clearance notice.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Saint Mary's, four years (2014-18). Grew from a 2.1-point freshman to 16.9 points and 9.5 rebounds as a junior and 21.1 points and 10.2 rebounds as a senior, winning WCC Player of the Year and consensus second-team All-America honors in 2018; his No. 34 is retired by the program. Three seasons abroad followed: Partizan Belgrade in 2018-19 (12.0 points, 5.6 rebounds on 56.6% shooting across 24 ABA League games, ABA Ideal Starting Five; 11.2 points and 6.5 rebounds in 16 EuroCup games), Žalgiris Kaunas in 2019-20 (11.0 points, 4.4 rebounds, Lithuanian league and cup double), and Melbourne United in the 2020-21 NBL (16.4 points, 7.8 rebounds, 2.3 assists, championship and Grand Final MVP). Draft: undrafted (2018).

Durability: 54, 69, 56, 42 and 68 games across five seasons — never a full slate, but the absences have been short and scattered rather than one long shutdown. The 42-game 2024-25 in Houston was the thinnest, and a knee problem kept him out of the Rockets' first-round series. A high right ankle sprain closed his 2025-26 regular season and cost him Atlanta's playoff run.

Trajectory: Four seasons of 11-to-14 minutes as a third big, then a genuine role change in 2025-26 — 23.6 minutes in Memphis at 11.3 and 6.5, with a three-point shot (1.1 makes a game) that had not existed before. That stretch is the case for him; the 19.4-minute Atlanta sample after the trade is the more realistic baseline behind Onyeka Okongwu. He is a streamer, not a hold: he becomes rosterable if Okongwu misses time, since Atlanta's only other true center depth is unproven, and the 2025-26 Memphis line shows what he does with starter minutes. The one-year deal and the ankle both argue against paying up in dynasty.
