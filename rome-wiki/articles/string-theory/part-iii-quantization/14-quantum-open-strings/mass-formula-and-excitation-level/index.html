    Mass formula and excitation level

    A quantum open string weighs exactly what its internal vibration carries: \alpha' M^2 = N^\perp + a , where N^\perp counts the excitation and a is a universal shift fixed to -1 . Because N^\perp is a non-negative integer, the masses form a discrete tower — and the shift a=-1 is precisely what drags the first excited level down to zero mass, turning it into a photon.

    The mass ladder

    Think of the string as a bundle of independent harmonic oscillators, one per vibration mode. Quantum mechanics says each oscillator can only hold a whole number of quanta, and each quantum of the nnn-th mode costs nnn units of energy. A string's mass is nothing but its stored vibrational energy. Pluck it harder — excite higher modes — and you get a heavier particle. Since the energy comes in integer packets, the mass comes in a discrete ladder. Everything below makes that ladder precise.

    From p−p^-p− to M2M^2M2

    We want the mass, and mass is a statement about momentum. In light-cone variables the mass-shell relation is

    M2=−pμpμ=2p+p−−pIpI,M^2=-p_\mu p^\mu=2p^+p^--p^Ip^I,M2=−pμ​pμ=2p+p−−pIpI,

    which is −p2-p^2−p2 (mostly-plus signature; natural units ℏ=c=1\hbar=c=1ℏ=c=1) written in components p±=12(p0±p1)p^\pm=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2}(p^0\pm p^1)p±=2​1​(p0±p1); the transverse pieces pIp^IpI (I=2,…,D−1I=2,\dots,D-1I=2,…,D−1) enter with the ordinary spatial sign (see Mass-shell relation). The point of light-cone gauge is that p−p^-p− is not independent — it was solved from the constraints and packaged into the transverse Virasoro operator L0⊥L_0^\perpL0⊥​ (see Transverse Virasoro operators):

    2\alpha'p^-=\frac1{p^+}\big(L_0^\perp+a\big).\tag{1}

    Here aaa is the leftover constant from normal-ordering L0⊥L_0^\perpL0⊥​ — the only place quantum ordering ambiguity survives. Multiply (1) by p+/α′p^+/\alpha'p+/α′:

    2p+p−=1α′(L0⊥+a).2p^+p^-=\frac1{\alpha'}\big(L_0^\perp+a\big).2p+p−=α′1​(L0⊥​+a).

    Now feed in the explicit form L0⊥=α′pIpI+N⊥L_0^\perp=\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perpL0⊥​=α′pIpI+N⊥ (from Transverse Virasoro operators) and substitute both into M2M^2M2:

    M2=2p+p−−pIpI=1α′(α′pIpI+N⊥+a)−pIpI.M^2=2p^+p^--p^Ip^I=\frac1{\alpha'}\big(\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perp+a\big)-p^Ip^I.M2=2p+p−−pIpI=α′1​(α′pIpI+N⊥+a)−pIpI.

    The 1α′⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI\tfrac1{\alpha'}\cdot\alpha'p^Ip^I=+p^Ip^Iα′1​⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI term cancels the −pIpI-p^Ip^I−pIpI exactly, leaving the clean operator statement

    \boxed{\;M^2=\frac1{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp+a\big)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \alpha' M^2=N^\perp+a\;}\tag{2}

    The mass came out independent of the center-of-mass momentum pIp^IpI, as any relativistic mass must. That is not automatic. It happens only because the coefficient of pIpIp^Ip^IpIpI inside L0⊥L_0^\perpL0⊥​ is exactly α′\alpha'α′ — not 12\tfrac1221​, not 2α′2\alpha'2α′ — so that 1α′⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI\tfrac1{\alpha'}\cdot\alpha'p^Ip^I=+p^Ip^Iα′1​⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI kills the −pIpI-p^Ip^I−pIpI from the mass shell. The cancellation is a built-in sanity check on the factors in L0⊥L_0^\perpL0⊥​: a residual pIp^IpI dependence in M2M^2M2 means a factor is wrong upstream.

    What the level N⊥N^\perpN⊥ contributes

    N⊥N^\perpN⊥ is the total-excitation operator (from Open-string Fock space); on a Fock state with occupation numbers λn,I\lambda_{n,I}λn,I​,

    N⊥=∑n=1∞n anI†anI,N⊥∣λ⟩=Nλ⊥∣λ⟩,Nλ⊥=∑n,In λn,I.N^\perp=\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,a_n^{I\dagger}a_n^I,\qquad N^\perp|\lambda\rangle=N_\lambda^\perp|\lambda\rangle,\quad N_\lambda^\perp=\sum_{n,I}n\,\lambda_{n,I}.N⊥=∑n=1∞​nanI†​anI​,N⊥∣λ⟩=Nλ⊥​∣λ⟩,Nλ⊥​=∑n,I​nλn,I​.

    The weight nnn is the crucial feature: exciting the nnn-th mode once raises N⊥N^\perpN⊥ by nnn, not by 111. So

    α′M2=a+∑n=1∞n anI†anI,\alpha' M^2=a+\sum_{n=1}^\infty n\,a_n^{I\dagger}a_n^I,α′M2=a+∑n=1∞​nanI†​anI​,

    and each quantum of the nnn-th oscillator adds nnn units of 1/α′1/\alpha'1/α′ to M2M^2M2. The eigenvalues of N⊥N^\perpN⊥ are 0,1,2,3,…0,1,2,3,\dots0,1,2,3,…, so the spectrum is an evenly spaced ladder,

    α′M2=a, a+1, a+2,…\alpha' M^2=a,\ a+1,\ a+2,\dotsα′M2=a, a+1, a+2,…

    The classical string had a continuous range of masses; quantization forces this ladder. M2M^2M2 growing linearly with the level is the structural fingerprint of a string: the highest-spin state at each level traces a straight line in the spin–M2M^2M2 plane — a Regge trajectory, the pattern in hadron data that historically first suggested strings.

    Fixing the constant aaa

    The constant aaa is the c-number left behind when the symmetric L0⊥L_0^\perpL0⊥​ is normal-ordered. Written naively it is a zero-point sum over all D−2D-2D−2 transverse oscillator towers:

    a=formal12(D−2)∑n=1∞n.a\overset{\text{formal}}{=}\frac12(D-2)\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n.a=formal21​(D−2)∑n=1∞​n.

    This sum diverges — a warning that ordering can shift the entire mass ladder, not a number to be taken literally. A cutoff regulator (derived in Normal-ordering constant for open strings) separates a local UV divergence from a genuine finite Casimir remnant, ∑n≥1n→−112\sum_{n\ge1}n\to-\tfrac1{12}∑n≥1​n→−121​, giving the finite part

    a=−D−224.a=-\frac{D-2}{24}.a=−24D−2​.

    This is the honest content of the shorthand "ζ(−1)=−112\zeta(-1)=-\tfrac1{12}ζ(−1)=−121​": a regularized statement, never ordinary arithmetic. But regularization alone relates aaa to the number of transverse directions without pinning DDD.

    The pin comes from consistency. Demanding that the quantum boosts close, [M−I,M−J]=0[M^{-I},M^{-J}]=0[M−I,M−J]=0 (worked out in Light-cone Lorentz anomaly), forces two conditions simultaneously:

    D=26,a=−1,D=26,\qquad a=-1,D=26,a=−1,

    and these agree with the Casimir value, since −124(26−2)=−1-\tfrac1{24}(26-2)=-1−241​(26−2)=−1. So the physical formula is

    \boxed{\;\alpha' M^2=N^\perp-1\;}\tag{3}

    equivalently, the generator of τ\tauτ-translations is the light-cone Hamiltonian H=2α′p+p−=L0⊥−1H=2\alpha'p^+p^-=L_0^\perp-1H=2α′p+p−=L0⊥​−1.

    The payoff: the shift is what makes a photon

    Read the bottom of the ladder with a=−1a=-1a=−1:

    N⊥N^\perpN⊥ α′M2=N⊥−1\alpha' M^2=N^\perp-1α′M2=N⊥−1 character
    000 −1-1−1 tachyon (negative M2M^2M2, unstable)
    111 000 massless — a photon
    222 +1+1+1 massive tensor

    Without the shift, the level-1 states α−1I∣p+,p⃗T⟩\alpha_{-1}^I|p^+,\vec p_T\rangleα−1I​∣p+,p​T​⟩ would be massive. The downward shift of exactly 111 drags them to M2=0M^2=0M2=0 — and a massless state carrying a transverse vector index is precisely a photon. Open string theory contains electromagnetism, even though we started from the Nambu–Goto action, which knew nothing of it. And it works only because aaa is an integer: had aaa been any non-integer, no level would ever land on M2=0M^2=0M2=0 and there would be no massless particles at all. The photon is not put in by hand; it is squeezed out by the arithmetic −124(D−2)=−1-\tfrac1{24}(D-2)=-1−241​(D−2)=−1.

    The full state-by-state breakdown — basis states, degeneracies, particle content — lives in Critical dimension and the spectrum. This note supplies the engine: α′M2\alpha'M^2α′M2 is the level N⊥N^\perpN⊥ shifted down by exactly one, and that shift is the only reason the spectrum has a photon at all.

    One landmine: don't copy the closed-string formula

    The closed string obeys a formula that looks different:

    M2=2α′(N⊥+Nˉ⊥−2),M^2=\frac{2}{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp+\bar N^\perp-2\big),M2=α′2​(N⊥+Nˉ⊥−2),

    where Nˉ⊥\bar N^\perpNˉ⊥ is the level of the independent left-moving oscillator tower. With level matching N⊥=Nˉ⊥N^\perp=\bar N^\perpN⊥=Nˉ⊥ this reads α′4M2=N⊥−1\frac{\alpha'}{4}M^2=N^\perp-14α′​M2=N⊥−1: the closed-string ground state sits at α′M2=−4\alpha'M^2=-4α′M2=−4, not −1-1−1. Two traps when comparing sources:

    • Factor of 4. The closed string's zero mode is α0μ=α′/2 pμ\alpha_0^\mu=\sqrt{\alpha'/2}\,p^\muα0μ​=α′/2​pμ, versus the open string's α0I=2α′ pI\alpha_0^I=\sqrt{2\alpha'}\,p^Iα0I​=2α′​pI. The differing 2\sqrt22​'s square to a factor of 444 in the spacing: 4/α′4/\alpha'4/α′ (closed) vs 1/α′1/\alpha'1/α′ (open).
    • Sign of the shift. Some texts define the constant with the opposite sign, writing α′M2=N−a\alpha'M^2=N-aα′M2=N−a with a=+1a=+1a=+1 and calling aaa the intercept. This vault writes α′M2=N⊥+a\alpha'M^2=N^\perp+aα′M2=N⊥+a with a=−1a=-1a=−1, so the intercept is −a=1-a=1−a=1. Same downward physical shift, opposite bookkeeping sign — check which convention is in force before transplanting a value of aaa.

    For everything in this module: open string, spacing 1/α′1/\alpha'1/α′, shift −1-1−1.

    Open questions

    • Formula (3) is physical only once the Lorentz-anomaly argument in Light-cone Lorentz anomaly grants D=26D=26D=26; before that, (2) with general aaa is all we have.
    • Normal-ordering constant for open strings extracts the finite Casimir part, but why this subtraction is compatible with the underlying conformal symmetry is deferred to Worldsheet stress tensor and Virasoro algebra and central charge.

    Exercise

    The mass formula (2) is derived from the light-cone data 2α′p−=1p+(L0⊥+a)2\alpha'p^-=\frac1{p^+}(L_0^\perp+a)2α′p−=p+1​(L0⊥​+a) and L0⊥=α′pIpI+N⊥L_0^\perp=\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perpL0⊥​=α′pIpI+N⊥. Suppose a careless student instead wrote the wrong zero-mode relation L0⊥=2α′pIpI+N⊥L_0^\perp=2\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perpL0⊥​=2α′pIpI+N⊥ (a factor-of-2 slip). Redo the substitution into M2=2p+p−−pIpIM^2=2p^+p^--p^Ip^IM2=2p+p−−pIpI and show that the mass now depends on the center-of-mass momentum pIp^IpI. What physical principle does this violate, and what does the correct coefficient α′\alpha'α′ (not 2α′2\alpha'2α′) buy us?

    Solution

    With the wrong L0⊥=2α′pIpI+N⊥L_0^\perp=2\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perpL0⊥​=2α′pIpI+N⊥, the zero-mode relation gives 2p+p−=1α′(2α′pIpI+N⊥+a)=2pIpI+1α′(N⊥+a).2p^+p^-=\frac1{\alpha'}\big(2\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perp+a\big)=2p^Ip^I+\frac1{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp+a\big).2p+p−=α′1​(2α′pIpI+N⊥+a)=2pIpI+α′1​(N⊥+a). Substituting into M2=2p+p−−pIpIM^2=2p^+p^--p^Ip^IM2=2p+p−−pIpI: M2=2pIpI+1α′(N⊥+a)−pIpI=1α′(N⊥+a)+pIpI.M^2=2p^Ip^I+\frac1{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp+a\big)-p^Ip^I=\frac1{\alpha'}\big(N^\perp+a\big)+p^Ip^I.M2=2pIpI+α′1​(N⊥+a)−pIpI=α′1​(N⊥+a)+pIpI. The transverse momentum no longer cancels — a residual +pIpI+p^Ip^I+pIpI survives. This says the string's rest mass depends on how fast its center of mass is moving in the transverse directions, which is nonsense: M2=−pμpμM^2=-p_\mu p^\muM2=−pμ​pμ is a Lorentz invariant and cannot depend on the frame-dependent split into pIp^IpI. The correct coefficient α′\alpha'α′ in L0⊥=α′pIpI+N⊥L_0^\perp=\alpha'p^Ip^I+N^\perpL0⊥​=α′pIpI+N⊥ is exactly what makes 1α′⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI\tfrac1{\alpha'}\cdot\alpha'p^Ip^I=+p^Ip^Iα′1​⋅α′pIpI=+pIpI cancel the −pIpI-p^Ip^I−pIpI from the mass-shell relation, leaving M2M^2M2 built purely from the internal excitation N⊥N^\perpN⊥ and the constant aaa. So the factor α′\alpha'α′ is not cosmetic — it is what guarantees the mass formula is Lorentz-consistent.
