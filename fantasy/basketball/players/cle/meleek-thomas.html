Meleek Thomas

Prospect evidence

Thomas averaged 15.6 points, 3.8 rebounds, 2.5 assists, and 1.5 steals in 30.5 college minutes on 43% FG, 42% from three, and 84% FT. The NBA draft profile describes a movement/pull-up shooter, low-turnover secondary creator, and active defender who needs strength and downhill growth; it frames high-end role-player/starter upside rather than primary-star certainty. NBA draft profile

Cleveland traded pick 29 for pick 34, Thomas, and a 2032 second-rounder, then signed him July 1. The official roster feed said he will likely spend most of 2026-27 in the G League unless he dominates camp. NBA trade tracker · Cavaliers signing release · Cavaliers roster news

Fantasy profile

  • 9-cat redraft: undrafted; 3PM/FT%/STL are the future hooks, but the volume is absent.
  • Points leagues: dynasty taxi-squad asset; scoring without rebounds/assists is difficult to roster in shallow formats.
  • Dynasty: second-round rookie hold with a clean specialist-to-starter development path. Hashtag Basketball's July expert board ranked him 355th overall and its crowd board 352nd; the newly loaded profile had no statistical row, so those numbers are early sentiment rather than a mature projection. Josh Lloyd and Stephen Gillaspie discussed his NBA readiness and translation June 4; the accessible description confirms topics, not a verbatim ranking. Hashtag dynasty rankings · Locked On Fantasy Basketball

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: almost entirely G League, with no usable redraft output.
  • Base: occasional NBA shooting minutes plus a high-volume Charge role; dynasty hold only.
  • Ceiling: he wins 18-22 NBA minutes, producing roughly two threes and one steal; deep-league relevance, still unlikely standard-league value.

Thomas scored 35 points with two steals and a block in a July Summer League game, but the accompanying Reddit excitement is community signal, not role evidence. Reddit prospect discussion, July 14, 2026

RotoWire also featured Thomas in July 13 and July 14 Summer League videos centered on breakouts and what might translate to Year 1. The videos are logged as watch-list evidence; this page does not infer an untranscribed rank or projection from their titles. RotoWire July 13 · RotoWire July 14

Career record

Pre-NBA: Overtime Elite (2023-25, McDonald's All-American, consensus five-star), then one season at Arkansas (2025-26): 15.6 PTS, 3.8 REB, 2.5 AST, 1.5 STL in 30.5 MPG across all 37 games (21 starts) on .435/.416/.843 splits; SEC All-Freshman Team, led the Razorbacks in steals, second-most points ever by an Arkansas freshman (579). Draft: 2026, Round 2, Pick 34, Sacramento — draft rights traded to Cleveland (with a 2032 second) for pick 29.

No NBA record. Most recent competitive season is the 2025-26 Arkansas year above; he signed a standard (not two-way) contract with Cleveland on July 1, 2026.

Durability: Played all 37 games as a freshman — one of only three Razorbacks to do so. Nothing on the injury ledger.

Trajectory: The college record shows a real three-level scoring guard — 41.6% from three on volume with 84% FT and 1.5 steals — whose weaknesses (strength, rim pressure) are the developmental kind, not the skill kind. In 2026-27 he competes for nothing more than injury-contingent guard minutes behind Cleveland's set rotation, with the Charge (G League) as his stated primary assignment. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if a Strus/Merrill-type transaction plus camp dominance hands him 18+ NBA minutes as a shooter — a dynasty-stash timeline, not a redraft one.
