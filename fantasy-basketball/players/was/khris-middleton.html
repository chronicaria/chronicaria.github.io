Khris Middleton

Khris Middleton is now a low-minute veteran connector whose remaining fantasy usefulness is concentrated in FT%, low turnovers and injury-created creation bursts.

Role and baseline

  • 2025-26: 63 games, 22.8 MPG overall; 34 Washington games at 24.3 MPG. The total-season line was 10.2 points, 3.7 rebounds and 2.8 assists on .420 FG and .875 FT.
  • Transaction: returned July 8 in a six-team sign-and-trade on a reported three-year, $17.6 million contract.
  • 2026-27 base role: managed backup power forward/secondary creator; 8 base minutes. His 35-year-old body and the development priority make a larger assumption too aggressive.

Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: deep streamer only. FT% and low turnovers are still useful, but 8 minutes cannot support standard-league volume.
  • Points: injury streamer when the team needs veteran shot creation.
  • Dynasty: no meaningful premium; age and recurring maintenance outweigh the contract length.
  • Trade sensitivity: a move to a thinner contender rotation could raise his immediate minutes, while a Washington stay preserves mentoring value more than fantasy value.

Floor, median and ceiling

  • Floor: frequent rest, single-digit minutes or a non-rotation role.
  • Median: 8-minute connector outside the top 200.
  • Ceiling: 24 healthy minutes during injuries and a temporary top-140 run.

Three-time All-Star (2019, 2020, 2022), 2021 NBA champion, Tokyo Olympic gold medalist.

Durability: The defining risk for a decade: 2016 hamstring surgery cost him the first 50 games of 2016-17 (29 GP); offseason wrist surgery plus knee soreness cut 2022-23 to 33 games; recurring lower-body maintenance held him to 37 games in 2024-25. Even his last full-strength seasons (2019-22) ran 62-68 games with managed minutes. The 63 games of 2025-26 were his most since 2021-22, but at 21-24 minutes a night.

Trajectory: The table is a complete star arc: second-round pick, seven seasons as a 18-21-point three-level scorer and secondary creator, then a post-2022 body-driven decline into a 10-point connector on falling FG%. The .875 FT and low turnovers survived the decline; the burst did not. In 2026-27 he competes only for backup-forward organizer minutes in a development-first Washington rotation at age 35. He becomes fantasy-relevant solely as a short-window streamer when injuries hand him 20-plus minutes of creation — the 24.3-MPG Dallas stretch last season shows that version still produces a usable points-league line, and nothing more.
