Fantasy case

LeVert averaged 7.4 points, 2.7 assists, 0.9 steals, and 0.7 blocks in 19.2 minutes across 60 games in 2025-26. Milwaukee acquired him with second-round picks for Taurean Prince and Gary Harris on July 8. The picks and salary structure may matter as much as LeVert's on-court role.

Low case: waived, traded, or DNP-heavy. Base: six minutes of emergency creation. High: injuries or consolidation restore 20+ minutes, creating assists-and-stocks streaming value. His historical FG% and FT% volatility keep him less attractive in nine-cat than in deep points formats.

Learning notes

LeVert is valuable as evidence of Milwaukee's transaction flexibility, not as a draft target. Watch for a clean 18-minute role before treating his name recognition as fantasy relevance.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Michigan, four seasons (2012-16): 2.3 PPG as a freshman, 12.9/4.3/2.9 as a sophomore (second-team All-Big Ten), then injury-shortened junior (14.9 PPG, 18 GP) and senior (16.5 PPG, 15 GP) years — three foot surgeries in 22 months, ending with a Jones fracture. Draft: 2016, round 1, pick 20, Indiana Pacers; traded on draft night to Brooklyn for Thaddeus Young.

Durability: Has never played more than 74 games. The file is long: three college foot surgeries; a right-leg subtalar dislocation in November 2018 (42 games missed); a thumb injury in 2019-20; renal cell carcinoma discovered in the January 2021 Harden-trade physical, with tumor surgery that month. Since 2022-23 he has been steadier (74, 68, 64, 60 games) with rotation-status rather than injury driving the recent dips.

Trajectory: A decade-long slide from fringe All-Star candidate (20.2 PPG in 2020-21) to bench creator: scoring has fallen five straight seasons since that peak, and 2025-26 (7.4 PPG on .417 in 19 minutes) was the worst full season of his career. What survives is the secondary playmaking and stocks — around 1.0 steals with rising block numbers. In 2026-27 he competes for Milwaukee's second-unit handling role on a post-Giannis roster that acquired him as much for salary and picks as for play. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if he holds a clean 18-20 minute role, which would make him an assists-and-stocks deep-league stream; his FG% makes anything more a points-league-only case.
