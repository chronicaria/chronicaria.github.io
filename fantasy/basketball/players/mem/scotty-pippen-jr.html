Range and risks

  • Strengths: elite steals rate, assists and rim-pressure scoring.
  • Liabilities: turnovers, low threes and potentially negative FT impact.
  • Floor/base/ceiling: cut, traded or medically limited / 8-minute third guard / Jerome exits and Clayton slides off the ball, putting Pippen at 22-24 minutes for roughly 11/5 with 1.8 steals.
  • Branches: a Jerome departure is the main ceiling trigger; renewed toe trouble or a roster cut creates the floor. He is now the guard most exposed to the six-player consolidation.

Pippen had left-toe surgery before 2025-26, then underwent a right-big-toe sesamoidectomy March 17, 2026. Memphis expected him ready for 2026-27, but no later full-clearance report was located. Public roster analysis also treats him as one of several guards exposed to consolidation. The model therefore discounts both GP and team security rather than extrapolating the 1.9-steal sample. Career record

Pre-NBA: Three seasons at Vanderbilt. 12.0 points and 3.6 assists on .393/.362/.709 as a freshman (SEC All-Freshman), then 20.8 points, 4.9 assists and 1.8 steals on .428/.358/.850 as a sophomore and 20.4 points, 4.5 assists and 1.9 steals on .416/.325/.749 as a junior, leading the SEC in scoring and taking first-team all-conference honors in both years. Draft: undrafted (2022). Signed a two-way with the Lakers on July 1, 2022, waived October 2023, then produced heavily in the G League for South Bay — 21.0 points, 6.0 assists and 2.0 steals across 12 Showcase Cup starts including a 41-point game, then 18.2 points, 6.4 rebounds, 5.8 assists and 2.8 steals in five regular-season games — before Memphis signed him to a two-way on January 16, 2024 and converted him to a standard deal in October 2024.

Durability: One healthy season out of four. 2022-23 was a two-way year with only six NBA appearances by assignment rather than injury. 2024-25 is the outlier at 79 games. 2025-26 collapsed to 10 games around two sesamoidectomies — a left-toe procedure that cost him the start of the season and a right-big-toe procedure on March 17, 2026 that ended it. Memphis expected him available for 2026-27, but no public full-clearance report has been located.

Trajectory: The rate profile has been consistent and good every time he has played real minutes: about 21 minutes, 10-13 points, 4.5 assists, and a steals rate that is the best on the Memphis roster. Nothing about the skill is in question. What is in question is his feet and his roster spot — he is behind Ty Jerome and Walter Clayton Jr. in the guard order and is the name most often cited in Memphis's need to shed standard contracts. The path to fantasy relevance is not a performance leap but an opening: a Jerome trade or injury pushing him to 22-24 minutes would restore roughly 11 points, five assists and 1.8 steals, which is rosterable in categories on the steals alone. Two toe surgeries in one season is the argument for treating that as a late-season pickup rather than a draft-day bet.
