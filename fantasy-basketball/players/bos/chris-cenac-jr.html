Chris Cenac Jr

Why the line looks like this

Boston selected Cenac 27th and signed him to a rookie-scale contract. In one Houston season, he averaged 9.5 points and 7.6 rebounds while shooting 48.5% overall and 33.3% from three, with six double-doubles. Boston's official profile emphasized size, athleticism, energy and upside rather than NBA-ready offensive polish.

Summer League reinforced both sides of the profile. Cenac posted 14 points, 10 rebounds and four blocks in his debut, later added 16 points and four blocks, and had a 10-point, 12-rebound game. The rim protection and activity looked translatable; the shooting remained uneven. None of it solves a depth chart containing Queta, Robinson and Garza, so the exact 240-minute team baseline assigns only one minute and treats meaningful NBA work as contingent.

Sources: Celtics official Cenac draft profile, NBA Summer League Celtics–Kings recap, Celtics community event with Cenac and Dillon Mitchell.

Category shape

  • Projectable strengths: blocks, rebounds, lob finishing and transition activity.
  • Swing skill: three-point and free-throw touch; a real jumper changes the archetype materially.
  • Costs now: raw reads, fouls/turnovers and no secure NBA minutes.
  • Build fit: dynasty taxi squad; in redraft, only an emergency blocks stream after multiple injuries.

Floor, base, ceiling

  • Floor: spends almost the entire year in Maine and remains a multi-year project.
  • Base: 25 NBA appearances at one simultaneous competitive minute, with development occurring outside Boston's regular rotation.
  • Ceiling: injuries and fast processing earn 12-16 NBA minutes in the second half; about 5 points, 4 rebounds and one block, useful only in deep leagues but a major dynasty signal.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Houston, one season (2025-26): 37 GP (36 starts), 9.5 PTS, 7.9 REB (team-leading), 0.7 AST, 0.8 STL, 0.5 BLK on .485 FG / .333 3P / .621 FT; NABC All-Gulf District second team on a 30-7 Sweet Sixteen team. Draft: 2026, Round 1, Pick 27, Boston Celtics (signed rookie-scale contract July 6, 2026).

No NBA record — 2026 draftee; most recent competitive season is Houston 2025-26 plus the 2026 Las Vegas Summer League (debut of 14 points, 10 rebounds, 4 blocks).

Durability: 37 of 37 games in his lone college season; no injury flags on record.

Trajectory: The record is one college season of rebounding, defensive activity and modest scoring volume — a classic tools-over-polish first-rounder. In 2026-27 he competes for the fourth-big spot behind Queta, Robinson and Garza, with most development expected in Maine. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if multiple Boston centers miss time and he shows he can defend without fouling; the dynasty case rests on the blocks/rebounds base plus the unproven jumper.
