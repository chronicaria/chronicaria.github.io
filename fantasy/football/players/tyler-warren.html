A move TE in Indianapolis — a tight end used as a receiver rather than a blocker — and the reason to take him is scarcity at his position, not his raw score.

The role

Tight end is the position where the job, not the talent, decides everything: most starting tight ends are hired to block and are on the field constantly without the ball ever being thrown to them. A move TE is the other kind — split out wide and used as a receiver, effectively a large slot receiver. In this 10-team league, TE10 is the replacement reference, so a receiving role must clear that higher baseline to matter.

The number

The promoted file lists 191.6 projected points, 78 catches, and 10-team proxy ADP 64.8. In this league each catch pays a full point; the line remains a projection, not an observation.

The risk

His risk column reads low, so the honest version is the projection caveat rather than a named threat: this is a modelled target share for a player at a position whose usage swings hardest on one coaching decision, and roles are not settled until the August 30 roster cutdown. Indianapolis also feeds a three-down bell cow (Jonathan Taylor) and a field-stretching Z (Alec Pierce) out of the same passing budget — see The conserved team touch budget. Availability, though, is the one thing not in question: he has missed nothing at all, and his catch line is close to a repeat of a full season he already played rather than a modelled leap.

The context

He is 24.3 on opening day, and Indianapolis took him 14th overall in 2025 out of Penn State — first-round capital, spent by the team still playing him, which is about the strongest pre-season signal that a role is intended (Draft capital dominance). He has one NFL season and it is a clean one: 17 games of 17, nothing missed, never listed Out. The official report listed him Questionable twice, for a toe and an illness. He was on the field for 83.8% of Indianapolis's offensive snaps (Fantasy football glossary) and drew 112 targets — throws in his direction, 21.1% of the team's — catching 76 for 817 yards. That matters: the projected 78 catches for 836 yards is not a forecast of growth, it is last season restated. Rookie contract, fully guaranteed, free agent in 2029.
