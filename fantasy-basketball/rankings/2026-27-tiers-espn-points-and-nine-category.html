How these tiers were cut

Within a tier, adjacent players are close enough that preference, build fit, and injury appetite should decide the pick; across a tier break, they are not. Breaks are placed at natural gaps in the value metric — z-score for nine-category, projected fantasy points per game for ESPN — with tighter thresholds at the top of the board where a small gap is real and looser ones later where noise grows. Generated by Data/build_tiers.py from the corrected exports; regenerate after any projection change.

The two formats do NOT share tiers. A player can sit two tiers apart between formats — that is the format divergence, not an error. The older editorial 2026-27 fantasy basketball tiers note predates the engine repair and is kept for its scenario prose only; these tables supersede its tier assignments.

Reading the two boards together

  • A player high in both formats is safe at cost in either league. A player high in exactly one is a target only in that league — check which before falling in love with a price.
  • ESPN tiers reward accumulation, so games played does more work: the Season FP column is the tiebreak inside every ESPN tier.
  • Nine-category tiers price scarcity, so specialists (elite stocks, elite percentages on volume) jump tiers that ESPN ignores.

Open questions

  • Tier thresholds are editorial constants; a leave-one-out stability check (do tiers survive one player's GP moving ±8?) has not been run.
  • The tiers inherit the projections' GP assumptions wholesale — the weakest input, per Projecting games played and availability.
  • Position is deliberately ignored here; centre scarcity can justify reaching one tier early in 12-team leagues, per Positional scarcity and eligibility in 2026-27.
