Team Desk · Northwest Division

Denver Nuggets

The Denver build concentrates fantasy value in Nikola Jokić and Jamal Murray, while health, two unresolved restricted free agents, and an unproven bench create unusually wide role tails behind them.

Executive fantasy read

  • Nikola Jokić is a defensible pick at either No. 1 or No. 2 in every redraft format. ESPN's live preseason data had him first in its standard/points ordering and second in roto on July 18; ESPN's April points article and Dan Titus' NBA-hosted early nine-category/High Score list both ranked him first, while Athlon placed him second behind Victor Wembanyama.4 11 12 13
  • Jamal Murray is the only other Denver player with a stable standard-league case: ESPN showed him No. 32 standard and No. 25 roto. His 2025-26 leap was real—25.4 points, 7.1 assists, 3.3 threes and 88.7% FT—but the projection regresses a career season slightly.14
  • Christian Braun is the format trap: ESPN's early snapshot ranked him 65th in standard scoring but 189th in roto. Minutes and efficient real basketball do not automatically create scarce-category fantasy value.
  • Peyton Watson is the best upside bet behind the two stars, and this build now treats his return as the base case rather than a branch. He averaged 14.6 points, 4.9 rebounds, 1.1 blocks and 1.5 threes in 29.6 minutes in 2025-26; he remained an unsigned restricted free agent at the cutoff and finished the season with a hamstring strain, but Denver's reported posture is that it expects to keep him.14 15 16 2
  • The current signed bench is a deep-league watch list, not a standard-league draft target. Trevon Brazile and Bryce Hopkins produced useful July Summer League lines, but Summer League changes the tail of a role forecast, not its NBA baseline.17

Other RFA branches

  • Spencer Jones returns without Watson: project 18 MPG off the signed-only contingency, primarily removed from Strawther, Diallo, Brazile and Braun. Jones remains a low-usage deep-league player.
  • Both return: project Watson 25 and Spencer Jones 12; Denver then faces a roster/cost move, so a static “everyone stays” minute table is not a coherent final-state forecast.

Market update: NBC Sports reported on July 17 that Milwaukee had joined the Clippers and Hawks among potential Watson sign-and-trade suitors. Interest is not a transaction, but it makes a Denver-only projection especially fragile.3

Injury and transaction uncertainty

  • Jokić: missed nearly a month with a hyperextended knee and played exactly 65 regular-season games; his rate confidence is elite but availability should no longer be modeled as automatic 70-plus.16
  • Gordon: played 36 games, missed 39 of Denver's first 62 with a right hamstring injury, then missed most of the first-round series with calf trouble. This is the team's largest recurring availability risk.22 16
  • Watson: a right hamstring strain kept him out of the full playoff series. A lucrative RFA outcome or sign-and-trade can change both team and role.16 15
  • Braun/Cam Johnson: both missed meaningful time in an injury season in which Denver players lost 245 combined games counting the playoffs.16
  • Jokić contract: he publicly said he wants to remain in Denver but plans to wait until next summer for a larger potential extension. That is not an immediate redraft role threat, but it increases 2027 dynasty/team-direction uncertainty.23
  • Schedule: the full 2026-27 schedule was not available at the cutoff, so no playoff-week or back-to-back edge is claimed.

Team outcome range

The refreshed Andrew RAPM-decomp public projection dated July 18 has Denver at 49.8 mean wins, a 42-57 p10-p90 band, 96.0% playoff probability, and 1.96% title probability. Its roster mirrors the signed-only state and omits Watson and Spencer Jones, so it is not an unconditional final-roster forecast. This supersedes the stale 45.3-win July 9 briefing preserved elsewhere in the vault.9

Scenario Encyclopedia projection Fantasy mechanism
Floor 40-43 wins Gordon/Jokić availability repeats, both RFAs leave, reserve creation and defense fail; Murray and Jokić carry extreme volume but totals suffer.
Base 48-51 wins Jokić/Murray play mid-60s games, Watson is retained on the modal path, the top five remains intact, and one reserve stabilizes the non-Jokić minutes.
Ceiling 55-58 wins Better health, Watson retained and healthy through a full season, Brazile/Diallo defend enough to earn minutes, and the starting offense recreates the 54-win 2025-26 floor.

Bet365 listed Denver at +2200 for the 2027 title on July 10, behind San Antonio, Oklahoma City, Boston and New York. That market number is a sentiment cross-check, not a win projection.10

  5. Whether Braun's ankle/role restores his rim pressure after a down 2025-26.
