Durability: GP totals (10, 35, 49) reflect a late 2024 arrival from Partizan and end-of-rotation status, not injuries; no significant health record.

Trajectory: Three part-time seasons say the per-minute skill is real and stable: roughly 9 points, a three and 0.7 blocks in 14 minutes every year, at .476/.778 — a genuine stretch-five scoring rate that has never been given a rotation. The equally stable warning is the rebounding (3.4 per game, weak for a 7-footer). In 2026-27 he competes for the fourth/fifth big spot behind Davis, Sarr and Ayton; he becomes fantasy-relevant only if two of those bigs are out simultaneously and he holds 20+ MPG, at which point the threes-plus-blocks combination is a legitimate deep-league specialist line — exactly the conditional this dossier already prices at zero base minutes.
