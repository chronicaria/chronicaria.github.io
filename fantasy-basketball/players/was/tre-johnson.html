Fantasy dossier

  • 9-cat: top 150-190 base. Threes and positive FT% are draftable in deep leagues; low boards, assists and stocks keep him narrow.
  • Points: deep-league scoring streamer; the format rewards his clearest skill.
  • Dynasty: top 80-110 because the shooting foundation is real and the veteran wing logjam can clear.
  • Community context: a July Wizards discussion imagined roughly 22 minutes as a sixth-man scorer. It is sentiment, not evidence; this model is slightly lower at 18.

Floor, median and ceiling

  • Floor: crowded bench, sub-16-minute role and waiver status.
  • Median: 18 minutes, nearly two threes and specialist value.
  • Ceiling: 26-28 minutes, high-volume scoring and top-100 fantasy value.

Durability: 60 of 82 as a rookie; the dossier attributes the gaps to foot, ankle and hip interruptions (not confirmed by the Wikipedia ledger, which lists no injuries), and he carried a game-time-decision tag late in the season. Washington also rested him out of July Summer League after one 26-point outing.

Trajectory: The record shows a lottery shooting guard who delivered exactly what the scouting report promised in year one: real scoring volume for a teenager (12.2 PTS, 1.9 3PM, .874 FT in 24 minutes) with thin rebounds, assists and stocks, and a sub-.42 FG% typical of rookie volume shooters. In 2026-27 he competes for the sixth-man scoring role behind George, projected at 18 minutes with a 26-28-minute ceiling if the veteran wing logjam clears. He becomes standard-league relevant when the minutes cross roughly 24 again with better shot quality — threes plus elite FT% is a draftable deep-league base already, and his dynasty case (top 80-110) rests on the shooting foundation being real.
