Career record

Four-year career: 6.4 PPG, 5.7 RPG, 1.7 BPG. Draft: 2026, Round 2, Pick 46 (Orlando Magic), draft-night trade of his rights to Washington; two-way signed July 7, 2026.

No NBA record — 2026 second-round rookie; most recent season is the 2025-26 Tennessee year above.

Durability: No notable injury history; 35 games in his final college season and 70 in two Ohio State years. Trajectory: Four seasons of the same shape — elite FG%, rebounds, and blocks with almost no scoring growth or free-throw progress — which is exactly what makes him a clean punt-FT specialist projection rather than a developmental mystery. In 2026-27 he competes for Capital City Go-Go minutes and emergency-center priority over Reese and Vukcevic in Washington's four-deep room, capped at 50 NBA games by the two-way. He becomes fantasy-relevant only if center injuries force him into 18+ NBA minutes, where the block rate could make him a top-180 blocks/FG% streamer despite the .635 FT%.
