<p>Moonshot upside: 8/10. Kill risk: 7/10.</p>
<h2 id="evidence">Evidence</h2>
<p>GSI Technology is a legacy high-performance SRAM company trying to turn Gemini-II, a compute-in-memory Associative Processing Unit, into an edge-AI hardware platform for defense, drones, smart-city surveillance, vector search, SAR, and low-power inference.</p>
<p>FY2026 revenue was about <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>25.1</mn><mi>M</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>u</mi><mi>p</mi><mn>22</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">25.1M, up 22% YoY, with 54.5% gross margin and a </annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">25.1</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mpunct">,</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1667em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">u</span><span class="mord mathnormal">p</span><span class="mord">22</span></span></span></span>13.2M net loss. Cash was about $67.2M at March 31, 2026, no debt, and pro forma cash may be higher after ATM completion.</p>
<p>The problem is explicit: company filings say APU revenue has not been material and about 99% of revenue is SRAM. Government funding, the Sentinel POC, Army SBIR work, Taiwan smart-city Phase I, and third-party benchmark work are validation, not production revenue.</p>

The balance sheet gives GSIT time: about <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>67.2</mn><mi>M</mi><mi>c</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>h</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>n</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>d</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>b</mi><mi>t</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>a</mi><mi>n</mi><mi>d</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>u</mi><mi>g</mi><mi>h</mi><mi>l</mi><mi>y</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">67.2M cash, no debt, and roughly </annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">67.2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mord mathnormal">c</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal">s</span><span class="mord mathnormal">h</span><span class="mpunct">,</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1667em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">d</span><span class="mord mathnormal">e</span><span class="mord mathnormal">b</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mpunct">,</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1667em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">an</span><span class="mord mathnormal">d</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">ug</span><span class="mord mathnormal">h</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0197em;">l</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">y</span></span></span></span>70M working capital at March 31, 2026. That is the strongest survival feature in the thesis.</p>
<p>The technical case is real but narrow. Gemini-II looks most interesting for data-movement-bound edge workloads: vector search, RAG retrieval, SAR/image/sensor processing, GPS-denied navigation, object detection, smart-city video analytics, and low-power inference where time-to-first-token matters more than cloud-scale throughput. The Cornell/MICRO/arXiv work is the best independent technical evidence, but it does not prove broad replacement of NVIDIA Jetson/Thor, Qualcomm edge NPUs, AMD/Xilinx/Intel FPGA paths, Hailo, Ambarella, or custom defense silicon.</p>
<p>The investment case fails if validation never becomes procurement. FY2026 revenue was <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>25.1</mn><mi>M</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>g</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>m</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>g</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi><mi>w</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>s</mi><mn>54.5</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">25.1M, gross margin was 54.5%, and net loss was </annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8778em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">25.1</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mpunct">,</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1667em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord mathnormal">oss</span><span class="mord mathnormal">ma</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span><span class="mord mathnormal">in</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0269em;">w</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal">s</span><span class="mord">54.5</span></span></span></span>13.2M, but APU revenue remained immaterial and SRAM supplied about 99% of revenue. Avnet concentration and end-customer swings still matter while R&#x26;D rises toward Plato and future APU work.</p>
<h2 id="bear-case">Bear case</h2>
<p>This may be a technically elegant architecture that never beats NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Jetson, FPGA, or custom ASIC ecosystems in actual procurement. If Gemini-II remains a demo platform, the market can reprice GSIT as a slow memory business plus cash.</p>
<h2 id="falsifiers">Falsifiers</h2>
<ul>
<li>FY2027 passes with no named Gemini-II production customer or material APU revenue.</li>
<li>SRAM revenue rolls over while quarterly burn stays high.</li>
<li>Sentinel, Taiwan, or Army programs fail to produce follow-on orders.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="verdict">Verdict</h2>
<p>Active research, but behind DUOT and LTRX in practical urgency. GSIT is one of the few true AI-hardware moonshots here, but promotion requires signed Gemini-II orders above $5M and then visible repeat APU revenue.</p>
