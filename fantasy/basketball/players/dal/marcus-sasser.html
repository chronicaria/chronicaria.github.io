Marcus Sasser

Sasser averaged 5.2 points, 2.0 assists, 1.0 rebound, 0.5 steals, and 41.5% from three in 12.0 minutes across 38 games for Detroit. The official game notes also record 24 missed games across right-hip impingement, soreness, and strain.

Fantasy translation

  • Nine-category: waiver watch for threes and scoring only when Irving or another guard sits.
  • Points: clearer streamer path than in categories because a shot-volume spike matters immediately.
  • Dynasty: age-25 depth hold in very deep leagues; he needs a 20-minute role to appreciate.

Role, floor, ceiling, uncertainty

  • Median role: 14-minute reserve scoring guard, often beside a larger initiator.
  • Floor: irregular rotation and renewed hip trouble.
  • Ceiling: 22 MPG with 11 points, two threes, and four assists during an Irving absence.
  • Role distinction: Nembhard is the better organizer; Sasser is the stronger pull-up scoring bet. Neither reproduces Irving's whole job.

Career record

Pre-NBA: Four seasons at Houston. 8.1 points on .363/.352/.758 as a freshman, 13.7 on .380/.335/.852 as a sophomore, then a 12-game junior year cut short on December 24, 2021 by a fractured foot while he led the team at 17.7 points on .437 from three. He returned for 2022-23 and averaged 16.8 points, 3.1 assists, 2.8 rebounds and 1.6 steals on .438/.384/.848 in 36 starts for the No. 1-ranked Cougars. Consensus first-team All-American, Jerry West Award, and AAC Player of the Year in 2023. Draft: 2023, first round, No. 25 by Memphis; traded to Detroit on draft night.

Durability: The trend is the problem: 71 games, then 57, then 38. The 2025-26 shortfall is 24 missed games logged by Detroit as right-hip impingement, soreness, and strain. The college record adds a fractured foot that ended 2021-22 after 12 games.

Trajectory: Three straight years of declining minutes, games, and scoring on a Detroit team that kept adding guards ahead of him — the rookie season remains his best. The shooting has held up throughout (.859 career FT, .386 career 3P), which is the skill that keeps him employed, but the .390 field goal percentage in 2025-26 shows what happens when the volume drops and the looks get harder. In Dallas he is competing with Ryan Nembhard for backup guard minutes behind Kyrie Irving; he is the better pull-up shooter, Nembhard the better organizer. He becomes streamable if Irving misses time and Dallas chooses shot creation over ball movement, and only becomes rosterable in categories if that opening lasts more than a week.
