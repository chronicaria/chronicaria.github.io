That displayed market cap likely trails pro forma dilution after the ATM and underwritten offering; mechanical pro forma market cap is probably closer to the mid-$200M range depending on final incremental share count.</p>
<p>LTRX is the cleanest operating story in the top three. Q3 FY2026 revenue was <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>30.2</mn><mi>M</mi><mo separator="true">,</mo><mi>g</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>m</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>r</mi><mi>g</mi><mi>i</mi><mi>n</mi><mi>s</mi><mi>t</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>y</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>d</mi><mi>n</mi><mi>e</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>r</mi><mn>43</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">30.2M, gross margin stayed near 43%, operating cash flow was positive for the nine-month period, debt was manageable, and the June offering added roughly </annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.8889em;vertical-align:-0.1944em;"></span><span class="mord">30.2</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mpunct">,</span><span class="mspace" style="margin-right:0.1667em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord mathnormal">oss</span><span class="mord mathnormal">ma</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">g</span><span class="mord mathnormal">in</span><span class="mord mathnormal">s</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0359em;">y</span><span class="mord mathnormal">e</span><span class="mord mathnormal">d</span><span class="mord mathnormal">n</span><span class="mord mathnormal">e</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.0278em;">r</span><span class="mord">43</span></span></span></span>32.3M net after full option exercise. <p>The bear case is also straightforward. Nine-month FY2026 revenue was down 4.6% YoY because a prior Gridspertise program disappeared, and Software and Services was still only 7.6% of nine-month revenue. If drones are mostly hardware modules and payload integration while customers capture the strategic value, LTRX remains an industrial IoT hardware company with AI-adjacent upside rather than a platform.</p>
<h2 id="bear-case">Bear case</h2>
<p>LTRX may be a hardware turnaround with AI vocabulary rather than a high-multiple AI platform. If drone revenue fails to scale or software/services do not expand, the equity is less moonshot and more cyclical small-cap hardware.</p>
<h2 id="falsifiers">Falsifiers</h2>
<ul>
<li>FY2027 guidance fails to show double-digit growth.</li>
<li>Drone revenue misses the <span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>10</mn><mi>M</mi><mo>−</mo></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">10M-</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.7667em;vertical-align:-0.0833em;"></span><span class="mord">10</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.109em;">M</span><span class="mord">−</span></span></span></span>14M FY2026 range.</li>

</ul>
<h2 id="verdict">Verdict</h2>
<p>Active research as the risk-adjusted anchor. LTRX is not the flashiest candidate, but it gives the basket one name with real revenue, improving liquidity, positive operating cash flow, and identifiable drone/edge-AI exposure.</p>
